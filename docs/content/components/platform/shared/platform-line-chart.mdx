## Purpose

Chart 4 of the owner's «Gráficas del perfil» (2026-10-25): a series in time and a slider that chooses one of its points. The line runs over its filled area, with a dashed grid line every `gridStep` and its value at the left; the chosen point is marked, a hatched slice drops from it to the axis, and its value rides in a tag over it. Under the chart, the slider — a gradient track from the panel's wash to the primary — with the first date at its left and the chosen one under its thumb. It starts on the last point.

## Props

| Prop          | Type                           | Required           | Meaning                                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `points`      | `readonly PlatformLinePoint[]` | yes                | `{ label, value }`, oldest first; fewer than two draw nothing.         |
| `label`       | `string`                       | yes                | The chart in words, its `aria-label`: every point with its value.      |
| `sliderLabel` | `string`                       | yes                | The slider's name («Partida elegida»).                                 |
| `valuePrefix` | `string`                       | no · default `""`  | Before a value in the tag and the slider's text: «≈ ».                 |
| `gridStep`    | `number`                       | no · default `50`  | Every how much a grid line crosses.                                    |
| `width`       | `number`                       | no · default `580` | The viewBox; it stretches to its container's width keeping this ratio. |
| `height`      | `number`                       | no · default `120` | Its height.                                                            |

## Behaviour

The slider is Radix's (`Slider` of `radix-ui`, no styles of its own): the arrows move a point, Inicio and Fin go to either end, and the pointer drags or clicks; its thumb says «13 oct, ≈ 850» (`aria-valuetext`). The control is inset by the chart's side padding, so its ends line up with the first and last points. Nothing animates.
