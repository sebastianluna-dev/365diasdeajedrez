## Purpose

How an exercise is solved (D01, D19–D21, D33, D37, D39; PLAN.md §5.3). It receives the exercise as data — `{ id, title, prompt, kind, mateIn, solutionPgn, hints, explanation, difficulty }` —, where it is solved, what comes after it, how it counts and the action that records it, so its story stubs the action and it imports no `.actions.ts`.

`ExerciseSolverView` takes the same data plus a `SolverSession`, the clock, the save state and the handlers: it is what `ExerciseSolver` draws from its hook, and what the stories draw from a session reached through `reduceSession`, because chessground ignores synthetic pointer events.

## Behaviour

- **Playing** (D01, D02). The student moves (drag or tap; the board's promotion chooser). A move of the solution — the main line or any variant — says «¡Bien!», and after 450 ms the opponent's main reply is played with its sound. The exercise is over when a line ends.
- **The first wrong move fails** (D19). It stays on the board for 600 ms in the danger colour (`lastMoveTone="error"`, and a red ring that comes and goes), then it is taken back; «No es la jugada. Sigue intentándolo o mira la solución.»; the head gains «Fallado». The attempt is **submitted at that moment**, so leaving the page cannot hide a failure. The student may go on to learn: later moves are not scored.
- **«Pista»** (D20, D37): the author's texts first, numbered under the board; after them, or with none, the piece to move is circled (`hintSquare`). Each press counts; a pass with a hint is «Resuelto con pista» (D33).
- **«Ver la solución»** (D20): submits a failure with `solutionShown` if nothing was submitted yet, then walks through the main line with its comments — ‹ ›, and an autoplay at 900 ms a step that is off under reduced motion.
- **The result** replaces the actions: «¡Resuelto!», «Resuelto con pista», «Fallado» with «Jugaste Cxe5; la jugada era Dxh7+.», or «Viste la solución»; the explanation; «Ver la solución completa» (a read-only `GameViewer` over the solution, variants and comments, under the screen); «Siguiente ejercicio» (accent) or «Volver a la serie», and «Reintentar», which starts again **unscored**: nothing is submitted, and the result says only how it went this time («Esta vez, sin errores»), never «¡Resuelto!». The same holds when a `solve` page's attempt comes back `scored: false` (a stale page after Back, a second tab): the server's answer wins over the page's mode — and one that comes back `stored: true` (a class block's) is told in the `block` mode, «¡Resuelto!» with the class note.
- **The submit** is `submitExerciseAttempt` (`services/exercises/exercises.actions.ts`): the moves up to the decision, the hints and whether the solution was shown; the server replays them. «No se guardó el intento.» when it failed; «Ya lo intentaste hoy: este intento no cuenta.» when the server stored nothing because another tab took the day first; «… cuenta para la clase, no para tu racha.» when it kept a class block's row (`stored`).
- The clock counts up `mm:ss` from the first paint (D21) and stops at the end.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercise-solver` (553 lines): a grid. From 1024px the board (up to 560px, sticky) on the left and, on the right, the head, the feedback line (`aria-live="polite"`), the panel (hints, the solution's walk, the actions or the result) and the moves played; the full solution under both. Below 1024px one column — the head, the board at `max(min(100vw − 32px, 100svh − 240px), 280px)` so a short screen keeps a board to play on, the feedback, the actions at `_size_lg` for the thumb — and `(width < 1024px) and (orientation: landscape)` puts the board beside the rest again, so a phone on its side (844 × 390) keeps a board to solve on. The head is two lines on a phone: the prompt, then the side's disc (cream for White, ink for Black, the same in both themes), the clock, «3 / 10» and «Fallado».
