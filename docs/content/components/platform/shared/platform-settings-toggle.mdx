## Purpose

The gear of Gmail's bar (2026-10-23): a page with settings puts it in the bar, through [`PlatformHeaderContext`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-header-context), and it opens and closes that page's [`PlatformSettingsPanel`](/components/platform/shared/platform-settings-panel). It takes no props: the panel it opens is the page's one, `#platform-settings`.

## Behaviour

- Material's standard icon button with the gear (Classic Solid). It carries `aria-controls="platform-settings"` and `aria-pressed`, and its name says what pressing it does: «Ajustes», or «Cerrar los ajustes» while the panel is open.
- It sets and removes the root's `data-settings="open"` and follows it, so the panel's ✕ closing the panel unlights it too.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-settings-toggle` (10 lines): `margin-left: auto`, so it sits at the right of the bar's middle, against the tools; open, `.platform-settings-toggle_state_on` lights it in the secondary container (Material's `--container-color` and `--icon-color`), as Gmail lights its own.
