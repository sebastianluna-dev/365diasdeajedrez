## Purpose

The row every list of classes is made of, for the two roles that manage them. **It receives data, not components**: `ClassRowData` is what a service maps a class to, and the action at its end is a datum too (`ClassRowActionData`: a `link` the row draws itself, or a `meeting` that opens the meeting-link popup through [`ClassRowAction`](/components/platform/shared/class-row-action); finalizing joins with phase 6). The staff's chips say what needs them («Sin enlace», «1 sin pago», «Gratis», «Grupal · 4 de 6 plazas»); the teacher's say what there is to prepare («2 para preparar», «1 para después», «1 en tu guion», «Sin contenido», «Sin tema») and never a word about money.

## Props

| Prop            | Type                    | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                             |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `row`           | `ClassRowData`          | yes      | `{ id, href, title, phase, startIso, durationMin, timeZone, nowIso, people, chips, action? }`.                                      |
| `serverActions` | `ClassRowServerActions` | no       | `{ setMeeting? }`: what a `meeting` action opens its popup with ([`ClassRowAction`](/components/platform/shared/class-row-action)). |
| `audience`      | `"staff" \| "teacher"`  | yes      | Whose list it is: the tag's words differ.                                                                                           |

`ClassRowChip` is `{ label, icon, tone? }` with `icon` one of `no-link`, `unpaid`, `free`, `group`, `prepare`, `after`, `script`, `no-content`, `no-topic` and `tone` `warning`, `success` or `neutral`.

## Behaviour

- **The whole row opens the class**: the title is the `<a>`, and its `::after` covers the row (`position: absolute; inset: 0`); the action sits above it (`position: relative; z-index: 1`). Nothing interactive is nested.
- Today's tile wears the accent (`dayKeyIn` of the start and of `nowIso`, in the reader's zone).
- Without an action, a chevron says the row leads somewhere.

## Markup & styling

Block `.class-row` (173 lines): a 64px grid of five columns — the date strip (112px), the phase (148px), the topic and the people, the chips (200px), the action (96px).

**Elements:** `.class-row__when`, `.class-row__hours`, `.class-row__status`, `.class-row__what`, `.class-row__title`, `.class-row__people`, `.class-row__chips`, `.class-row__chip`, `.class-row__chip-icon`, `.class-row__action`, `.class-row__chevron`

**Modifiers and states:** `.class-row__chip_tone_warning`, `.class-row__chip_tone_success`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` folds the phase and the chips under the topic; `(width < 640px)` makes the row a card with a border, the strip and the phase on top. The hover fade is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-success-ink`, `--platform-success-soft`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-surface-neutral`, `--platform-surface-soft`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`, `--platform-warning-ink`, `--platform-warning-soft`
