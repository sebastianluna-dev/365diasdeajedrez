## Purpose

For what helps the first time and is noise every time after: how a field behaves, what a figure means. The text leaves the page and waits behind an "i". A tip is never the only place a rule is said. What the person **needs** in order to fill a field in either stays a visible hint, or is said again where it is broken: the cover's formats and its 1 MB live in the tip of the «Portada» card, and the file picker already offers those four alone and the error names them when a wrong file is dragged in.

## Props

| Prop    | Type                                     | Required | Meaning                                                                                   |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `text`  | `string`                                 | yes      | What the tip says: plain text, a sentence or two.                                         |
| `label` | `string`                                 | yes      | The button's accessible name, which says what the tip is about («Sobre el slug»).         |
| `side`  | `"top" \| "right" \| "bottom" \| "left"` | no       | The side it opens on when there is room. `top` by default; the primitive flips it if not. |

## Behaviour

- **Opens** on hover (after 120 ms), on keyboard focus and on press. The press is ours: a touch screen has no hover, and the primitive on its own _closes_ on press, so the button's `onPointerDown` and `onClick` call `preventDefault` and the click sets it open.
- **Closes** when the pointer leaves, on blur and with Escape.
- While open, the primitive ties the text to the button with `aria-describedby` and gives it `role="tooltip"`; the button is a real `<button type="button">`, so it never submits the form it sits in.
- **Not portalled**, like [`PlatformSelect`](/components/platform/shared/platform-select)'s list: a portal to `<body>` would be inert under a modal `<dialog>`.
- **Kept inside its dialog.** Not being portalled, a tip inside a `<dialog>` is clipped by it, and the primitive on its own only keeps a tip within the window: beside a label at a dialog's left edge it came out cut (seen in the game's form, 2026-10-05). When it opens, the button looks for the `<dialog>` it lives in (`closest("dialog")`) and hands it to the primitive as the box to stay within (`collisionBoundary`, with 8px of `collisionPadding`); outside a dialog it is the window, as before.
- It places nothing: whoever uses it puts the button where it goes (inside an input, next to a heading).

## Markup & styling

Two blocks, because the primitive renders the tip as the button's sibling and not inside it: `.platform-info-tip`, a 28px round button in the subtle grey with `cursor: help`, and `.platform-info-tip__content`, the dark tip (`--platform-text` as background, `--platform-surface` as text), at most 300px wide.

**Elements:** `.platform-info-tip__icon`, `.platform-info-tip__content`, `.platform-info-tip__arrow`

**States (data attributes set by the primitive):** `[data-state="delayed-open"]`, `[data-state="instant-open"]`, `[data-state="closed"]` on the button and on the tip.

**Animation:** `platform-info-tip-in` (160 ms) and `platform-info-tip-out` (120 ms), both off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-selection`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-surface-neutral`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-subtle`

No media queries.

## Usage

A field's «i» is not written by hand: [`PlatformFormField`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form) draws it from its `info`, inside the input's right end. From `components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-name-fields.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
<PlatformFormField name="slug" label="Slug" info={SLUG_INFO} messages={{ patternMismatch: `${SLUG_FORMAT}.` }}>
  <input ref={slugRef} type="text" defaultValue={slug} maxLength={160} pattern={SLUG_PATTERN} required />
</PlatformFormField>
```

Anywhere else, the button must not sit inside a `<label>` around its input: its name would end up in the input's. Give the input an `id` and the label an `htmlFor`, and put the tip beside the input, as the field does.
