## Purpose

The attendance of a student is written once by whoever passes the list and read many times after, by the staff and by the teacher, and both have to read the same thing: the mark, who marked it («Marcó GM Profesor Demo · mié 14 oct, 20:12») and, after a report, who changed it and why («Cambió Staff Demo · jue 15 oct, 10:05 · «Faltó» → «Asistió»: «…»», §3.5 of the plan). So the row is one piece with two modes, and the services map a seat to `AttendanceMarkView` instead of each screen spelling the lines.

## Props

| Prop           | Type                  | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `student`      | `{ id, displayName }` | yes      | The initials and the tone come from them.                                                                                       |
| `details`      | `string[]`            | no       | Lines under the name: the email, how the seat is covered.                                                                       |
| `mark`         | `AttendanceMarkView`  | yes      | `attended` (null is «Sin pasar lista»), `markedLine?`, `changedLine?`, `locked?`, `moved?: { label, href? }`, `replacesLabel?`. |
| `mode`         | `"read" \| "edit"`    | yes      | A locked or moved seat reads even in `edit`.                                                                                    |
| `name`         | `string`              | no       | The field's name in `edit`; `attendance.<student id>` by default; the cards send `yes` or `no`.                                 |
| `defaultValue` | `"yes" \| "no"`       | no       | The current mark, when changing one's own marks.                                                                                |
| `serverError`  | `string`              | no       | What the server said about this seat.                                                                                           |

## Behaviour

- **Edit**: the two cards in a line inside a `PlatformFormGroup` named by the student, `required` with nothing chosen, and `valueMissing` «Marca si Diego Salas asistió o faltó.» when the form is sent without a mark.
- **Read**: «Asistió» in the success tone with `UserCheckIcon`, «Faltó» neutral with `UserXmarkIcon`, «Sin pasar lista»; the lines under the tag; a moved seat shows its label as the tag, a link when the new class is known («Faltó · reprogramada al vie 16 oct»); a locked change carries a lock.

## Markup & styling

Block `.attendance-row` (78 lines): a grid of the portrait, the name with its details and the mark at the end, right-aligned; `_mode_edit` drops the third column; under 640px the mark goes under the name.

**Elements:** `.attendance-row__who`, `.attendance-row__name`, `.attendance-row__detail`, `.attendance-row__mark`, `.attendance-row__moved`, `.attendance-row__line`, `.attendance-row__lock`

**Modifiers:** `.attendance-row_mode_edit`, `.attendance-row__line_kind_changed`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`

## Usage

```tsx
<AttendanceRow
  student={{ id: seat.userId, displayName: seat.displayName }}
  details={[seat.email, seat.coverage.label]}
  mark={seat.mark}
  mode="read"
/>
```
