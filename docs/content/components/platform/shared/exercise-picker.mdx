## Purpose

One piece for the two places a teacher picks exercises (2026-10-16, phase 3 of [Exercises](/platform/exercises)): a series and a class block. The host reads what may be picked — `buildPickableExercisesWhere`, the teacher's usable exercises and the library's (D08), minus what the container already holds — and hands it over as cards; a draft or an archived exercise is never offered. The chosen ids leave the form as hidden inputs **in the order they were ticked**: the cards' DOM order is the list's (the last changed first), and a series or a block takes the tick order, so the cards carry no `name` of their own.

## Behaviour

- From six exercises (`EXERCISE_PICKER_SEARCH_FROM`) a search over the title and the topics, without accents (`foldForSearch`); a chosen card the search hides stays mounted and sent (`hiddenValues`). With nothing matching: «Ningún ejercicio coincide con la búsqueda.»
- The host says what happens to what is chosen and how many fit («Van al final de la serie…», «De 1 a 10 ejercicios…»), and shows its own empty state when there is nothing to offer: the picker only picks.
- Inside a form with controlled Radix cards, so the host submits by hand (`onSubmit` → `startTransition(formAction)`), as every such form does.
