# `PlatformStreakCalendar`

| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Purpose

Chart 8 of the owner's «Gráficas del perfil» (2026-10-25): the weeks of `StreakOverview` (sixteen of them, Monday to Sunday) as pills of the panel's white, one per week, Monday on top. Each day is a dot as deep a blue as what was done that day (`activityLevel`: `--platform-m3-heat-1` to `-3`), a grey speck for a day of nothing; the days still to come this week are left empty. The current streak is hatched across the weeks it spans, back from today — or from yesterday, when today is still to study —, today is ringed, the months' first three letters sit over their first week and every other day's letter at the left. A tag over this week's column says the streak.

## Props

| Prop     | Type                                | Required | Meaning                                      |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `weeks`  | `readonly (readonly StreakDay[])[]` | yes      | `StreakOverview.weeks`.                      |
| `streak` | `StudyStreak`                       | yes      | `{ days, studiedToday }`: what is hatched.   |
| `label`  | `string`                            | yes      | The calendar in words, its `aria-label`.     |
| `tag`    | `string`                            | no       | The pill over this week's column: «12 días». |

## Behaviour

None. It is drawn at its own size (22px a week) and only shrinks, never grows, because its letters are SVG text. The hatch is an SVG `<pattern>` with its id from `useId`. Its legend is [`PlatformStreakLegend`](/components/platform/shared/platform-streak-legend), placed by the host.

## Usage

```tsx
<PlatformStreakCalendar
  weeks={overview.weeks}
  streak={overview.streak}
  tag="12 días"
  label="Tus últimas 16 semanas: …"
```
