## Purpose

The legend of [`PlatformStreakCalendar`](/components/platform/shared/platform-streak-calendar): a grey speck for a day of nothing and the three blues, between «Menos» and «Más». A piece of its own so the host puts it where its layout wants it (beside the calendar on the profile). `aria-hidden`: the calendar's label says the data.
