## Behaviour

- Above the table, «Resolvieron N de M»: the students who solved every exercise of the series, of those it reaches now. Per row, «3 de 5».
- A cell carries an icon and a text, never a bare glyph: `CircleCheckIcon` in the success ink with «Resuelto» for the reader; `CircleXmarkIcon` in the danger red with the count beside it and «Fallado, 2 intentos» for the reader; nothing drawn and «Sin intentar» for the reader. The reader's words are an element clipped out of view (`.series-progress-table__reader`).
- The columns are the exercises' numbers; the legend under the table says which exercise each number is.
- A cell is solved with a pass in that series, failed with attempts and no pass, untried with none: the student's own marks, so the two screens agree. An attempt elsewhere marks nothing.
- With no student, the empty state in place of the table.

## Markup & styling

Block `.series-progress-table` (98 lines): a column of the summary, the table and the legend.

**Elements:** `.series-progress-table__summary`, `.series-progress-table__figure`, `.series-progress-table__summary-note`, `.series-progress-table__mark`, `.series-progress-table__icon`, `.series-progress-table__count`, `.series-progress-table__reader`, `.series-progress-table__legend`, `.series-progress-table__legend-item`, `.series-progress-table__legend-n`

**Modifiers and states:** `.series-progress-table__mark_state_solved`, `.series-progress-table__mark_state_failed`

**Tokens:** `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted` (the note and the legend sit on the page's grey, where the subtle grey does not read), `--platform-success-ink`, `--platform-danger`, `--platform-surface-neutral`

No media queries: the table scrolls sideways below its `minWidth`, as every `PlatformTable` does.
