## Purpose

How an exercise is written (D06, D09, D10, D13, D38; PLAN.md §4.2): a start position, a solution played on the board with its variants and comments — never typed — and the data the student reads. The name is not `ExerciseEditor` on purpose: the staff lesson panel's `ExerciseEditor`, with its global `.exercise-editor` block, lives until phase 2 replaces it, and two stylesheets on one block would leak rules into each other on a client-side navigation.

It receives data, never components: the exercise (or a new one's prefill), the catalogs, the author's games, what the author may do and the buttons of the save bar, all mapped on the server (`services/teacher-exercises/teacher-exercises.mapper.ts` for a teacher), and its actions as props, so its story stubs them and it imports no `.actions.ts`.

## Behaviour

**One structure for state, tabs and the save.** Radix's `Tabs.Content` unmounts an inactive panel and `PlatformForm` relies on the browser's validation, which would block a save over an invalid field in a hidden tab with nothing said. So:

- Every value lives in **lifted client state** (`useExerciseDraft`): the setup of «Colocar piezas», the solution PGN and each «Datos» field, all controlled. Switching tabs loses nothing.
- **The save is one `PlatformForm` outside the tabs**, holding the hidden inputs built from that state (`draftFields`) and the buttons, in a bar under the tabs on every tab, sticky at the bottom of the window. It is submitted by hand (`onSubmit` → `startTransition(() => formAction(formData))`, `pending` on the pressed `SubmitButton`), as AGENTS.md asks of a form with controlled Radix controls.
- **Before sending**, `draftIssues` checks the same rules the server does: the position (`validateSetup`), the title, the difficulty (400–3000, by 50), the N of «Mate en N», the year; and, when the button asks for the exercise complete, `exerciseReadiness` (the solution's rules and a topic). The editor opens the tab of the first problem and focuses its field; every tab with a problem carries the mark of [`PlatformTabs`](/components/platform/shared/platform-tabs). From then on the pressed button's rules are checked live, so a mark goes when it is fixed.
- **A refused save** returns its errors with what was sent (`PopupFormState`); each is mapped to its tab (`tabOfField`: «position», «solutionPgn», or a field of «Datos») and shown while the draft is the one that was sent. A saved one redirects to the exercise with `?aviso=exerciseSaved` (or `exerciseReady`), and the section mounts the editor again under the exercise's `updatedAt`.
- The «Datos» fields sit in a `PlatformForm` of their own whose `onSubmit` only prevents the default (Enter in a field does not save); «Pegar FEN o PGN» is its own small form; no form is nested in another.

**«Posición»** (D09), three ways in (`PlatformChoiceCards` «Colocar piezas · Pegar FEN o PGN · Desde una partida»), one result:

- «Colocar piezas»: [`PiecePlacementBoard`](/components/chess/piece-placement-board) beside the turn («Juegan blancas» / «Juegan negras»), the four castling rights (`PlatformCheckbox` in a `PlatformFormGroup` «Enroques», each disabled with its reason when its king or rook is not home), «Captura al paso» (`PlatformSelect` with «Ninguna» and only the squares `enPassantOptions` allows) and three small buttons, «Posición inicial», «Vaciar el tablero» and «Girar el tablero». Every change goes through `normalizeSetup` (a right or a square the placement no longer allows is dropped), and `validateSetup`'s refusal shows under the board; while it stands, «Solución» says to fix the position first.
- «Pegar FEN o PGN»: the analysis board's two fields read with `readPositionInput` (the same start check as an exercise's), and «Usar sus jugadas como solución».
- «Desde una partida»: a `PlatformSelect` of the author's games grouped by study, then [`MovePathPicker`](/components/platform/shared/move-path-picker): «Usar esta posición» seeds the exercise with `exerciseSeedFromGame` (what follows the move is the solution), fills the empty «Origen» fields from the game's headers and keeps `sourceGameId`, and opens «Solución».
- **Changing the position once the solution has moves asks first**: a `PlatformConfirmPopup` «¿Eliminar la solución?»; cancelling mounts the placement board again on the position that stays.

**«Solución»** (D10): [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer) in edit mode over the solution PGN, oriented to the side to move, with its tools (comments, NAGs, arrows) and no engine (D03). Under the board, `analyzeSolution`'s problems (danger) and warnings (muted) in words (`solutionProblemText`, `solutionWarningText`): «La línea de 23. Dxg7+ no termina en mate.», «Se aceptan: Ab8#, Da8#.», and with a player variant marked as a mistake, the caution button «Eliminar las variantes marcadas como error», which asks first in a `PlatformConfirmPopup` («Eliminar las variantes»: they leave the solution with their sub-lines and comments) and then runs `dropMistakeVariants`.

**«Datos»** (D13, D38): «Título», «Enunciado» (its placeholder says who plays, and the mate's length), «Tipo» (cards), «Mate en» (1–5, only for «Mate en N»), «Dificultad» with «Nivel: Intermedio (1200–1599)» under it (`levelOfDifficulty`), «Temas» (`PlatformCheckCards` with `groups`: the first-level topics, then «Táctica» over its motifs, `topicCardGroups`), three «Pista», «Explicación», and «Origen», four optional fields said once with `OptionalFields`, in a `role="group"` named by its title, so «Blancas» and «Negras» read as the source game's players. The start position is drawn beside them.

**«Probar»**: [`ExerciseSolver`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-solver) in `mode="preview"` over the draft as it stands — the prompt, the hints, the replies, the variants accepted and the explanation —, before anything is saved; nothing is recorded, and it starts again whenever the draft changes (it is mounted under the solution, the kind, the N and the hints as its `key`). With no moves yet it says «Juega la solución en «Solución» para probarla aquí como la verán tus alumnos.»

**Locked** (a teacher's exercise in review or in the library): the reason over the tabs («Está en la biblioteca: duplícalo para hacer tu versión.»), the board and every field read-only, and «Duplicar» in place of the save.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercise-authoring` (213 lines): a column of the lock line, the tabs and the save bar. From 1024px each step is a grid of the board (280–520px, sticky; 200–320px on «Datos») and its controls; below, they stack, the board first (at most 560px, 280px on «Datos»).

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button` (`_variant_accent`, `_size_sm`, `_variant_caution`), `.platform-form__field`, `.platform-form__label`.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` stacks each step; `(width < 640px)` puts the castling rights and «Origen» in one column and stretches the save buttons.
