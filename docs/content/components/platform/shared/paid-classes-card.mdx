## Purpose

What the student has, counted when it is read from their payments (`paymentSeats`, `services/shared/payment-seats.ts`) and never stored, **as counts only**: the figure «3 clases por tomar» in the display face, one plain square per class — ticked in green when taken, ringed in the accent when scheduled, a dashed outline when it is still to schedule —, the same counts in words («2 programadas · 1 por programar»), «Tus clases pagadas no caducan.» and «¿Quieres más clases? Escríbenos», which opens the academy's WhatsApp with the message written (`academyWhatsappHref`). Up to twelve classes are drawn as squares; more read as the counts alone. Without a payment: «Todavía no tienes clases pagadas.» and «¿Quieres clases? Escríbenos».

The counts are of the payments still open, added together (with none open, of the last one). Since 2026-10-15 the card never says which payment a class belongs to — no «de tu pago del 1 de octubre», no dates or titles on the squares —, the owner's rule for every student screen: the student reads classes, not packages. Nor an amount: a payment reaches the student's mapper as a day and a count of classes.
