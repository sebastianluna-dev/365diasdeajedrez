## Purpose

What the student has, counted when it is read from their payments (`paymentSeats`, `services/shared/payment-seats.ts`) and never stored: the figure «3 clases por tomar» in the display face, the sentence «Te quedan 3 clases de tu pago del 1 de octubre: 2 programadas y 1 por programar.», a bar (`role="img"`, its label the same counts in words) with its legend «● 1 tomada ● 2 programadas ○ 1 por programar», and «La que falta la programa administración contigo. Tus clases pagadas no caducan.». «¿Quieres más clases? Escríbenos» opens the academy's WhatsApp with the message written (`academyWhatsappHref`). Without a payment: «Todavía no tienes clases pagadas.» and «¿Quieres clases? Escríbenos». The student never sees an amount: a payment reaches the card as a day and a count of classes.
