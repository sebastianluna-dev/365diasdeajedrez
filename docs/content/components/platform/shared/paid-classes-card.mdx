## Purpose

What the student has, counted when it is read from their payments (`paymentSeats`, `services/shared/payment-seats.ts`) and never stored, **as counts only**: «Tus clases pagadas», the figure «3 clases por tomar» in the display face, one small square per class — a tick on the success container when taken, a calendar on the secondary container when scheduled, a calendar with a clock on amber when it is still to schedule —, the same counts in words («2 programadas · 1 por programar»), «Tus clases pagadas no caducan.» and «¿Quieres más clases? Escríbenos» as Material's text button, which opens the academy's WhatsApp with the message written (`academyWhatsappHref`). Up to sixteen classes are drawn as squares; more read as the counts alone. Without a payment: «Todavía no tienes clases pagadas.» and «¿Quieres clases? Escríbenos».

Since 2026-10-23 it is drawn for the new design system (the owner's «M4» canvas, where it reads «Tus clases pagadas»): a white card with `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg` corners on the canvas of the workspace's side column, under the views of «Mis clases». It is only used there.

The counts are of the payments still open, added together (with none open, of the last one). Since 2026-10-15 the card never says which payment a class belongs to — no «de tu pago del 1 de octubre», no dates or titles on the squares —, the owner's rule for every student screen: the student reads classes, not packages. Nor an amount: a payment reaches the student's mapper as a day and a count of classes.
