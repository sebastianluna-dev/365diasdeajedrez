## Purpose

On a phone the button to enter has to be where the thumb is (D20). The bar is drawn by the PAGE as a sibling of `.platform-page`, never inside it: while the page's entrance animation runs, its transform makes it the containing block of anything fixed (§2.4 of the plan; since 2026-10-16 the animation fills `backwards`, so the transform goes once it ends, but a bar must not jump during those 220 ms either). The page reserves the bar's height with `platform-page_foot_join-bar` (`platform.css`), under 1024px only, where the bar is drawn.

## Props

The join card's data, `audience` and `look`, without `density`. With `look="material"` (the page of «Mis clases», moved to the new design system on 2026-10-23) the strip is Material's panel (`.class-join-bar_look_material`) and the button its filled one.

## Markup & styling

Block `.class-join-bar` (37 lines): fixed to the bottom, drawn only under 1024px, with the safe area's inset; it rises when mounted (`class-join-bar-in`, 200 ms), stilled under reduced motion.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-surface`, `--platform-border`
