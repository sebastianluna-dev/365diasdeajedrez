## Purpose

The segmented donut of the owner's «Estadísticas · C» (2026-10-25): each segment a separate piece of the ring with round corners and a 7° gap to the next, the first one hatched in the primary with a white tag on its middle — its share («56 %») or the `tag` given («6 de 10», the wins of the finished games) — and the legend beside it, each label with its figure at the right. A segment of nothing is left out of the ring but kept in the legend, so the four outcomes are always named; with nothing at all the ring is a grey track and no tag is drawn.

A segment's fill is named by what it is, never by its colour (`tone`): `hatch` the one to look at, `deep`, `mid` and `light` the blues after it, `ink` the dark grey of what went wrong, `neutral` the light grey of the rest, `open` an outline only, for what is not over yet (a game in progress).

The ring is one image (`role="img"`, `aria-label` = `label`, which says the total and every segment); the legend is a list a screen reader reads too, its swatches hidden.

## Markup & styling

Each segment is a path drawn a corner's radius (5px) inside its arc and stroked 10px wide with round joins, which gives it back its size with round corners — the drawing of the owner's sheet. The hatch is a `<pattern>` with its id from `useId`; the legend's hatched swatch is the same stripes as a `repeating-linear-gradient`. The colours are the charts' roles and the streak's blues: `--platform-m3-chart-hatch` on `-chart-hatch-ground`, `-chart-line` (deep), `-heat-2` (mid), `-heat-0` (light), `-on-surface-variant` (ink), `-outline-variant` (neutral), the panel with the primary's outline (open), and the track `-chart-neutral`.
