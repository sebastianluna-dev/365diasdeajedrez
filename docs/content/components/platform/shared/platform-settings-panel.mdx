## Purpose

A page's settings, as Gmail's «Configuración rápida» (2026-10-23, after the owner's design): a white panel at the right of the [workspace](/ui/styling#the-new-design-system-material-3-expressive), beside the page's own panel, opened and closed by the gear in the bar ([`PlatformSettingsToggle`](/components/platform/shared/platform-settings-toggle)) and by its own ✕. What a page lets the student set about it lives here, and no longer in the side column, which is left to what the page shows.

## Exports

| Export                  | Props                                  | Meaning                                                                                    |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `PlatformSettingsPanel` | `title: string`, `children: ReactNode` | The panel: its name at the top, its ✕, and its groups. A container.                        |
| `PlatformSettingsGroup` | `title: string`, `children: ReactNode` | A group: its name, and its options under it, set apart from the group above by a hairline. |

## Behaviour

- **Whether it is open lives on the root**, `:root[data-settings="open"]` (`settings-panel-state.ts`: `SETTINGS_ATTRIBUTE`, `useSettingsOpen`, `setSettingsOpen`), as the side column's `data-side-nav` does: the gear and the ✕, two leaves far apart, hear the same thing, and the panel is shown by the stylesheet alone, with no flash on a reload. It is not kept: a new visit starts with it closed. `useSettingsOpen` follows the attribute with a `MutationObserver` (`useSyncExternalStore`), closed on the server.
- The ✕ closes it and gives the focus back to the gear (the button with `aria-controls="platform-settings"` and `aria-pressed`).
- It is the `<aside id="platform-settings">` the gear names in `aria-controls`, labelled by its title.
- **Each option is a row the page draws** with the block's elements, since what an option is — a form, a link, the trigger of a confirmation — is the page's: the control's mark at the left (`__ring` for one of several, `__lead` with an icon for an action), its words (`__option-label`, `__option-hint`), and at the right the drawing of what it does (`__option-art`, a 96×60 white frame, the primary's once the option is chosen). The whole row is the control.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-settings` (195 lines). Closed, `display: none`; open, a third column of the workspace, 344px wide, stuck under the bar (`top: 70px`) like the side column and scrolling on its own when it is taller than the window, its head stuck to its top. It comes in with a fade and a slide of 16px in 200 ms (`platform-settings-in`), off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Under 1024px, where the workspace is one column, it takes the column's width after the page.

**Elements:** `.platform-settings__head`, `.platform-settings__title`, `.platform-settings__body`, `.platform-settings__group`, `.platform-settings__group-title`, `.platform-settings__option`, `.platform-settings__lead`, `.platform-settings__lead-icon`, `.platform-settings__ring`, `.platform-settings__option-text`, `.platform-settings__option-label`, `.platform-settings__option-hint`, `.platform-settings__option-art`, `.platform-settings__note`

**Modifiers and states:** `.platform-settings__option_state_on` (the option chosen: its ring filled and its frame in the primary), `.platform-settings__option_tone_danger` (what destroys, in `--platform-m3-error`)

**Keyframes:** `platform-settings-in`, at the top level because `@keyframes` cannot be nested.

## Usage

```tsx
<PlatformSettingsPanel title="Ajustes del curso">
  <PlatformSettingsGroup title="Qué aprender">…</PlatformSettingsGroup>
</PlatformSettingsPanel>
```
