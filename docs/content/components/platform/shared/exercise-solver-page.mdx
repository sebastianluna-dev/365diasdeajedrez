## Purpose

The Lichess-like screen of «Ejercicios» as the owner drew it in «M4» (2026-10-23), in Material 3 Expressive. It is a view of the solver and not a solver of its own: it receives the same `ExerciseSolverViewProps` as `ExerciseSolverView` — the exercise, the session, the save state and the handlers — and tells the result in the same words (`resultTextOf`, `resultModeOf`, `saveNoteOf`, `BLOCK_NOTE`), so the scoring rules — the first wrong move fails the attempt, «Ver la solución» too, one scored attempt per exercise and study day — are the hook's and the server's, never this drawing's. The page's toolbar (its trail, the series' place, the level) is the section's, around it.

## Props

`ExerciseSolverViewProps` (see [`ExerciseSolver`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-solver)); it reads `aside` for the history's scope and the topic's chip. `context` is not drawn: the series' place is in the toolbar.

## Behaviour

- **The board**: `ChessBoard look="material"`, seen from the player's side, with the piece of a hint circled and the wrong move in red for a moment (a ring that comes and goes, still under reduced motion). Under it, Material's wide keys as the explorer's — «Al principio», «Jugada anterior», «Reproducir la solución» (only while the solution is walked; «Pausar» while it plays), «Jugada siguiente» (filled), «Al final» —, in Sharp Light. Once it is over they, and the moves, take the board back along the line.
- **The status card**, tinted by how it stands: «Tu turno» in the pale blue of `--platform-m3-info-container`, with the side to move as a dot, the prompt, the chips (the topic, «Secuencia · 1250», «Fallado» once the first move was wrong), the feedback, the author's hints and «Pista» (tonal) and «Ver la solución» (outlined); «Ésa no es la jugada» in the error container; and the result — «¡Resuelto!» in the success container, «Resuelto, después de un error» in the warning container, «Viste la solución» in the error container — with «Jugaste …; la jugada era …», the explanation, «Siguiente ejercicio» (filled), the way back (filled when nothing comes after) and «Reintentar» (text). The notes of a retry, a class block and the preview, and the save's, are lines on the panel's white at its foot.
- **«Jugadas»**: the line on the board, loose, each move a pill and the one on the board in the primary; the student's wrong tries crossed on a line of their own, the first «tu intento».
- **«Esta sesión»** (`ExerciseSessionHistory`, only with `aside`): a pill per exercise of the visit, named and coloured by how it went, and a line saying what the colours mean.
- **The two switches** at the column's foot: «Cuenta para tu puntuación» with the sample rating and its last change, and «Pasar al siguiente al resolver» (`exercise-rating.store.ts`: this browser's storage, nothing reaches the server).

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercise-solver-page` (492 lines): a grid of the board's track and the column. On a laptop or wider the board is `max(320px, min(100vh - 254px, 600px))` — the window less the bar, the panel's toolbar and the keys —, so the page fits the window; under 1280px it takes up to 480px, and under 1024px the two stack, the board as wide as the screen allows while leaving the status in sight (`max(min(100vw - 32px, 100svh - 240px), 280px)`). The column's cards have a hairline of `--platform-m3-row-edge` and `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg` corners.
