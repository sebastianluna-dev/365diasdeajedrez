## Purpose

The other half of the owner's design for the student's classes (2026-10-15): a class leaves the agenda for this card once it is over and counts as finished (`studentClassView` says «review»: half an hour after the end). The top row says whether the student came («Asististe» on the success container, «No asististe» on amber, «Cancelada» in grey) and, for a free class, «Gratis», with the hours on the right; then the topic (the link to the class, its day read out for a screen reader), «Finalizada · con GM Profesor Demo», a make-up's or a moved seat's line («No asististe · tu clase pasó al vie 9 oct», a link to the new class), the summary the teacher left (two lines at most), and at its foot «Grabación», «4 bloques» and «Ver la clase».

Since 2026-10-23 it is drawn for the new design system: an outlined card on the panel (`--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`), whose title's link covers it whole, so the card is the way in and lifts under the pointer as a row of Gmail's (`--platform-m3-row-edge`, `--platform-m3-row-shadow`); the moved seat's link sits over it. A cancelled class steps back onto the container.

**The board only when the class has chess.** `past.board` is the position of the class's first block with chess that the student may see now (`pastBoardOf`, `services/classes/classes.mapper.ts`): a position's FEN as it is, a game's or a lesson's at the move the block points at or, without one, at the end of its main line. A class with only text, videos or files — or a PGN without a move — has no board, and the card is the same without it. The board is a `StaticDiagram` on Material's blue board, 128px wide beside the text, named «Tablero de la clase: …» with the position's title, the game's players or the lesson's name. Never which payment the class came from.
