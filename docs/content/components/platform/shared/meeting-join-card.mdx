## Purpose

One piece for the three roles: the services map a class to `MeetingJoinData` — the link as the reader may see it (null for the student until the server lets it through), whether a link exists, the platform, the instants — and the card decides what to draw from `meetingJoinState`: `none` (no link: «El enlace todavía no está. Administración lo agregará antes de la clase.» and «Escríbenos» to the academy's WhatsApp), `waiting` («Zoom · el enlace se abre a las 11:30 · …»), `open`, `live` and `tail` («Entrar a la clase», or «Volver a entrar» from the end to half an hour after, with the countdown and «Copiar el enlace»), `closed` («La clase terminó a las 13:00.»). The staff only copies. «Entrar a la clase» opens in a new tab and says so to a screen reader.
