## Purpose

The twin of `PlatformChoiceCards` for a set instead of one: when a course teaches two or three things out of eight and each deserves a line of explanation, a row of cards shows what a list of checkboxes hides. Every card is a checkbox (`role="checkbox"`) on Radix's `Checkbox` primitive, so each one is a tab stop, Space toggles it, and inside a form every chosen card submits through the hidden native checkbox the primitive keeps in sync: `formData.getAll(name)` reads the set, which is what `parseForm` does for an array field (`formCodeList`).

It is a sibling of `PlatformChoiceCards` and not a mode of it: a radio group is one tab stop with one value and can be `required`; a checkbox group is many of each, and "at least one" is the form's rule, not the browser's.

## Behaviour

- A card rests on the white surface, lifted a hair (`--platform-shadow-lift`). A chosen one carries `data-state="checked"` from the primitive and **sinks**: the neutral fill (`--platform-surface-neutral`) pressed in (`--platform-shadow-inset`), with no colour but its box, so a form of several chosen cards is not a wall of red (2026-10-06, the «C» of the checkbox study). On that grey the description stays in the muted grey, which reads on it.
- The mark (`Checkbox.Indicator` with `forceMount`) is always in the DOM: an empty **square** box (20px, 4px corners, pressed in) when off, the selection's blue box with the tick when on (`--platform-selection`, not the accent: in the light theme that is the red of an error) (`CheckIcon` in Classic Solid), so nothing shifts on toggle. A square and not a circle: a round mark reads as a radio, which is what [`PlatformChoiceCards`](/components/platform/shared/platform-choice-cards) draws for its one choice.
- Clicking anywhere on a card toggles it; Space toggles the focused one; Tab moves between cards.
- Uncontrolled by default (`defaultValue`); with `value` the parent owns the set, which the «Nuevo curso» form uses to keep its submit off until one card is chosen.
- **It starts again from what was saved.** The cards are mounted under the set they open with (`savedValueKey(defaultValue)`, in `components/platform/shared/saved-value-key.ts`), so a save that changed the set mounts them anew from it. React resets a `<form action>` when its action finishes, and the primitive answers the form's `reset` by going back to the value it had when it was MOUNTED. On a form that stays on screen after saving, that is the value from before the save: until 2026-09-28 the control showed what had just been replaced, the page had to be reloaded to see what was stored, and saving again without reloading sent the old value and undid the change. The key ignores the order of the set, and is constant for a controlled group.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-check-cards`, a grid of `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(170px, 1fr))` at `width: 100%` (so it fills a `PlatformFormGroup`'s column; the group draws no frame, because the cards are already boxes): as many cards per row as fit, one column on a phone.

**Elements:** `.platform-check-cards__card`, `.platform-check-cards__mark`, `.platform-check-cards__mark-icon`, `.platform-check-cards__icon`, `.platform-check-cards__label`, `.platform-check-cards__meta`, `.platform-check-cards__description`

**Modifiers and states:** `.platform-check-cards_layout_line` on the block: `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))`, the card as a row aligned on the baseline with a `--platform-space-1` (4px) gap — tighter than a word space, so the fact reads as part of the name, the mark centred vertically, the label in regular weight (400, not the 700 of the stacked card) and the meta regular too, `white-space: nowrap` with an ellipsis on the description, and one column under 640px. On a line the card's `meta` is written **exactly like its label** — same size, weight and colour, checked or not: «Avanzado (Elo 1600–1999)» is one phrase, and a second style inside it makes two things of what is one. The two wrap together rather than either being cut, so a card grows to two lines before a name is lost.

**States (data attributes set by the primitive):** `[data-state="checked"]`, `[data-disabled]` on a card.

**Tokens:** `--platform-border`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-on-selection`, `--platform-selection`, `--platform-shadow-inset`, `--platform-shadow-lift`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-surface-neutral`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-faint`, `--platform-text-muted`

**Breakpoints:** none in the default layout, where the grid reflows on its own; `(width < 640px)` takes the `line` layout from two columns to one. Transitions are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/staff/courses/new-course/new-course-form.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
<PlatformFormGroup label="Contenido">
  <PlatformCheckCards
    name="contentTypeCodes"
    value={contentTypeCodes}
    onValueChange={setContentTypeCodes}
    options={contentTypes.map((type) => ({ value: type.code, label: type.label, description: type.description }))}
  />
</PlatformFormGroup>
```
