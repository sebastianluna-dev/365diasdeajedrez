## Purpose

The games of a study or of a chapter's collection, beside the board, to jump between them without going back — and, for whoever may, to put them in the order they are to be read in. The two screens had a list each, the same rows written twice with the same stylesheet: the study's still moved on the browser's own drag events (`draggable`, `onDragOver`), with no gap opening where the row would land and nothing announced, while the chapter's was already on [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd). Since 2026-10-03 they are this one piece, on dnd-kit, and each aside hands it its games and its bound action.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                                      | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `games`     | `GameListItem[]`                          | yes      | `{ id, name, players?, resultLabel, href }[]`, in their order, the current one included. `name` heads the row; `players` is the second line, left out when the name already is the pairing. |
| `currentId` | `string`                                  | yes      | The current game: its row gets `aria-current="page"` and the active state, and is centred in the list.                                                                                      |
| `onReorder` | `(orderedIds: string[]) => Promise<void>` | no       | Saves a new order, a server action already bound to its study or chapter. Without it the rows are links only: the aside leaves it out where nobody may reorder.                             |

## Behaviour

- **Rows** show the game's name — in a study, the student's title or the mapper's `label` fallback (round, event or position); in a collection, its label —, who played it as a second line, and the result. Long names are cut, with a `title` tooltip.
- **Five rows tall.** A row is as tall as two lines of it — the name, the gap, the players — and the link's padding, worked out from the scale (`--game-list-row`, set on the block), so a one-line row measures the same as a two-line one; the block caps its height at five of them, their gaps and its padding, and scrolls the rest on its own (it followed a fixed 58px until 2026-10-03, while rows measured 60 and, from 1536px, 63), so a long study does not push the record off the screen. On arrival, and whenever `currentId` changes, an effect scrolls the current row into the middle of the list by arithmetic on its offset, so only the list moves and never the page.
- **Reordering**, only with `onReorder`. Each row is dragged by its grip and, from the keyboard, picked up on the grip with space and moved with the arrows, through [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd), which opens the gap where the row would land and says everything out loud in Spanish. The order is applied at once and the whole list goes to `onReorder` in a transition (`useSortableRows`); new `games` from the server replace it during render.
- **No «Subir» and «Bajar».** The list had two arrows at the end of each row for a few hours on 2026-10-03, to move a row one place without dragging (WCAG 2.5.7). The owner took them out the same day: with a grip already there they read as noise. A pointer therefore reorders only by dragging — a known gap for whoever cannot hold a drag —; the keyboard still moves a row from its grip. `SyllabusList` keeps its «Subir» and «Bajar», in its row menu.
- **The row is the card.** The link fills the row and the grip sits inside it, before the name, so the current game's mark — the accent's wash and a 3px rule on the row's left edge — takes the grip in: the grip reads as part of the game it moves and not as something beside the card (2026-10-03; before, the mark was on the link alone and the grip was left outside it). A hovered row takes the page's grey whole, too.
- **The grip stays out of the way** until its row is hovered or holds the focus (`:focus-within`): what one comes here for is to jump between games, not to sort them. Where nothing hovers — a touch screen, `(hover: none)` — it is always drawn, since nothing there would ever bring it out. It is drawn in the muted grey, and in the accent's ink on the current row.

## Markup & styling

Block `.game-list` (193 lines), the `<ul>` itself.

**Elements:** `.game-list__row`, `.game-list__grip`, `.game-list__item`, `.game-list__main`, `.game-list__name`, `.game-list__players`, `.game-list__result`

**Modifiers and states:** `.game-list__row_state_active` (the current game: wash, rule and accent ink, grip included), `.game-list__row_state_dragging` (lifted, with the card's shadow)

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-wash`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle` (the players), `--platform-type-md`, `--platform-type-sm`, `--platform-space-0-5`, `--platform-space-1`, `--platform-space-2`, `--platform-space-3`

No width media queries; `(hover: none)` draws the handles, and `(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` drops their fade.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/game-aside.comp.tsx`, where the order can only be changed with the whole study in hand (one page):

```tsx
import { GameList } from "@/components/platform/shared/game-list/game-list.comp";

<GameList
  games={study.games.map(listItem)}
  currentId={game.id}
  onReorder={canReorder ? reorderStudyGames.bind(null, study.id) : undefined}
/>;
```

From `components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-game/chapter-game-aside.comp.tsx`, always sortable: `onReorder={reorderChapterGames.bind(null, game.courseId, game.chapterId)}`.
