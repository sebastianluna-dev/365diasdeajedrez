## Purpose

The games of a study or of a chapter's collection, beside the board, to jump between them without going back — and, for whoever may, to put them in the order they are to be read in. The two screens had a list each, the same rows written twice with the same stylesheet: the study's still moved on the browser's own drag events (`draggable`, `onDragOver`), with no gap opening where the row would land and nothing announced, while the chapter's was already on [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd). Since 2026-10-03 they are this one piece, on dnd-kit, and each aside hands it its games and its bound action.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                                      | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `games`     | `GameListItem[]`                          | yes      | `{ id, name, players?, title?, orientationCode?, analysisModeCode?, pinnedComment?, resultLabel, href }[]`, in their order, the current one included. `name` is what the row says; `players` the small line under it, who played; `title` is the name the game was given, absent while it is shown by its players, and is what its settings start from, with the three settings after it. |
| `currentId` | `string`                                  | yes      | The current game: its row gets `aria-current="page"` and the active state, and is centred in the list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `onReorder` | `(orderedIds: string[]) => Promise<void>` | no       | Saves a new order, a server action already bound to its study or chapter. Without it the rows are links only: the aside leaves it out where nobody may reorder.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `settings`  | `GameSettingsActions`                     | no       | What a game's settings do — rename, clean, delete —, server actions already bound to the study. With them each row ends in the gear of [`GameSettings`](/components/platform/shared/game-settings); the staff's collection does not pass them.                                                                                                                                            |

## Behaviour

- **Rows** show the game's name, who played it under the name, and how it ended. The name — in a study, the student's title or the mapper's `label` fallback (round, event or pairing); in a collection, its label — is in the scale's `sm`, medium weight, on two lines at most (`line-clamp`), bold on the current game, with a `title` tooltip for a longer one. The players are a line a step under the name (`xs`, 14px), cut with an ellipsis, and are left out when the name already is the pairing, so nothing is said twice; they were taken out with the names-only list on 2026-10-04 and put back the day after — in the scale's smallest step (`2xs`, 12px) for a few hours, until the owner asked for 14px. The result is the PGN token (`1-0`, `½-½`); a game that has not ended (`*`) shows none.
- **About eight rows tall.** A row is as tall as its name, one line or two; the block's ceiling is counted in one-line rows — a line of the name and the link's padding, worked out from the scale (`--game-list-row`) —, eight of them with their gaps and the block's padding, and the rest scrolls on its own, so a long study does not push the record off the screen. On arrival, and whenever `currentId` changes, an effect brings a current row that is **out of sight** into the middle of the list, by arithmetic on its offset, so only the list moves and never the page. A row already in sight is left where it is (2026-10-05): a study's list now stays on screen from game to game, and a row that jumped to the middle when clicked would move under the pointer.
- **Reordering**, only with `onReorder`. Each row is dragged by its grip and, from the keyboard, picked up on the grip with space and moved with the arrows, through [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd), which opens the gap where the row would land and says everything out loud in Spanish. The order is applied at once and the whole list goes to `onReorder` in a transition (`useSortableRows`); new `games` from the server replace it during render.
- **No «Subir» and «Bajar».** The list had two arrows at the end of each row for a few hours on 2026-10-03, to move a row one place without dragging (WCAG 2.5.7). The owner took them out the same day: with a grip already there they read as noise. A pointer therefore reorders only by dragging — a known gap for whoever cannot hold a drag —; the keyboard still moves a row from its grip. `SyllabusList` keeps its «Subir» and «Bajar», in its row menu.
- **The row is the card.** The link fills the row and the grip sits inside it, before the name, so the current game's mark — the accent's wash and a 3px rule on the row's left edge — takes the grip in: the grip reads as part of the game it moves and not as something beside the card (2026-10-03; before, the mark was on the link alone and the grip was left outside it). A hovered row takes the page's grey whole, too.
- **The grip is always there, but faint**, as in the staff's tables ([`SyllabusList`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/syllabus-list)): the strong border's grey at rest, and the subtle grey — a control's 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11), on the hovered row's grey too — when its row is hovered or holds the focus (`:focus-within`); on the current game the same two steps are the accent's, `--platform-accent-faint` and its ink. Until 2026-10-07 it was hidden (`opacity: 0`) until the row was reached, and always drawn on a touch screen; the owner asked for it always in sight, quieter, as the staff's tables have it. What one comes here for is to jump between games, not to sort them, so it stays faint.

## Markup & styling

Block `.game-list` (205 lines), the `<ul>` itself.

**Elements:** `.game-list__row`, `.game-list__grip`, `.game-list__item`, `.game-list__main`, `.game-list__name`, `.game-list__players`, `.game-list__result`

**Modifiers and states:** `.game-list__row_state_active` (the current game: wash, rule and accent ink, grip included; it sets `--quiet-ink: var(--platform-accent-ink)`, so the gear of [`GameSettings`](/components/platform/shared/game-settings) is rose too and its shade under the pointer shows on the wash, which the quiet button's old grey did not — see [Styling → Buttons](/ui/styling#buttons)), `.game-list__row_state_dragging` (lifted, with the card's shadow)

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-faint`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-wash`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle` (the players, on the card's white), `--platform-type-sm`, `--platform-type-xs`, `--platform-space-0-5`, `--platform-space-1`, `--platform-space-2`, `--platform-space-3`

No media queries: the grip's fade is a colour's, which is not motion.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-games.comp.tsx`, which lives in a layout and so reads the current game from the address; the order can only be changed with the whole study in the list (one page):

```tsx
import { GameList } from "@/components/platform/shared/game-list/game-list.comp";

<GameList
  key={page}
  games={gamesOfPage(games, page, pageSize)}
  currentId={gameId}
  onReorder={onReorder}
  settings={settings}
/>;
```

From `components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-game/chapter-game-aside.comp.tsx`, always sortable: `onReorder={reorderChapterGames.bind(null, game.courseId, game.chapterId)}`.
