## Behaviour

- Each row: the grip (dnd-kit; space picks it up, the arrows move it, as every sortable list of the platform), its number, the title — a link to the editor when the exercise is the teacher's own; a library exercise has no page of theirs —, «Mate en 2 · 1400 · Intermedio», «Quitar» (`.platform-row-action`, a `<form>` bound to the exercise) and a «…» with «Subir» and «Bajar», the single-click way to reorder (WCAG 2.5.7).
- An item its students no longer see says so in the danger's ink with a triangle: «Archivado: tus alumnos ya no lo ven.» for the teacher's archived one, «Ya no está disponible: tus alumnos ya no lo ven.» for anything else out of reach (another teacher's exercise taken out of the library). The condition is `seriesItemAvailability`, the visibility rule's own.
- A move is applied at once and the whole order is sent (`useSortableRows`); a refused one (an incomplete list, a race) writes nothing, and the page's revalidation brings the stored order back. «Quitar» takes the exercise out of the series and closes the gap; it stays in the bank, and the attempts made in the series keep it.
- Empty, it says how to add the first ones.
