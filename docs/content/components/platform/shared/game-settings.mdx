## Purpose

What Lichess keeps behind the gear of a study's chapter, for a game of a study: its name, which side its board opens from, how a reader goes through it, a comment pinned at the head of its notation, and the three things that destroy — its annotations, its variations, the game itself. It was added on 2026-10-04, when the list of games became a list of names; before, a game was renamed through its whole record (`EditGame`) and only the game on screen could be deleted.

Of Lichess's four analysis modes, two are here: «Análisis normal» and «Ocultar los siguientes movimientos». «Practicar con el ordenador» and «Lección interactiva» are each a feature of their own that does not exist yet, and the select does not offer what does nothing; each will be a row of the `GameAnalysisMode` catalog and a label in `GAME_ANALYSIS_MODE_LABELS` when it does.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                  | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `game`      | `GameSettingsGame`    | yes      | `{ id, name, title?, orientationCode, analysisModeCode?, pinnedComment?, citedInClass? }`: `name` is what the list calls it; `title` the name it was given, absent while it is shown by its players; the next three are what the settings open on; `citedInClass` says some class shows the game, so deleting it asks for one confirmation more. |
| `isCurrent` | `boolean`             | yes      | Whether it is the game on screen: after a clean-up the page is loaded again (see below).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `actions`   | `GameSettingsActions` | yes      | `{ save(gameId, formData), clearAnnotations(gameId), clearVariations(gameId), remove(gameId, formData) }`, server actions already bound to the study the game belongs to.                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Behaviour

- **The gear** is a button named «Ajustes de «…»» after the game, always drawn — on a touch screen nothing would bring it out —, in the muted grey on every row, the current game's too, and in the accent under the pointer, with no box behind it, that opens a [`PlatformPopup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-popup) named «Ajustes de la partida» (size `sm`) with the click, so the focus comes back to the gear on closing. The popup focuses the first control of its content, which is the name: what one comes to change (until 2026-10-06 the component focused it by hand, over a `<dialog>` of its own whose first control was the cross).
- **The form** is a `PlatformForm` over `save`, which reads its four fields; the popup closes on submit, and each control is keyed by its saved value, so reopened after a save it starts from what was saved.
  - **Nombre** (`title`) starts from the game's own name, or empty with the pairing as its placeholder; saved empty, the game goes back to being shown by its players. That is said by the "i" inside the input (`PlatformFormField`'s `info`), and not by a line under it (2026-10-05, the owner's choice).
  - **Orientación** (`orientationCode`): «Blancas» or «Negras», the side the board opens from for everyone who opens the game. It and the analysis mode are `PlatformSelect`s inside `PlatformFormField`s, which give each its `name` and the `id` its label points at; they were `PlatformFormGroup`s, which are for group controls, until 2026-10-05.
  - **Modo de análisis** (`analysisModeCode`): «Análisis normal», the select's empty option, or a mode of `GAME_ANALYSIS_MODE_LABELS`. A mode is the reader's: it changes nothing for whoever edits the game (see [`GameViewSection`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/game-view-section)).
  - **Comentario fijo** (`pinnedComment`, up to 1000 characters): the text the viewer draws as the notation's first comment, before the moves (under the board until 2026-10-05). Its "i" says so, «Se muestra al principio de la notación, antes de las jugadas, a quien abra la partida.», in the strip [`PlatformFormField`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form) draws under a textarea with `info`, beside the count of its 1000 characters; it was a line under the field until 2026-10-05. It and the name say «Opcional» by themselves, as every field with no `required` does; the selects do not.
- **«Eliminar anotaciones», «Eliminar variantes», «Eliminar partida»** are three words in the danger ink under a rule. None acts at once: each opens the confirmation on top of the settings, a single [`PlatformConfirmPopup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-confirm-popup) mounted as a sibling of the popup and given the words of the step pressed (`CONFIRMATIONS[pending]`: its question as the `title`, what goes and what stays as the `description`, the step's verb and object on the button; the note «Esta acción no se puede deshacer.» is the piece's own). The step is set with `flushSync` and the confirmation opened right after, so it is named by its question as it opens. The settings wait beneath, with what was typed; «Cancelar» and Escape close only the confirmation (until 2026-10-06 each step swapped the dialog's body for its confirmation, and cancelling brought the form back). Annotations are the comments and what lives inside them (arrows, marked squares, evaluations), the signs and the opening comment; the moves and the variations stay. Variations leave the main line alone.
- **A clean-up of the game on screen reloads the page.** The two clean-ups rewrite the PGN on the server (`clearGameAnnotations`, `clearGameVariations`), while the game's page holds its own copy in the browser, with its undo history and its autosave: left there, the next autosave would write the old PGN back. So with `isCurrent` the page is loaded again once the action settles, from the confirmation's `onDone`; for any other game the confirmation has closed itself and `onDone` closes the settings beneath.
- **Deleting** hands the confirmation's form to `remove`, which sends the browser on (the `onDone` that follows closes the settings, harmless on a page being replaced). A game some class shows (`game.citedInClass`) asks for one thing more: the confirmation's `acknowledgement`, a box that says «Esta partida se usa en el contenido de alguna clase. Entiendo que esos bloques se quedarán vacíos.» and sends `confirmClassBlocks=yes` only while ticked; «Eliminar partida» stays disabled until it is. It is the server's own condition — `deleteStudyGame` wants `confirmClassBlocks=yes` for such a game and bounces with `gameInClasses` without it —, said before sending. Until 2026-10-05 that confirmation was only in `DeleteGame`, the dialog behind «Eliminar» at the foot of the game's record, and a cited game could not be deleted from here; the owner took that button out, so this is now the one place a game is deleted from.

## Markup & styling

Block `.game-settings` (88 lines): the gear and the popup's content. The strip, the width and the frame are `PlatformPopup`'s; the confirmation, whole, is `PlatformConfirmPopup`'s.

**Elements:** `.game-settings__open`, `.game-settings__open-icon`, `.game-settings__form`, `.game-settings__pair` (the two selects side by side; one under the other below 640px), `.game-settings__actions`, `.game-settings__danger`, `.game-settings__destroy`

No modifiers.

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button` (`_variant_ink`), and the two pieces'.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-danger`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-accent`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)` stacks the two selects.

## Usage

From `components/platform/shared/game-list/game-list.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
{
  settings && <GameSettings game={game} isCurrent={isCurrent} actions={settings} />;
}
```

and the actions, from `components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-aside.comp.tsx`, which reach the list through [`StudyGames`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-games):

```tsx
<StudyGames
  games={study.games.map(listItem)}
  pageSize={STUDY_GAMES_PAGE_SIZE}
  settings={{
    save: updateGameSettings.bind(null, study.id),
    clearAnnotations: clearGameAnnotations.bind(null, study.id),
    clearVariations: clearGameVariations.bind(null, study.id),
    remove: deleteStudyGame.bind(null, study.id),
  }}
/>
```
