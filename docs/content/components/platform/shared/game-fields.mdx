## Purpose

The fields of a game (name, players with Elo, title and federation, result, date, event, site, round, ECO) defined once. Every form that edits a game's record — the study's and the chapter collection's, both through `EditGame` — offers exactly this set: if something could be set in one place that cannot be corrected in another, the data would be trapped. All fields are optional, because when analysis starts one does not yet know which game it is going to be, so they are said optional **once**: the fields sit in [`OptionalFields`](/components/platform/shared/optional-fields), which writes «Todos los campos son opcionales.» over them and hides every field's own «Opcional» (2026-10-05).

It lives in `shared/` because two products write a game's record with it: the student's studies and the staff's chapter collections.

**No small print under the fields** (2026-10-05). The two clarifications the form has — what the name is for (`titleHint`) and what an ECO is («Código de apertura, como «B40».») — were lines under their inputs; the owner asked for them in an "i" instead — inside the input, at its end, as the staff's slug has it —, which is [`PlatformFormField`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form)'s `info`. They are read once, and a form of fifteen fields is shorter without them.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | ------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `values`    | `GameFieldValues`   | no       | Current values for the edit form; absent on creation. `playedAtValue` is short ISO (`yyyy-mm-dd`), what the date picker expects. |
| `results`   | `{ code, label }[]` | yes      | The results catalog; `ONGOING` is the default.                                                                                   |
| `titleHint` | `string`            | yes      | What the "i" inside the name's input says, different on creation and on edit.                                                    |

## Behaviour

- Each field is a [`PlatformFormField`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form), which gives its control the `name` the actions read and the `id` its label points at. A field needs the context of the form around it, so `GameFields` renders inside a `PlatformForm`: `EditGame`'s, or the story's.
- Plain inputs with `defaultValue`; no state.
- `CodeSelect` handles the title and federation dropdowns, each a [`PlatformSelect`](/components/platform/shared/platform-select) with the `emptyOption` ("Sin título", "Sin federación"): a PGN may bring a title or a federation that is not in our lists (headers are written by whoever wants), so a stored value outside the options is added as one more option, "X (del PGN)", and saving without touching it keeps it. `CodeSelect` passes on to the select what the field hands its control (`id`, `name`, the ref the form validates through, the ARIA of its messages). The result is a `PlatformSelect` over `results`.
- Elo inputs are `type="number"` between 100 and 4000.

- **`look="material"`** (2026-10-23, «Formulario A» of the owner's canvas), which `EditGame` passes on a page moved to the new design system: the same fields, in three sections titled in the primary and divided by a rule — «La partida» (name, event, place, and date and round sharing a column), «Jugadores» (each side a row of name, Elo, title and federation, a `role="group"` named «Blancas» or «Negras», so the short labels «Elo», «Título», «Federación» are still told apart), «Resultado y apertura» (result, ECO) — in two columns. The name's label is «Nombre» under «La partida». The fields are still all optional and said so once (`OptionalFields`): the design's «Opcional» on every field is the rule's single note here.

## Markup & styling

Block `.game-fields` (78 lines): the two-column rows; and `.game-fields_look_material`, «Formulario A»: `.game-fields__section` (a column, each after the first under a rule of the outline-variant), `.game-fields__section-title` (14px, semibold, the primary), `.game-fields__grid` (two columns) and `.game-fields__player` (name at 2fr, Elo, title and federation at 1fr; under 768px the name takes its own row over the other three, under 640px everything is one column).

**Elements:** `.game-fields__row`

**Other selectors it styles:** `.platform-form__field` (spacing inside the rows: each grows from 220px).

No tokens, no media queries of its own.

## Usage

From `components/platform/shared/edit-game/edit-game.comp.tsx`, its only importer:

```tsx
import { GameFields, type GameFieldValues } from "@/components/platform/shared/game-fields/game-fields.comp";

<GameFields values={values} results={results} titleHint={titleHint} />;
```
