| `look`    | `"material"`             | no       | The new design system's drawing (2026-10-23, the student's «Historial»): a row of a list across the panel instead of a card, the outcome a tag of Material's containers, «Ver el error» Material's text button and the position on the blue board. |

## Behaviour

- A list item (`<li>`): the section lists them in an `<ol>`, the latest first. The title links to the editor when the exercise is the teacher's; a library exercise is named and not linked.
- The outcome is a `platform-tag`: «Resuelto» in the success tone, «Resuelto con pista» in the warning tone, «Fallado» and «Vio la solución» plain — the same four words the student saw (D20, D33).
- `whereLabel` says where it was solved: «Serie: Táctica 1», «Práctica libre», «De una serie eliminada» when the series is gone (D45), «Clase: Repaso de la Najdorf» or «Clase del mié 14 oct» (with the year once it is not the reader's) for a class block (D29), «De un bloque de clase eliminado» when the block is gone — the attempt's `classBlockId` goes null when the block is deleted or changes kind, while the class may still be there.
- **«Ver el error»**, only with a `mistake`: a `platform-button` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that shows the panel under the row — the position at the mistake (`StaticDiagram`, with the move played as a danger arrow and the one expected as a success arrow) beside «Jugó Td7 en vez de Td8#», the two moves in the same tones. The panel is `hidden` while closed; «Ocultar el error» closes it. «Ver la solución» has no wrong move, so no button.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercise-attempt-row` (200 lines): a card of the surface with the card's shadow — or, with `look="material"` (`.exercise-attempt-row_look_material`), a row across the panel with a light hairline under it, which lifts under the pointer as Gmail's (`--platform-m3-row-edge`, `--platform-m3-row-shadow`) and keeps its colour, its outcome in `.exercise-attempt-row__outcome` (`_tone_success`, `_tone_warning`, `_tone_plain`: the success and warning containers, or the container's grey) —; the main row, and the detail as a grid of a 240px diagram and the sentence, one column under 640px.

**Elements:** `.exercise-attempt-row__main`, `.exercise-attempt-row__body`, `.exercise-attempt-row__title`, `.exercise-attempt-row__link`, `.exercise-attempt-row__meta`, `.exercise-attempt-row__separator`, `.exercise-attempt-row__chevron`, `.exercise-attempt-row__detail`, `.exercise-attempt-row__diagram`, `.exercise-attempt-row__mistake`, `.exercise-attempt-row__move`

**Modifiers and states:** `.exercise-attempt-row_state_open` (turns the chevron), `.exercise-attempt-row__move_tone_danger`, `.exercise-attempt-row__move_tone_success`

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-tag` and its `_variant_success`/`_variant_warning`, `.platform-button`, `.platform-button_size_sm`, `.platform-button__icon`; in the material look, `mdButton("text", "sm")` and `.platform-md-icon` instead.

**Tokens:** `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-subtle`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-danger`, `--platform-success-ink`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)`.
