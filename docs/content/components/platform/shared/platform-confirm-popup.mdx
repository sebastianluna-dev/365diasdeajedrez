## Purpose

One confirmation for everything that destroys (decided 2026-10-06, when three dialogs confirmed in three styles and the game's settings swapped their own body for the question). It is a different popup from [`PlatformPopup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-popup) on purpose, and the heavier of the two: no strip and no cross (a question is answered, not dismissed: «Cancelar» and Escape are the ways out), a disc with the bin, the largest title at the heaviest weight, a rule in the danger colour along its top, a longer shadow and a darker backdrop of its own.

**It is opened on top of whatever popup is open, never in its place.** A second native `<dialog>` shown with `showModal()` stacks over the first in the top layer, with its own backdrop painted right over the first popup, which waits beneath, inert, with what was typed in it. Nothing is done to the popup beneath by code: the top layer does it.

## Behaviour

- **Confirming closes both; cancelling closes one.** The form's action is the one given, wrapped: it runs with this popup still on top (the button says «Eliminando…»), and when it settles this popup closes itself and then calls `onDone`, where the host closes the popup beneath (`popup.current?.close()`): the top one first, so each `close()` returns the focus a step back, to the red word and then to the trigger. The closing is in a `finally`: an action that `redirect()`s rejects on the client with the redirect the router then follows, and the page, with both popups, is replaced; a bounce with `?error=` is a redirect too. «Cancelar» and Escape close only this popup; the one beneath never closed, so what was typed is still there.
- **The box.** Radix's `Checkbox` keeps a hidden native input in the form that carries `name`/`value` only while it is checked, so the server's own check (`confirmDelete`, `confirmClassBlocks`, `RESET_SCHEMA.confirm`) holds for a direct POST as well. The tick is reset on every close.
- **The warning goes with what goes** (2026-10-07). «Esta acción no se puede deshacer.» was a note at the left of the foot, beside the two buttons, and a long confirming label such as «Eliminar anotaciones» squeezed it into a column of one word a line. Among five ways of ordering the popup, Sebastian chose to lift it under the description, after the consequences, in the danger colour with `TriangleExclamationIcon` (Classic Solid): the last thing read before deciding, and part of the dialog's description (`aria-describedby` names it). The foot keeps only the buttons.
- **A sibling, never a child.** The confirmation is mounted next to the popup it is opened from, not inside it: a `<dialog>` under an ancestor that is `display: none` is not drawn, and the host owns both handles anyway. One instance can serve several questions (the game's settings have three): the host sets the words with `flushSync` and then opens it.
- **Alone.** From a card's cross or a list's button, with no popup beneath, `onDone` is omitted: the action's redirect does the rest.
- **The stack, in the browser.** Each modal dialog has its own `::backdrop`, painted immediately beneath it in the top layer; Escape reaches only the top one; everything but the top dialog is inert; `close()` returns the focus to what had it when the dialog opened. Checked in the stories `Stacked` and `CancelKeepsPopup`, which axe scans with both layers open, and in headless Chromium with real clicks (2026-10-06): Escape and «Cancelar» leave the popup beneath open with its typed value and the focus on the red word; confirming closes both and returns the focus to the trigger. One thing to know: a dialog opened **without user activation** (a `play` function's synthetic click, a script) shares a close-watcher group with the one before it, and a single Escape then closes both; a person's click carries the activation, so each layer gets its own.

- **Material's basic dialog on a moved page** (2026-10-23, «Confirmación 1» of the owner's canvas). Like `PlatformPopup`, it asks its own element as it opens whether it is inside `.platform-workspace` (`lookOf`), and there it is Material 3's basic dialog: the frame's `platform-dialog_look_material` (28px corners, `--platform-m3-dialog`, its shadow and a 32 % scrim of its own) and the block's `platform-confirm-popup_look_material` — 460px, no rule along the top, no disc: the verb's icon alone, centred, 24px (Duotone Light), in the error's red for «Eliminar» (`__icon_tone_error`) and in the primary for «Quitar» and «Cancelar»; the question centred in `--platform-font-display`, 24px at 600 (Poppins ships in 600 and 700 only); the description, the consequences and the warning as supporting text (14px, the warning in the error's red, without its triangle); the box to tick as a soft card (the panel's white, a hairline of the outline-variant, Material's medium corners, an 18px box that fills with the primary); and two text buttons at the right with no hairline over them, the way out (`mdButton("text")`, the primary) and the action (`mdButton("text")` + `.platform-md-error`). Its fields read the look too (`PlatformLookContext`) and are outlined text fields. Verbs, «Volver», fields, the box and the gate on it, the stacking, `onDone` and the focus are the same.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-confirm-popup`, on the element that also carries the frame `.platform-dialog`.

**Elements:** `.platform-confirm-popup__form`, `__head`, `__icon`, `__icon-glyph`, `__heading`, `__title`, `__description`, `__consequences`, `__consequence`, `__warning`, `__warning-icon`, `__fields` (the fields to write first, hung under the description), `__acknowledgement`, `__check` (with the primitive's `[data-state="checked"]`), `__check-mark`, `__check-icon`, `__foot`; the buttons are the globals `platform-button` and `platform-button_variant_danger`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-danger`, `--platform-danger-soft`, `--platform-danger-border`, `--platform-shadow-popup`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-track`, `--platform-text-muted`, the type and space scales.

**Breakpoints:** none; the foot wraps where its two buttons do not fit side by side, and the danger one goes under the way out («Cancelar», or «Volver»).

## Usage

Stacked, from `components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-settings.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
const edit = useRef<PlatformPopupHandle>(null);
const remove = useRef<PlatformConfirmPopupHandle>(null);

<PlatformPopup ref={edit} title="Editar el estudio" size="sm">
  <EditStudyForm … onDelete={() => remove.current?.open()} />
</PlatformPopup>
<PlatformConfirmPopup
  ref={remove}
  subject={study.name}
  description="Se elimina el estudio con sus 12 partidas, y con ellas sus variantes, comentarios y flechas."
  acknowledgement={{ name: "confirmDelete", label: "Entiendo que se eliminará todo y que no se puede deshacer." }}
  action={deleteStudy.bind(null, study.id)}
  confirmLabel="Eliminar estudio"
  onDone={() => edit.current?.close()}
/>;
```
