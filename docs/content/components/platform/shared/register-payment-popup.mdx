## Purpose

The one form where money enters: what a student paid, and what it buys. Registering it also dates its classes — a weekly rhythm filled from the student's last class, each row with its day, its hour and its duration, or left «por programar», or taken to a group class — so the staff does not come back to date them one by one. The amount is written with the currency beside it and the help «$350.00 por clase» under it (D14: no «$» drawn inside the field).

## Behaviour

- **The context of a student** («Profesor: … · Le queda 1 clase (hoy 19:00) · Último pago: …») is read through `loadPaymentContext` when the student is known, and «Repetir ese pago» fills the amount, the currency and the count from the last payment.
- **The rows follow the rhythm** until one is touched: the first class's day, hour and duration, every seven days, as many as the payment buys minus the «Sin pago» classes it covers. A row's «…» leaves it «por programar» or takes it to a group class; «Programar» brings it back to the rhythm.
- **Warnings, not refusals**: an overlap of the teacher (`loadTeacherBusy` when the teacher changes), of the student, of another row of the same payment, or a date past, is said under the row.
- **The foot sums it up** in a live region: «Valentina Cruz · $1,400.00 MXN · 4 clases ($350.00 por clase) · 3 con fecha y 1 por programar», recounted with `planPaymentSeats`.
- The [popup-form pattern](/conventions#server-actions): a failure comes back field by field with what was typed, every control reopening on it; a success closes the popup and the page says «Pago registrado: …» from `?aviso=paymentRegistered.<n>.<fechadas>.<por programar>`.
