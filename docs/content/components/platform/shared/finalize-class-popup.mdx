## Purpose

Three moments, one popup. `finalize` (the teacher, PROF-3): «¿Quién vino?», the summary and the recording, both optional, and «Al finalizar, Diego Salas verá si asistió, tu resumen y la grabación.». `change` (D3): the teacher corrects their own marks of a finished class, «Cambiar una marca no duplica la actividad del alumno.»; a mark the staff overrode and a moved seat read, without cards. `staff` (D1): «Finalizar la clase en nombre de GM Profesor Demo», only the list, with «Motivo». In a group class, «Marcar a todos» and «3 de 4 asistieron» in `aria-live`. No trigger of its own; `FinalizeClassButton` draws one (`variant` `accent`, `button` or `row`; `openOnMount` for `?finalizar=1`).

## Behaviour

The [popup-form pattern](/conventions#server-actions). A row sent without a mark gets «Marca si Diego Salas asistió o faltó.» from the browser and, if the server is reached anyway, `missingMark` on the row and on the form. On success the address says `?aviso=classFinalized` (the page composes «Clase finalizada. Diego Salas ya ve tu resumen.») or `attendanceChanged`.
