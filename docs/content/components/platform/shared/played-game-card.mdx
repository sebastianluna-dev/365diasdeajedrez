## Purpose

L2, chosen on 2026-10-08: a played game drawn like a study's card. The final position (`PlayedGameCard.finalFen`, a static diagram, no chessground) from the side the person played, the result in the corner, the rival with their portrait and label, and «Jaque mate · 17 jugadas · hace 2 días». The whole card is a link to the game: to read it, or to go on with it while it is open.

`ResultBadge` says the result from the person's side in one letter or word: «G» on the success's soft fill, «P» on the danger's, «T» and «Anulada» on the neutral fill, «En curso» on the accent's. `resultOf` reads it from the card (an open game, or a closed one without a result: cancelled); `movesText` says «17 jugadas», counting moves and not plies.
