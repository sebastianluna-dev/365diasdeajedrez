## Purpose

The Move Trainer works on chapters the student has chosen. This section lists "Mis capítulos de entrenamiento" (with "Entrenar" per row and "Entrenar todo" in the head) and, below, "Capítulos entrenables": chapters with exercises the student has not added yet. Adding and removing is the same bound action as on the chapter page.

## Props

This component takes no props.

## Behaviour

- Awaits `{ myChapters, availableChapters }`. Each `ChapterRow` links the chapter name to its page, shows course and exercise count, and renders "Entrenar" (a link to `/entrenador?chapter=<id>`) only for chapters in the trainer, plus a `<form action={toggleTrainerChapter.bind(null, chapterId, !inTrainer)}>` with "Agregar"/"Quitar".
- "Entrenar todo" links to `/entrenador?session=all`.
- The available group renders only when non-empty; the own group shows an empty state otherwise.
