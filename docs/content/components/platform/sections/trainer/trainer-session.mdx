## Purpose

The training session. Each exercise is a frozen line (`lineSans`) from a lesson, a starting FEN and the colour the student plays. The board is controlled: the student's move is compared with the expected one, a correct move is applied and, in play-the-line mode, the opponent's reply follows after 450 ms; a wrong move counts a mistake and shows feedback. When the line ends (or, in find-the-move mode, after the first correct move) the attempt is recorded and the next exercise loads; after the last one the [`SessionSummary`](/components/platform/sections/trainer/session-summary) replaces the board.

## Behaviour

- **State:** `index`, `fen`, `plyIndex` (position in `lineSans`), `mistakes`, `feedback`, `results`, `finished`; a start timestamp and a list of pending timers in refs, cleared on unmount.
- **Comparison:** `normalizeSan` strips `+`, `#`, `!`, `?` so "Nf3+" matches "Nf3" and a NAG in the source line does not break the exercise.
- **Correct move:** `applySan` advances the FEN. In `FIND_MOVE` mode or at the end of the line the exercise passes; otherwise the reply is scheduled and `plyIndex` jumps two.
- **Finishing:** `recordTrainingAttempt` receives the exercise id, `PASSED`/`FAILED`, the mistake count and the elapsed milliseconds; the action resolves the user. The next exercise loads after the feedback delay (1.6 s on success, 400 ms on surrender).
- **Feedback** renders in an `aria-live="polite"` area and clears itself unless a newer message replaced it.
- **Stale exercises** (`isStale`: the lesson was edited after the exercise was created) show a "Desactualizado" tag with a tooltip.
- "Rendirse" fails the current exercise; "Terminar sesión" links back to the trainer home.
