## Purpose

The student's classes as the owner chose them on 2026-10-15 («8 · Boletos y tarjetas, sin pagos»), moved whole to the new design system on 2026-10-23 (the owner's «M4» canvas, `MaterialM4Clases`). The page bleeds onto the canvas and the section draws the workspace (`.platform-workspace`); the bar shows «Clases» with its drawing (`NavArt` `teach`), and there is no search: none maps onto a student's classes.

- **The side column**: the two views as [`PlatformSideNav`](/components/platform/shared/platform-side-nav) links with their counts and no group title — «Próximas» (`/clases`, the calendar) and «Pasadas» (`/clases?vista=pasadas`, the arrow back) —, and under them [`PaidClassesCard`](/components/platform/shared/paid-classes-card): the seats the student has left, in counts. «Clases pagadas» on the canvas is that balance, never money. Under 1024px the column goes above the panel and the two sit side by side.
- **«Próximas»** is the agenda by day: each day's heading in the display face with its date or its distance small beside it («Hoy jueves 22 de octubre», «Domingo 25 de octubre en 3 días»). Today's class (and one whose link is open on another day) is `TodayClass`: a band of the primary's wash with the stub of the day on the secondary container — the day, the start in large figures, the end and how long —, the phase as a `ClassPhaseChip` («Enlace abierto» in the primary on white) and the countdown, the topic, «Profesor», «Sala», «Tipo» and the classmates' faces, and the way in: «Entrar a la clase» (Material's filled button) and «Copiar el enlace» (tonal) while the link is open, «Ver la clase» before, and «N para preparar» as a text button to the class's `#para-preparar`. On a phone the page's [`ClassJoinBar`](/components/platform/shared/class-join-bar) (`look="material"`) takes «Entrar a la clase» and the band offers «Ver la clase» instead (`withJoinBar`). A later class is `AgendaRow`: one outlined row, the whole of it the link, that lifts under the pointer — the hours, the topic, who gives it, the kind (with the classmates) and the room, a make-up's note, the phase, and at the right when the link opens («El enlace se abre a las 16:50», «Administración agregará el enlace») and «N para preparar». At the panel's foot, the classes paid and not dated yet as one amber band, «Tienes 1 clase pagada sin fecha. Administración te escribirá para fijarla.» with «Escríbenos». Nothing ahead: «No tienes más clases programadas…».
- **«Pasadas»** is the months, newest first («Octubre 2026 1 clase»), each class beside its day (the number and the weekday) as its [`PastClassCard`](/components/platform/shared/past-class-card): the board of its chess when it has some, the same card without it when it has none. Months, never payments. Ten classes a page: «Ver más» (Material's outlined button) asks for one more (`?vista=pasadas&pagina=<n>`).

The page's heading is «Mis clases · Próximas» (or «Pasadas») for a screen reader only: the bar and the lit view already say it on screen. Without a class of any kind and nothing paid: a dashed box with the faint ghost of a class, «No tienes clases programadas», «Cuando administración programe una clase contigo, aparecerá aquí con su fecha y su enlace.» and «¿Quieres tomar clases? Escríbenos». Nothing on the page says which payment a class belongs to, nor any amount.

While it loads, `ClassesSkeleton` draws the same workspace in the containers' greys (markup and CSS only, as a `loading.tsx` needs).

**Adapted from the canvas**: the canvas's «Profesora» is «Profesor», as the data does not say; its «3 por hacer» is the class's «N para preparar» (the blocks the student may see now); a later row says the link's hour or the staff's, never a countdown; the classmates' faces are the secondary container, not each person's colour.

| Prop        | Type                      | Required | Meaning                                                                |

| `view`      | `StudentClassesView`      | yes      | What `getUserClasses(pastPages)` gives.                                |
| `tab`       | `"proximas" \| "pasadas"` | yes      | The view from `?vista=`.                                               |
| `pastPages` | `number`                  | yes      | How many pages of past classes are shown; «Ver más» asks for one more. |

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/clases/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const tab: ClassesTab = vista === "pasadas" ? "pasadas" : "proximas";
const view = await getUserClasses(pastPages);

<div className="platform-page platform-page_width_full platform-page_frame_bleed">
  <ClassesListSection view={view} tab={tab} pastPages={pastPages} />
</div>;
```
