## Purpose

The student's classes as the owner chose them on 2026-10-15 («8 · Boletos y tarjetas, sin pagos»). Two tabs, links with their counts: «Próximas» (`/clases`) and «Pasadas» (`/clases?vista=pasadas`).

- **«Próximas»** is an agenda by day: a heading per day drawn out into a rule («Hoy · jueves 15 de octubre», «Domingo 18 de octubre · en 3 días»), and under it each class as its [`ClassTicket`](/components/platform/shared/class-ticket) — today's tinted, the one whose link is open with «Entrar a la clase» (on a phone the page's [`ClassJoinBar`](/components/platform/shared/class-join-bar) takes that button, `withJoinBar`). After the days, «Sin fecha»: the classes paid and not dated yet as one blue notice, «Tienes 1 clase pagada sin fecha. Administración te escribirá para fijarla.» with «Escríbenos». Nothing ahead: «No tienes más clases programadas…».
- **«Pasadas»** is a line with a stop per month, newest first («Octubre 2026 · 1 clase»), and each class beside its day (the number and the weekday), drawn as its [`PastClassCard`](/components/platform/shared/past-class-card): the board of its chess when it has some, the same card without it when it has none. Months, never payments: a month of four classes of one payment is four cards. Ten classes a page: «Ver más» asks for one more (`?vista=pasadas&pagina=<n>`).
- **The lane**, beside both tabs (sticky from 1024px, under the list below it): [`PaidClassesCard`](/components/platform/shared/paid-classes-card), in counts.

Without a class of any kind and nothing paid: a dashed box with the faint ghost of a ticket, «No tienes clases programadas», «Cuando administración programe una clase contigo, aparecerá aquí con su fecha y su enlace.» and «¿Quieres tomar clases? Escríbenos». Nothing on the page says which payment a class belongs to.

| `pastPages` | `number`                  | yes      | How many pages of past classes are shown; «Ver más» asks for one more. |
