## Purpose

The page from half an hour after the end on, whether or not the teacher finalized: the phase («Finalizada»), the mark beside it — «Asististe», «No asististe», or where a moved seat went («No asististe · tu clase pasó al vie 16 oct», a link; «… tu clase queda por programar») —, the topic, when, and «Ver la grabación» as the page's accent when there is one. Then the teacher's summary, the material («Material de la clase», every block the student may see now, a game with «Guardar en un estudio» under it when the page gives the studies and the action) and the transcript, a `<details>` only when it is «Lista». The lane holds the index («En esta clase», a `nav` of anchors `#bloque-<id>`, drawn with two blocks or more) and «Tu clase» with the people line, «Esta clase es gratis.» when it is, how many classes are left in counts («Te quedan 3 clases: 2 programadas y 1 por programar.»; never which payment) and «Ver mis clases». The lane sticks from 1024px.
