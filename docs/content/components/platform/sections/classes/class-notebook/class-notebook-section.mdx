## Purpose

The class page as a notebook (2026-10-19, option 5c of the class page canvas), moved whole to the new design system on 2026-10-23 (the owner's «M4» canvas, `MaterialM4Clase`). The page bleeds onto the canvas and the section draws the workspace.

- **The side column.** On top, the way in (`ClassJoinSide`): before the class, «Entrar a la clase» as Material's extended FAB while the link is open («Volver a entrar» once it ended), with the room and the countdown under it; before the link opens, when it does and the countdown (which asks for the page again at that instant, so the link never travels ahead of time); without a link, that the staff will add it and «Escríbenos». After the class, «Ver la grabación» as the FAB and «Asististe» or «No asististe». A cancelled class has nothing there. Under it, «Contenido de la clase»: «2 de 4 hechas · 50 %» over a bar (`NotebookProgressBar`; the exercises tried and the questions answered on this visit), and the article's index as `PlatformSideNav` links to anchors — each heading of the text and of a game, «Ahora tú» with how many questions, each EXERCISE block with «1/3» tried, «Lo que te llevas». At its foot, the seats left in words («Te quedan 4 clases: 3 programadas y 1 por programar.»), never an amount.
- **The panel.** A toolbar stuck under the bar: the arrow and «Clases › Finales de torres» (the class's title), and the link to copy while the student may see it. Then the article: «Clase grupal · jueves 22 de octubre · 18:00», the title in the display face, the teacher's initials and name with the classmates, the course and the reading time; the notices (an error, a notice, cancelled, rescheduled, a make-up, a moved seat); after the class, the teacher's summary on the container; the blocks — text, positions as outlined cards «Posición · juegan blancas» on Material's blue board, «Ahora tú» questions on the container with options as rows with a radio's ring, a game as a small widget with «Abrir en el tablero», the lessons and videos it cites as cards that lift —; «Lo que te llevas» on the primary's wash; and the foot (the next class after it, the course's previous and next, «Ver mis clases» and «¿Algún problema? Escríbenos»). The reading carries `#para-preparar`, where the agenda's «N para preparar» leads.
- **The exercises** of the class's EXERCISE blocks are cards in a column beside the article from 1280px (under it, below the article): each row its number and name, the facts while it is untried, «Resuelto» or «Fallado», and «Resolver» (tonal) or «Volver a ver» (text). An attempt from there counts for the class, not the streak (D49); the solver at `/clases/[classId]/ejercicios/[blockId]/[n]` says so and is not on the new design system yet.

Front-only: the diagrams, the questions, the course, the takeaways and the next class are `NOTEBOOK_SAMPLE`, the same for every class. While it loads, `ClassSkeleton` draws the workspace in the containers' greys (markup and CSS only, as a `loading.tsx` needs).

**Adapted from the canvas**: its index of three entries is the article's own headings plus «Ahora tú», the exercises and «Lo que te llevas», none lit (they are anchors on one page); «Ahora tú» counts the questions, since what is answered lives only in the visit; the canvas's position card beside the text stays in the text where the teacher put it, and only the exercises go to the side column; the exercises lose their small boards.
