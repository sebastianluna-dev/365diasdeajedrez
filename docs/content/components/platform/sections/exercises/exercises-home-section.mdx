## Behaviour

The home of «Ejercicios» (D17, PLAN.md §5.1). Its first card is «De tu profesor»: each series given to the student that they may open now — a teacher's only while that teacher is assigned to them (D07) — with its teacher and «4 de 10», and its row's word, «Empezar» or «Continuar» (`.platform-row-action`), to the first exercise not solved yet. Only the exercises they may solve are counted (`seriesItemAvailability`, the visibility rule's own condition).

The screen's one accent is the first card's last line, «Continuar con «…»»: the newest series with an exercise left that can count today (not solved, not tried this study day), the teacher's before the academy's. With nothing given, the empty state says «Tu profesor aún no te ha dado ejercicios.», or, for a student with no teacher, «Todavía no tienes ejercicios de un profesor. Cuando uno te dé una serie, aparecerá aquí.»

The second card, «Práctica libre» (phase 4, D18, D32), holds [`PracticeFilters`](/components/platform/sections/exercises/practice-filters) in its `home` variant: a topic, a level and «Empezar», which is the accent when no series is left to continue. The third, «Series de la academia» (D32), lists the academy's published series the same way (`academySeriesWhere`: `teacherId` null and `publishedAt` set), the last published first, and is drawn only when there is one. Both kinds open at `/ejercicios/series/[seriesId]` (`getStudentSeries`, through `studentVisibleSeriesWhere`).
