## Props

| Prop         | Type                                                         | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `trail`      | `{ label; href? }[]`                                         | yes      | From «Ejercicios» down to the page; the last crumb is the page's heading, `h1`.                                                                         |
| `summary`    | `string`                                                     | no       | A fact at the right, in the variant ink: «Ejercicio 3 de 10», «4 de 10 resueltos».                                                                      |
| `tallyTopic` | `string`                                                     | no       | The topic on screen, by code: «72 % de acierto · 18 resueltos», as this browser keeps it (the sample rating, `exercise-rating.store.ts`).               |
| `level`      | `{ levels; value; topicCode }`                               | no       | The trainer's level: every level («Todos los niveles») or one band of the Level catalog; a change replaces the address, the topic kept, `tras` dropped. |
| `actions`    | `{ label; href?; onClick?; tone: "filled" \| "outlined" }[]` | no       | Material's buttons at the right end: a link, or, from a Client Component host, a press.                                                                 |

## Behaviour

Data in, as the rule asks: the two parts that live in the browser — the topic's tally (a store of this browser) and the level (it follows the address) — are leaves of their own. The level is a filter, not a form: `PlatformSelect` in the accent's tone, drawn as a small outlined trigger of Material's look, its focus ring the primary; it never says an error.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercises-toolbar` (85 lines): on a hairline (`--platform-m3-divider`) across the panel's top — the panel's padding is undone on three sides —, at least 56px tall; the trail and the end wrap.

**Elements:** `__trail`, `__crumb`, `__separator`, `__title`, `__end`, `__summary`, `__level` (on the select's trigger)

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-on-surface`, `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`, `--platform-m3-outline`, `--platform-m3-divider`, `--platform-m3-panel`, `--platform-m3-primary`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)`.
