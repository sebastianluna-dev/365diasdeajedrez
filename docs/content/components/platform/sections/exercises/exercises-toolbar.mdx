## Behaviour

No trail since 2026-10-23 (the owner's request): the side column lights where the student is, so the page's heading is read out and not drawn, and with no fact, tally nor button the bar draws nothing at all (`_display_heading`) and only the heading is left. Data in, as the rule asks: the topic's tally, a store of this browser, is a leaf of its own. The trainer has no toolbar since 2026-10-23: its level, which this toolbar offered as a select, is the side column's «Nivel» group.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercises-toolbar` (57 lines): on a hairline (`--platform-m3-divider`) across the panel's top — the panel's padding is undone on three sides —, at least 56px tall, its end at the right; the heading is visually hidden, and so is the whole bar with `_display_heading`.
