## Props

| Prop         | Type                                                         | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `trail`      | `{ label; href? }[]`                                         | yes      | From «Ejercicios» down to the page; the last crumb is the page's heading, `h1`.                                                           |
| `summary`    | `string`                                                     | no       | A fact at the right, in the variant ink: «Ejercicio 3 de 10», «4 de 10 resueltos».                                                        |
| `tallyTopic` | `string`                                                     | no       | The topic on screen, by code: «72 % de acierto · 18 resueltos», as this browser keeps it (the sample rating, `exercise-rating.store.ts`). |

## Behaviour

Data in, as the rule asks: the topic's tally, a store of this browser, is a leaf of its own. The trainer has no toolbar since 2026-10-23: its level, which this toolbar offered as a select, is the side column's «Nivel» group.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercises-toolbar` (68 lines): on a hairline (`--platform-m3-divider`) across the panel's top — the panel's padding is undone on three sides —, at least 56px tall; the trail and the end wrap.
