## Props

| Prop    | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                  |
| ------- | ------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `view`  | `"board" \| "list"` | yes      | An exercise on the board, or a list (the series, a series, the history). |
| `label` | `string`            | yes      | Said to a screen reader: «Cargando el ejercicio…», «Cargando la serie…». |

## Behaviour

The layout keeps the side column in place (2026-10-23), so only the panel waits, in the shape of the page that is coming: the toolbar's line, then the board — the new design system's blue, with a ring turning on it — with its keys' place and the column's three cards beside it, at the solve page's own sizes; or six rows of a list on their hairlines. Nothing jumps when the page arrives.

## Markup & styling

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1280px)`, `(width < 1024px)`, `(width < 640px)`.
