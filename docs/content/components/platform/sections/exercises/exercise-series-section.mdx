## Behaviour

A series (D18, D31; PLAN.md §5.2). The section draws the page's head: the way back to «Ejercicios», the title, «De Profesor Demo · 2 de 5 resueltos» and, when an exercise is left, the page's accent, «Empezar» (nothing tried yet) or «Continuar», to the first one not solved. Under it the description and the exercises in order, each a row that opens it: the start drawn small (from the side that plays), «3. Título», «Mate en 2 · 1400 · Intermedio», and the student's mark **in this series** — «Resuelto» with `CircleCheckIcon` in the success ink (a pass there), «Fallado» with `CircleXmarkIcon` in the danger colour (attempts, none passed), or nothing. The word is always written beside the icon.

Only the exercises the student may solve through this series are listed, numbered and counted: the series owner's usable ones and the library's (`seriesItemAvailability`); a draft or an archived one is not there. A series the student may not open — another student's, or a former teacher's — never reaches here: the page answers 404.
