## Behaviour

The service picks at random among the best tier — never solved and not tried today, then never solved, then not tried today, then any (D18; solved by family, so a copy in a lesson counts) — skipping the exercise just solved (`?tras=`) while another is left. The solver is keyed by the exercise's id, so «Siguiente» mounts a new board; an exercise tried this study day opens unscored (§2.7). With every candidate solved, a notice says they repeat; with none, the empty state says «No hay ejercicios de ese tema en ese nivel todavía.»; with nothing chosen, a line asks to choose.
