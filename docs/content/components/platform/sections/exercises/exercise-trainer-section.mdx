## Behaviour

Since the move to the new design system (2026-10-23) «Ejercicios» and «Ejercicios por tema» are one page: the topic is chosen in the side column, the level in the toolbar, and both live in the address (`/ejercicios?tema=<Topic code>&nivel=<Level slug>`). The service is the one it was (D18, D32): a random library exercise of the topic (its subtopics included) in the level's Elo range, the ones never solved and not tried today first, with the context `PRACTICE`; with no topic, any topic, and with no level, any level (`open`); «Siguiente» (`&tras=<id>`) deals another of the same choice; one tried this study day opens unscored (§2.7); every one of the choice solved, a note says they repeat.

The toolbar: «Ejercicios › Táctica › Clavada» (the topic its `h1`, its parent a crumb that keeps the level), the topic's tally, and the level. With nothing dealt the panel says why: an empty library («La biblioteca aún no tiene ejercicios.», with the way to the series), a choice with no exercise («No hay ejercicios de ese tema en ese nivel todavía.»), or a code that is not in the catalogs.
