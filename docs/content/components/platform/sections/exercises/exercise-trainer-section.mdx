## Behaviour

Since the move to the new design system (2026-10-23) «Ejercicios» and «Ejercicios por tema» are one page: the topic and the level are chosen in the side column (the level in its «Nivel» group since 2026-10-23; it was a select in the panel's toolbar), and both live in the address (`/ejercicios?tema=<Topic code>&nivel=<Level slug>`). The service is the one it was (D18, D32): a random library exercise of the topic (its subtopics included) in the level's Elo range, the ones never solved and not tried today first, with the context `PRACTICE`; with no topic, any topic, and with no level, any level (`open`); «Siguiente» (`&tras=<id>`) deals another of the same choice; one tried this study day opens unscored (§2.7); every one of the choice solved, a note says they repeat.

**No toolbar** (2026-10-23, the owner's request): the trail, the topic's tally and the level went; the topic on screen and the level are the side column's lit links, and the page's `h1` is the topic, read out and not drawn (`.exercise-trainer__heading`). With nothing dealt the panel says why: an empty library («La biblioteca aún no tiene ejercicios.», with the way to the series), a choice with no exercise («No hay ejercicios de ese tema en ese nivel todavía.»), or a code that is not in the catalogs.

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercise-trainer` (24 lines): the heading read out and not drawn (`__heading`), and the repeat note, a pill of the info container; the rest is the solve page's.
