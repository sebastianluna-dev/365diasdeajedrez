## Purpose

«Ejercicios» moved to the new design system on 2026-10-23, from the owner's «M4» canvas, and «Puzzle Rush» with it. The two route folders live in one route group, `app/(platform)/(exercises)/`, whose layout draws this frame once, so moving between a topic, a series, the history and the rush keeps the column in place and only the panel changes, as Gmail's labels do. The URLs did not change. The bar carries no search: the canvas drew «Buscar un tema o una serie», but the column already lists every topic and series, so a search would find nothing more (the owner took such searches out of other pages).

## Props

`children`: the page, its `.platform-workspace__panel`, or the loading skeleton of one. A container: its one job is to place the panel.

## Behaviour

- **«Puzzle Rush»** is the column's action, Material's extended FAB with the stopwatch; it is filled with the primary (`current`) while the rush is the page on screen.
- **«Temas»**: «Todos los temas» (`/ejercicios`, lit too on `/ejercicios/biblioteca/[id]`), then each first-level topic that has a library exercise (a parent through its children). The topic of the address (`?tema=`), or the parent of the subtopic of the address, is open: its first five subtopics under it (`SUBTOPIC_LIMIT`; the one on screen always among them), and «Ver los N subtemas» shows the rest. A topic's link keeps the level chosen (`?nivel=`).
- **«Tuyo»**: «De tu profesor» with the count of the series given, «Series de la academia» when the academy has published any, and «Historial». A series' page and its exercises light the view the series belongs to.
- Which link is lit is the browser's to say (`exercisesNavGroups(data, location, expanded)`): the layout does not render again when the address changes.
- It waits for nothing: the column's data streams in behind `ExercisesSideSkeleton` (the FAB's place and the two groups of rows, pulsing in the container grey; markup and CSS only), and the page behind its own `loading.tsx` ([`ExercisesPanelSkeleton`](/components/platform/sections/exercises/exercises-panel-skeleton)).

## Markup & styling

Block `.exercises-side-skeleton` (80 lines): the same rhythm as `PlatformSideNav`, so the links land where its shapes are; its pulse is off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

**Elements:** `__label` (for a screen reader), `__shape`, `__action`, `__group`, `__title`, `__row`, `__icon`, `__text`

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-container-high`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`.
