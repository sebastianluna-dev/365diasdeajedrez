## Props

| Prop     | Type                   | Required | Meaning                                                 |
| -------- | ---------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rows`   | `ExerciseHistoryRow[]` | yes      | Every scored attempt of the student.                    |
| `nowIso` | `string`               | yes      | The instant the periods are counted from, the server's. |
| `sample` | `boolean`              | no       | The rows are a sample: a line says so.                  |

## Behaviour

«Historial» (D51; PLAN.md §9, phase 6): the attempts, the latest first, each as the row the teacher reads, in its material look (a row across the panel, as Gmail lists mail), narrowed by three filters that are not a form — the result, where it came from, the period (`filterExerciseHistory`) — and shown 25 at a time («Ver más», Material's outlined button, with «25 de 41»).
