## Purpose

«Cursos» is one place with two collections (2026-10-22): the student's courses at `/cursos` and the catalog at `/cursos/catalogo`. Each has its own address — the catalog is not a filter of the student's courses —, and both are drawn in this frame, so moving between them feels like one page, as Gmail's labels do: the `.platform-workspace`, the side column with «Seguir donde iba» and the groups of `coursesNavGroups` (`courses-nav.const.ts`: «Tus cursos» by state, «Catálogo» whole and by level, each with its count, the view on screen lit), and the catalog's search in the bar. A container: its one job is to place the panel it is given (`children`, a `.platform-workspace__panel`).

## Props

| Prop         | Type                     | Required | Meaning                                                          |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `groups`     | `PlatformSideNavGroup[]` | yes      | `coursesNavGroups(mine, catalog, levels, view)`.                 |
| `resumeHref` | `string`                 | no       | The next lesson of the course to go on with: «Seguir donde iba». |
| `query`      | `string`                 | no       | What the search already holds, on the catalog's results.         |
| `children`   | `ReactNode`              | yes      | The panel.                                                       |

No stylesheet of its own: the workspace's rules live in `platform.css`.
