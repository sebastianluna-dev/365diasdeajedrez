## Purpose

What the side column shows while [`CoursesSideSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/courses-side-section) reads its data (2026-10-22): the extended FAB's place (56px, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`) and two groups — a title and four or five rows of an icon, a label and a count — at the rhythm of `PlatformSideNav`, so the links land where these are. The shapes are `--platform-m3-container-high` and pulse softly together (`opacity`, 1.2s), standing still under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. To a screen reader it is one status, «Cargando tus cursos…». Markup and CSS only.
