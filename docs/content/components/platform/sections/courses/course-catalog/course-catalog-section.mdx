## Purpose

Every course the academy has published, to find the ones to add (2026-10-22). It replaced «Explorar cursos» (`/cursos/explorar`, which now answers 308 here, keeping `?nivel=`), its head and its column of filters (`CatalogBrowser`). It is the panel of [`CoursesWorkspace`](/components/platform/sections/courses/courses-workspace), so it shares the side column and the bar's search with «Mis cursos», and moving between them feels like one page, as Gmail's labels do; but it has its own address, because the catalog is another collection, not a filter of the student's.

From top to bottom:

1. **The heading**: the level chosen in the side column with its dot, or «Todo el catálogo», or «Resultados de «…»» for a search; beside it «N cursos · M que aún no llevas».
2. **The filters**, Material's filter chips (`mdChip("filter", selected)`): the topics the catalog's courses have, one at a time, and after a divider «Que aún no llevas». A chosen one carries the check and `aria-current`; pressing it again takes it away. Each is a link (`catalogHref`).
3. **The courses** ([`CourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-card)), as many to a row as fit of at least 200px. One not added gets «Agregar» (`addCourse` bound to its id), which adds it and takes the student to it. With nothing left by the filters, an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state) leads to the whole catalog.

The filters only narrow what is listed, on the server and from the address (`?nivel=`, `?tema=`, `?nuevos=1`, `?q=`): nothing is closed to anybody by their rating.
