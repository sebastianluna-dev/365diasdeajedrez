## Purpose

Two small cards beside the course list: "Racha diaria" (days in a row with study activity) and "Objetivo diario" (minutes studied today against the goal, with a bar).

Neither figure is stored. The streak is counted backwards from the activity log (`lib/study-streak`) and today's minutes are the durations of the lessons finished today. The only thing persisted is the goal itself, because it is a decision, not a computation.

## Props

| Prop        | Type        | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------- | ----------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `goal`      | `StudyGoal` | yes      | `{ streakDays, minutesToday, goalMinutes, percent }`, from `getStudyGoal()` in the page.                                                                                    |
| `errorCode` | `string`    | no       | What `updateDailyGoal` bounced back with in `?error=` (`goal`, `throttled`). Renders a `PlatformNotice` inside the editor and opens the `<details>` so the form is in view. |

## Behaviour

- The streak note adapts its wording: "Estudia hoy para empezar una racha." at zero, otherwise "n días seguidos estudiando."
- "Cambiar objetivo" is a `<details>` rather than a modal: choosing from a short list needs no JavaScript. Inside, a `<form action={updateDailyGoal}>` with a `<select name="goalMinutes">` over `GOAL_OPTIONS` and a save button. The action validates the value against the same list and revalidates `/cursos`.
- The bar's width is `goal.percent` inline; the service clamps it.
