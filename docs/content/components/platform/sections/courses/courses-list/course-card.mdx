## Purpose

One course, built for **resuming**, not for choosing. Whoever is enrolled already knows what the course is about, and an introduction paragraph on the card pushed down exactly what one comes to look at: the two progress measures and the button that returns to the last lesson. The description, the levels and the authors are therefore folded into a "···" menu, one click away, and shown in full on the course page.

The two meters are the card's own (`Meter`), not the shared [`ProgressIndicator`](/components/platform/shared/progress-indicator): the card shows progress in two units at once (lessons and minutes) in a compact layout the shared bar does not have.

## Behaviour

- **Cover link** goes to the same place as the title, so it is taken out of the tab order (`tabIndex={-1}`, `aria-hidden`): a screen reader gains nothing from the link twice, and a cover without text could not name itself. `next/image` with `fill` and `sizes="(max-width: 720px) 100vw, 340px"`.
- **Menu** is a native `<details>`/`<summary>`, not a JavaScript menu: three facts and a link, opened and closed without hydrating anything. The summary has an `aria-label` of "Más sobre …" followed by the course name.
- `Meter` computes the percentage with a guard for zero totals.
