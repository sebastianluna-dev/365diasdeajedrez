## Purpose

The chapters and rows of the column, and one of its two client pieces (the other is the filter's switch, [`PriorityLessonsFilter`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/priority-lessons-filter)).

Client because of one thing: **which lesson is open**. The column lives in the chapter's layout, and a layout is not rendered again when one moves within it — that is the whole point, the list is not fetched between lessons — so the server cannot mark the row. `usePathname` does, and it does it the instant the link is followed, before the lesson's own PGN has arrived: the row lights up first and the board follows.

Keeping the place in the list is no longer anyone's job either. The element is never unmounted, so its scroll stays where it was; what used to remember it in `sessionStorage` (`OutlineScroll`) went away with the move.

## Props

| Prop       | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                               |
| ---------- | ------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapters` | `OutlineChapter[]` | yes      | Every chapter of the course with its `lessons` (`LessonSibling[]`), in reading order. |

## Behaviour

- Each row's name is drawn on one line and cut with an ellipsis when it does not fit (`lesson-outline.comp.css`); the whole name is the name's `title`, read on hover (2026-10-06).
- The row whose `href` is the current pathname is not a link — it leads where one already is — and carries `aria-current="page"`, which is what says so to a screen reader and what the stylesheet paints in the accent.
- **On arriving, the column opens on the lesson's chapter** (2026-10-06): the chapter's head at the top of the column and its lessons under it, at once. The browser stops the scroll short where the course ends, so a last chapter too short to fill the column shows what is above it too. Only when the lesson being read would be left under the bottom edge — deep in a long chapter — is the row brought up instead, as below, and the chapter's head is still the first thing in view, stuck to the top. The column may still be settling when it is first drawn — a font, a stylesheet (under `next dev`, a client component's sheet can land after the effect) or an image arriving late changes the height of what is above the chapter and left it a few px off the top, a slice of the previous head showing — so a `ResizeObserver` on the list puts it back each time the list changes size, and once more when `document.fonts.ready` resolves, until the person scrolls the column (wheel, touch, keys, pointer) or two seconds go by. A cleanup before it settles (React's development double run) leaves the next run an arrival still. Measured on the running app at 1440, 1600 and 1728px wide, on a cold load and on a click from another chapter: the head at 0px from the top every time.
- **After that, it brings the lesson being read back into view when it is cut off**, smoothly, on every change of pathname: above the fold it goes to the top of the list — **under its chapter, not under the top edge**, since that chapter's name is stuck there and what it covers is not room to be seen from (the row's own `<section>` gives its `[data-sticky-head]`, which is measured); below the fold, far enough to leave a row's height under it, so it lands as the next to last one in view rather than pressed against the edge. A rect would carry any zoom on an ancestor and `scrollTop` would not, so the distances cross over with `currentCSSZoom` (1 since the platform lost its zoom on 2026-10-02). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the scroll is instant, and so is the first one on arriving, which smoothly would sweep through every chapter above the lesson.
- **Each chapter is a `<section>`** labelled by its name, with its head (`.lesson-outline__chapter`, `data-sticky-head`: the name, one line and cut with an ellipsis, its `title` the whole name, and the ring of how much of it is done, counted from the lessons drawn) and its `<ol>` of lessons. The head is sticky inside its section, so the next chapter's head pushes it away when its lessons end (2026-10-06).
- It reaches the scroller through its own `parentElement`, which is the `.lesson-outline__body` it is mounted in, so nothing has to hand it a ref and the column around it stays a Server Component.
