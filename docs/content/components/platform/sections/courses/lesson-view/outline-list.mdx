## Purpose

The chapters and rows of the column, and one of its two client pieces (the other is the filter's switch, [`PriorityLessonsFilter`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/priority-lessons-filter)).

Client because of one thing: **which lesson is open**. The column lives in the chapter's layout, and a layout is not rendered again when one moves within it — that is the whole point, the list is not fetched between lessons — so the server cannot mark the row. `usePathname` does, and it does it the instant the link is followed, before the lesson's own PGN has arrived: the row lights up first and the board follows.

Keeping the place in the list is no longer anyone's job either. The element is never unmounted, so its scroll stays where it was; what used to remember it in `sessionStorage` (`OutlineScroll`) went away with the move.

## Props

| Prop       | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                               |
| ---------- | ------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapters` | `OutlineChapter[]` | yes      | Every chapter of the course with its `lessons` (`LessonSibling[]`), in reading order. |

## Behaviour

- Under the title «Capítulos», **each chapter is a `<section>`** labelled by its name, whose head is a `<button>` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that opens and closes its `<ol>` of lessons, as a label folds in Gmail (2026-10-22, the new design system; until then every chapter was open, its head stuck to the top of the column). The head carries the chapter's number — a check on the success green once all its lessons are learned —, its name on one line cut with an ellipsis (the whole name its `title`), «learned/total» and a chevron.
- **The chapter of the lesson being read is open on arrival**, and a move into another chapter opens that one too; what was opened or closed by hand stays so. It is state set during render from the pathname (the chapter last shown is remembered beside it), not an effect.
- The row whose `href` is the current pathname is not a link — it leads where one already is — and carries `aria-current="page"`, which is what says so to a screen reader and what the stylesheet lights. A lesson learned carries `CircleCheckIcon` (Duotone Solid) in the success green, the one being read a play (when not learned), the rest a clock.
- **On each change of pathname it brings the lesson being read into view** when the column leaves it cut off (`scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest" })`), smoothly unless `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` says otherwise.
