## Purpose

The chapter of the column, and its one client piece. Since 2026-10-23 it is **that chapter alone** — the one the lesson is in, whose layout the column belongs to —, and no longer every chapter of the course folded under its head: what one moves through while reading is the chapter's lessons, and the course's other chapters are on its page.

Client because of one thing: **which lesson is open**. The column lives in the chapter's layout, and a layout is not rendered again when one moves within it — that is the whole point, the list is not fetched between lessons — so the server cannot mark the row. `usePathname` does, and it does it the instant the link is followed, before the lesson's own PGN has arrived: the row lights up first and the board follows.

## Props

| Prop      | Type             | Required | Meaning                                                                                                |
| --------- | ---------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `chapter` | `OutlineChapter` | yes      | The chapter the lesson is in (`id`, `order`, `name`, `href`, `lessons`), its lessons in reading order. |

## Behaviour

- **The chapter's head** is a link to its page: «Capítulo 2» over its name (one line, cut with an ellipsis, the whole name its `title`) and «learned/total».
- The row whose `href` is the current pathname is not a link — it leads where one already is — and carries `aria-current="page"`, which is what says so to a screen reader and what the stylesheet lights. A lesson learned carries `CircleCheckIcon` (Classic Solid) in the primary's blue, the one being read a play (when not learned), the rest a clock.
- **On each change of pathname it brings the lesson being read into view** when the column leaves it cut off (`scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest" })`), smoothly unless `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` says otherwise.
