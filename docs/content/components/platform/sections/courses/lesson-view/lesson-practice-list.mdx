## Purpose

A lesson may have no game at all (D25): the staff writes only exercises into it, and `isPracticeOnly` is derived on reading (`mapLessonView`: no content by `lessonHasContent`, and exercises in play) — no column stores it. Its page then has no board, so this stands in its place: the lesson's name and badge as the viewer's head would carry them, its description, the way in as the page's one accent, and the exercises in order, each the start position small, its title and facts («Secuencia · 1200 · Intermedio»), and «Resuelto» — by solve key — on the ones solved.

| `practice`    | `LessonPractice`                                                                 | yes      | The exercises with the student's standing and `start`.                                                                                           |
| `onwards`     | `{ skipHref?, skipLabel, skipTitle?, finishLabel, finish: () => Promise<void> }` | yes      | The lesson's way through, as [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer)'s `onwards`: «Saltar» to the next lesson and «Continuar». |

## Behaviour

- The head: «3 ejercicios · 1 de 3 resueltos».
- `start` is the page's accent while an exercise can still count today; once none is left, the accent moves to the lesson's «Continuar».
- Under the list, the lesson's way through: «Saltar» (a link, when there is a next lesson) and «Continuar», a `<form>` over `completeLesson` — always there, because the exercises are optional (D26).
