## Purpose

Where a student studies, in three columns: where the lesson falls in the course ([`LessonOutline`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-outline)), the board, and what is said about the position.

The lesson is a PGN with commentary, so the middle and the right are the [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer) with `moveList="flow"`: a lesson is **read**, the moves run on until a comment breaks the line, whereas the table is for consulting a game move by move. The viewer's header carries the lesson's name, its minutes and a "Prioridad" badge.

Under the viewer, the actions: mark as completed (or the completed state), next lesson or back to the chapter, "Entrenar esta lección (n)" when it has exercises, and nothing that goes back: the chapter is a click away in the menu and the browser does the rest.

«Entrenar esta lección» is not drawn today: the Move Trainer is switched off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) and `getLessonView` drops `trainerHref`.

## Behaviour

- **A lesson is not numbered, and it no longer names its chapter either.** The viewer's title is the lesson's name and its subtitle its minutes alone («20 min»): the column on the left already says the course, the chapter and where in it this lesson falls.
- [`LessonTracker`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-tracker) is rendered first and marks the lesson as opened from the client, because writes never happen during the server render.
- "Marcar como completada" is `<form action={completeLesson.bind(null, lesson.id)}>`; the action records completion, updates the course's return point and revalidates. When `statusCode` is `COMPLETED` the form is replaced by the check mark and "Lección completada".
- The viewer's `skip` slot gets a "Saltar" link to the next lesson (titled "Pasar a la siguiente lección sin marcar esta") when there is one.
- Next and previous hrefs respect the priority filter: the mapper computes them over the visible lessons.

## Markup & styling

Block `.lesson-view` (89 lines). A grid of `260px` and the rest, the outline in the first column and everything else in `.lesson-view__main`; it lowers the viewer's board ceiling to `490px` through `--viewer-board-max`, so that with the column taken out of the width the text panel is still a column and not a margin. At `(max-width: 1080px)` it becomes one column, the board gets its `600px` back and the outline drops under the lesson.

**Elements:** `.lesson-view__main`, `.lesson-view__skip`, `.lesson-view__actions`, `.lesson-view__action`, `.lesson-view__action-icon`, `.lesson-view__completed`, `.lesson-view__completed-icon`

No modifiers.

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button`, `.platform-button_variant_secondary`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-success-ink`, `--platform-success-soft`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`

No breakpoints of its own: the viewer and the buttons bring theirs.

## Usage
