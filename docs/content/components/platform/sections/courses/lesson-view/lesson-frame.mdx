## Purpose

The screen a lesson is read on: the column of lessons and, beside it, the lesson.

It is mounted by the **chapter's** layout, and that is the whole reason the lessons hang from `/cursos/<curso>/<capítulo>/<lección>`. A layout is fetched once and is not rendered again while one moves inside it, so going from one lesson to the next costs its PGN and nothing else: the column is not queried, not drawn, not scrolled back to the top, and the board is the only thing that blinks.

It takes `children` because it is a container and nothing else — the exception the conventions allow. What goes in them is [`LessonBoardSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-board-section), or [`LessonSkeleton`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-skeleton) while that is on its way.

## Props

| Prop       | Type            | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `outline`  | `CourseOutline` | yes      | The course, its chapters and their lessons: what the column lists. It names no lesson, because the layout does not know which one is open. |
| `children` | `ReactNode`     | yes      | The lesson's own segment.                                                                                                                  |

## Markup & styling

Since 2026-10-22 the lesson is drawn in the new design system (Material 3 Expressive; see [Styling](/ui/styling#the-new-design-system-material-3-expressive)), as the course and the chapter are. The frame draws the **workspace** (`.platform-workspace`, in `platform.css`): the outline in `.platform-workspace__side` on the canvas, and the lesson in `.platform-workspace__panel`, the white panel, which is also the `<section>` of block `.lesson-frame` (`aria-label="Lección"`). The panel is a column holding `.lesson-frame__main` alone, which takes all of it. It had a toolbar above the lesson — back to the chapter, the trail course › chapter › lesson and the arrows to the lessons beside it — until 2026-10-23, when the owner took it out: the column of lessons beside the panel already says where one is and moves on, and the toolbar only took height from the board. The way back to the chapter is now the chapter's line above the lesson's title («Capítulo 2 · La Najdorf», a link).

**The page is the window.** The layout's own `<div>` carries `.platform-page`, `.lesson-page`, `.platform-page_width_full`, `.platform-page_frame_bleed`, `.platform-page_height_screen` and `.platform-page_scale_base` (the lesson keeps the base type and space scale from 1536px too, where every other page takes a step up: see [Styling](/ui/styling#type-and-space)), and the workspace takes the modifier `.platform-workspace_height_screen`: exactly the height under the bar, the side scrolling on its own and the panel not at all. Nothing here scrolls the page — the outline and the panel of text each move on their own — because what is looked at is a board, and a board that falls below the fold is a board nobody sees whole.

So the board is measured by the **height** that is left, not by the width, and that height is **read off the column itself**: `.lesson-frame__main` is a size container (`container-type: size`; it is a flex item that takes what the toolbar leaves, so what it holds never sizes it), and `--viewer-board-max` is `max(320px, calc(100cqh - var(--lesson-board-footer)))`, with no ceiling. `--lesson-board-footer` is what the board's footer takes under it, the 40px of Material's keys plus the card's `--platform-space-4` gap, and it is also the `--viewer-panel-trim` that ends the panel of text where the board ends. At `(width < 1280px)` or `(max-height: 620px)` the workspace lets the page scroll again (its height becomes a minimum), the board gets a flat `600px` back and the column is as tall as what it holds, which a size container would collapse to nothing; under 1024px the side drops above the panel, as on every page of the workspace.

The column of notes beside the board runs down to the foot of the panel (`--viewer-panel-trim: 0px`) and is never narrower than 384px (`--viewer-panel-min`), as in the owner's design: the board gives way for the line, the note and the practice card, which is now at the foot of that column and no longer takes its height off the board (`.lesson-frame__main_with_practice` went on 2026-10-22). `.lesson-frame__main_kind_practice`, for a lesson with no game, is no size container (`container-type: normal`) and scrolls, since the list it holds is as tall as what it has.

The board and its panel of text are the [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer)'s in its material look (`look="material"`): the blue board with the «elegida» pieces, Material's keys under it, and the notation loose on the panel.

**Elements:** `.lesson-frame__main`

**Modifiers:** `.lesson-frame__main_kind_practice`.

**Variables it hands down:** `--lesson-board-footer` (its own), `--viewer-board-max`, `--viewer-column-gap`, `--viewer-panel-min`, `--viewer-panel-trim`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/[courseRef]/[chapterOrder]/(lesson)/layout.tsx`:

```tsx
const outline = await getCourseOutline(courseRef, order);
if (!outline) notFound();

<div className="platform-page lesson-page platform-page_width_full platform-page_frame_bleed platform-page_height_screen platform-page_scale_base">
  <LessonFrame outline={outline}>{children}</LessonFrame>
</div>;
```
