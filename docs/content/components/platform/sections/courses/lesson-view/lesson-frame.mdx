## Purpose

The screen a lesson is read on: the column of lessons and, beside it, the lesson.

It is mounted by the **chapter's** layout, and that is the whole reason the lessons hang from `/cursos/<curso>/<capítulo>/<lección>`. A layout is fetched once and is not rendered again while one moves inside it, so going from one lesson to the next costs its PGN and nothing else: the column is not queried, not drawn, not scrolled back to the top, and the board is the only thing that blinks.

It takes `children` because it is a container and nothing else — the exception the conventions allow. What goes in them is [`LessonBoardSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-board-section), or [`LessonSkeleton`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-skeleton) while that is on its way.

## Props

| Prop       | Type            | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `outline`  | `CourseOutline` | yes      | The course, its chapters and their lessons: what the column lists. It names no lesson, because the layout does not know which one is open. |
| `children` | `ReactNode`     | yes      | The lesson's own segment.                                                                                                                  |

## Markup & styling

Since 2026-10-22 the lesson is drawn in the new design system (Material 3 Expressive; see [Styling](/ui/styling#the-new-design-system-material-3-expressive)), as the course and the chapter are. The frame draws the **workspace** (`.platform-workspace`, in `platform.css`): the outline in `.platform-workspace__side` on the canvas, and the lesson in `.platform-workspace__panel`, the white panel, which is also the `<section>` of block `.lesson-frame` (`aria-label="Lección"`). The panel is a column: the lesson's toolbar (back to the chapter, the trail course › chapter › lesson, and the arrows to the lessons beside it, drawn by [`LessonBoardSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-board-section)) and, under it, `.lesson-frame__main`, which takes the rest.

**The page is the window.** The layout's own `<div>` carries `.platform-page`, `.lesson-page`, `.platform-page_width_full`, `.platform-page_frame_bleed`, `.platform-page_height_screen` and `.platform-page_scale_base` (the lesson keeps the base type and space scale from 1536px too, where every other page takes a step up: see [Styling](/ui/styling#type-and-space)), and the workspace takes the modifier `.platform-workspace_height_screen`: exactly the height under the bar, the side scrolling on its own and the panel not at all. Nothing here scrolls the page — the outline and the panel of text each move on their own — because what is looked at is a board, and a board that falls below the fold is a board nobody sees whole.

So the board is measured by the **height** that is left, not by the width, and that height is **read off the column itself**: `.lesson-frame__main` is a size container (`container-type: size`; it is a flex item that takes what the toolbar leaves, so what it holds never sizes it), and `--viewer-board-max` is `max(320px, calc(100cqh - var(--lesson-board-footer)))`, with no ceiling. `--lesson-board-footer` is what the board's footer takes under it, the 48px of its buttons plus the card's `--platform-space-4` gap, and it is also the `--viewer-panel-trim` that ends the panel of text where the board ends. At `(width < 1280px)` or `(max-height: 620px)` the workspace lets the page scroll again (its height becomes a minimum), the board gets a flat `600px` back and the column is as tall as what it holds, which a size container would collapse to nothing; under 1024px the side drops above the panel, as on every page of the workspace.

The lesson's exercises (phase 2 of Exercises, D25) change the measure in two ways, both the frame's: `.lesson-frame__main_with_practice`, set by [`LessonBoardSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-board-section) when the lesson has exercises, takes `--lesson-practice-card` — the 72px of the «Ahora practica» row plus its gap — off the board as well, so the row is seen whole without scrolling; and `.lesson-frame__main_kind_practice`, for a lesson with no game, is no size container (`container-type: normal`) and scrolls, since the list it holds is as tall as what it has.

The board and its panel of text are still the [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer)'s, in the style it has everywhere: it is shared with the studies and the site, and moves to the new design system on its own.

**Elements:** `.lesson-frame__toolbar`, `.lesson-frame__trail`, `.lesson-frame__steps`, `.lesson-frame__crumb`, `.lesson-frame__crumb-step`, `.lesson-frame__crumb-here`, `.lesson-frame__main`

**Modifiers:** `.lesson-frame__crumb_kind_course` (the course's name, hidden under 1280px: the chapter leads back), `.lesson-frame__step_state_off` (an arrow with no lesson beyond it), `.lesson-frame__main_with_practice`, `.lesson-frame__main_kind_practice`.

**Variables it hands down:** `--lesson-board-footer`, `--lesson-practice-card` (its own), `--viewer-board-max`, `--viewer-column-gap`, `--viewer-panel-trim`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/[courseRef]/[chapterOrder]/(lesson)/layout.tsx`:

```tsx
const outline = await getCourseOutline(courseRef, order);
if (!outline) notFound();

<div className="platform-page lesson-page platform-page_width_full platform-page_frame_bleed platform-page_height_screen platform-page_scale_base">
  <LessonFrame outline={outline}>{children}</LessonFrame>
</div>;
```
