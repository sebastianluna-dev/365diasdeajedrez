## Purpose

A lesson's exercises are optional (D26) and come after its game (D25): one row under the board says how many there are, how many the student solved and offers the way in. «Resuelto» is counted **by solve key** (§2.7 of the plan): a lesson's copy of a library exercise counts as solved when any row of its family — itself or its original — has `firstSolvedAt`, so the same exercise is never solved twice for the streak. Behind `EXERCISES_ENABLED`: off, `getLessonView` hands no `practice` and the card is not drawn.

## Props

| Prop       | Type             | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ---------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `practice` | `LessonPractice` | yes      | `total`, `solved`, `items` and `start` (the first exercise not solved yet and not tried today, with its label), or null. |

## Behaviour

- «Ahora practica: 3 ejercicios» and «1 de 3 resueltos» («Todos resueltos» once they are).
- The accent is `start`: «Empezar» with nothing tried, «Continuar» once something was; it skips an exercise tried this study day, which would open unscored (§2.7). With no `start` left, a plain «Repasar» to the first exercise.
- The lesson is completed by the board's own button, never here.
