## Purpose

Where the lesson stands: the course at the top, the chapter under it and the chapter's lessons below, with the one open marked. It is the chapter's page condensed into a column, so moving on to the next lesson does not mean going back twice — which is what the screen used to ask for, since the only way on was the «Siguiente lección» button at the foot.

It is **pinned to the window**, not laid out in the page: it begins right under the bar, touches the left edge and runs to the bottom one, so the lessons read as a panel of the screen rather than a card floating on it.

It belongs to the **chapter's layout** and not to the lesson's page, which is what keeps it from being fetched and drawn again on every move — and is also why it does not know which lesson is open: the list does, in the browser (see [`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list)).

One object, read by the chapter's segment: the column takes the model and decides what it draws. |

## Behaviour

- The lesson being read **is not a link**: it would lead where one already is. It is drawn as a `<span>` with `aria-current="page"` and the accent background, which is also what tells it apart in the list. Which row that is, is decided in the browser by [`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list), since a layout does not learn the pathname.
- A completed lesson carries `CircleCheckIcon` in Duotone Solid (`variant="duotone-solid"`), the same mark the chapter's page uses. The current one paints it in its own colour so the row reads as one piece.
- The list is the **same sequence** `prevLessonHref`/`nextLessonHref` walk: the mapper builds both from `flattenLessons`, so a lesson hidden by the priority filter is missing from the column and from the buttons alike, and the two never disagree.
- **The course's head carries its filter.** Under the course's name, beside the image, «Sólo lecciones de prioridad» — the same per-course setting the chapter's page offers through [`LearnMode`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/learn-mode) — as a small switch with no card (`PlatformSwitch` with `layout="inline"` and `tone="accent"`, in [`PriorityLessonsFilter`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/priority-lessons-filter), the column's client leaf), as in the design the screen was drawn after (2026-10-06). It takes effect the moment it is pressed, which is what a switch promises. It is still a `<form action={setOnlyPriorityLessons…}>`: the switch only submits it, so the server action writes the preference and draws the page again, and where it comes back to is the lesson being read, which [`CurrentPathField`](/components/platform/shared/current-path-field) puts in a hidden `returnTo`.
- **The chapter carries its progress on a ring.** The percentage is counted from the very list the column draws — the lessons whose `statusCode` is `COMPLETED` over the lessons shown — so what the ring says and what the marks say can never differ, and the priority filter takes lessons out of both at once. It is an SVG of two circles: the track, and an arc drawn by holding back part of the circle's own length (`stroke-dasharray`/`stroke-dashoffset`), rotated so it starts at twelve o'clock. At 0% only the track is drawn. The ring is **48px** and its circle as wide as its 36-unit box allows (radius 16, stroke 3), with the figure at `--platform-type-2xs`, weight 600 and a hair of negative tracking: «100%» (31.6px) fits inside its 38.7px with air on both sides, where at 40px it was cut into «00%». The arc is the **accent**, and not the `--platform-progress` blue every other bar of a course is filled with: this ring sits in a column that is already the accent's — the lesson being read, its marks, its thread — and a blue arc in the middle of it read as something else's.
- **It keeps its place between lessons by not being rendered again.** Living in the chapter's layout, the element is never unmounted on a move to another lesson and its scroll simply stays where it was — what used to remember it in `sessionStorage` went away with the move. What is still done is bringing the open lesson into view when that place leaves it cut off ([`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list)).
- **The whole course is there, and the chapter one is in does not leave** (2026-10-06; until then the column held the open chapter alone). The panel keeps its scroll inside: `.lesson-outline__course` stays out of it — where one is should not scroll away — and only `.lesson-outline__body` moves, through every chapter of the course ([`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list)). Each chapter is a `<section>` with its head `position: sticky` at the top, its own background and hairline covering the lessons that pass underneath; a sticky element stays inside its own section, so when a chapter's lessons end the next chapter's head pushes it away and is the one stuck there, as in the design the screen was drawn after. It carries `data-sticky-head`, which is how `OutlineList` knows how much of the top of the list it covers. That scroller carries `overscroll-behavior: none`: at either end of the list the elastic bounce dragged the sticky chapter with it, and there is nothing behind the panel for the scroll to carry on into — the page itself does not move.
- **The list is a body of colour.** Every lesson's row is `--platform-accent-mist`, a pale rose (`#fdf2f2` in the light theme since 2026-10-06; it was a warm grey, `#f8f4f4`), and the one being read is the accent itself with its text and mark in white: the two ends of one colour, so where one is takes no second glance. The head above them stays white, which is what makes the list read as a list.
- **Every lesson carries its stop on a thread.** A dotted line runs down the marks' column behind them — a tract per row (`.lesson-outline__lesson::before`) and not one line behind the whole list, since the rows are filled in and would cover it; the marks are positioned so they are painted over their own row's tract, which is what cuts the thread at each stop. The dots are **drawn** (a `radial-gradient` repeated every 12px) and not left to a `dotted` border, which packs them tight against one another: a thread wants air between its stops, and this leaves four of them between two marks, and each row ends in a filled check (done) or an empty ring (pending), painted with the row's own background so the line stops at it instead of crossing. Where that column falls is one number, `--mark-center` (36px from the right edge, which is where the chapter's 40px ring already sits): the lessons' marks line up under the ring instead of drifting in by the difference between the two, and the thread and the hairlines are measured from it. The hairline between rows stops short of that column (`::after`) — a rule across the thread would cut it into pieces — and the row being read draws none at all, since its background is what tells it apart.
- The whole `<nav>` is labelled "Lecciones del curso", and each chapter's `<section>` by its name.

## Markup & styling

Block `.lesson-outline` (357 lines).

**Pinned, with a shadow.** It carries its hairline and a shadow falling to the right, so the panel sits ON the page rather than beside it — which is what says the board is behind it and not next to it.

**Pinned, which takes a page with no transform.** The column is set at **`--platform-type-lg` (17px)** for the course and the chapters, both bold — **18px from 1536px wide** (2026-10-07): the large-screen step of `--platform-type-lg`, which this page does not take (`platform-page_scale_base`), written by hand and listed as the sheet's one exception in `type-and-space.test.ts` — and the lessons under it, regular — **`--platform-type-md` (16px) from 1536px wide and `--platform-type-sm` (15px) under it** (`@media (width < 1536px)` on the row; set by hand, since this page does not take the scale's step at 1536px) — on rows of `--platform-space-4` above and below. **The chapter and every lesson are one line**, cut with an ellipsis (`white-space: nowrap`, `text-overflow: ellipsis`), and the whole name of the chapter or of a lesson is its `title`: a column of names that reads at a glance, as in the design it was drawn after (2026-10-06; until then it was 20px everywhere and names took two lines). The marks' column, `--mark-center`, is **40px** from the right edge, the centre of the chapter's 48px ring inside its 16px of padding. It is `position: fixed` at `top: 70px` (the bar's height, in the px the bar is drawn with) with `left: 0`, `bottom: 0` and `width: var(--outline-width, 360px)`, set by `.lesson-frame`. A `fixed` element is anchored to the window only while no ancestor carries a transform, which is why `.platform-page_height_screen` turns off the page's entrance animation: `animation: … both` left a transform on the page for good, and with it the panel hung from the page's box instead of the window's.

**Elements:** `.lesson-outline__course` (the head: image, name and filter), `.lesson-outline__cover-link`, `.lesson-outline__cover` (72px), `.lesson-outline__course-text`, `.lesson-outline__course-name`, `.lesson-outline__filter` (the switch's form, drawn by `PriorityLessonsFilter`), `.lesson-outline__body` (what scrolls), `.lesson-outline__chapter` (the sticky row), `.lesson-outline__chapter-name`, `.lesson-outline__gauge` with `__gauge-ring`, `__gauge-track`, `__gauge-fill` and `__gauge-value`, `.lesson-outline__list` (its `::before` is the dotted thread), `.lesson-outline__item`, `.lesson-outline__lesson`, `.lesson-outline__name`, `.lesson-outline__done`, `.lesson-outline__pending`

**Modifiers and states:** `.lesson-outline__lesson_state_current`

**Tokens:** `--outline-width` (given by `.lesson-frame`), `--mark-center` (its own: where every mark's centre falls), `--platform-accent-mist` (the rows), `--platform-accent-faint` (the marks and the thread), `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-title`, `--platform-track`

**Breakpoints:** at `(width < 1280px)` or `(max-height: 620px)` it stops being an edge of the window — `position: static`, its rounding and card shadow back — and goes into the flow under the lesson, at whatever height its lessons need, with nothing inside scrolling on its own.
