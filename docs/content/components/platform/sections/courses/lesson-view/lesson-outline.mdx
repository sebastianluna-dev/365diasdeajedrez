## Purpose

Where the lesson stands: the course at the top, its chapters under it and, in the open one, its lessons, with the one being read lit. It is the course condensed into a column, so moving on to the next lesson does not mean going back twice.

Since 2026-10-22 it is the **side of the workspace** of the new design system (see [Styling](/ui/styling#the-new-design-system-material-3-expressive)), as [`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side) is on the course's and the chapter's pages: on the canvas, at the left of the panel, with the rhythm of [`PlatformSideNav`](/components/platform/shared/platform-side-nav). On a wide window the side scrolls on its own and the page does not ([`LessonFrame`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-frame)).

It belongs to the **chapter's layout** and not to the lesson's page, which is what keeps it from being fetched and drawn again on every move — and is also why it does not know which lesson is open: the list does, in the browser (see [`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list)).

## Props

| Prop      | Type            | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------- | --------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `outline` | `CourseOutline` | yes      | The course (`courseId`, `courseName`, `courseHref`, `courseThumbnail?`, `onlyPriorityLessons`) and its `chapters` (`OutlineChapter[]`: `id`, `name`, `lessons`). One object, read by the chapter's segment: the column takes the model and decides what it draws. |

## Behaviour

- **The course's head**: its picture (40px), its name — the column's way back to the course's page, underlined under the pointer — and how far it is, «X % · N de M lecciones», counted from the very lessons the column lists, so the figure and the marks can never differ and the priority filter takes lessons out of both at once.
- **The priority switch** under it, «Sólo lecciones de prioridad» — the same per-course setting the course's side offers through [`LearnMode`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/learn-mode) — in [`PriorityLessonsFilter`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/priority-lessons-filter), the column's client leaf. It takes effect the moment it is pressed; it is still a `<form action={setOnlyPriorityLessons…}>`, and where it comes back to is the lesson being read, which [`CurrentPathField`](/components/platform/shared/current-path-field) puts in a hidden `returnTo`.
- **The chapters**, in [`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list): each folds open on its lessons, and the lesson's chapter is open on arrival.
- The list is the **same sequence** `prevLessonHref`/`nextLessonHref` walk: the mapper builds both from `flattenLessons`, so a lesson hidden by the priority filter is missing from the column and from the toolbar's arrows alike.
- The whole `<nav>` is labelled "Lecciones del curso", and each chapter's `<section>` by its name.

## Markup & styling

Block `.lesson-outline` (219 lines), drawn with the `--platform-m3-*` roles. The rows are the workspace side's: 36px high, square on the left and round on the right (`0 1000px 1000px 0`), `--platform-m3-container-high` under the pointer, and the lesson being read on `--platform-m3-secondary-container`, bold. A chapter's number sits in a 20px disc, the success green with a check once all its lessons are learned; a learned lesson's mark is the success green's `CircleCheckIcon` (Duotone Solid), the one being read a play, the rest a clock. The chapter's chevron turns when it opens; under `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not animate.

**Elements:** `.lesson-outline__course`, `.lesson-outline__thumb`, `.lesson-outline__thumb-image`, `.lesson-outline__about`, `.lesson-outline__course-name`, `.lesson-outline__figures`, `.lesson-outline__filter` (the switch's form, drawn by `PriorityLessonsFilter`), `.lesson-outline__chapters`, `.lesson-outline__title`, `.lesson-outline__section`, `.lesson-outline__chapter`, `.lesson-outline__badge`, `.lesson-outline__badge-check`, `.lesson-outline__chapter-name`, `.lesson-outline__count`, `.lesson-outline__fold`, `.lesson-outline__list`, `.lesson-outline__lesson`, `.lesson-outline__name`, `.lesson-outline__mark`

**Modifiers and states:** `.lesson-outline__chapter_state_current`, `.lesson-outline__badge_state_done`, `.lesson-outline__lesson_state_current`, `.lesson-outline__mark_state_done`; `aria-expanded` on a chapter turns its chevron.
