## Purpose

Where the lesson stands: the course at the top and, under it, the chapter the lesson is in with its lessons, the one being read lit. It is the chapter's page condensed into a column, so moving on to the next lesson does not mean going back to it.

Since 2026-10-22 it is the **side of the workspace** of the new design system (see [Styling](/ui/styling#the-new-design-system-material-3-expressive)), as [`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side) is on the course's and the chapter's pages: on the canvas, at the left of the panel, with the rhythm of [`PlatformSideNav`](/components/platform/shared/platform-side-nav). On a wide window the side scrolls on its own and the page does not ([`LessonFrame`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-frame)).

It belongs to the **chapter's layout** and not to the lesson's page, which is what keeps it from being fetched and drawn again on every move — and is also why it does not know which lesson is open: the list does, in the browser (see [`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list)).

## Props

| Prop      | Type            | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------- | --------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `outline` | `CourseOutline` | yes      | The course (`courseId`, `courseName`, `courseHref`, `courseThumbnail?`, `progress`) and its `chapter` (`OutlineChapter`: `id`, `order`, `name`, `href`, `lessons`). One object, read by the chapter's segment: the column takes the model and decides what it draws. |

## Behaviour

- **The course's head**: its picture (40px), its name — the column's way back to the course's page, underlined under the pointer — and how far it is, «X % · N de M lecciones», counted over the course's lessons in view (`progress`, from the mapper), so the priority filter takes lessons out of it as it does from the list.
- **The chapter the lesson is in**, alone (2026-10-23; until then every chapter of the course, folding), in [`OutlineList`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/outline-list). There is no switch for the priority lessons any more: that setting is the course's settings panel's ([`CourseSettings`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-settings)).
- The list is the **same sequence** `prevLessonHref`/`nextLessonHref` walk: the mapper builds both from `flattenLessons`, so a lesson hidden by the priority filter is missing from the column and from the toolbar's arrows alike.
- The whole `<nav>` is labelled "Lecciones del curso", and each chapter's `<section>` by its name.

## Markup & styling

Block `.lesson-outline` (171 lines), drawn with the `--platform-m3-*` roles. The rows are the workspace side's: 36px high, square on the left and round on the right (`0 1000px 1000px 0`), `--platform-m3-container-high` under the pointer, and the lesson being read on `--platform-m3-secondary-container`, bold. The chapter's head is a link to its page: «Capítulo 2» small over its name in bold, and «learned/total». A learned lesson's mark is `CircleCheckIcon` (Classic Solid) in the primary's blue, as on the chapter's page (2026-10-23; it was the success green), the one being read a play, the rest a clock.

**Elements:** `.lesson-outline__course`, `.lesson-outline__thumb`, `.lesson-outline__thumb-image`, `.lesson-outline__about`, `.lesson-outline__course-name`, `.lesson-outline__figures`, `.lesson-outline__chapters`, `.lesson-outline__chapter`, `.lesson-outline__chapter-text`, `.lesson-outline__title`, `.lesson-outline__chapter-name`, `.lesson-outline__count`, `.lesson-outline__list`, `.lesson-outline__lesson`, `.lesson-outline__name`, `.lesson-outline__mark`

**Modifiers and states:** `.lesson-outline__lesson_state_current`, `.lesson-outline__mark_state_done`
