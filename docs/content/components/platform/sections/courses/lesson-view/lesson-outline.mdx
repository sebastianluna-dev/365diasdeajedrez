## Purpose

Where the lesson stands: the course at the top, the chapter under it and the chapter's lessons below, with the one open marked. It is the chapter's page condensed into a column, so moving on to the next lesson does not mean going back twice — which is what the screen used to ask for, since the only way on was the «Siguiente lección» button at the foot.

It is **pinned to the window**, not laid out in the page: it begins right under the bar, touches the left edge and runs to the bottom one, so the lessons read as a panel of the screen rather than a card floating on it.

## Behaviour

- The lesson being read **is not a link**: it would lead where one already is. It is drawn as a `<span>` with `aria-current="page"` and the accent background, which is also what tells it apart in the list.
- A completed lesson carries `CircleCheckSolidIcon`, the same mark the chapter's page uses. The current one paints it in its own colour so the row reads as one piece.
- The list is the **same sequence** `prevLessonHref`/`nextLessonHref` walk: the mapper builds both from `flattenLessons`, so a lesson hidden by the priority filter is missing from the column and from the buttons alike, and the two never disagree.
- **The course's head carries its filter.** Under the name, «Sólo lecciones de prioridad» — the same per-course setting the chapter's page offers through [`LearnMode`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/learn-mode), drawn here as the checkbox it always was. It is set smaller than the list it sits over (14px, with an 18px box), being a setting rather than one more thing to read. It is a `<form action={setOnlyPriorityLessons…}>` and not a checkbox with an `onChange`: the preference is saved and the page is drawn again, so there is no state to keep nor JavaScript to send. The box is a `<button aria-pressed>`, which is what a toggle is to a screen reader.
- **The chapter carries its progress on a ring.** The percentage is counted from the very list the column draws — the lessons whose `statusCode` is `COMPLETED` over the lessons shown — so what the ring says and what the marks say can never differ, and the priority filter takes lessons out of both at once. It is an SVG of two circles: the track, and an arc drawn by holding back part of the circle's own length (`stroke-dasharray`/`stroke-dashoffset`), rotated so it starts at twelve o'clock. At 0% only the track is drawn.
- **The course's head does not scroll and the chapter does not leave.** The panel keeps its scroll inside: `.lesson-outline__course` stays out of it — where one is should not scroll away — and only `.lesson-outline__body` moves, with the chapter's name `position: sticky` at its top, its own background and hairline covering the lessons that pass underneath. That scroller carries `overscroll-behavior: none`: at either end of the list the elastic bounce dragged the sticky chapter with it, and there is nothing behind the panel for the scroll to carry on into — the page itself does not move.
- **The list is a body of colour.** Every lesson's row is `--platform-accent-mist`, the accent barely tinted, and the one being read is the accent itself with its text and mark in white: the two ends of one colour, so where one is takes no second glance. The head above them stays white, which is what makes the list read as a list.
- **Every lesson carries its stop on a thread.** A dotted line runs down the marks' column behind them — a tract per row (`.lesson-outline__lesson::before`) and not one line behind the whole list, since the rows are filled in and would cover it; the marks are positioned so they are painted over their own row's tract, which is what cuts the thread at each stop. The dots are **drawn** (a `radial-gradient` repeated every 12px) and not left to a `dotted` border, which packs them tight against one another: a thread wants air between its stops, and this leaves four of them between two marks, and each row ends in a filled check (done) or an empty ring (pending), painted with the row's own background so the line stops at it instead of crossing. Where that column falls is one number, `--mark-center` (36px from the right edge, which is where the chapter's 40px ring already sits): the lessons' marks line up under the ring instead of drifting in by the difference between the two, and the thread and the hairlines are measured from it. The hairline between rows stops short of that column (`::after`) — a rule across the thread would cut it into pieces — and the row being read draws none at all, since its background is what tells it apart.
- The whole `<nav>` is labelled "Lecciones del capítulo".

## Markup & styling

Block `.lesson-outline` (179 lines).

**Pinned, with a shadow.** It carries its hairline and a shadow falling to the right, so the panel sits ON the page rather than beside it — which is what says the board is behind it and not next to it.

**Pinned, which takes a page with no transform.** The whole column is set at **17px**, with the weight and not the size telling the course, the chapter and the lessons apart. It is `position: fixed` at `top: 70px` (the bar's height, in the page's own px, which under the zoom are the ones the bar is drawn with) with `left: 0`, `bottom: 0` and `width: var(--outline-width, 360px)`, set by `.lesson-view`. A `fixed` element is anchored to the window only while no ancestor carries a transform, which is why `.platform-page_height_screen` turns off the page's entrance animation: `animation: … both` left a transform on the page for good, and with it the panel hung from the page's box instead of the window's.

**Elements:** `.lesson-outline__course` (the head: image, name and filter), `.lesson-outline__cover-link`, `.lesson-outline__cover` (72px), `.lesson-outline__course-text`, `.lesson-outline__course-name`, `.lesson-outline__filter` with `__filter-button`, `__filter-box` and `__filter-check`, `.lesson-outline__body` (what scrolls) `.lesson-outline__body` (what scrolls), `.lesson-outline__chapter` (the sticky row), `.lesson-outline__chapter-name`, `.lesson-outline__gauge` with `__gauge-ring`, `__gauge-track`, `__gauge-fill` and `__gauge-value`, `.lesson-outline__list` (its `::before` is the dotted thread), `.lesson-outline__item`, `.lesson-outline__lesson`, `.lesson-outline__name`, `.lesson-outline__done`, `.lesson-outline__pending`

**Modifiers and states:** `.lesson-outline__lesson_state_current`

**Tokens:** `--outline-width` (given by `.lesson-view`), `--mark-center` (its own: where every mark's centre falls), `--platform-accent-mist` (the rows), `--platform-accent-faint` (the marks and the thread), `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-progress` (the arc, the same blue a course's bar fills with), `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-title`, `--platform-track`

**Breakpoints:** at `(max-width: 1080px)` or `(max-height: 620px)` it stops being an edge of the window — `position: static`, its rounding and card shadow back — and goes into the flow under the lesson, at whatever height its lessons need, with nothing inside scrolling on its own.

## Usage

From `lesson-view.section.tsx`:

```tsx
<LessonOutline
  courseName={lesson.courseName}
  courseHref={lesson.courseHref}
  courseThumbnail={lesson.courseThumbnail}
  chapterName={lesson.chapterName}
  chapterHref={lesson.chapterHref}
  lessons={lesson.chapterLessons}
  currentLessonId={lesson.id}
/>
```
