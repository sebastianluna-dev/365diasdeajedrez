## Purpose

The lessons of one chapter. Each card shows **how the lesson opens** — «1.e4 c5 2.Cf3 d6 3.d4 cxd4 …», its first six moves in Spanish notation — so a game is recognised before opening it. A lesson without moves is a diagram and shows its description instead.

## Behaviour

- **State of a lesson**, in one word: «Aprendida», «Siguiente», «Empezada» or «Pendiente». «Siguiente» is one alone in the whole course — the one the header's «Aprender» opens (`lesson.isNext`) — and wins over «Empezada». Its card is tinted and its button is the filled «Aprender»; the rest read «Ver» (or «Ver ✓» once learned).
- The number of a lesson is its **real** one, with the filter on too: 01, 04, 07. Renumbering would hide that lessons are missing.
- With only the essential lessons asked for and none in the chapter, the list says so and how many «Todo» would show.
- At the foot, the neighbour chapters: «← Capítulo 1» and «Capítulo 3 →».
- The previews are read by the service for this chapter alone (`movesPreview` over `lessonPgnOf`): the course is loaded without the PGN of its lessons, which is most of its weight.

The previous chapter page had an «Agregar al Move Trainer» button. It is no longer here; the chapter is still added from the trainer's home.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/[courseRef]/[chapterOrder]/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const chapter = await getChapterView(courseRef, Number(chapterOrder));
if (!chapter) notFound();
if (!chapter.course.isAdded) redirect(chapter.course.infoHref);

<ChapterStudySection chapter={chapter} errorCode={error} />;
```
