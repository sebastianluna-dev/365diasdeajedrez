## Purpose

`/cursos/10000004/2`: a chapter of a course the student has; whoever has not added it is redirected to its information page. Since 2026-10-22 it is drawn in the new design system («M4» of the owner's canvas): the course's side column ([`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side)) with this chapter lit and its own «Eliminar mi progreso del capítulo», and the panel. Before, it was a list of lesson cards inside `CourseStudyFrame`, removed with this change.

## Props

| Prop        | Type          | Required | Meaning                                                            |

| `chapter`   | `ChapterView` | yes      | The chapter, its lessons, its neighbours and the course around it. |
| `errorCode` | `string`      | no       | What a setting bounced back with: a `PlatformNotice` on the panel. |

## Behaviour

The panel, from top to bottom:

1. **A toolbar**: Material's icon button and the course's name back to the course, «Capítulo N · name» as the trail's current step. «Capítulo N de M» and the arrows to the chapters before and after, at its right, went on 2026-10-23: the pager under the lessons and the side column lead there. It **stays under the bar** while the page scrolls, as Gmail's (`position: sticky; top: 70px`), spanning the panel on its white with the panel's top corners, drawn over a strip of the canvas so nothing scrolling under it shows through the corners; under 1024px, where the bar is no longer one row of 70px, it scrolls away.
2. **No head of its own** (2026-10-23): the chapter's big name and its description went, since the trail already says it; the end of the trail, «Capítulo 2 · La Najdorf», is the page's `<h1>` (`__crumb-here`, at the trail's size) and what labels the panel.
3. **Its lessons, as rows** (as Gmail lists mail), the whole row the link: an icon that says whether the lesson is learned — a filled check (Classic Solid) in the primary's blue, or an empty ring (Classic Light) while it is not (2026-10-23; until then a green check, a play on the next one and a clock) —, its name, in bold only on the next one «Aprender» opens (the row on the container, with a «Siguiente» label), the moves of its game with how it ended (one line, cut where the row ends; a diagram's description instead) — pointing at a move shows its position on a small board ([`MoveLinePreview`](/components/chess/move-line-preview), 2026-10-23), which hangs over the rows below, and its labels: «Prioritaria» and its length. The columns are the same on every row (the labels' one fixed at 180px), so the moves start at the same place down the list. Between the rows a light hairline (`--platform-m3-divider`), and under the pointer a row **lifts**, as Gmail's (a hairline at its sides, `--platform-m3-row-edge`, and a soft shadow under it of `--platform-m3-row-shadow`, over its neighbours), and keeps its colour (2026-10-23; it used to take a grey tint).
4. **At the foot**, the chapters before and after by name.

With no lessons it says why: none marked as priority with the filter on, or none yet. The tabs that filtered the lessons («Todas», «Por aprender», «Aprendidas») went on 2026-10-22, the day they came: a chapter has few lessons.

**The course's settings** (2026-10-23): the page puts the gear in the bar ([`PlatformSettingsToggle`](/components/platform/shared/platform-settings-toggle), through `PlatformHeaderContext`) and [`CourseSettings`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-settings) as the workspace's last column, at the right of the panel, as Gmail's quick settings: what to learn, the course's information, erasing the progress and leaving the course, which until then were the bottom of the side column.
