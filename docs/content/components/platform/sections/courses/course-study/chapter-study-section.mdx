## Purpose

`/cursos/10000004/2`: a chapter of a course the student has; whoever has not added it is redirected to its information page. Since 2026-10-22 it is drawn in the new design system («M4» of the owner's canvas): the course's side column ([`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side)) with this chapter lit and its own «Eliminar mi progreso del capítulo», and the panel. Before, it was a list of lesson cards inside `CourseStudyFrame`, removed with this change.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapter`   | `ChapterView`        | yes      | The chapter, its lessons, its neighbours and the course around it.   |
| `view`      | `ChapterLessonsView` | yes      | The tab (`chapterLessonsViewOf(lecciones)`); `undefined` is «Todas». |
| `errorCode` | `string`             | no       | What a setting bounced back with: a `PlatformNotice` on the panel.   |

## Behaviour

The panel, from top to bottom:

1. **A toolbar**: Material's icon button and the course's name back to the course, «Capítulo N · name» as the trail's current step; at the right «Capítulo N de M» and the arrows to the chapters before and after (named after them; switched off at either end).
2. **Material's tabs** (`tabs`, `tab`, `selected`, links with `aria-current`): «Todas», «Por aprender» and «Aprendidas», each with its count, as `?lecciones=`.
3. **The chapter**: its name, and its description when the staff wrote one.
4. **Its lessons, as rows** (as Gmail lists mail), the whole row the link: an icon that says where the lesson is — learned (the success green's check), the next one «Aprender» opens (the primary's play, the row on the container, and a «Siguiente» label), or still to come (a clock) —, its name, in bold while it is still to learn, the moves of its game with how it ended (one line, cut where the row ends; a diagram's description instead), and its labels: «Prioritaria» and its length. The columns are the same on every row (the labels' one fixed at 180px), so the moves start at the same place down the list.
5. **At the foot**, the chapters before and after by name.

With no lessons it says why — none marked as priority with the filter on, or none yet —, and a tab with nothing in it says so.
