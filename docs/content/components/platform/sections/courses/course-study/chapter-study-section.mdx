## Purpose

The lessons of one chapter. Each card shows **the game whole** — «1.e4 c5 2.Cf3 d6 3.d4 cxd4 …» to the last move, in Spanish notation, and after it how the game ended («1-0») —, so a game is recognised and can be read through without opening it. Only the main line: the variations and the commentary are what the lesson is opened for. A lesson without moves — a diagram — shows its description instead.

| `errorCode` | `string`      | no       | Passed on to the frame.                                                                                                                        |

## Behaviour

- **The chapter is not repeated here**: its name is at the head of the page, in the frame, and the page is the list of its lessons. It carried a summary with the name, the «Prioridad» tag and the description until 2026-09-28.
- **No figure is drawn here**: neither the chapter nor a lesson has a percentage or a bar, and the frame draws none of the course either. A learned lesson has its check; how far the chapter is, is the count on its card in [`CourseStudySection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-study-section).
- **A lesson carries one tag at most**, «Prioridad», the staff's mark: right under its moves, 20px high with 4px corners, as Chessable draws its own (2026-10-07; it sat in the foot beside the button, 24px high with 8px corners, and read as a loose piece of the band's rose). Where the lesson is for the student is not written in words: the one it left off at — the one the header's «Aprender» opens (`lesson.isNext`) — is told by its tinted band, and a learned one by the check beside its name. The four words it used to carry («Aprendida», «Siguiente», «Empezada», «Pendiente») are gone.
- **The button is «Ver» on every card, and quiet**: no fill, outlined in grey (`--platform-border-strong`). The next lesson's is no different: the filled «Aprender» is the course's, at the head of the page.
- **The band is set into the card**: the card has `--platform-space-2` (8px) of white padding and the band, rounded on its four corners, does not touch the card's edges.
- **The card**, top to bottom: a band with the name and the check of a learned lesson; the moves; the tag, right under them (`margin-top` of minus `--platform-space-2`, so it sits closer to the moves than the body's gap); and a foot with the button alone, at the right. **A lesson says no minutes**: what it is worth is read by opening it.
- **A lesson is not numbered**: its card is headed by its name. `lesson.order` is still in the model, the real one with the filter on too, but no page draws it.
- A learned lesson carries that same check in its header band, in the same accent.
- With only the priority ones asked for and none in the chapter, the list says so and how many «Todo» would show.
- **Before the list and at its foot**, the neighbour chapters, each by its name and cut short when it does not fit: «← Los años de aprendizaje (1957–1959)». The ones before the list came on 2026-10-07, so a long chapter can be left without going down to its last lesson; they are not drawn over the empty notice, where the two would sit one right above the other. Both are drawn by one function (`Neighbours`), and each `<nav>` has a name of its own — «Capítulos vecinos, antes de la lista» and «Capítulos vecinos, al final de la lista» — since two landmarks of one name cannot be told apart.
- The moves are read by the service for this chapter alone (`gameMoves` over `lessonPgnOf`): the course is loaded without the PGN of its lessons, which is most of its weight.

The previous chapter page had an «Agregar al Move Trainer» button. It is no longer here, and on 2026-10-03 Sebastian decided it does not come back for now: a course's «Repasar» is the way back to what was learned, and the chapter is still added from the trainer's home.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/[courseRef]/[chapterOrder]/(chapter)/page.tsx` — the route group is what keeps the chapter's own screen out of the lessons' layout:

```tsx
const chapter = await getChapterView(courseRef, Number(chapterOrder));
if (!chapter) notFound();
if (!chapter.course.isAdded) redirect(chapter.course.infoHref);

<ChapterStudySection chapter={chapter} errorCode={error} />;
```
