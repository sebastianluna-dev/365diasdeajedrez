## Purpose

The course's layout: both pages of a course being studied are the same frame with a different middle column — the chapters, or the lessons of one. Everything it draws comes from the model (`course`); `children` is the page's column and nothing else, which is the one case the project's "data, not components" rule allows.

## Props

| Prop        | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course`    | `CourseDetail`     | yes      | Name, `thumbnail` (the square image), progress, `learnHref?`, `reviewCount`, `reviewHref`, `infoHref`, `studyHref`… |
| `chapter`   | `{ name: string }` | no       | The chapter the page is of, for the line under the name. Absent on the course's own page.                           |
| `returnTo`  | `string`           | yes      | The page being drawn, where a failed setting is reported.                                                           |
| `errorCode` | `string`           | no       | What a setting or the reset bounced back with in `?error=` (`throttled`, `confirm`).                                |
| `children`  | `ReactNode`        | yes      | The page's own column.                                                                                              |

## Behaviour

- **The head** carries the course's square image (96px, `course.thumbnail`, the same one its row shows in «Mis cursos»), its name and, under it, where one is: «Capítulos», or the chapter's name.
- **«Aprender»** is the one filled button of the course's pages — the lessons' cards open with a quiet «Ver» —; it is raised on a hard edge (`0 4px 0 var(--platform-accent-deep)`), as the button of the blog's header, sinks onto it while it is held down (`:active`), and opens `course.learnHref`, where there is something to learn: the lesson the student left off at if it is unfinished, the first unfinished one otherwise and, in a finished course, its first lesson. **«Repasar n»** opens the Move Trainer with the exercises of the lessons already learned in this course (`/entrenador?course=<id>`); `n` is how many there are.
- **«Repasar» is not drawn today**: the Move Trainer is switched off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) and the service counts nothing to review, so `course.reviewCount` is always 0.
- A button is only drawn when it leads somewhere: a course without lessons in view has no «Aprender», and one without learned exercises no «Repasar». With neither, the block is not rendered. **A finished course keeps its «Aprender»**: it opens the first lesson, to start the course over.
- Under the name, **the trail**: «Capítulos» on the course's page and «Capítulos ▸ Los años de aprendizaje (1957–1959)», the step drawn with `CaretRightIcon` on a chapter's. It reads, it does not lead: neither half is a link, because going back is the browser's since the frame lost its button.
- **No figure of the course is drawn here.** «Tu progreso» — its percentage, its `completed/total` and a bar — was in the aside until 2026-09-28: a chapter's card says how far that chapter is, and the course's own progress is read in «Mis cursos», where a course is chosen.
- **«Opciones del curso»**: «Información del curso» (the slug page) and «Borrar mi progreso», the latter only when there is something to erase.
- **«Configuración»**: [`LearnMode`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/learn-mode).

The design this comes from also had «Poner en pausa todos los capítulos»; it was left out on purpose — there is no scheduling a pause could stop.

## Markup & styling

Block `.course-study-frame` (227 lines). A wrapping flex row: the page's column and an aside 240px wide (`flex: 0 0 240px`, a width of its own and not a share of what is left), which drops under it when both do not fit.

**Elements:** `.course-study-frame__head`, `.course-study-frame__cover`, `.course-study-frame__cover-image`, `.course-study-frame__heading`, `.course-study-frame__name`, `.course-study-frame__place`, `.course-study-frame__place-step`, `.course-study-frame__place-here`, `.course-study-frame__actions`, `.course-study-frame__learn`, `.course-study-frame__review`, `.course-study-frame__review-count`, `.course-study-frame__layout`, `.course-study-frame__main`, `.course-study-frame__aside`, `.course-study-frame__card`, `.course-study-frame__card-title`, `.course-study-frame__option`, `.course-study-frame__options`, `.course-study-frame__setting`

**Modifiers and states:** `.course-study-frame__card-title_kind_head`

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-bright`, `--platform-accent-deep`, `--platform-accent-hover`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-ink`, `--platform-on-accent`, `--platform-success-ink`, `--platform-success-soft`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text-faint`, `--platform-text-subtle`, `--platform-text-title`

**Breakpoints:** `(max-width: 900px)` the aside takes the width of the page's column; `(max-width: 620px)` the buttons take a row of their own.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-study.section.tsx`:

```tsx
<CourseStudyFrame course={course} returnTo={course.studyHref} errorCode={errorCode}>
  <ol className="course-study">…</ol>
</CourseStudyFrame>
```
