## Purpose

The course's layout: both pages of a course being studied are the same frame with a different middle column — the chapters, or the lessons of one. Everything it draws comes from the model (`course`); `children` is the page's column and nothing else, which is the one case the project's "data, not components" rule allows.

## Props

| Prop        | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `course`    | `CourseDetail`     | yes      | Name, cover, progress, `learnHref?`, `reviewCount`, `reviewHref`, `infoHref`, `studyHref`… |
| `chapter`   | `{ name: string }` | no       | The chapter the page is of, for the line under the name. Absent on the course's own page.  |
| `returnTo`  | `string`           | yes      | The page being drawn, where a failed setting is reported.                                  |
| `errorCode` | `string`           | no       | What a setting or the reset bounced back with in `?error=` (`throttled`, `confirm`).       |
| `children`  | `ReactNode`        | yes      | The page's own column.                                                                     |

## Behaviour

- **The way back**, on top: a button that goes up one step — «Mis cursos» (`/cursos`) from the course's page, «Capítulos» (the course's page) from a chapter's.
- **«Aprender»** opens `course.learnHref`, the next lesson to learn: the one the student left off at if it is unfinished, the first unfinished one otherwise. **«Repasar n»** opens the Move Trainer with the exercises of the lessons already learned in this course (`/entrenador?course=<id>`); `n` is how many there are.
- **«Repasar» is not drawn today**: the Move Trainer is switched off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) and the service counts nothing to review, so `course.reviewCount` is always 0.
- A button is only drawn when it leads somewhere: a course with every lesson learned has no «Aprender», and one without learned exercises no «Repasar». With neither, the block is not rendered.
- Under the name, the place: «Capítulos» on the course's page; the chapter's name, not numbered, on a chapter's.
- **«Tu progreso»** is drawn on a chapter's page only (`chapter` present): the page that lists the chapters does not show the course's progress, each card there says how far its chapter is. It is the percentage, `completed/total` and a bar, over the lessons the student sees, and the one bar of the course's pages: a chapter says its progress as a count. A finished course (`course.isCompleted`) gets an orange check next to its 100 %, the same filled one a finished chapter has. The course's cards do not carry it.
- **«Opciones del curso»**: «Información del curso» (the slug page) and «Borrar mi progreso», the latter only when there is something to erase.
- **«Configuración»**: [`LearnMode`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/learn-mode).

The design this comes from also had «Poner en pausa todos los capítulos»; it was left out on purpose — there is no scheduling a pause could stop.
