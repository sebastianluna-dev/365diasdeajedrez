## Purpose

The course's own column in the new design system (2026-10-22), from top to bottom:

1. **The course**: its square picture (40px), its name and «40 % · 2 de 5 lecciones».
2. **«Aprender»**, the extended FAB of `PlatformSideNav`, to `learnHref`; none without lessons in view.
3. **«Capítulos»**, the column's links: each chapter's name, a badge with its number (the success green with a check once it is learned) and its count, «1/3».
   What to learn and the course's options («Qué aprender», «Información del curso», «Eliminar mi progreso», «Quitar de mis cursos») moved on 2026-10-23 to the course's settings panel, [`CourseSettings`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-settings), opened by the gear in the bar.

On a chapter's page (`chapter`) that chapter's link is lit. Under 1024px the parts sit side by side above the panel.
