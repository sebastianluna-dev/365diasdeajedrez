## Purpose

The course's own column in the new design system (2026-10-22): «Aprender», the extended FAB of `PlatformSideNav`, to `learnHref` (none without lessons in view), and «Capítulos», the column's links: each chapter's name, a badge with its number (the success green with a check once it is learned) and its count, «1/3». On a chapter's page (`chapterId`) that chapter's link is lit.

**The course itself is no longer at the top** (2026-10-23, the owner's call): its picture, name and «40 % · 2 de 5 lecciones» repeated what the page already says — the course's page in its trail, title and hero; the chapter's page in its trail. What to learn and the course's options («Qué aprender», «Información del curso», «Eliminar mi progreso», «Quitar de mis cursos») moved the same day to the course's settings panel, [`CourseSettings`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-settings), opened by the gear in the bar.

## Props

| Prop        | Type           | Required | Meaning                                                      |
| ----------- | -------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `course`    | `CourseDetail` | yes      | The course, as the page reads it.                            |
| `chapterId` | `string`       | no       | The chapter on screen, on a chapter's page: its link is lit. |

## Markup & styling

No block of its own: it renders a [`PlatformSideNav`](/components/platform/shared/platform-side-nav), which draws itself.
