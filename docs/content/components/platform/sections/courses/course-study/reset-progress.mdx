## Purpose

Starting a course over — or just one of its chapters. It cannot be undone, so it takes two things to go through — opening the confirmation and ticking its box — and the button that does it is the red one.

**One component for the two scopes and not two**: it is the same confirmation, the same box and the same red button; what changes is which action it posts to and three lines of copy. `chapterId` is what says which one (2026-09-30). Since 2026-10-06 the confirmation is [`PlatformConfirmPopup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-confirm-popup), the one every destruction of the platform asks with; what this component owns is the trigger and the words.

## Props

| Prop               | Type     | Required | Meaning                                                                      |
| ------------------ | -------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courseId`         | `string` | yes      | The course the erasing happens in.                                           |
| `name`             | `string` | yes      | The course's name, or the chapter's: whichever is named in the confirmation. |
| `completedLessons` | `number` | yes      | What is about to be lost, by its figure; not mentioned when it is zero.      |
| `chapterId`        | `string` | no       | The chapter whose progress is erased. Absent = the whole course.             |

## Behaviour

- The link-styled trigger — «Eliminar mi progreso», or «Eliminar mi progreso del capítulo» with a `chapterId` — opens the confirmation through its handle (`confirmRef.current?.open()`); the popup is mounted next to it, alone, with no popup beneath, so `onDone` is omitted and the action's redirect does the rest. Focus trapping, Escape, the backdrop and the motion are the popup's.
- **The words**: `verb="Eliminar"` (the bin, and the warning «Esta acción no se puede deshacer.» that the verb gives by itself), the question `¿{label}?` from the trigger's label, one sentence of what goes — `Vas a eliminar tu progreso en «{name}»`, with «: N lecciones aprendidas» only when the figure is above zero —, two consequences (for a course, it starts again from the first lesson and stays among the student's; for a chapter, its lessons are back to not started and the rest of the course stays as it is; then the streak and the activity are not touched, naming the trainer's attempts only while `MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) and the confirming button with the trigger's label, «Eliminando…» while it works.
- **The box** is the popup's acknowledgement («Sí, quiero eliminar mi progreso en este curso» or «… en este capítulo»), with `name="confirm"` and `value="on"`: exactly what `RESET_SCHEMA` reads. The confirming button waits for the tick, and the action asks for the field again because it is reachable by direct POST; without it the answer is `?error=confirm` — «Marca la casilla para confirmar.»
- **What is erased, for a course**: the student's `LessonProgress` and `ChapterProgress` rows of it, and the course's own progress goes back to `NOT_STARTED` with no dates and no last lesson. The course **stays** among the student's.
- **For a chapter**: only its lessons' rows and its own, and then the course's row is resealed around what is left — `completeLesson` run backwards. It stays `IN_PROGRESS` while the student has touched any lesson of the course, and goes back to `NOT_STARTED` only when none is left; `completedAt` is cleared either way (a finished course is no longer finished), and the return point of «continuar estudiando» only moves when it pointed at a lesson of that chapter.
- **What is not**: the activity log, the streak and the trainer's attempts. They are the record of days that happened. (The course's settings survive a reset; they only go when the course is taken out of «Mis cursos» — see [`RemoveCourse`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/remove-course).)

Block `.reset-progress` (53 lines): the trigger alone.

**Elements:** `.reset-progress__open`

**Other classes it renders:** the popup's, `.platform-confirm-popup` on `.platform-dialog`, with its `platform-button` and `platform-button_variant_danger`.

## Usage

```tsx
{
  /* The whole course, on its own page and on a chapter's. */
}
<ResetProgress courseId={course.id} name={course.name} completedLessons={progress.completedLessons} />;

{
  /* Only on a chapter's page, above it. */
}
<ResetProgress
  courseId={course.id}
  chapterId={chapter.id}
  name={chapter.name}
  completedLessons={chapter.completedLessons}
/>;
```

## Looks

`look` (default `"link"`): one more link of the options card, on the chapter's page; `"side"` (2026-10-22), a row of [`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side) in the new design system, with `ArrowRotateLeftIcon` (`.reset-progress__open_look_side`, `.reset-progress__icon`).
