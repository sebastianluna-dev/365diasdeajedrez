## Purpose

Starting a course over. It cannot be undone, so it takes two things to go through — opening the dialog and ticking its box — and the button that does it is the red one.

## Props

| Prop               | Type     | Required | Meaning                                                                 |
| ------------------ | -------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courseId`         | `string` | yes      | The course whose progress is erased.                                    |
| `courseName`       | `string` | yes      | Named in the dialog's text.                                             |
| `completedLessons` | `number` | yes      | What is about to be lost, by its figure; not mentioned when it is zero. |

## Behaviour

- A native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`: focus trapping, Escape and the backdrop come with it, and the entry and exit motion from `.platform-dialog`.
- The box is `required`: the browser stops the form without it. The action asks for it again (`confirm=on`), because it is reachable by direct POST, and answers `?error=confirm` — «Marca la casilla para confirmar que quieres borrar tu progreso.» — when it is missing.
- **What is erased**: the student's `LessonProgress` and `ChapterProgress` rows of the course, and the course's own progress goes back to `NOT_STARTED` with no dates and no last lesson. The course **stays** among the student's.
- The dialog's sentence names the trainer's attempts only while the Move Trainer is on (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`); today it reads «Tu racha y tu actividad no se tocan.»
- **What is not**: the activity log, the streak, the trainer's attempts and the course's settings. They are the record of days that happened, and preferences.
