## Purpose

`/cursos/10000004`: the course once it is the student's. Whoever opens it without having added the course is redirected by the page to its information page, which is where it is added.

Since 2026-10-22 it is drawn in the new design system («M4» of the owner's canvas, Material 3 Expressive): the `.platform-workspace`, with the course's own side column ([`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side)) and the panel. Before, it was the chapters as cards inside `CourseStudyFrame`, removed when the chapter's page moved too.

## Props

| Prop        | Type           | Required | Meaning                                                            |
| ----------- | -------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `course`    | `CourseDetail` | yes      | The course with its chapters and the student's progress in each.   |
| `errorCode` | `string`       | no       | What a setting bounced back with: a `PlatformNotice` on the panel. |

## Behaviour

The panel, from top to bottom:

1. **A toolbar**: Material's icon button and «Tus cursos» back to `/cursos`, and the course's name as the trail's current step.
2. **The course**: its picture (the cover, or the square picture, at 16:10 with 16 corners), its name in the display font, a line with its authors, its levels (`levelSpan`), its topics and its chapters, its progress (`WavyProgress`, large, with the percentage), and its buttons — «Continuar», «Empezar» or «Repasar» (`ctaLabel`, Material's filled button, 56px) to `learnHref`; «Repasar N» (outlined) when the Move Trainer has something due, or switched off as «Repasar · Al día» when it is up to date. A course without lessons in view draws no progress and no «Aprender».
3. **Its chapters**: a heading with «N de M lecciones aprendidas», and one card per chapter (Material's outlined card, the whole card the link): «Capítulo N», «Prioridad» when the staff marked it, its state — «Aprendido», «Siguiente» (the first not learned, where «Aprender» leads; it stands on the container, without outline), «En curso» or «Por empezar» —, its name, its description (two lines), its progress, and at the foot its count and what pressing it does («Repasar», «Seguir», «Empezar»).

A chapter's count is of the lessons the student **sees**: with only the priority ones asked for, it is over those. A course without chapters shows an `EmptyState`.

**Tokens:** the `--platform-m3-*` roles, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 768px)` puts the picture above the text. The chapters are as many to a row as fit of at least 260px.

```tsx
    </div>

}
```
