## Purpose

The course's settings (2026-10-23, after the owner's design, «M4 · /cursos/[curso] con «Ajustes del curso»» on the canvas): a panel at the right of the workspace, as Gmail's «Configuración rápida», opened by the gear the course's and the chapter's pages put in the bar ([`PlatformSettingsToggle`](/components/platform/shared/platform-settings-toggle)). Until then these options were the bottom of the side column ([`CourseSide`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-side)).

## Props

| Prop      | Type                             | Required | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course`  | `CourseDetail`                   | yes      | The course.                                                                                               |
| `chapter` | `{ id, name, completedLessons }` | no       | The chapter on screen, on a chapter's page: the settings come back to it, and its progress can be erased. |

## Behaviour

Three groups, each option a row with its words and the drawing of what it does:

1. **Qué aprender**: [`LearnMode`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/learn-mode) — «Todas las lecciones» and «Sólo las prioritarias», with a radio's ring —, and what the filter hides. It comes back to the chapter on a chapter's page, else to the course.
2. **El curso**: «Información del curso», a link to `infoHref`.
3. **Tu progreso**: on a chapter's page with something to erase, «Eliminar mi progreso del capítulo» first — it is the page's own, and the course's is the bigger step —; «Eliminar mi progreso» when the course has been started ([`ResetProgress`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/reset-progress)); and «Quitar de mis cursos», in the error's red ([`RemoveCourse`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/remove-course)). Each opens its confirmation, as before.

## Markup & styling

No block of its own: the panel and its rows are [`PlatformSettingsPanel`](/components/platform/shared/platform-settings-panel)'s.

## Usage

From `course-study.section.tsx`, as the last column of the workspace, with the gear in the bar:

```tsx
<PlatformHeaderContext>
  <PlatformSettingsToggle />
</PlatformHeaderContext>
…
<CourseSettings course={course} />
```
