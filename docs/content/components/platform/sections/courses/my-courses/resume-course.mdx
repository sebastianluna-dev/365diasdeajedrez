## Purpose

The one course to go on with, as the design canvas draws it (2026-10-22): its cover at the left (16:10, 280px), «Sigue donde ibas» in the primary's blue, the name linking to the course, «2 de 5 lecciones», the wave with its percentage, and «Continuar» (Material's filled button, `mdButton("filled", "md")`, 56px) to the next lesson. «Repasar N» (`mdButton("outlined", "md")`) goes beside it only while the Move Trainer is on and something is due, and the line says how many exercises.

## Props

| Prop     | Type                                   | Required | Meaning                                           |
| -------- | -------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `course` | `MyCourseItem & { learnHref: string }` | yes      | What `resumeCourseOf` found: begun, not finished. |

## Markup & styling

Block `.resume-course`: a block on `--platform-m3-container`, corners `--md-sys-shape-corner-xl` (28), the cover's `-lg` (16). **Elements:** `__cover`, `__image`, `__body`, `__eyebrow`, `__name`, `__link`, `__figures`, `__meter`, `__percent`, `__actions`. Under 768px the cover goes above the text.
