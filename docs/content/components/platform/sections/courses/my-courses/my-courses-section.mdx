## Purpose

The student's courses, and how constant their study is. Since 2026-10-22 it is the first page of the new design system ([Material 3](/ui/styling#the-new-design-system-material-3), «M4» of the owner's design canvas); before it, «P1» of 2026-10-07: a head with «Explorar cursos», the courses as covers in two columns and the streak as a column at the right.

**The side column** ([`PlatformSideNav`](/components/platform/shared/platform-side-nav)):

1. «Seguir donde iba», the extended button, to the next lesson of the course «Sigue donde ibas» shows; absent when there is none.
2. «Tus cursos»: Todos, En curso, Completados and Sin empezar, each with its count, as links to `?estado=` (`en-curso`, `completados`, `sin-empezar`; anything else is «Todos»). A course is «completado» when every lesson is learned, «en curso» with at least one, «sin empezar» with none (`courseStateOf`).
3. «Catálogo»: «Todo el catálogo» and each level in use with the published courses aimed at it, as links to `/cursos/explorar?nivel=`. A level's dot is the level's colour, in order (`--platform-level-1` … `-6`).

**The panel**, from top to bottom:

1. [`ResumeCourse`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/resume-course): the first course begun and not finished with a lesson to open (`resumeCourseOf`, in the list's order). It stays in every view.
2. [`StreakBand`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/streak-band), the streak across.
3. The courses of the view: a heading («Tus cursos», or the view's name) with the count, and the cards ([`MyCourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-course-card)), as many to a row as fit of at least 200px — four on a laptop. A view without courses says so in a line; with no course added at all, an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state) leads to the catalog.

The page's `<h1>` («Mis cursos») is for screen readers: on screen the page is named by the bar and by the lit view.

## Props

| Prop      | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                            |
| --------- | ------------------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courses` | `MyCourseItem[]`   | yes      | `getUserCourses()`: the courses the student added.                                 |
| `filter`  | `MyCoursesFilter`  | yes      | The view (`myCoursesFilterOf(estado)`); `undefined` is «Todos».                    |
| `streak`  | `StreakOverview`   | yes      | `getStreakOverview()`: the streak, its sixteen weeks and its figures.              |
| `catalog` | `MyCoursesCatalog` | yes      | `{ total, levels: { slug, name, count }[] }`: the catalog as the column counts it. |

## Markup & styling

Block `.my-courses`, set on the `.platform-workspace`.

**Elements:** `.my-courses__sr-title`, `.my-courses__list`, `.my-courses__head`, `.my-courses__title`, `.my-courses__count`, `.my-courses__empty`, `.my-courses__grid`

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`; the workspace's in `platform.css`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const [courses, streak, catalog, levels, { estado }] = await Promise.all([
  getUserCourses(),
  getStreakOverview(),
  getCourseCatalog(),
  listCourseLevels(),
  searchParams,
]);

<div className="platform-page platform-page_width_full platform-page_frame_bleed courses-page">
  <MyCoursesSection courses={courses} filter={myCoursesFilterOf(estado)} streak={streak} catalog={counts} />
</div>;
```
