## Purpose

The list of the student's courses and, beside it, their daily streak. Until this page was redesigned it listed every published course with a level filter and the daily goal beside it; now a course is **added** before it is studied, the catalog is a page of its own ([`CourseCatalogSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-catalog/course-catalog-section)) and the daily goal is looked at on the dashboard ([`StudyGoalSection`](/components/platform/sections/dashboard/study-goal/study-goal-section)).

## Props

| Prop      | Type             | Required | Meaning                                                                                        |
| --------- | ---------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courses` | `MyCourseItem[]` | yes      | The courses the student added, by name, each with its next lesson and what there is to review. |
| `streak`  | `StudyStreak`    | yes      | Days in a row studying and whether today is one of them: the card beside the list.             |

## Behaviour

- **Two columns**: the list and, at its right, «Racha diaria» ([`StudyStreak`](/components/platform/shared/study-streak)) in a column 240px wide, `--platform-space-12` (48px) from the list, which leaves the list 656px of the page's 944. The column has a width of its own (`flex: 0 0 240px`), not a share of what is left, so the room between the two is the list's to give. It drops under the list when both do not fit.
- **One course to a row.** Each is a [`MyCourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-course-card): horizontal, as wide as the page, with the progress in minutes and in lessons and the course's two buttons, «Repasar» and «Aprender». What a course is — description, levels, content types — is the catalog's to say, on `/cursos/explorar`.
- The head is the page's own (`.platform-page__head` and `__title`), the same one «Mis estudios» opens with, and it is the title alone: the list is right under it, so counting it in words («4 cursos · 3 en curso», until 2026-09-28) said nothing the list does not.
- After the cards, always, the dashed row «Explorar cursos», a link to `/cursos/explorar`. The page is therefore never empty and needs no `EmptyState`: with no course added that row is the only thing in the list and carries a hint, «Elige uno del catálogo y agrégalo para empezar.»

## Markup & styling

Block `.my-courses` (100 lines). A wrapping flex row: the list, one column with its rows `--platform-space-4` (16px) apart, and the aside.

**Elements:** `.my-courses__layout`, `.my-courses__list`, `.my-courses__aside`, `.my-courses__explore`, `.my-courses__explore-mark`, `.my-courses__explore-text`, `.my-courses__explore-label`, `.my-courses__explore-hint`

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-page__head`, `.platform-page__title`.

**Modifiers and states:** none

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-text-muted`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` the aside, on a row of its own, takes the width of the list. The hover transition is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const [courses, streak] = await Promise.all([getUserCourses(), getStudyStreak()]);

<MyCoursesSection courses={courses} streak={streak} />;
```
