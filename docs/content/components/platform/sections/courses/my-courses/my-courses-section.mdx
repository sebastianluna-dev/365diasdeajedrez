## Purpose

The list of the student's courses and nothing else. Until this page was redesigned it listed every published course with a level filter and the daily goal beside it; now a course is **added** before it is studied, the catalog is a page of its own ([`CourseCatalogSection`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-catalog/course-catalog-section)) and the day is looked at on the dashboard ([`StudyGoalSection`](/components/platform/sections/dashboard/study-goal/study-goal-section)).

## Props

| Prop      | Type             | Required | Meaning                                                                                        |
| --------- | ---------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courses` | `MyCourseItem[]` | yes      | The courses the student added, by name, each with its next lesson and what there is to review. |

## Behaviour

- **One course to a row.** Each is a [`MyCourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-course-card): horizontal, as wide as the page, with the progress in minutes and in lessons and the course's two buttons, «Repasar» and «Aprender». What a course is — description, levels, content types — is the catalog's to say, on `/cursos/explorar`.
- The header says how many there are and how many are being studied — «4 cursos · 3 en curso» —, the latter being the ones started and not finished (`ctaLabel === "Continuar"`). With none: «Todavía no has agregado ninguno».
- After the cards, always, the dashed row «Explorar cursos», a link to `/cursos/explorar`. The page is therefore never empty and needs no `EmptyState`: with no course added that row is the only thing in the list and carries a hint, «Elige uno del catálogo y agrégalo para empezar.»

## Markup & styling

Block `.my-courses` (89 lines). The list is one column, its rows 16px apart.

**Elements:** `.my-courses__head`, `.my-courses__title`, `.my-courses__summary`, `.my-courses__list`, `.my-courses__explore`, `.my-courses__explore-mark`, `.my-courses__explore-text`, `.my-courses__explore-label`, `.my-courses__explore-hint`

**Modifiers and states:** none

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-text-subtle`

No breakpoints of its own: the card stacks by itself. The hover transition is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const courses = await getUserCourses();

<MyCoursesSection courses={courses} />;
```
