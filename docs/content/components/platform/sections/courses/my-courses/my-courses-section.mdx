## Purpose

The student's courses and, beside them, how constant their study is. Redesigned on 2026-10-07 from two rounds of variants the owner chose from (page «P1», card «M2a», streak «S4»): until then each course was a row with a square picture and a ring of progress, «Explorar cursos» was a dashed row at the end of the list, and the streak was a small card.

From top to bottom:

1. **The head**: the platform's title and a line under it, and «Explorar cursos» at the right — the platform's secondary button with the glass — which leads to the catalog. On a phone the button goes under the title.
2. **The courses**, as covers ([`MyCourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-course-card)), two to a row, one on a phone. With none added, an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state) says so and leads to the catalog.
3. **The streak's column**, 300px at the right ([`StreakPanel`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/streak-panel)): its own height, sticky under the shell's bar while a long list scrolls past it (`top: calc(70px + space-6)`). Stretched to the list's height it left a blank in the middle of the card. Under the courses below 1024px.

## Props

| Prop      | Type             | Required | Meaning                                                               |
| --------- | ---------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courses` | `MyCourseItem[]` | yes      | `getUserCourses()`: the courses the student added.                    |
| `streak`  | `StreakOverview` | yes      | `getStreakOverview()`: the streak, its sixteen weeks and its figures. |

## Markup & styling

Block `.my-courses` (61 lines).

**Elements:** `.my-courses__head`, `.my-courses__explore`, `.my-courses__layout`, `.my-courses__grid`

**Other selectors it styles:** `.streak-panel` (sticky in the column).

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` puts the streak under the courses and unsticks it; `(width < 640px)` stacks the head and takes the grid to one column.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/cursos/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const [courses, streak] = await Promise.all([getUserCourses(), getStreakOverview()]);

<div className="platform-page platform-page_width_wide courses-page">
  <MyCoursesSection courses={courses} streak={streak} />
</div>;
```
