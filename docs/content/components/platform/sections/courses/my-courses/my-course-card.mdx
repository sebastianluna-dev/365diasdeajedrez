## Purpose

The card of a course the student **already chose**: how far they are, and going on. Since 2026-10-22 it is a card of the new design system (Material 3: an outlined card, 12px corners, on the white panel); from 2026-10-07 it was a cover on a shadow («M2a»), and before that a row with a square picture and a ring.

From top to bottom: the cover (21:9, `cover`, or the square `thumbnail` cropped to the frame; the frame stays, in the container's grey, without either); the name, two lines at most, and under it «40 % · 2 de 5 lecciones»; the [`WavyProgress`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/wavy-progress) (a straight green line once finished); and at the foot, at the right, the button.

## Behaviour

- **The whole card leads to the course**, through its name: the link's `::after` covers the card, so there is one link to announce. Its keyboard focus rings the card in the primary's blue.
- **The button** is Material 3's text button (`_variant_text`, small, with a chevron), raised above the stretched link: «Empezar» when nothing is done, «Continuar» after, and it leads to the lesson that comes next (`learnHref`). A course without lessons in view has none, and says «Sin lecciones todavía».
- **A finished course** (`isCompleted`) says so: the line straight and in the success green, «Completado · 2 de 2 lecciones», and «Completado» — a green label with its check — where the button was.
- **«Repasar»** is the Move Trainer's: while it is off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) nothing is drawn; on, a small text button «Repasar N» goes before the other when something is due.
