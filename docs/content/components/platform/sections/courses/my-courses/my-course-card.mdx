## Purpose

The card of a course the student **already chose**. It is a row as wide as the page and it is about how far they are and what to do next.

It says nothing of what the course is — **no description, no levels, no content types, no authors**. That is for whoever is choosing a course, and it is what the catalog's card shows ([`CourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-card), on `/cursos/explorar`).

## Props

| Prop     | Type           | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | -------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course` | `MyCourseItem` | yes      | A `CourseSummary` plus `learnHref?`, `reviewCount` and `reviewHref`. Read here: `name`, `thumbnail?`, `href` and `progress` (lessons and minutes, each with its percentage). |

## Behaviour

- **Horizontal**: the square image at the left (96px, `course.thumbnail`; a course without one shows its cover cropped square, which the mapper writes into the URL); beside it the name on top and, at the foot, the two measures and, at their right, the button. In a narrower list the button goes under them, still at the right. The button is 36px tall and written at 14px in 700, smaller than the one at the head of the course's pages: here it closes a row, it does not open a page. Under 760px the cover goes across the card, and under 480px the two measures stack.
- **Two measures, each with its bar**: «120/1220 minutos» and «9/61 lecciones», a rule between them. Each is one line of small text (14px), figure and unit alike and in one element: it is the bar that is looked at. They are different measures of the same progress (`progress.minutesPercent`, `progress.percent`). A course whose lessons say no minutes shows the lessons alone.
- **A finished course carries no mark**: its bars are full and its count says «61/61 lecciones». The check is drawn inside the course — a finished chapter, a learned lesson, «Tu progreso» —, not on its card. The bars are filled with `--platform-progress`, the blue a course's progress is drawn in.
- **«Repasar» is not drawn today**: it is the Move Trainer's, which is switched off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`), so «Aprender» is the row's one button. With the switch on, «Repasar» opens the Move Trainer with the exercises of the lessons already learned in the course (`course.reviewHref`) and carries how many there are. **«Aprender»** opens the next lesson to learn (`course.learnHref`); it is the accent button, written in white (`--platform-on-accent`) and raised on a hard edge (`0 4px 0 var(--platform-accent-deep)`), as the button of the blog's header; switched off it lies flat. Held down it sinks onto its edge (`:active`). They are the two buttons of the course's own page ([`CourseStudyFrame`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-study-frame)).
- **A button that leads nowhere keeps its place**, switched off (`_state_off`, a `<span aria-disabled="true">`): «Repasar» with nothing learned to review, «Aprender» in a course without lessons in view. The frame does not draw it; a list does, so its rows line up.
- **A finished course does not switch «Aprender» off**: `course.learnHref` is then its first lesson, to start the course over.
- **The card is not a link.** What leads to the course to study (`course.href`, `/cursos/<id>`) is its **image** and its **name**, and nothing else; each button leads where it says. Neither has a hover effect — no change of colour, no fade —: the pointer's hand is all that says they are pressed. The cover's link is `aria-hidden` and out of the tab order: it has no name of its own and the title's, next to it, leads to the same place.
- The picture is decorative (`alt=""`). Without one the box stays, in the ink colour, and is still the link.

## Markup & styling

Block `.my-course-card` (242 lines). A white card on the page's grey, over a light shadow; no outline.

**Elements:** `.my-course-card__cover`, `.my-course-card__cover-image`, `.my-course-card__body`, `.my-course-card__name`, `.my-course-card__link`, `.my-course-card__foot`, `.my-course-card__meters`, `.my-course-card__meter`, `.my-course-card__figure`, `.my-course-card__track`, `.my-course-card__fill`, `.my-course-card__actions`, `.my-course-card__review`, `.my-course-card__review-count`, `.my-course-card__learn`

**Modifiers and states:** `.my-course-card__review_state_off`, `.my-course-card__learn_state_off`

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-bright`, `--platform-accent-deep`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-ink`, `--platform-on-accent`, `--platform-progress`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-surface-neutral`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-faint`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-track`

**Breakpoints:** `(max-width: 760px)` stacked; `(max-width: 480px)` the measures one under the other. The press of «Aprender» takes 80 ms, and none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-courses.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { MyCourseCard } from "./my-course-card.comp";

{
  courses.map((course) => <MyCourseCard key={course.id} course={course} />);
}
```
