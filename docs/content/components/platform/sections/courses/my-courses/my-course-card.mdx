# `MyCourseCard`

| File       | `components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-course-card.comp.tsx`                 |
| Kind       | Server Component                                                                          |

## Purpose

The card of a course the student **already chose**. It is a row as wide as the page and it is about how far they are and what to do next.

It says nothing of what the course is — **no description, no levels, no content types, no authors**. That is for whoever is choosing a course, and it is what the catalog's card shows ([`CourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-card), on `/cursos/explorar`).

## Props

| Prop     | Type           | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | -------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course` | `MyCourseItem` | yes      | A `CourseSummary` plus `learnHref?`, `reviewCount` and `reviewHref`. Read here: `name`, `thumbnail?`, `href` and `progress` (lessons and minutes, each with its percentage). |

## Behaviour

- **Horizontal**: the square image at the left (96px, `course.thumbnail`; a course without one shows its cover cropped square, which the mapper writes into the URL); beside it the name on top and, at the foot, the two measures and, at their right, the button. In a narrower list the button goes under them, still at the right. The button is 36px tall and written at 14px in 700, smaller than the one at the head of the course's pages: here it closes a row, it does not open a page. Under 760px the cover goes across the card, and under 480px the two measures stack.
- **The progress is a ring and a sentence, not two bars.** «36 de 61 lecciones · 720/1220 minutos» is read as one line, and the percentage sits on a ring beside it (`progress.percent`). A bar has to be measured by eye and there were two of them on every row; the ring gives the figure one actually wants. It is the same ring the chapter carries in the column beside a lesson ([`LessonOutline`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-outline)), drawn the same way: an SVG of two circles, the arc held back with `stroke-dasharray`/`stroke-dashoffset` and turned back a quarter so it starts at twelve o'clock; at 0% only the track is drawn. A course whose lessons say no minutes shows the lessons alone.
- **A finished course carries no mark**: its ring is whole and its count says «61 de 61 lecciones». The check is drawn inside the course — a finished chapter, a learned lesson —, not on its card. The arc is **the accent** (`--platform-accent`) and not `--platform-progress`, the blue the bars on other pages still use: the row is the accent's already, from its button, and a blue arc in the middle of it read as something else's.
- **«Repasar» is not drawn today**: it is the Move Trainer's, which is switched off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`), so «Aprender» is the row's one button. With the switch on, «Repasar» opens the Move Trainer with the exercises of the lessons already learned in the course (`course.reviewHref`) and carries how many there are. **«Aprender»** opens the next lesson to learn (`course.learnHref`); it is the platform's **quiet button** — `platform-button` with `_variant_secondary`, the same one «Ver detalle» uses on the dashboard: a grey line that darkens to the text's ink under the pointer, with the word in ink. Neither the accent filled nor the accent outlined: a list of courses is a column of these rows, and a red block on each one turned the page into a row of buttons. The card gives it its size and nothing else, so what the button looks like stays written in one place (`platform.css`). Switched off, the line goes to the thin border and the word to the faint grey, which is what tells it from the one that leads somewhere. They are the two buttons of the course's own page ([`CourseStudyFrame`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-study-frame)).
- **A button that leads nowhere keeps its place**, switched off (`_state_off`, a `<span aria-disabled="true">`): «Repasar» with nothing learned to review, «Aprender» in a course without lessons in view. The frame does not draw it; a list does, so its rows line up.
- **A finished course does not switch «Aprender» off**: `course.learnHref` is then its first lesson, to start the course over.
- **The card is not a link.** What leads to the course to study (`course.href`, `/cursos/<id>`) is its **image** and its **name**, and nothing else; each button leads where it says. Neither has a hover effect — no change of colour, no fade —: the pointer's hand is all that says they are pressed. The cover's link is `aria-hidden` and out of the tab order: it has no name of its own and the title's, next to it, leads to the same place.
- The picture is decorative (`alt=""`). Without one the box stays, in the ink colour, and is still the link.

## Markup & styling

Block `.my-course-card` (219 lines). A card on the page's canvas, over the platform's card shadow; no outline. One row of four: the square (76px), the name with its figures, the ring (52px) and the buttons. Below 760px it wraps — the cover takes the full width, the name and the ring share the next row and the buttons take one of their own.

**Elements:** `.my-course-card__cover`, `.my-course-card__cover-image`, `.my-course-card__body`, `.my-course-card__name`, `.my-course-card__link`, `.my-course-card__figures`, `.my-course-card__dot`, `.my-course-card__gauge`, `.my-course-card__ring`, `.my-course-card__ring-track`, `.my-course-card__ring-fill`, `.my-course-card__percent`, `.my-course-card__actions`, `.my-course-card__review`, `.my-course-card__review-count`, `.my-course-card__learn`

**Modifiers and states:** `.my-course-card__review_state_off`, `.my-course-card__learn_state_off`

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button`, `.platform-button_variant_secondary` (on «Aprender»).

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-ink`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text` (the name, in the body's ink: the title grey is for a wall of names, and here there is one per card), `--platform-text-faint`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-track`

**Breakpoints:** `(max-width: 760px)` stacked; `(max-width: 480px)` the measures one under the other. The press of «Aprender» takes 80 ms, and none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-courses.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { MyCourseCard } from "./my-course-card.comp";

{
  courses.map((course) => <MyCourseCard key={course.id} course={course} />);
}
```
