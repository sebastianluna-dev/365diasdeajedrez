## Purpose

The card of a course the student **already chose**: how far they are, and going on. Since 2026-10-07 it is a cover («M2a», the owner's choice among two rounds of variants), so a course is recognised as it is in the catalog; until then it was a row with a square picture, the progress as a sentence and a ring, and «Aprender».

From top to bottom: the cover (21:9, `cover`, or the square `thumbnail` cropped to the frame; the frame stays, in the sunken grey, without either); the name, two lines at most; a bar of how far they are (`--platform-accent-bright`, the progress bars' fill; the success green once finished); and at the foot «40% · 2 de 5 lecciones» and the button.

## Behaviour

- **The whole card leads to the course**, through its name: the link's `::after` covers the card, so there is one link to announce. Its keyboard focus rings the card in the accent.
- **The button** is the platform's soft button, small, raised above the stretched link: «Empezar» when nothing is done, «Continuar» after, and it leads to the lesson that comes next (`learnHref`). A course without lessons in view has none, and says «Sin lecciones todavía».
- **A finished course** (`isCompleted`) says so: the bar in the success green, the foot «2 de 2 lecciones», and «Completado» — a green label with its check — where the button was.
- **«Repasar»** is the Move Trainer's: while it is off (`MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) nothing is drawn; on, a small ink button «Repasar N» goes before the other when something is due.
