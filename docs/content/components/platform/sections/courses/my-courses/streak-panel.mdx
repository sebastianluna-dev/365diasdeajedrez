## Purpose

The owner asked for a longer streak, a whole column high, with more data (2026-10-07), and chose «S4» among four designs: the last months in squares, as GitHub draws a year of contributions, so that what is constant is seen at a glance. From top to bottom:

- **The streak**: the figure beside the platform's flame, in its two colours (`--platform-flame`, `--platform-flame-core`, as the streak card draws it; a grey hollow flame with no streak), «días seguidos estudiando», and a pill that says whether today counts already — «Hoy ya estudiaste» in the success green, or «Estudia hoy para mantenerla» («…para empezarla» with no streak) in the accent's wash.
- **«Últimos 4 meses»**: sixteen columns of seven days, Monday at the top and this week last, a square each, deeper the more was done that day (four steps of the flame's colour, `activityLevel`); today ringed; the days of this week still to come keep their place, empty. Each day says itself under the pointer («7 de octubre: 3 actividades»); to a screen reader the grid is one image named by its count. A legend «Menos … Más» under it.
- **Four figures**, the number over its name on the canvas's grey: «Días estudiados» (in those sixteen weeks), «Mejor racha» (the longest run in the streak's 400-day window), «Días esta semana», «Minutos esta semana» (the finished lessons' durations, as today's minutes are counted).

All of it derived — the activity log and the finished lessons —, nothing stored: [`getStreakOverview`](/platform/student).
