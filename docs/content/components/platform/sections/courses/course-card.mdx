## Purpose

What a course is, for whoever is choosing one, and the way to take it. Since 2026-10-22 it is Material's outlined card (the new design system; before it, «K2a» of 2026-10-07, with the level and the topics as labels): the cover; the name, two lines at most; one line with who it is for and what it teaches («Intermedio · Estrategia · Estructuras»); and at the foot what can be done.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                  | Required | Meaning                                                                               |
| ----------- | --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course`    | `CourseSummary`       | yes      | The course as `mapCourseSummary` hands it over.                                       |
| `addAction` | `() => Promise<void>` | no       | `addCourse` bound to the course, handed down by the section; no «Agregar» without it. |

## Behaviour

- **The whole card is the link**, through the title's: its `::after` covers the card. A course the student added leads to the course (`href`); one they have not, to its information page (`infoHref`), for whoever wants to read about it before adding it.
- **Already the student's**: «Lo llevas», Material's assist chip with a check, and a text button with `ctaLabel` («Continuar», «Empezar», «Repasar») to the lesson that comes next (`continueHref`).
- **Not yet**: its length («24 lecciones · 6 h») and «Agregar», Material's tonal button inside a form whose action is `addAction`; adding takes the student to the course. Its accessible name says which course («Agregar Planes en el medio juego»).
- The buttons are raised above the stretched link, so what is pressed is the button.

## Markup & styling

Block `.course-card` (92 lines), on Material's `card card-outlined`, which draws the box, its outline and its corners.

**Elements:** `__cover`, `__cover-image`, `__body`, `__name`, `__link`, `__about`, `__foot`, `__length`, `__action`, `__owned`

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-on-surface`, `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`, `--platform-m3-panel`, `--platform-m3-container-high`, `--platform-m3-primary`, the type and space scales.

No media queries: the grid around it decides its width.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/courses/course-catalog/catalog-browser.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
{
  shown.map((course) => <CourseCard key={course.id} course={course} />);
}
```
