## Purpose

The card of the catalog (`/cursos/explorar`): what a course is, for whoever is choosing one. «Mis cursos» has its own, [`MyCourseCard`](/components/platform/sections/courses/my-courses/my-course-card), which is about the progress. Here it is still the **course** that says which it is, not a prop:

- A course the student **added** (`course.isAdded`) shows their progress — percentage, «2 de 5 lecciones», a bar — and leads to the course to study (`course.href`, `/cursos/<id>`). Its button reads what the mapper decided: «Empezar», «Continuar» or «Repasar».
- A course **not added** shows how long it is («61 lecciones · 20 h») and leads to its information page (`course.infoHref`, `/cursos/<slug>`), which is where it is added. Its button reads «Ver curso».

It lives at the root of `sections/courses/` from when two sections used it.

## Props

| Prop     | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | ------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course` | `CourseSummary`     | yes      | `name`, `description?`, `cover?`, `contentTypes`, `levels`, `authorNames`, `isAdded`, `href`, `infoHref`, `ctaLabel`, `progress: { percent, completedLessons, totalLessons, totalMinutes }`. |
| `levels` | `CourseLevelItem[]` | yes      | Every level of the catalog, the easiest first: the scale the dots are drawn over.                                                                                                            |

## Behaviour

- **The whole card is the link**, through the title's: `.course-card__link::after` is stretched over the card, so there is one link to announce and not a link around another. The focus ring is drawn on that `::after`, around the card.
- The button at the foot is a `<span aria-hidden="true">`: it is the card's link, which the title already names. «Continuar» is the filled accent one (`_emphasis_strong`); the rest are outlined.
- The dots sit above the stretched link (`.course-card__levels`, `z-index: 1`) because they open a tip of their own. The wrapper is only rendered for a course with levels.
- The description is clamped to two lines: the card says what the course is about, the information page tells it.
- What the course teaches is a line of text under it, not tags: the content types' long names, in catalog order, joined by « · », and «por» with the authors after them. The short names are the tags of the staff's catalog ([`CoursesAdminSection`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/courses-admin-section)).
- The bar is `--platform-progress`, the blue a course's progress is drawn in; a finished course (`course.isCompleted`) paints it green instead (`.course-card__fill_state_done`). It carries no check: that mark is drawn inside the course, not on its card.
- The cover is decorative (`alt=""`): the staff's covers carry the course's name drawn on them and the title is right under. Without a cover the box stays, in the ink colour.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/courses/course-catalog/course-catalog.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { CourseCard } from "../course-card.comp";

{
  courses.map((course) => <CourseCard key={course.id} course={course} levels={levels} />);
}
```
