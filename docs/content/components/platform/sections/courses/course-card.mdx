## Purpose

What a course is, for whoever is choosing one. Since 2026-10-07 it is compact («K2a», the owner's choice among two rounds of variants, with the level **and the topics** in its labels, which the owner asked for): the cover (21:9; the square picture cropped when there is none); the name, two lines at most; its labels — the level it is for with the signal (`levelSpan`: one name, or the span of a course aimed at several), and one per topic it teaches with the book, all in the same grey label —; and «24 lecciones · 6 h 15 min».

It replaced a card with the description, the levels as dots with a tip of their own (`LevelDots`, removed) and, at the foot, the student's progress or the course's length with «Ver curso»: the catalog is for choosing, and «Mis cursos» is where the progress is. The description is the course's page.

## Props

| Prop     | Type            | Required | Meaning                                         |
| -------- | --------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `course` | `CourseSummary` | yes      | The course as `mapCourseSummary` hands it over. |

## Behaviour

- **The whole card is the link**, through the title's: its `::after` covers the card. A course the student added leads to the course (`href`); one they have not, to its information page (`infoHref`), which is where it is added. Its keyboard focus rings the card in the accent.
- The labels are a list (`aria-label="Nivel y temas"`); a course with neither has none.
- Under the pointer the shadow grows; nothing moves.
