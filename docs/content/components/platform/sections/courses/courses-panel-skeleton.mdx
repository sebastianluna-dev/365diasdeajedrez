## Purpose

The loading state of «Mis cursos» and the catalog (2026-10-22). Their layout keeps the side column and the bar's search on screen, so while a view loads only the panel waits, and it draws the shape of **the page that is coming**, in the container's grey, so nothing jumps when it arrives (the owner asked for each page's own shape on 2026-10-22):

- **«Mis cursos»** (`view="mine"`): «Sigue donde ibas» in its block — the cover at the left, and beside it the eyebrow, the name, the figures, the progress and the 56px «Continuar» —; the streak's band in its blue — the flame's disc, two lines and the sixteen weeks —; the heading with its count at the right; and a row of four of the student's cards — cover, name, figures, progress and the text button at the right.
- **The catalog** (`view="catalog"`): the heading — two shapes, the title and the count, without the level's dot, which «Todo el catálogo» does not have —; the row of filter chips with the divider before «Que aún no llevas»; and two rows of its cards — cover, name, the line about it, its length and «Agregar».

Every shape pulses softly together (`opacity`, 1.2s), and stands still under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. To a screen reader it is one status, «Cargando tus cursos…» or «Cargando el catálogo…» (visually hidden).

Each page has its own `loading.tsx` because each is its own segment: «Mis cursos» sits in the route group `(mis-cursos)` inside `(lista)`, so a loading file there wraps only its page and not the catalog's. It is a `loading.tsx`'s, so it is markup and CSS only: no Client Component behind it (`components/loading-boundaries.test.ts`). Arriving at `/cursos` from another screen, before the layout itself is ready, is the platform's other wait, `BoardLoading` (`app/(platform)/cursos/loading.tsx`).
