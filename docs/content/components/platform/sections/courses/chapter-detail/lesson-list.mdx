## Purpose

The lessons of a chapter as an `<ol>` of whole-row links. A completed lesson is recognisable before reading anything: its order number turns green (`_state_done`). Essential lessons carry a "Prioridad" mark next to the name; every row shows its duration and a status label.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Required | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lessons` | `ChapterLessonItem[]` | yes | `{ id, order, name, description?, href, isPriority, estimatedDuration?, statusCode }[]`. |

## Behaviour

None. `statusCode` is one of the `PROGRESS_STATUS` codes (`NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`); a local map turns it into "Sin empezar", "En curso", "Completada", and the lowercased code becomes the status modifier. The chevron is an inline `aria-hidden` SVG.
