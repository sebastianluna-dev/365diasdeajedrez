## Purpose

Everything a teacher may know about an assigned student, in cards: assignment facts and note, progress per course, upcoming and past classes **with this teacher**, the student's own studies, and recent activity. All of it is read-only: the student's studies are theirs and the teacher consults them, which is why there is not a single write control on the page. The service already checked the assignment is active; the section never sees a student who is not the teacher's.

## Props

| Prop      | Type                    | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------- | ----------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `student` | `AssignedStudentDetail` | yes      | `id`, `email`, `assignedAtIso`, `assignedAtLabel`, `note?`, `courses[]` (with `percent`, `statusLabel`, `lastLessonName?`), `upcomingClasses[]`, `pastClasses[]`, `studies[]`, `activity[]`. |

## Behaviour

- The local `ClassList` renders shared classes with title link, local date, status tag and an "Asistió" tag.
- Studies are a [`PlatformTable`](/components/platform/shared/platform-table) whose name links to `teacherRoutes.studentStudy(studentId, studyId)`, the teacher's read-only study view.
- Activity rows show type and subject labels with a date-only local time; empty renders an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state).
