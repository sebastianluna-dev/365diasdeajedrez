## Behaviour

D24, phase 1b. The service answers only for a student assigned to the teacher now; by direct URL, someone else's student is a 404, as with their studies. What is listed is `teacherVisibleAttemptsWhere` ([Exercises → Who sees what](/platform/exercises#who-sees-what)): the teacher's own series — deleted since or not (D45) —, and the neutral contexts at their own or the library's exercises; never by the exercise's current status, so «Archivar» hides nothing.

- The three figures: «Ejercicios superados» (distinct exercises with a pass among the visible attempts), «Intentos» and «Últimos 7 días».
- The attempts, the latest first, each an [`ExerciseAttemptRow`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-attempt-row); «Ver más» (`?pagina=`) adds a page, with «25 de 41» beside it, the way the teacher's classes list grows.
- `StudentPageTabs` draws «Resumen» and «Ejercicios» as pills on both pages of the student, and nothing while the exercises are off.
