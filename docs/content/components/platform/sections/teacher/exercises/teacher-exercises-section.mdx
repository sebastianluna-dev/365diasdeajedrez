## Behaviour

«En uso · n», «Archivados · n» and «Series · n» are links (`?vista=archivados`, `?vista=series`); the cards are the teacher's own exercises (`buildOwnExercisesWhere`), two to a row from 1024px. Empty, «En uso» says how to start and links to «Nuevo ejercicio», the page's accent in its header. The row actions redirect back with their notice.

On «Series» each of the teacher's series is a row: its title (the link to [its page](/components/platform/sections/teacher/exercises/teacher-series-editor-section)), «5 ejercicios · la tienen 2 alumnos» (only the shares to a student still assigned, `activeSeriesSharesWhere`), its description clamped to two lines, and, in the danger's ink with a triangle, how many of its exercises the students no longer see (`seriesItemAvailability`, the visibility rule's own condition). «Nueva serie», the head's secondary button, opens the popup that creates one and goes to it.
