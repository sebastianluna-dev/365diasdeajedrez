## Behaviour

«En uso · n» and «Archivados · n» are links (`?vista=archivados`); the cards are the teacher's own exercises (`buildOwnExercisesWhere`), two to a row from 1024px. Empty, «En uso» says how to start and links to «Nuevo ejercicio», the page's accent in its header. The row actions redirect back with their notice.

## Markup & styling

Block `.teacher-exercises` (22 lines): the pills and the list, `(width < 1024px)` one column.
