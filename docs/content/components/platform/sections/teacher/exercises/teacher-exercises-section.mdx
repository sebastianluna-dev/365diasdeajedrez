## Behaviour

«En uso · n», «Archivados · n», «Series · n» and «Biblioteca · n» are links (`?vista=archivados`, `?vista=series`, `?vista=biblioteca`); the cards are the teacher's own exercises (`buildOwnExercisesWhere`), two to a row from 1024px. Empty, «En uso» says how to start and links to «Nuevo ejercicio», the page's accent in its header. The row actions redirect back with their notice; «Proponer a la biblioteca» and «Retirar la propuesta» are among them (D07, D16), and a returned exercise's card carries the staff's note.

On «Biblioteca» (D08, phase 4) the cards are the library's exercises (`buildLibraryExercisesWhere`: the academy's and every teacher's approved one, the last published first), read-only: no page of the teacher's (their own open from «En uso»), no words, and the author named. It is what they may pick for a series or a class block.

On «Series» each of the teacher's series is a row: its title (the link to [its page](/components/platform/sections/teacher/exercises/teacher-series-editor-section)), «5 ejercicios · la tienen 2 alumnos» (only the shares to a student still assigned, `activeSeriesSharesWhere`), its description clamped to two lines, and, in the danger's ink with a triangle, how many of its exercises the students no longer see (`seriesItemAvailability`, the visibility rule's own condition). «Nueva serie», the head's secondary button, opens the popup that creates one and goes to it.
