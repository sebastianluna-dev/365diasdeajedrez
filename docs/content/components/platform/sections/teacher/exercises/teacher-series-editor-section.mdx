## Behaviour

A series (D18) is an ordered list of exercises a teacher gives to their students (D31). The section draws the page's head itself, because the pen that edits the title sits beside it: the way back to «Series», the title, «5 ejercicios · la tienen 2 alumnos», the description, and «Agregar ejercicios», the page's accent. Under it, from 1024px, the exercises beside an aside with the students who have it and «Eliminar la serie»; one column under that.

Another teacher's series never reaches here: the service reads it with its `teacherId` in the `where` and the page answers 404. What «Agregar ejercicios» offers is `buildPickableExercisesWhere` (the teacher's own `READY`, `IN_REVIEW` or `PUBLISHED` exercises and, from phase 4, the library's) minus what the series holds; a draft or an archived exercise is never offered. A share counts only while the student is assigned to the teacher (`activeSeriesSharesWhere`), because only then does the series reach them.
