## Behaviour

- Submitted by hand (`onSubmit` → `startTransition(() => formAction(data))`, `pending` on its `SubmitButton`), as every form with controlled Radix cards is, so React's reset does not reach them.
- The search (from six exercises) narrows the cards by title and topics without accents; a chosen card it hides is still sent (`hiddenValues`).
- The line over the cards says where they go («Van al final de la serie, en el orden de la lista.») and how many are chosen; «Agregar a la serie» waits while more are chosen than fit.
- The server checks again that each one may be picked now (`seriesNotPickable`), that they fit (`seriesFull`), and skips one already in the series. Two adds at once race for the same orders: the loser's unique violation, recognised by `isUniqueConstraintError` on `ExerciseSeriesItem_pkey` or `ExerciseSeriesItem_seriesId_order_key`, answers «La serie cambió mientras tanto: vuelve a intentarlo.» (`seriesChanged`). On success the address says `?aviso=seriesExercisesAdded`.
- With nothing to offer it says why: everything ready is already in the series, or the teacher has nothing ready yet.
