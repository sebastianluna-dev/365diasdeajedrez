## Purpose

Not a form since 2026-10-15: the fields only, inside the popup's `PlatformForm`. For a game, «Transcribir» (the board) and «De un estudio» (a game of the teacher's or of an assigned student's) are mutually exclusive cards; in a group class a student's game warns «Los 4 alumnos de la clase verán esta partida de Mateo Ríos.». The lesson is cited by id, and its viewer under the field is the check. An exercise block (D29, 2026-10-16) ticks one to ten of the teacher's pickable exercises in [`ExercisePicker`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-picker), the line above saying «De 1 a 10 ejercicios, en el orden en que los elijas. El alumno los resuelve desde la clase.» and how many are ticked; with nothing to offer, «Todavía no tienes ejercicios listos…». The ticked ids are what the form sends as `exercises`, in tick order. A block edited after one of its exercises was archived, or left the library, hands the picker those as `unavailable` (`block.unavailableExercises`): ticked, apart, to untick or to keep with the block's attempts; the empty state is shown only when there is nothing to offer **and** nothing kept.

| `block`             | `Partial<TeacherClassBlock>` | no       | The block being edited, or the preset of a new one.                                                                                                        |
| `values`, `errors`  | `Record<string, …>`          | no       | What was sent before the server refused, and what it said, by field.                                                                                       |
| `groupSize`         | `number`                     | no       |                                                                                                                                                            |
| `fetchReferencePgn` | `FetchReferencePgn`          | yes      | For the position picker (`services/teacher-classes/teacher-classes.types.ts`); bound to the block's kind and handed to `MovePathPicker` as its `fetchPgn`. |

## Markup & styling

Block `.block-form` (34 lines): the fields one under the other, a warning in the warning tone, an error in the danger's, a note in the muted grey over the exercise picker.
