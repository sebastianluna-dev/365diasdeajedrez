## Purpose

No trigger of its own: the block list opens it through its `ref` with a preset — a kind and a section for a new block, the block to edit, a game chosen elsewhere («Usar en una clase»). «¿Qué vas a agregar?» (Texto · Partida · Lección · Video; fixed when editing) and «¿Cuándo lo ve el alumno?» (Para preparar · Después de la clase · Guion del profesor) are cards; the fields are [`BlockForm`](/components/platform/sections/teacher/classes/class-block-editor/block-form)'s. Submitted by hand (`onSubmit` → `startTransition(formAction)`), so React's reset does not reach the controlled pieces inside the fields.
