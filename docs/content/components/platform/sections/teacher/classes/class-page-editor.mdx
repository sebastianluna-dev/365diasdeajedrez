## Purpose

Option A of the editor canvas (2026-10-19): the page the teacher writes looks like the student's notebook and is typed where it stands, instead of a list of blocks edited in popups ([`ClassBlockList`](/components/platform/sections/teacher/classes/class-block-list), which it would replace).

- **Adding.** «/» in an empty paragraph, or in the line that ends the page, opens the menu of blocks under it (`EditorSlashMenu`): Texto, Título, Subtítulo, Lista, Cita, Destacado and Separador; Diagrama and Partida; Ahora tú and Ejercicios; Lección, Video and Archivo. What follows the slash filters it, without accents; the arrows walk it, Enter takes a kind, and Escape, or Backspace with nothing typed, closes it. Chosen from an empty paragraph the kind takes its place; from a block's «+», it goes under it. Typing in the last line starts a paragraph.
- **Writing.** Each text is plain text on a `contenteditable` (`EditableText`, uncontrolled so React never rewrites what is under the caret). «# », «## », «- » and «> » at the start of a paragraph turn it into a title, a subtitle, a point of a list or a quote. Enter splits the block at the caret (a point makes another point; an empty point leaves the list); Backspace at the start turns a title, a point or a quote back into a paragraph, joins a paragraph to the text above, or takes an empty one away; the arrows go up and down between texts.
- **Each block.** Pointing at a block, or giving it the focus, shows in its left margin its «+» and its ⋮⋮ handle. The handle is the row's dnd-kit handle in [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd), like every sortable list of the platform: dragged (with the pointer, a finger, or Space and the arrows) it moves the block, the others making room as it travels, and every move is said to the screen reader. A click on it, or Enter, opens its menu (`EditorBlockMenu`): who sees it (Para preparar, Después de la clase, Guion del profesor; an exercise block is never the script, D30), «Convertir en» for a text, Duplicar, Mover arriba, Mover abajo (the list's steps, `useSortableStep`, for whoever does not drag) and Eliminar, which asks first with `PlatformConfirmPopup`. A block that is not «Para preparar» says so in the right margin (above it under 1280px), and the script sits on a grey of its own.
- **The chess blocks.** A diagram is its PGN (`[FEN]`, caption, `[%csl]`/`[%cal]`): drawn with `StaticDiagram`, its caption typed under it, and «Editar el diagrama» opens `DiagramEditor` in a popup (`EditorBoardPopup`). «Ahora tú» is typed in place: the prompt, each option with the line the student reads on choosing it, the round mark of the right one, «Agregar opción» (up to five) and «Quitar» (down to two); «Cambiar la posición» opens the same editor on its position step alone (`positionOnly`).
- **What it cites.** A game, a lesson, a series, a video or a file starts as a picker — a search over what `options` offers, the link checked as a URL, a file chosen from the computer — and becomes its card once chosen, with «Cambiar».
- **«Vista del alumno».** The page as the student reads it after the class: no script, no empty block, no controls, and «Ahora tú» answers when an option is chosen.

Front-only: the blocks live in the component's state, and the page says so («Vista previa del editor: lo que escribas aquí todavía no se guarda.»). Text has no inline marks yet (bold, links).

## Props

| Prop          | Type                     | Required | Meaning                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------ | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`       | `string`                 | yes      | The class's title, typed over the blocks.             |
| `dateline`    | `string`                 | yes      | «Jueves 29 de octubre · 18:00».                       |
| `blocks`      | `EditorBlock[]`          | yes      | Where it starts.                                      |
| `options`     | `ClassPageEditorOptions` | yes      | The games, lessons and series the pickers offer.      |
| `endHour`     | `string`                 | yes      | When «Después de la clase» begins, in the block menu. |
| `defaultMode` | `"edit" \| "student"`    | no       | The view it opens in. Default `edit`.                 |

## Markup & styling

Block `.class-page-editor` (259 lines): the reading column, padded on the left for the margin of handles; the bar with the dateline and the two views; the title; the rows, each with its gutter (`opacity` while it is not pointed at), its tag and its body, and the row being dragged lifted on the page's grey; the line that ends the page.

`editor-block.comp.css` (538 lines): the article set as the notebook sets it — the serif for the text, the display face for the headings — and the question, the game widget, the cards and the pickers. An empty text shows its placeholder (a paragraph only while it has the caret).

`editor-slash-menu.comp.css` (156 lines) and `editor-block-menu.comp.css` (188 lines): the two menus, white cards that come in in 160ms and go out in 120 on Radix's `data-state`. `editor-board-popup.comp.css` (14 lines): the diagram editor and its foot.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1280px)` (the tag goes above its block), `(width < 768px)` (a narrower margin, its handles one over the other).
