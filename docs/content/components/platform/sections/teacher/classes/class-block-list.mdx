## Purpose

Three sections — «Para preparar» («Alumno Demo ya lo ve.»), «Después de la clase» («Lo verá cuando termine la clase, a las 13:00.»), «Guion del profesor» («Sólo lo ves tú.») — and at the foot of each «+ Texto · + Partida · + Lección · + Video», plus «+ Ejercicio» behind `EXERCISES_ENABLED` and never under «Guion del profesor» (D17; D29, D30). The class has ONE order however the screen splits it: a drop within a section or a step from the menu rebuilds the whole list with `blockOrderAfterDrop` and sends it entire to `reorderClassBlocks`; «Pasar a «Después de la clase»» moves the block to the end of another section and changes its visibility in the same call. The order is applied on the client and saved in a transition; a refusal puts the server's order back.
