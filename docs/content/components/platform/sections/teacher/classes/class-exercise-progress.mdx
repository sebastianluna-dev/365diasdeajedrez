## Purpose

D29's «the teacher sees «2 de 3 lo resolvieron»» (2026-10-16, phase 3 of [Exercises](/platform/exercises)). In the teacher's record of a class, an EXERCISE block folds to a line like any other; unfolded, it shows this instead of the student's list: each exercise with its number, title and facts, and the count as its action (`.platform-row-action`). The count opens a popup named after the exercise: the figure again, every attempt made from the block by a seated student — the latest first, each an [`ExerciseAttemptRow`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-attempt-row) whose title is the student (a link to their «Ejercicios» tab when they are the teacher's, a name alone when they are not), with the mistake to unfold — and «Sin intentar: Mateo Ríos y Diego Salas.». Nothing is stored: the staff's sheet reads the same count through the renderer's rows.

## Behaviour

- The label is `solvedCountLabel`'s: «2 de 3 lo resolvieron», «1 de 3 lo resolvió», «Sin alumnos» when nobody is seated. The denominator is the seats not moved; an attempt from a seat moved since counts for nothing.
- The popup's content is remounted on each close (`onClose` bumps a key), so the rows' disclosures start folded on the next exercise. With no attempt: «Nadie lo ha intentado todavía desde la clase.»
- `aria-haspopup="dialog"` on the count; the popup is a `PlatformPopup`, named by its title.
