## Purpose

The bot's personality lives here and nowhere else: there are no phrases during the game (planning decision §H). Its Elo is said as «Elo ≈ 783», approximate until calibrated. A bot named after a real player shows that player's facts — FIDE title and rating with the date they were true — and never claims to play like them: it is Stockfish, weakened. Since 2026-10-09 the sheet of every bot outside the invented characters says so — «Bot con el nombre de un jugador real: es Stockfish ajustado a un nivel aproximado, no juega como esa persona y ella no participa en la plataforma.» —, as the image-rights research recommends.
