## Purpose

The lobby of games against bots, as chosen on 2026-10-08 (J1): a gallery by groups with the suggested rival on top and a row per group that scrolls sideways — the invented characters, Mexico's Olympiad teams and its legend, the world champions, today's stars —, each row headed by its span of labels («De 412 a 1868»). A card opens the bot's popup: its sheet, the colour (Blancas, Negras, Al azar) and «Jugar».

**The suggested rival** (`suggestedBot` in `services/bots/bot-rules.ts`, with its test) is the first bot stronger than the strongest one already beaten; the weakest of all to start with; the strongest once every bot has fallen. A draw does not move it.

**An open game against a bot** comes first, as [`OpenGameNotice`](/components/platform/sections/play/lobby/open-game-notice) with «Continuar», and the popup warns before starting another: it sends `replace`, and `startPlayedGame` closes the open one in the same transaction — lost by abandonment after two moves of one's own, cancelled before them.

`/jugar?rival=<slug>` opens that bot's popup on arrival (a profile's link to play a bot again). The popup's content changes with the bot, so `BotGallery` draws it before opening (`flushSync`): the popup then focuses the content it finds, the colour cards.

## Behaviour

The section reads in one round: the groups, the open game, the scoreboard and the person's own card (for the scoreboard's other side). It writes nothing. `startPlayedGame` redirects to the new game; a failure comes back as `?error=` and the page shows it in a [`PlatformNotice`](/components/platform/shared/platform-notice).
