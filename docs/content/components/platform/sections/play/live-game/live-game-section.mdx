## Purpose

The page of a game against a bot — one URL, live or finished — mounts this with the game, the bot's strength and what comes after (the scoreboard, the person's studies, the way on and the copy bound to the game). It plays the game on **the** viewer: there is no second board.

**In the new design system** since 2026-10-25, as the analysis board and the explorer play theirs: the viewer in its material look (`look="material"`: the blue squares, the «elegida» pieces, Material's keys, the notation in an outlined card beside the board), «Abandonar» as Material's outlined button in the error's red, the strips loose on the panel and tinted in Material's containers once it is over, and [`GameOverActions`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-over-actions) in Material's buttons. The page draws the workspace around it: [`PlaySide`](/components/platform/sections/play/play-side) for whoever holds a seat, and the head of the panel — for a game of an adventure «Modo aventura» and the zone over the two names, and the facts as pills («Página 5 · Intento 2», the colour, «Sin reloj», the day), which the notebook's paper frame (`PaperFrame`, gone) said until then. The popups (`AbandonPopup`, `SaveToStudyPopup`) open as Material's dialog by themselves (`lookOf`).

## Behaviour

**`useLiveGame`** wires the pure reducer (`lib/play/live-game-machine.ts`) to the engine and the server. Every ply, the person's or the bot's, is saved on its own with its ply as the version (`appendPlayedMove(gameId, { expectedPly, san })`), at most one in flight; a throttled save is retried after 1, 2 and 4 s. The bot is asked as soon as the person's move is on the board — while it is still being saved — and its move is shown after its human-like delay (`thinkDelayMs`), never on top of a move the server may still refuse. The bot's engine (`useEnginePlayer`) lives only while the game does, so «Analizar» never meets a second Worker. A worker that dies — it throws, or stops answering past the client's watchdog — is replaced once (`useEnginePlayer`'s `instance`) and the same position is asked again; only a second death ends in «Recargar» (verified on 2026-10-09 by killing the bot's worker mid-search: a third worker answered, then a second kill brought the banner).

**`liveGamePlayers`** (pure, tested) says what each strip says: the portraits, the turn as a dot, «Te toca mover» on the person's own, «Pensando…» and the turning arc on the bot's, and once it is over who won and why — «Ganaste» on the person's own strip, «Ganó» on the other's, «Perdió», «Tablas», the reason under the name (G6). A cancelled game has no winner and no tint.

**When the board stops** — another tab moved (`stale`), or the engine died twice — [`LiveGameBanner`](/components/platform/sections/play/live-game/live-game-banner) says why, with «Recargar»; nothing is lost.

**Over**, the viewer reads the game with its tools: «Analizar» opens the review, whose evaluations are stored in the game (`storePlayedGameReview`, refused if a move changed). The page is refreshed once so the scoreboard under the board counts the game just finished. A game finished before this visit is read from the server's PGN, with its review.

**A game of an adventure** — when `ADVENTURE_ENABLED` is on and `game.adventure` is set, the page passes `adventure={{ mapHref, priorWins }}` (the victories already counted against that rival when the page was drawn). Over, the section hands the viewer `onwards` «Volver al mapa» (see [`GameOverActions`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-over-actions)); an old free game (there are no new ones since 2026-10-25) goes on to «Ir a Jugar» (`playHref`), or nowhere while «Jugar» is off. The address carries `?victoria=<order>` only when the end is a win of the viewer's seat and `priorWins` is 0, the first win: the map plays its sequence once; otherwise it is the map as it is. An aborted game offers nothing.

**Whoever only reads it** — the person's teacher, the staff — gets the board, the moves and the strips, with no engine and nothing to do; the page gives them no side column (`platform-workspace_side_none`), since «Tus partidas» would be their own games, not the student's.

**Announcer:** a visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` line: «Beto jugó Cf3. Tu turno.», «Ganaste.».
