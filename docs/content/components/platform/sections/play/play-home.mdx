## Purpose

The owner's «M4 · /jugar: el modo aventura en grande, partida libre y contra una persona; tus partidas en la barra lateral» (2026-10-25), the first page of «Jugar» in the new design system. **Every game against a bot is an encounter of the adventure** since the same day, so the page is the adventure's: of the design, «Partida libre» (the gallery of bots, gone with free play) and «Contra una persona» (playing a person does not exist in the platform) are left out, with no «Próximamente» in their place. The page bleeds onto the canvas (`platform-page_width_full` + `platform-page_frame_bleed`) and the section draws the workspace:

- **The side column** is [`PlaySide`](/components/platform/sections/play/play-side): «Continuar la partida» as the extended FAB while a game against a bot is open, with «En curso contra Chayo Ríos · con blancas · 14 jugadas» under it (an old free game left open counts too); then «Tus partidas», the last four games with their G, P or T, the record of them all and «Ver todas en tu perfil». With no game open there is no FAB: the panel's own button is the way in.
- **The adventure, big**, on an amber block (`--platform-m3-warning-container`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-xl`): the journey's picture at the left ([`JourneyMiniature`](/components/adventure/cover/journey-miniature), the wide one, on the adventure's own paper, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`); at the right «Modo aventura», the notebook's title and premise, a stamp per rival — filled with its initials once beaten, framed in white the one to play, dashed with a padlock the rest —, «4 de 12 rivales superados · 9 estrellas», the next rival on a white card with its portrait («Próximo rival · N.º 05 · Intento 2», name and role, «≈ 783 · Fácil · Club La Torre»), and «Continuar la aventura» (filled, to the map) with «Ver los cuadernos» (text, to the shelf below). A finished notebook says so instead of the next rival, and its button is «Abrir el mapa».
- **The shelf** (`#cuadernos`), which was `/jugar/aventura` until 2026-10-25 (that address redirects here): [`AdventureShelf`](/components/adventure/shelf/adventure-shelf), «Elige tu cuaderno», the adventure's paper bookshelf, **an island of the adventure's look inside the Material panel** (its wood, its covers, its fonts and its tokens). Every notebook that can be opened stands open at the left ([`OpenNotebook`](/components/adventure/shelf/open-notebook): its journey, its place, «4 de 12 rivales» with the stars, a bar of twelve and «Abrir el mapa»), the one drawn big above included, and the ones that are coming stand closed on two boards ([`NotebookSpine`](/components/adventure/shelf/notebook-spine): the cloth cover with the motif of its state, «Próximamente», its name and what it will explore, and no action). From 2026-10-25 to 2026-10-26 the shelf was drawn as Material's outlined cards; the owner preferred the paper one, and it came back. The amber block stayed as it is: it already says more than the old cover's page did (the next rival's portrait among it), and the shelf's open notebook is the notebook's place among the others.

The notebook drawn big is the first that is not finished, or the first of all when every one is. The page's `h1` («Jugar») is said to a screen reader only: the bar already names the page.

## Behaviour

Server only. The section makes four reads on top of the session — the games against each notebook's rivals (shared by the shelf and the progress, `cache`), the open game, the last games and the scoreboard; nothing is written. **Playing never counts toward the streak**: nothing it reaches names the activity log (`services/play/no-activity.test.ts`).

**Reflow.** Under 1280px the picture narrows to 280px and the shelf stands its open notebook over the boards; under 1024px the block stacks the picture over the story; under 640px the title steps down.
