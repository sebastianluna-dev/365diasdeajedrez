## Purpose

The owner's «Estadísticas · C · Por secciones» (2026-10-25), in the new design system: a `.platform-workspace` whose side column has «Seguir estudiando» as the extended FAB (the course in progress, or «Mis cursos»), the profile's views — «Visión general», «Estadísticas» lit and «Partidas» with its count, only with `PLAY_ENABLED`, where the profile exists — and «Periodo», the four calendar periods as links with a radio's ring (`choice`), the current one with its dates («1–25 oct»); under them, what the streak counts and that playing does not. The panel is the title with the period's dates and the streak's pill, then three tabs as links, each with its drawing, its figure and its sparkline of the last eight weeks: «Estudio» (the minutes), «Ejercicios» (passed, behind `EXERCISES_ENABLED`), «Jugar» (the level, behind `PLAY_ENABLED`). The period and the tab are both in the address (`?periodo=`, `?seccion=`), so a view can be shared and the back button undoes it; the tabs are therefore a `nav` of links with `aria-current="page"`, not a tab list.

- **Estudio**: the hero is the minutes of the period in [`PlatformPillColumns`](/components/platform/shared/platform-pill-columns) — a column a day for a week or a month (the 1st and every fifth day named in a month), a month or a year each beyond —, today hatched, the daily goal dashed across; beside it the total, the change against the period before at the same point («+12 % que septiembre a estas alturas») and its legend (today, the goal, the days that reached it). Under it the streak's sixteen weeks, the period by topic as a donut (the root of each topic's tree, four and «Otros»), and «Lo que hiciste»: lessons, exercises, classes and games analysed, the large figure this period and the whole period before under it, the one that is largest in the primary.
- **Ejercicios**: passed per week, the last eight, beside the period's figure and the share of its attempts solved; how the attempts went (resolved, with a hint, failed, «Ver la solución»), the four topics most tried (solved of tried) and the reviews due today.
- **Jugar**: the level against the bots after each of the last games with the slider that picks one, and the period's record as a donut (the open game an outline) with the share of wins.

Every card is a `StatsCard` — the drawing in a ring, the title and what it is of, the round arrow to see more, the chart, and one line saying what it reads in the student's words. A card's `children` is its chart: it is a container whose one job is to place it.

## Markup & styling

The cards are on `--platform-m3-container` with `--md-sys-shape-corner-xl` corners, and set `--chart-ground` to that grey so the goal's name over the pill columns hides the dashes. The tabs are as tall as their figure (68px), the current one on the container with the primary's underline. Under 1280px the sparklines leave the tabs and the row of cards goes to two columns; under 768px the hero's figure goes above its chart and the cards to one column; under 640px the tabs drop their drawing. Under 1024px the side column goes above the panel, as on every workspace.
