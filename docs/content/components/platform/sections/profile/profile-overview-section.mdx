## Purpose

What N5 puts under the tabs: three cards in a row (one column under 1024px), then «Partidas N» with the last eight games (`PROFILE_RECENT_GAMES`) as L2 cards, four to a row and two under 1024px, and «Ver todas» to `/perfil/<slug>/partidas` when there are more. With no games, an empty state that offers «Jugar» to the owner. Everything comes in the `ProfileView`; the section reads nothing.
