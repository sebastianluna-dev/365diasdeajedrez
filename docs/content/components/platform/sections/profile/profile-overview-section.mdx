## Purpose

The owner's «Perfil E2 · Ficha fija y el perfil como historia: estudio, racha, juego» (2026-10-25), the profile's first page in the new design system and the first page with charts. The page bleeds onto the canvas (`platform-page_width_full` + `platform-page_frame_bleed`) and the section draws the workspace:

- **The side column**: «Jugar» as the extended FAB; the group «Perfil» with «Visión general» (current) and «Partidas» with its count; and «Lo tuyo» — «Estudios» and «Cursos» with their counts, «Actividad» (`/inicio`). On one's own profile «Perfil» also has «Estadísticas» (`/perfil/estadisticas`, 2026-10-25), and the sheet ends with «Ver estadísticas», a tonal button to the same page ([`ProfileStatisticsSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-statistics-section)).
- **The sheet** (`ProfileSheet`, 296px, stuck under the bar while the story scrolls; a block on top of the story under 1024px, its three figures side by side): the portrait at 72px with the flag in its corner, the name (the page's `h1`) and the badge, a pen to «Ajustes» («Editar perfil»), the status line — or «Escribe una línea de estado» on one's own empty profile —, «Se unió el…», three tiles with the courses, studies and games, and three [`PlatformBigNumber`](/components/platform/shared/platform-big-number)s between hairlines: the streak, the level against bots with its sparkline (or a dash and what is missing), and the share of finished games won.
- **The story**, four chapters, each with its eyebrow in the primary, a sentence that reads its chart, a lead, the chart on a card (`--md-sys-shape-corner-xl`, the container, or the panel's blue wash for the minutes) and a line under it that says what the chart counts:
  1. «Estudio · octubre»: this month's minutes of lessons against the last month's, running totals day by day ([`PlatformBandChart`](/components/platform/shared/platform-band-chart)), with the total so far and where last month was by the same day beside it. Games never count here.
  2. «Estudio · racha»: the sixteen weeks ([`PlatformStreakCalendar`](/components/platform/shared/platform-streak-calendar)), with this week's days and minutes and the legend beside it. «Jugar no suma a la racha».
  3. «Jugando»: the level after each of the last ten finished games ([`PlatformLineChart`](/components/platform/shared/platform-line-chart), with its slider), then «Contra cada bot» — the games against each bot, `wins–losses–draws` inside each column, the bot with a game still open highlighted ([`PlatformCompareBars`](/components/platform/shared/platform-compare-bars)). Either is left out when there is nothing to draw.
  4. «Jugando · lo último»: the last five games as rows — the final position from the person's side, the result (`G`, `P`, `T`, «En curso», «Anulada» in Material's success, error and neutral containers), the rival, their rating, how it ended and the moves, and when — and «Ver las N» to «Partidas».

Under 1280px a chart's card stacks its figure over the chart. The round arrows at a chapter's right lead to where its data comes from («Ver tu actividad», «Ver todos los bots»).

**On somebody else's profile** (a teacher's view of their student, the staff's; who may see it is `profileVisibleWhere`): every sentence is in the third person, and «Jugar», «Lo tuyo», the pen, the arrows to one's own pages and the counts' links other than «Partidas» are not drawn.

**The sentences** are `profile-words.ts`, pure and tested (`profile-words.test.ts`): `studyWords` («Octubre ya rebasó a todo septiembre», «… va por delante de / por detrás de …», «… todavía no tiene minutos de lecciones»), `streakWords` («12 días seguidos, y hoy ya cuenta», «Sin racha por ahora»), `playWords` («Tu nivel contra los bots subió a ≈ 850», «… aparece a las 5 partidas»), each with the chart's `aria-label` stating its data, and `shortDate` («13 oct», in the study day's calendar).
