## Purpose

`levelSeries` gives the level after each finished game, each over the ten up to it; this draws it as a line over its wash in the selection's blue, scaled to a 220 × 48 box with a little headroom. No chart library. It is a `role="img"` with a `<title>` and an `aria-label` that say it in words: «De ≈ 775 a ≈ 925 en 12 partidas». Fewer than two points draw nothing.
