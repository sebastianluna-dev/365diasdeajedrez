## Purpose

The level is `botLevelOf` (`services/play/play-rules.ts`): the average of the bots' labels plus 400 per net win, over the last ten finished games, rounded to 25. It is computed when read and never stored, and it is never a rating: the card says «Aproximado, sobre tus últimas 10 partidas» («sus» on somebody else's profile). Under five finished games it shows no number, only how many are missing.
