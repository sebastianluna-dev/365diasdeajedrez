## Purpose

N5, after the reference the owner sent (Chess.com's profile): a 120px square portrait; the name large, with the flag of `User.country` and the badge «Alumno» or «Profesor»; the status line (`User.statusLine`) — or, on one's own empty profile, «Escribe una línea de estado», a link to «Ajustes» —; «Se unió el 30 de agosto, 2026 · 4 cursos · 3 estudios · 4 partidas · Nivel contra bots ≈ 850» on one line; and, only for the person themselves, «Editar perfil» (to «Ajustes») and «Jugar».

**Tabs** (planning decision §K): «Visión general» and «Partidas» are the two pages of the profile, the open one with `aria-current="page"`; «Estudios», «Cursos» and «Actividad» are links to `/estudios`, `/cursos` and `/inicio`, drawn only for the owner — a teacher or the staff reading the profile see the two pages.
