## Purpose

The "Partidas" card. The list is capped by the service and the total is not: the explorer's toolbar says how many games went through the position, and when fewer are listed the heading says of how many these are the most recent («Las 20 más recientes de 88»).

Each game carries its **origin** (a student's study, a course, the teaching staff) because a continuation played by twenty students does not mean the same as seeing it in twenty master games from the course material. Games seen in one of the student's classes are also marked.

## Behaviour

None beyond rendering. In the new design system (2026-10-23) it is Material's outlined card (`mdCard("outlined")`, corners `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`), and each game is a row as Gmail lists mail, over a `--platform-m3-divider` hairline: the players with their Elos (`—` between them, cut with an ellipsis), under them the event and the date, the origin's tag and «Vista en clase», and at the right the result in a pill of the container (`1/2-1/2` written `½-½`). Under the pointer a row that opens lifts over its neighbours as the chapter's lessons do: `--platform-m3-row-edge` at its sides and a shadow of `--platform-m3-row-shadow`, its colour unchanged.

**The tags' colours**, all `--platform-m3-*` pairs `TEXT_PAIRS` holds: a student's study in the success green (`success-container`), a course in the secondary container, the teaching staff in amber (`warning-container`), and «Vista en clase», which is a mark over the origin and not one, in a violet of its own (`--platform-m3-class-container` and `--platform-m3-on-class-container`, added 2026-10-23: Material's own purple is never drawn).

A game without `href` (no screen the viewer could open it in) renders as a static row rather than a link to a 404, in the lighter ink (`--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`) and without the lift. An Elo is shown whenever the game has one (`!== undefined`), not whenever it is truthy: with `&&`, an Elo of 0 would have printed a bare "0" outside its span.
