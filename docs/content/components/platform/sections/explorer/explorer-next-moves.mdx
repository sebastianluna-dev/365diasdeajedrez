## Purpose

The "Continuaciones" card: what was played from this position and how often. Each row is a button that plays the move, which is the natural way to walk the tree of the games in the database. The percentages are over the total of continuations, so they add up to 100, and are written as Spanish writes them, with `formatSpanishPercent`: "69,5 %", the sign after a non-breaking space, not "69.5%".

## Props

| Prop          | Type                    | Required | Meaning                                                                               |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `moves`       | `ExplorerNextMove[]`    | yes      | `{ uci, san, count, percentage }[]`, ordered by the service.                          |
| `onPlay`      | `(san: string) => void` | yes      | Plays the row's move.                                                                 |
| `isSearching` | `boolean`               | yes      | Chooses "Buscando…" over "Ninguna partida siguió desde aquí." when the list is empty. |

## Behaviour

None beyond rendering. The frequency bar is an `aria-hidden` span whose width is the percentage, placed behind the text so it reads at a glance without depending on the number.

In the new design system (2026-10-23) it is Material's outlined card (`mdCard("outlined")`, its corners `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg` through the card's own `--container-shape`), and each row is a grid of three columns — the move (72px), the count, the share (64px, at the right) — on a `--md-sys-shape-corner-md` button that takes the container's grey under the pointer.
