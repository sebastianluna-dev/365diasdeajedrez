## Purpose

The card that answers "where was I?". The service picks the most recently touched course and its return lesson (the one marked in progress by [`LessonTracker`](/components/platform/sections/courses/lesson-view/lesson-tracker)); the card shows the course name, "Te quedaste en: …", the lessons-completed bar and a "Continuar" button straight to that lesson. Without any course in progress it shows an empty state linking to "Mis cursos".

## Props

This component takes no props.

## Behaviour

Awaits `getUserDashboard()` and branches on `continueStudying` being present. The progress bar is inline with "n de m lecciones" as detail. No client code.

## Markup & styling

Block `.continue-studying` (26 lines), rendered inside a `.platform-card`.

**Elements:** `.continue-studying__body`, `.continue-studying__course`, `.continue-studying__lesson`, `.continue-studying__cta`

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-card`, `.platform-card__title`, `.platform-button`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`

No media queries; the page's grid places it.
