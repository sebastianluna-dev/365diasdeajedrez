## Purpose

The student's home page as the owner drew it on 2026-10-23 («Inicio: un solo foco», with «CabeceraInicio» for the bar), moved whole to the new design system. The page bleeds onto the canvas on `.platform-workspace` with **no side column** (`platform-workspace_side_none`: as much canvas at the panel's left as at its right), and the bar names it «Inicio» with the academy's mark ([`PlatformPageName`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-page-name)). In the panel, one column of at most 760px:

- **The greeting**: «¿Qué vamos a estudiar hoy, Andrea?» (the first word of the display name), in the display face, and «Lo más importante primero; lo demás, debajo.»
- **«Lo siguiente»** (`HomeFocus`), a block of the primary in its «on» colour, `--md-sys-shape-corner-xl`. What it holds is `homePlanOf`'s choice, in this order: **today's class** — entering it is what there is to do —, else **the course in progress**, else **the next class** on whatever day, else **choosing what to study**.
  - Today's class: «Lo siguiente · tu clase empieza en 8 minutos» (`HomeClassLead`: «es hoy a las 18:00» more than an hour ahead, «ya empezó», «terminó»), its topic, «Hoy a las 18:00 · con Laura Méndez · grupal, con Ana, Diego y Mateo», and «Entrar a la clase» (Material's filled button, white on the primary) with «Ver la clase» while the link is open, «Ver la clase» alone before; without a link yet, «Administración agregará el enlace antes de la clase.». In its last hour the ring at the right (`HomeClassRing`) empties with the minutes left, which it shows in large figures (`role="img"`, «Faltan 8 minutos»). The lead asks for the page again at each instant the class changes phase (`useRefreshAtInstants`), which is how «Entrar a la clase» appears without the link travelling ahead of time.
  - Another day's class: «Lo siguiente · tu próxima clase», its day and hour, «Ver la clase».
  - The course: «Lo siguiente · tu curso», its name, «Te quedaste en: … · 4 de 10 lecciones», «Continuar» and «Ver mis cursos».
  - Nothing: «Elige qué estudiar», «Ver el catálogo» and «Ver mis cursos».
    Material's buttons are drawn inverted inside the block by pointing the roles they read (`--md-sys-color-primary`, `-on-primary`, `-secondary`) at the block's own colours, so the filled one is white with the primary's ink and the text one and the focus ring are white.
- **«Después»** (`HomeLater`): what is not the focus, as tiles side by side on the container's grey, each the whole of it a link with an icon on its own tone — «Continuar el curso» (the success container), «Tu próxima clase» (the secondary container) and «Ejercicios de tu clase · 1 de 3 hechos» (the warning container, leading to the first exercise not done). Nothing to show, no group.
- **The day** (`HomeDay`, «Tu día de estudio»), at the foot of the first screen over a hairline: the flame and «12 días de racha» («Empieza tu racha hoy» at zero), «Hoy 12 de 20 min» with its bar, and «Ver tus estadísticas», which goes down the page (`#estadisticas`). The first screen (`.home__today`) is at least as tall as what the window shows of the panel, so the day sits at its foot and what follows starts under the fold.
- **Under it**, «lo demás»: «Tus estadísticas» ([`StatsCards`](/components/platform/sections/dashboard/activity-stats/stats-cards)) and «Actividad reciente» ([`ActivityList`](/components/platform/shared/activity-list), or a line when there is none yet).

While it loads, `HomeSkeleton` draws the same panel in the containers' greys (markup and CSS only, as a `loading.tsx` needs).

**From the design, left out or adapted** (2026-10-23): the daily goal is changed in «Ajustes» only — the canvas has no control for it, and the former «Cambiar objetivo» of the dashboard went with the old page —; «hechos» counts the exercises of the class's blocks that the student has a mark on, solved or failed, in that block (`getClassExercisesToDo`: the next class ahead or, failing it, the last one gone, whichever still has some undone); the canvas's classmates' first names are the names the class gives; the profile's email and «En la academia desde» of the old greeting are gone, and so is the old «Te quedan 3 clases» under the next class (it is in «Mis clases»).

## Props

| Prop             | Type                         | Required | Meaning                                                                      |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `dashboard`      | `DashboardData`              | yes      | The profile, the course in progress, the statistics and the recent activity. |
| `nextClass`      | `ClassCardData \| null`      | yes      | The first class ahead that is not cancelled.                                 |
| `classExercises` | `ClassExercisesToDo \| null` | yes      | A class's exercises still undone, or null.                                   |
| `goal`           | `StudyGoal`                  | yes      | The streak and today's minutes against the goal.                             |

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-workspace__panel`, Material's buttons (`mdButton`), `.platform-md-icon`.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` — the first screen no longer fills the window. `(width < 768px)` — the tiles one under the other, the day's bar on a row of its own. `(width < 640px)` — the ring above the focus's text and smaller, the titles a step down, the column without its extra padding.

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-*` (primary and its «on» colour, panel, containers, divider, on-surface and its variant, success, secondary, warning and info containers), `--platform-flame`, `--platform-flame-core`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-md`/`-lg`/`-xl`, `--platform-font-display`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/inicio/page.tsx`:

```tsx
const [dashboard, nextClass, classExercises, goal] = await Promise.all([
  getUserDashboard(),
  getNextClass(),
  getClassExercisesToDo(),
  getStudyGoal(),
]);

<div className="platform-page platform-page_width_full platform-page_frame_bleed">
  <div className="platform-workspace platform-workspace_side_none">
    <HomeSection dashboard={dashboard} nextClass={nextClass} classExercises={classExercises} goal={goal} />
  </div>
</div>;
```
