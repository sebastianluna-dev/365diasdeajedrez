## Behaviour

«Quitar» asks first: «¿Quitar a Lucía Herrera de la clase?», «Sale de «Taller de finales de torre» del sáb 17 oct · 10:00–11:30.», with what the seat was («Su clase vuelve a su pago del jue 1 oct.», «No tenía pago.», «Tenía la clase gratis.»); only a seat with no mark and never moved goes. A moved seat is history: its name in the muted grey, with no word at its end.

## Markup & styling

Block `.class-seat-row` (36 lines): the row and its words at the end, a hairline between rows; `_state_moved` mutes the name; under 640px the words go under the row.

**Elements:** `.class-seat-row__actions`

**Modifiers:** `.class-seat-row_state_moved`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-track`

## Usage

```tsx
<ClassSeatRow
  seat={seat}
  classDetail={classDetail}
  teachers={teachers}
  groupClasses={groupClasses}
  staffName={staffName}
  actions={seatActions}
/>
```
