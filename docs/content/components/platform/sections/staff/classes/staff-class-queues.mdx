## Purpose

Three cards: «Por programar · 1 clase pagada sin fecha» (one row per payment with classes left, «Alumno Demo — 1 de su pago del jue 1 oct», «Programar»); «Necesitan atención · 3», in groups — «Por finalizar:» (warning from a day after the end), «Sin enlace:» (the next 48 hours), «Sin pago:» («Lucía Herrera en Taller de finales de torre», «Registrar» with the class ticked), «Profesor desactivado:» —; «Por renovar · Les queda 1 clase o ninguna» («Valentina Cruz — le queda 1 clase (hoy 19:00)», «Registrar»). Each row's word opens its popup in place.

## Behaviour

Every card is a `<details open>`: it folds, on any width, and opens by default (the plan asked for `<details>` only under 640px; a summary that is a heading on every width is one piece instead of two).

## Markup & styling

Block `.staff-class-queues` (111 lines): the cards, the summary as a heading with its line, the groups with their small caps label, the rows with a hairline between them and the word at the end; a «Por finalizar» older than a day in the warning ink.
