## Purpose

One page for a class in every phase: ahead of time it is where students are added and taken out and the class is moved; finished, it is where a mark is corrected with a reason and a no-show is given another date. The head's actions are [`ClassHeaderActions`](/components/platform/sections/staff/classes/class-header-actions), drawn by the page.

## Props

| Prop                       | Type               | Required | Meaning                                       |

| `teachers`, `groupClasses` |                    | yes      | What the popups need.                         |
| `staffName`                | `string`           | yes      | Who is acting, for «por Staff Demo» in ADM-9. |
| `notice`, `errorMessage`   | `string`           | no       | What the address said, in words.              |

## Behaviour

- A cancelled class opens with who cancelled it, when, the reason and the message the students read; a rescheduled one with where it came from.
- **Alumnos**: «4 de 6 plazas · 1 sin pago» (or «Asistencia: 0 de 1» once finished); a `ClassSeatRow` per seat, the moved ones last and faded; after a no-show the note «Una falta gasta la clase pagada. …».
- **Después de la clase** (finished): «Finalizada por GM Profesor Demo el mié 14 oct a las 20:12», the summary and the recording, or «Sin resumen» and «Sin grabación».
- **Contenido**: «Lo prepara GM Profesor Demo. Lo ves como lo verán los alumnos.», the blocks by visibility, the teacher's script folded.
- **The lane**: Horario (with «Reprogramar» while the class has not started), Profesor (with «Cambiar» while it has not finished; «Desactivado» as a tag), Reunión (the platform, the link, «Los alumnos lo ven desde las 9:30.», with «Cambiar» or «Agregar»), Historial.
