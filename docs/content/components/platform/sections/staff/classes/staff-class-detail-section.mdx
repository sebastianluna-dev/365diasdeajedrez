## Purpose

Staff sees the whole class, including what the student does not (payments) and what the teacher composed (the blocks, through the same renderer). It may do exactly two things: set the recording URL, and cancel the class with a note that is stamped into the summary as a support action. Content, attendance and data belong to the teacher, and the card says so.

## Props

| Prop          | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------ | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `classDetail` | `StaffClassDetail` | yes      | Facts, `summary?`, `recordingUrl?`, `participants[]` with payment fields, `blocks`, `transcript?`. |
| `errorCode`   | `string`           | no       | `?error=` translated with `STAFF_ERROR_MESSAGES`.                                                  |

## Behaviour

Five cards: facts (teacher, when, status, provider) with the summary; support with the two forms (the cancel form only while the class is not already cancelled and its note is required); participants in a [`PlatformTable`](/components/platform/shared/platform-table) with attended, paid date, amount and reference; content or an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state); transcript status and text.
