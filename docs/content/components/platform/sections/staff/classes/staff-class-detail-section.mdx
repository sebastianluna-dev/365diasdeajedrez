## Purpose

Staff sees the whole class, including what the student does not (payments) and what the teacher composed (the blocks, through the same renderer). It may do exactly two things: set the recording URL, and cancel the class with a note that is stamped into the summary as a support action. Content, attendance and data belong to the teacher, and the card says so.

## Props

| Prop          | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------ | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `classDetail` | `StaffClassDetail` | yes      | Facts, `summary?`, `recordingUrl?`, `participants[]` with payment fields, `blocks`, `transcript?`. |
| `errorCode`   | `string`           | no       | `?error=` translated with `STAFF_ERROR_MESSAGES`.                                                  |

## Behaviour

Five cards: facts (teacher, when, status, provider) with the summary; support with the two forms, each a [`PlatformForm`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form) with one `PlatformFormField` and its button (the recording URL may be left empty and says «Opcional»; the cancel form shows only while the class is not already cancelled, and its note is required: sent empty, the field shows «Este campo es obligatorio.» instead of submitting); participants in a [`PlatformTable`](/components/platform/shared/platform-table) with attended («Sí», «No» or «Sin pasar lista») and one «Plaza» column (`seatLabel`: «Pago del jue 1 oct · $1,400.00 MXN · Transferencia 8841», «Gratis» or «Sin pago»); content or an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state); transcript status and text.

## Markup & styling

Block `.staff-class` (61 lines). Each support form is `PlatformForm`, a column of its own, so `.staff-class__support` says `flex-direction: row`: the field grows from 320px and its button sits at its foot.

**Elements:** `.staff-class__facts`, `.staff-class__fact`, `.staff-class__fact-label`, `.staff-class__fact-value`, `.staff-class__summary`, `.staff-class__note`, `.staff-class__support`

**Other selectors it styles:** `.platform-form__field` (`PlatformFormField`'s root).

**Tokens:** `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`

No media queries.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/administracion/clases/[classId]/page.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StaffClassDetailSection } from "@/components/platform/sections/staff/classes/staff-class-detail.section";

<StaffClassDetailSection classDetail={classDetail} errorCode={error} />;
```
