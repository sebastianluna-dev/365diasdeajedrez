## Purpose

The one screen that makes a class. Five sections — Formato, Tema, Cuándo, Profesor y reunión, Alumnos — and the summary, which is inside the `PlatformForm` with the `SubmitButton` (§2.4 of the plan: `SubmitButton` reads `useFormStatus`, which sees only its ancestor form), sticky beside the fields and, under 640px, a strip stuck to the foot of the form with the seats line and the button (D20).

## Props

| Prop                 | Type                                          | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `defaults`           | `NewClassDefaults`                            | yes      | Format, capacity, title, description, teacher, day, hour, duration, link, the student `?alumno=` named, and the «Copia de …» line. |
| `teachers`           | `TeacherOption[]`                             | yes      | The active teachers.                                                                                                               |
| `students`           | `SchedulableStudent[]`                        | yes      | Every student with their classes to schedule and the payment a seat would spend.                                                   |
| `timeZone`, `nowIso` | `string`                                      | yes      |                                                                                                                                    |
| `createClass`        | `(prev, formData) => Promise<PopupFormState>` | yes      | Redirects to the record on success; returns the errors by field otherwise.                                                         |
| `loadTeacherBusy`    | `(teacherId) => Promise<BusySlotView[]>`      | yes      | For the overlap warnings.                                                                                                          |

## Behaviour

- **Format** changes what is required: a group class needs a title and a capacity (2 to 12, `Plazas`); an individual one a student from a select, with «Entra» under it.
- **Students** (group): cards with «Usará una clase de su pago del jue 1 oct · 2 por programar», «Sin clases por programar» or «Sin clases pagadas»; from eight students a search box hides the cards that do not match without unmounting them, so a ticked one still travels. Under the cards, one row «Entra Alumno Demo» per ticked student (D6): «Con su pago» (only with classes left; the oldest payment, D13), «Con el pago pendiente», «Gratis», with the help «Queda en «Sin pago» hasta que registres su pago. «Gratis» no se cobra ni sale en los pendientes.». A student with another teacher, or none, is said under their row, never refused.
- **The counter** «4 de 6 plazas» is `aria-live`; the summary says «4 de 6 plazas · 3 con pago · 1 sin pago» (and «· 1 gratis») and lists «Alumno Demo — pago del jue 1 oct», «Lucía Herrera — sin pago».
- **The form is submitted by hand** (`onSubmit` → `startTransition(() => formAction(new FormData(form)))`, `pending` handed to the button): a `<form action>` is reset by React when the action settles, and the reset reaches Radix's controlled cards and selects, which answer it by changing their value — a ticked student would go.
- **Errors** come back by field with what was typed (`values`), the cards and selects included; «Elegiste 3 alumnos y la clase tiene 2 plazas.» sits on the students group.
