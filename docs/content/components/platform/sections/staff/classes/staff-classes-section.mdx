## Purpose

Where the staff lives: today's classes and the week's, what needs attention, and the way to every other screen of the module. The page composes the head («Hoy es miércoles 14 de octubre · 3 clases hoy y 5 esta semana», «Crear clase», «Registrar pago»); this section draws the rest.

## Props

| Prop                       | Type                                        | Required | Meaning                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `agenda`                   | `StaffAgendaView`                           | yes      | The filters, the days, the foot, the past page and the cancelled rows.       |
| `queues`                   | `StaffQueuesView`                           | yes      | What the queues list.                                                        |
| `teacherFilter`            | `{ id, displayName }[]`                     | yes      | Every teacher, for the filter (a deactivated one still has classes to list). |
| `teachers`, `groupClasses` | `TeacherOption[]`, `OpenGroupClassOption[]` | yes      | What the popups that date a class need.                                      |
| `notice`, `errorMessage`   | `string`                                    | no       | What the address said, already in words.                                     |

## Behaviour

- **«Próximas»** (the default): `ClassAgenda` with the rows by day until `hasta` (six days from today by default) and the foot «Hasta el martes 20 de octubre · Ver la semana siguiente», which adds a week to `hasta`. A row without a link offers «Agregar», which opens the meeting-link popup in place.
- **«Pasadas»**: a `PlatformTable` (date, class, teacher, phase, students, attended) fifty at a time with «Mostrando 1–2 de 2» and «Anteriores»/«Siguientes»; under 640px the same rows as `ClassRow` cards (§2.4 of the plan: the table is not touched).
- **«Canceladas»**: `ClassRow` cards, newest first.
- The filters are the address (`?vista=&profesor=&q=&hasta=&pagina=`): the pills and `AgendaFilters` push it, so a filter can be shared and the back button undoes it.
