## Purpose

The record of a student is two pages since 2026-10-15 («Cuenta», «Pagos y clases»), and this is what both share: the layout draws it, so the head does not blink between them. «Crear clase» joins the actions in phase 5 of the classes rebuild.

## Props

| Prop           | Type                     | Required | Meaning                            |
| -------------- | ------------------------ | -------- | ---------------------------------- |
| `student`      | `StaffStudentDetail`     | yes      | Read once by the layout (`cache`). |
| `teachers`     | `TeacherOption[]`        | yes      | For the payment popup.             |
| `groupClasses` | `OpenGroupClassOption[]` | yes      | For the payment popup.             |

## Markup & styling

Block `.student-record-head` (4 lines): the page's head with its actions ([page actions](/ui/styling#global-helpers)), the tabs under it.
