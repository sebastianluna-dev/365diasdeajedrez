## Purpose

What a payment bought and where it went, seat by seat, in the order the classes happen: a no-show counts, a cancelled class gave the seat back, a moved seat is history and the seat it went to says so. The card receives data and the actions as functions; it mounts no component it is given.

| `teachers`, `groupClasses`, `rhythm`, `timeZone`, `nowIso` |                                                                                                        | yes      | What the «Programar» and «Editar pago» popups need. |
| `actions`                                                  | `{ scheduleSeat, deletePayment, updatePayment, registerPayment, loadPaymentContext, loadTeacherBusy }` | yes      | The server actions; stubs in Storybook.             |

## Behaviour

- Each row: the ordinal in a disc (none for a moved seat), when, the class, its phase and its mark («Asistió», «Faltó», «Faltó · reprogramada al …»), «Grupal».
- A row «Por programar» per class still to date, each with a [`ScheduleSeatPopup`](/components/platform/shared/schedule-seat-popup).
- `allTaken` folds the card in a `<details>` whose summary says «Todas tomadas · Ver sus 4 clases».
- The «…»: «Editar pago» opens the register popup in edit mode; «Eliminar pago» asks with the confirmation (what stays «Sin pago» among its consequences) and is greyed with its reason as a second line once a class of the payment was given (D19).
