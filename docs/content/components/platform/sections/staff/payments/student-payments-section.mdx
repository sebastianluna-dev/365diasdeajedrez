## Purpose

Where the staff sees what a student paid and what is left: «1 por programar · 2 programadas · 5 tomadas», «Le quedan 3 clases: 2 programadas y 1 por programar. Las clases pagadas no caducan.», the payments newest first, and the classes «Sin pago» with «Registrar» at the end of each row, which opens the payment popup with that class ticked.
