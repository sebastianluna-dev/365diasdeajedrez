## Props

| Prop             | Type                                                      | Required | Meaning                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `seriesId`       | `string`                                                  | yes      | Passed to the action.                                                                        |
| `published`      | `boolean`                                                 | yes      | As stored: `publishedAt` set.                                                                |
| `publishedLabel` | `string \| null`                                          | yes      | «Publicada el 14 de octubre, 2026», the hint while on; null while off.                       |
| `itemCount`      | `number`                                                  | yes      | How many exercises it holds: with none, the switch is disabled and the hint says to add one. |
| `action`         | `(seriesId: string, published: boolean) => Promise<void>` | yes      | `setAcademySeriesPublished`, as a function; a stub in Storybook.                             |

## Behaviour

A switch, not a form's field: it takes effect on the press (`useOptimistic` shows the new state at once, `useTransition` runs the action, and the page's revalidation confirms it). On: «Todos los alumnos la ven en «Series de la academia».» Off: «Apagada: sólo la ve el equipo.» The action keeps `publishedAt` at the first publication's date while it stays on and clears it when switched off; the students' home and series page revalidate with it.
