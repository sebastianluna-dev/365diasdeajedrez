## Behaviour

The editor opens on «Probar» (`initialTab="try"`): a proposal is solved before it is decided. A teacher's exercise is locked (`contentLocked: "staffReview"`, no save bar): its content is the author's (D07), and once in the library the teacher duplicates it to change it (D48). The decisions are the bar's.

## Markup & styling

Block `.staff-exercise-editor` (6 lines): a column of the editor and the bar.
