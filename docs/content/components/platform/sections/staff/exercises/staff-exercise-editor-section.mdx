## Behaviour

What the staff may do is `staffExercisePermissions`/`staffSaveButtons` (`services/staff-exercises/staff-exercises.mapper.ts`): the academy's exercise is theirs to write — a new one or a draft is kept or published («Guardar y publicar», no review: the staff is the reviewer), a published one is saved and stays complete, an archived one is saved as it is; a teacher's exercise in the library is edited by the staff too (D48: its teacher duplicates it to change it); a teacher's proposal, or a returned one, is locked (`contentLocked: "staffReview"`, no save bar) and opens on «Probar», so it is solved before it is decided. The decisions are the bar's. «Desde una partida» offers the staff member's own studies, and «Crear ejercicio desde aquí» on one of their games (D11) opens `/administracion/ejercicios/nuevo?estudio=&partida=&jugada=` seeded on «Solución».
