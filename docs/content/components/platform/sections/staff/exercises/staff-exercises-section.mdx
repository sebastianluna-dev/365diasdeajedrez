## Behaviour

«Biblioteca · n» and «En revisión · n» are links (two pages, not a query). The library lists every PUBLISHED exercise of a bank (`buildLibraryExercisesWhere`), the last published first, each card naming its author («Academia» or the teacher); the queue lists the teachers' proposals, the oldest first, each under «Propuesto el … por …». The cards carry no row words: a decision is taken on the exercise's page, which the title opens.
