## Behaviour

The teacher's series editor with the academy's wording and without the share panel (PLAN.md §8, D32): the pieces are the teacher's, handed the academy's actions. An academy series holds only the library's exercises (`buildPickableExercisesWhere` with no teacher), so «Agregar ejercicios» offers the library minus what is in it, and an item taken out of the library since says «Ya no está en la biblioteca: los alumnos ya no lo ven». The side holds the «Publicada» switch — on, every student sees the series on «Series de la academia» — and «Eliminar la serie».
