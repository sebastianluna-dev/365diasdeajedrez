## Behaviour

- The facts: the status tag, the author («Academia» or the teacher), «Propuesto el 14 de octubre, 2026» and the last decision («Devuelto el … por …»); the note of that decision under them.
- On a proposal: «Devolver» (secondary) opens a `PlatformPopup` with the note the teacher reads — required, up to `EXERCISE_LIMITS.reviewNote` characters — and «Devolver al profesor» (`_variant_ink`); a refusal keeps what was written. «Aprobar» is the page's accent, a plain form.
- On one in the library: «Quitar de la biblioteca» (`_variant_caution`: it takes a part away and leaves the rest) opens a `PlatformConfirmPopup` with the verb «Quitar», what happens by owner (a teacher's goes back to «Listo» for its teacher; the academy's is archived) and the note to write.
- A returned exercise offers nothing: it is proposed again by its teacher. An archived academy exercise offers «Restaurar» (ARCHIVED → DRAFT), to publish again once it is complete.
