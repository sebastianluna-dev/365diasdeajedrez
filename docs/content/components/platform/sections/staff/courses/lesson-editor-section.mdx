## Purpose

Content, metadata and exercises are different decisions, so they are different cards and saving one does not drag the others along. The content card goes **first**, not in alphabetical order: the lesson's content is a game of its chapter's collection, chosen through [`LessonGamePicker`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/lesson-game-picker); the rest are settings on top of it.

The "Opciones" controls in the aside (priority and trainable, each a [`PlatformCheckbox`](/components/platform/shared/platform-checkbox) with its hint; training colour) are not inside any `<form>`: the `form` attribute ties them to the metadata form in the other column. Two forms against the same action would trample each other's fields, because `updateLesson` reads all of them. A `PlatformFormField` has to be inside its `PlatformForm`, so the training colour's select is named by a plain `<label htmlFor>` pointing at its trigger (`lesson-training-color`), drawn as a field's label is (`.lesson-editor__label`).

## Behaviour

- **A lesson with its own PGN and no game** — one that was unlinked, or born before the collections — says so in the «Partida de la lección» panel and offers «Archivar el PGN en la colección»: `fileLessonPgnAsGame` creates it as a game of the chapter's collection with the lesson's name, links it and indexes its positions, all in one transaction, which is the per-lesson version of what `npm run collections:by-chapter` did in bulk. Until this, that content could be read by the student and edited by nobody (2026-09-30). The PGN does not change, so `pgnUpdatedAt` is not stamped and the exercises frozen from it stay fresh.
- Metadata form, a [`PlatformForm`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form) with `id="lesson-metadata"`: name (required: an empty one is answered under it), description, board orientation, estimated duration — **the only place minutes are typed**, and what its chapter's duration adds up (`syncChapterDuration`) — , topics as [`PlatformCheckCards`](/components/platform/shared/platform-check-cards), the first level first and each parent's topics under its heading (`groups`, from `topicCardGroups` in `services/shared/topic-groups.ts`: «Táctica» over its nineteen motifs), in a [`PlatformFormGroup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form) («Temas», `optional`), `topicCodes` one hidden checkbox per chosen card. The starting position is not typed: it comes from the linked game's `[FEN]` header.
- Aside: the three option controls with `form="lesson-metadata"`, a status card with PGN date, exercise count and duration, and the delete form only when `canDelete` (draft course, no progress: once someone has studied it their history rules and the course is archived, not dismantled).
- Exercises: [`ExerciseEditor`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/exercise-editor) with the lesson's PGN for its board picker.

## Markup & styling

Block `.lesson-editor` (109 lines). The metadata form is `.platform-form` alone: its column and gap are the form's own, and `.lesson-editor__stack` gives the aside's options the same.

**Elements:** `.lesson-editor__select`, `.lesson-editor__label`, `.lesson-editor__adopt`, `.lesson-editor__adopt-note`, `.lesson-editor__stack`, `.lesson-editor__note`, `.lesson-editor__facts`, `.lesson-editor__fact`, `.lesson-editor__danger`, `.lesson-editor__delete`, `.lesson-editor__pair`, `.lesson-editor__submit`

No modifiers.

**Tokens:** `--platform-border`, `--platform-danger`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 768px)` collapses the pair.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/administracion/cursos/[courseId]/capitulos/[chapterId]/lecciones/[lessonId]/page.tsx`:

```tsx
import { LessonEditorSection } from "@/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/lesson-editor.section";

<LessonEditorSection
  lesson={lesson}
  exerciseModes={exerciseModes}
  topics={topics}
  courseGames={courseGames}
  gameQuery={partida}
  errorCode={error}
/>;
```
