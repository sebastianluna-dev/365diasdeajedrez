## Purpose

The same shape as the course editor one level down: metadata on the left with the lesson list under it, a summary and a back link on the right, and the chapter's game collection in its own tab because it is a different job.

The name of the record and its tabs are not here: they are the [layout](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-record-head) the three pages share.


## Props

| Prop        | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | -------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapter`   | `ChapterAdminDetail` | yes      | `id`, `courseId`, `courseName`, `courseStatusCode`, `order`, `name`, `description?`, `isPriority`, `estimatedDuration?`, `lessons[]` (with `hasPgn`, `exerciseCount`, `roleCode`, `isPriority`, `progressCount`). |
| `errorCode` | `string`             | no       | `?error=` translated with `STAFF_ERROR_MESSAGES`.                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Behaviour

- Metadata form: name, description, estimated duration and «Capítulo de prioridad», a checkbox that marks the whole chapter (`Chapter.isPriority`): the student sees «Prioridad» on its card, and every lesson of it passes their «Prioridad» filter, marked or not. The head shows the «Prioridad» tag of a chapter that is.
- Lessons: not here. They are [a tab of their own](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-lessons-section), «Lecciones», because the list is rows and rows want the whole width (2026-09-30).
- The aside counts lessons, lessons with PGN, exercises and duration.
