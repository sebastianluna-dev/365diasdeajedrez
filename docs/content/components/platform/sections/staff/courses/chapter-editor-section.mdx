## Purpose

The same shape as the course editor one level down: metadata on the left with the lesson list under it, a summary and a back link on the right, and the chapter's game collection in its own tab because it is a different job.

The name of the record and its tabs are not here: they are the [layout](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-record-head) the three pages share.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------- | -------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapter`   | `ChapterAdminDetail` | yes      | `id`, `courseId`, `courseName`, `courseStatusCode`, `order`, `name`, `description?`, `isPriority`, `estimatedDuration?` (the cached sum of its lessons' minutes), `lessons[]` (with `hasPgn`, `exerciseCount`, `roleCode`, `isPriority`, `progressCount`). |
| `errorCode` | `string`             | no       | `?error=` translated with `STAFF_ERROR_MESSAGES`.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

## Behaviour

- Metadata form, a [`PlatformForm`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form): name and description as [`PlatformFormField`](/components/platform/shared/platform-form)s (the name is required, so an empty one is answered under it, «Este campo es obligatorio.»), and «Capítulo de prioridad», a [`PlatformCheckbox`](/components/platform/shared/platform-checkbox) that marks the whole chapter (`Chapter.isPriority`): the student sees «Prioridad» on its card, and every lesson of it passes their «Prioridad» filter, marked or not. The head shows the «Prioridad» tag of a chapter that is.
- Lessons: not here. They are [a tab of their own](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-lessons-section), «Lecciones», because the list is rows and rows want the whole width (2026-09-30).
- The aside counts lessons, lessons with PGN, exercises and duration. **The duration is not a field**: it is the sum of its lessons' minutes, cached on the chapter by `syncChapterDuration` (2026-09-30), and a line under the figures says so — «La duración es la suma de los minutos de sus lecciones: se recalcula al guardar una.» A chapter whose lessons declare no minutes reads `—`, not «0 min».

**Elements:** `.chapter-editor__note`, `.chapter-editor__facts`, `.chapter-editor__fact`, `.chapter-editor__wide`, `.chapter-editor__submit`

No modifiers.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-accent-soft`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-border-strong`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`

**Breakpoints:** none of its own.
