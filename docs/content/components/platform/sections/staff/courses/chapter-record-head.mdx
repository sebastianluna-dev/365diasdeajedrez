## Purpose

The name of a chapter, where it sits — «Capítulo 3», the course it belongs to, «Prioridad» when it is marked — and the tabs, above whichever of its three pages one is on: «Capítulo», «Lecciones», «Partidas». Same reasons as [`CourseRecordHead`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-record-head), one level down.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                        |
| ----------- | -------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapter`   | `ChapterAdminDetail` | yes      | The record: its name, its number, its course and its lessons.  |
| `gameCount` | `number`             | yes      | How many games its collection has; the «Partidas» tab says it. |
