## Purpose

The name, the status and the tabs of a course, above whichever of its three pages one is on — «Curso», «Temario», «Partidas».

It is drawn by the record's **layout** and not by each page, for two reasons: the three cannot drift apart, and Next does not render a layout again when one moves between its children, so the header does not blink on the way (2026-09-30). The pages that hang FROM the record — a lesson, a game on the board — are outside the `(ficha)` group and keep their own header.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `course`    | `CourseAdminDetail` | yes      | The record: its name, its status, its slug and its chapters. |
| `statuses`  | `CatalogOption[]`   | yes      | The statuses on offer, for the pill that changes it.        |
| `gameCount` | `number`            | yes      | How many games the course has; the «Partidas» tab says it.  |

## Behaviour

- The status pill is [`CourseStatusMenu`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-status-menu) on the three pages, not only on «Curso»: the status is the record's, not that tab's.
- «Ver en la plataforma» appears only while the course is published, and leads to the student's information page by its slug.
- The tabs come from `courseTabs()` (`staff-tabs.const.ts`), and which one is current is read from the address by [`StaffTabs`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/staff-tabs).
