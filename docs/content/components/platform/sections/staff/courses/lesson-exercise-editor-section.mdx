## Behaviour

The same editor the teacher's bank uses ([`TeacherExerciseEditorSection`](/components/platform/sections/teacher/exercises/teacher-exercise-editor-section)), over an exercise owned by the lesson:

- the buttons are `lessonSaveButtons`'s: «Guardar borrador» and «Guardar y publicar» (the accent, which needs the exercise complete) on a new one or a draft, «Guardar» alone on a published one (it stays published, so it must stay complete) or an archived one;
- the content is never locked and «Duplicar» is not offered (`LESSON_EXERCISE_PERMISSIONS`);
- «Desde una partida» offers the chapter's collection, where the lesson's content comes from, read with `fetchChapterGamePgn`;
- the status in the page's head is worded by the lesson's panel («Publicado», «Borrador», «Archivado»), not by the catalog, whose «En la biblioteca» is never true of a lesson's exercise.

The editor is keyed by `editor.version` (the exercise's `updatedAt`): a save redirects to the exercise's own page with `?aviso=` and the board and the fields open again on what was stored. An exercise of another lesson, or of a bank, never reaches here: the page answers 404 when the service finds nothing.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/administracion/cursos/[courseId]/capitulos/[chapterId]/lecciones/[lessonId]/ejercicios/[exerciseId]/page.tsx`:

```tsx
<LessonExerciseEditorSection
  editor={editor}
  notice={readExerciseNotice(aviso) ?? undefined}
  errorMessage={error ? (EXERCISE_ERROR_MESSAGES[error] ?? EXERCISE_ERROR_MESSAGES.invalid) : undefined}
/>
```
