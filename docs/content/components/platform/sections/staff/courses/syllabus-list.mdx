## Purpose

**The path the student walks**, told in three bands: `INICIO`, what opens the course; the numbered steps; `FINAL`, what closes it. A course's chapters and a chapter's lessons are the same thing one level apart, so they are one component.

The steps are **one framed block with hairlines between them** and not a card each: a syllabus of forty chapters has to read as a single column, and forty boxes are forty boxes. The introduction and the closing sit outside that block, tinted, because their place is not their order — nothing is dropped onto them and they are not counted in the numbering.

## Props

| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `items`           | `SyllabusItem[]`                          | yes      | `{ id, name, facts, href, roleCode?, badge?, canDelete, deleteHint? }[]`. `roleCode` (the introduction's or the closing's) marks a fixed item; `deleteHint` is why deleting is refused. |
| `noun`            | `string`                                  | yes      | What one item is called, for the handle's `aria-label`, what is announced and the band's heading: "capítulo", "lección".                                                                |
| `onReorder`       | `(orderedIds: string[]) => Promise<void>` | yes      | Server action already bound to its course or chapter; receives the ids of the **normal** items only.                                                                                    |
| `createAction`    | `(formData: FormData) => Promise<void>`   | yes      | Creates one: it reads `name`, and `role` for the introduction and the closing.                                                                                                          |
| `deleteAction`    | `(formData: FormData) => Promise<void>`   | no       | Delete action; «Eliminar» is in the three dots' menu only with it and `canDelete`.                                                                                                      |
| `deleteFieldName` | `string`                                  | yes      | The hidden field the delete action reads (`chapterId`, `lessonId`).                                                                                                                     |
| `newPlaceholder`  | `string`                                  | yes      | What the field at the foot of the block says while it is empty.                                                                                                                         |

## Behaviour

- **A row** is its grip, its number on a tile, its name (a link to the editor), its facts at the end — "6 lecciones" for a chapter, "Con PGN · 3 ejercicios · 12 min" for a lesson, closed by «· Prioridad» when it is marked — and «Editar» as the platform's quiet button at the size of a row.
- **The three dots** open what moves a row one place and what destroys; «Editar» stays out of the menu, because hiding the everyday action behind a menu is what makes a list feel slow. On a numbered row the menu holds **«Subir» and «Bajar»**, the way to reorder with single clicks for whoever cannot hold a drag (WCAG 2.5.7, 2026-10-03): each takes the neighbour's place through `useSortableStep`, the same `onMove` — and so the same `onReorder` — a drop calls. At the ends of the block the one that would leave it stays, disabled (`aria-disabled`, so the arrows still reach it and the two never change places), and the menu opens on the first item that can be pressed: «Bajar» on the first row (in a list of one, where neither can be, on «Subir» all the same, so the arrows still walk it). After a step the menu closes and the focus goes to the row's three dots, which travelled with the row, and [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd) says where it landed. The introduction and the closing have no «Subir» or «Bajar»: their place is not their order. Under a rule comes «Eliminar», in the danger colour. The menu opens on **every** row: where the server would refuse to delete — a published course, a student's progress — it says so instead of the action (`deleteHint`), because a menu that is not there does not explain itself and what one comes looking for is precisely the option that is missing (2026-09-30). It is built by hand — not on Radix — like the bar's, closes on a click outside and with Escape, walks its items with the arrows, and lingers while it leaves (`useLingering`, `_state_closing`). For assistive technology: the items are a `menu` of `menuitem`s, which takes the focus when it opens; the reason deleting is refused is not an item, so it sits under the menu, outside it, as the menu's `aria-describedby`; and on the introduction or the closing, where the reason is all there is, the popup is a `status` whose text is announced as it appears, and the focus stays on the three dots.
- **Dragging is dnd-kit**, set up in [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/shared/sortable-dnd): the pointer travels 5px before a drag starts, so the name and the buttons are still pressed; with the keyboard, space picks the row up, the arrows move it, space drops it and escape puts it back. The gap opens where the row would land, and what happens is announced in Spanish.
- **Fixed items.** The introduction and the closing are drawn in their own band; they are left out of the ids sent, and the server would not accept them either. The numbering counts only normal items, so with an introduction in front the first real chapter is 1.
- **Creating is part of the list.** The last row of the block adds a normal item; the dashed slots of `INICIO` and `FINAL` add the introduction and the closing, with their `role` in a hidden field and «Opcional» at their end. Both are the same `createAction`, and both are gone from view once what they create exists.
- **Sync with the server.** New `items` after revalidation replace the local rows **during render** (via a `baseline` comparison), not in an effect, avoiding a frame with the old list.

## Markup & styling

Block `.syllabus-list`. Three bands under their headings; the middle one is a framed block of rows.

**Elements:** `.syllabus-list__label`, `.syllabus-list__band`, `.syllabus-list__hint`, `.syllabus-list__group`, `.syllabus-list__row`, `.syllabus-list__grip`, `.syllabus-list__number`, `.syllabus-list__piece`, `.syllabus-list__name`, `.syllabus-list__facts`, `.syllabus-list__badge`, `.syllabus-list__edit`, `.syllabus-list__more`, `.syllabus-list__more-space`, `.syllabus-list__menu-anchor`, `.syllabus-list__menu`, `.syllabus-list__menu-items`, `.syllabus-list__menu-item`, `.syllabus-list__menu-rule`, `.syllabus-list__menu-note`, `.syllabus-list__fixed`, `.syllabus-list__slot`, `.syllabus-list__add`, `.syllabus-list__add-sign`, `.syllabus-list__add-note`, `.syllabus-list__new`, `.syllabus-list__new-input`, `.syllabus-list__new-submit`

**Modifiers and states:** `.syllabus-list__row_state_dragging` (lifted, with the card's shadow), `.syllabus-list__row_kind_new`, `.syllabus-list__number_kind_new`, `.syllabus-list__piece_kind_intro`, `_kind_closing`, `.syllabus-list__menu_state_closing`, `.syllabus-list__menu-item_variant_danger` («Eliminar»), and `.syllabus-list__menu-item[aria-disabled="true"]` («Subir» on the first row, «Bajar» on the last, drawn like a disabled row action)

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button` — «Editar» is the platform's quiet button, given a row's padding by `.syllabus-list__edit` (see [Styling](/ui/styling)).

The row's only inline style is the `transform` dnd-kit measures frame by frame while it travels; it is geometry, not styling.

Under 768px the facts are dropped: they do not fit beside the name, and the name is what the row is for.

## Usage

From `chapter-lessons.section.tsx`:

```tsx
<SyllabusList
  noun="lección"
  items={chapter.lessons.map((lesson) => ({
    id: lesson.id,
    name: lesson.name,
    facts: lessonFacts(lesson),
    href: lesson.href,
    roleCode: lesson.roleCode,
    badge: lesson.isPriority ? "Prioridad" : undefined,
    canDelete: isDraft && lesson.progressCount === 0,
  }))}
  onReorder={reorderLessons.bind(null, chapter.courseId, chapter.id)}
  createAction={createLesson.bind(null, chapter.courseId, chapter.id)}
  deleteAction={deleteLesson.bind(null, chapter.courseId, chapter.id)}
  deleteFieldName="lessonId"
  newPlaceholder="Nombre de la nueva lección…"
/>
```
