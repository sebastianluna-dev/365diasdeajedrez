## Purpose

**The path the student walks**, told in three bands: `INICIO`, what opens the course; the numbered steps; `FINAL`, what closes it. A course's chapters and a chapter's lessons are the same thing one level apart, so they are one component.

The steps are **one framed block with hairlines between them** and not a card each: a syllabus of forty chapters has to read as a single column, and forty boxes are forty boxes. The introduction and the closing sit outside that block, tinted, because their place is not their order — nothing is dropped onto them and they are not counted in the numbering.

## Props

| Prop              | Type                                      | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `items`           | `SyllabusItem[]`                          | yes      | `{ id, name, facts, href, roleCode?, badge?, canDelete, deleteHint? }[]`. `roleCode` (the introduction's or the closing's) marks a fixed item; `deleteHint` is why deleting is refused. |
| `noun`            | `string`                                  | yes      | What one item is called, for the handle's `aria-label`, what is announced and the band's heading: "capítulo", "lección".                                                                |
| `onReorder`       | `(orderedIds: string[]) => Promise<void>` | yes      | Server action already bound to its course or chapter; receives the ids of the **normal** items only.                                                                                    |
| `createAction`    | `(formData: FormData) => Promise<void>`   | yes      | Creates one: it reads `name`, and `role` for the introduction and the closing.                                                                                                          |
| `deleteAction`    | `(formData: FormData) => Promise<void>`   | no       | Delete action; it is what the three dots open, and only when `canDelete`.                                                                                                               |
| `deleteFieldName` | `string`                                  | yes      | The hidden field the delete action reads (`chapterId`, `lessonId`).                                                                                                                     |
| `newPlaceholder`  | `string`                                  | yes      | What the field at the foot of the block says while it is empty.                                                                                                                         |

## Behaviour

- **A row** is its grip, its number on a tile, its name (a link to the editor), its facts at the end — "6 lecciones" for a chapter, "Con PGN · 3 ejercicios · 12 min" for a lesson, closed by «· Prioridad» when it is marked — and «Editar» as the platform's quiet button at the size of a row.
- **The three dots** open what destroys. «Eliminar» is the only thing there, because hiding the everyday action behind a menu is what makes a list feel slow. They open on **every** row, the introduction and the closing included: where the server would refuse — a published course, a student's progress — the menu says so instead of the action (`deleteHint`), because a menu that is not there does not explain itself and what one comes looking for is precisely the option that is missing (2026-09-30). The menu is built by hand — not on Radix — like the bar's, closes on a click outside and with Escape, and lingers while it leaves (`useLingering`, `_state_closing`).
- **Dragging is dnd-kit**, set up in [`SortableDnd`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/sortable-dnd): the pointer travels 5px before a drag starts, so the name and the buttons are still pressed; with the keyboard, space picks the row up, the arrows move it, space drops it and escape puts it back. The gap opens where the row would land, and what happens is announced in Spanish.
- **Fixed items.** The introduction and the closing are drawn in their own band; they are left out of the ids sent, and the server would not accept them either. The numbering counts only normal items, so with an introduction in front the first real chapter is 1.
- **Creating is part of the list.** The last row of the block adds a normal item; the dashed slots of `INICIO` and `FINAL` add the introduction and the closing, with their `role` in a hidden field and «Opcional» at their end. Both are the same `createAction`, and both are gone from view once what they create exists.
- **Sync with the server.** New `items` after revalidation replace the local rows **during render** (via a `baseline` comparison), not in an effect, avoiding a frame with the old list.

## Markup & styling

Block `.syllabus-list`. Three bands under their headings; the middle one is a framed block of rows.

**Elements:** `.syllabus-list__label`, `.syllabus-list__band`, `.syllabus-list__hint`, `.syllabus-list__group`, `.syllabus-list__row`, `.syllabus-list__grip`, `.syllabus-list__number`, `.syllabus-list__piece`, `.syllabus-list__name`, `.syllabus-list__facts`, `.syllabus-list__badge`, `.syllabus-list__edit`, `.syllabus-list__more`, `.syllabus-list__more-space`, `.syllabus-list__menu-anchor`, `.syllabus-list__menu`, `.syllabus-list__menu-item`, `.syllabus-list__menu-note`, `.syllabus-list__fixed`, `.syllabus-list__slot`, `.syllabus-list__add`, `.syllabus-list__add-sign`, `.syllabus-list__add-note`, `.syllabus-list__new`, `.syllabus-list__new-input`, `.syllabus-list__new-submit`

**Modifiers and states:** `.syllabus-list__row_state_dragging` (lifted, with the card's shadow), `.syllabus-list__row_kind_new`, `.syllabus-list__number_kind_new`, `.syllabus-list__piece_kind_intro`, `_kind_closing`, `.syllabus-list__menu_state_closing`

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button` — «Editar» is the platform's quiet button, given a row's padding by `.syllabus-list__edit` (see [Styling](/ui/styling)).

The row's only inline style is the `transform` dnd-kit measures frame by frame while it travels; it is geometry, not styling.

Under 720px the facts are dropped: they do not fit beside the name, and the name is what the row is for.

## Usage

From `chapter-lessons.section.tsx`:

```tsx
<SyllabusList
  noun="lección"
  items={chapter.lessons.map((lesson) => ({
    id: lesson.id,
    name: lesson.name,
    facts: lessonFacts(lesson),
    href: lesson.href,
    roleCode: lesson.roleCode,
    badge: lesson.isPriority ? "Prioridad" : undefined,
    canDelete: isDraft && lesson.progressCount === 0,
  }))}
  onReorder={reorderLessons.bind(null, chapter.courseId, chapter.id)}
  createAction={createLesson.bind(null, chapter.courseId, chapter.id)}
  deleteAction={deleteLesson.bind(null, chapter.courseId, chapter.id)}
  deleteFieldName="lessonId"
  newPlaceholder="Nombre de la nueva lección…"
/>
```
