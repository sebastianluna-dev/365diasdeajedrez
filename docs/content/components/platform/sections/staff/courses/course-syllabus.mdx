## Purpose

The chapters of a course read as what they are for the student: a path with a first step and a last one. **The list itself is [`SyllabusList`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/syllabus-list)**, the same one a chapter's lessons are drawn with — they are the same thing one level apart —; this component gives it the chapters, the actions that create, reorder and delete them, and the «Temario» panel around it. The card is three bands: the introduction, the numbered chapters with the field that adds one, and the closing.

## Props

| Prop       | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `courseId` | `string`            | yes      | The course the actions are bound to.                                                                                                    |
| `chapters` | `ChapterAdminRow[]` | yes      | `{ id, name, order, roleCode?, roleLabel?, lessonCount, progressCount, href }[]`, the introduction in front and the closing at the end. |
| `isDraft`  | `boolean`           | yes      | A chapter is only deleted from a draft course (and only without progress from any student).                                             |

## Behaviour

- **The header** is «Temario» with «3 capítulos · 15 lecciones».
- **Introduction and closing** have a band of their own, `INICIO` and `FINAL`, tinted and with a piece (a pawn, a king). While one does not exist its band is a dashed slot, «+ Agregar introducción» / «+ Agregar cierre», which creates it (`createChapter` with `role`); there is at most one of each, which the database's unique index guarantees.
- **Each chapter** is its number on a tile, its name (a link to its editor), «6 lecciones» — closed by «· Prioridad» when the chapter is marked as priority — and «Editar». A chapter says what it HOLDS and not how long it takes: the minutes are the lesson's own figure, and they are said there (2026-09-30).
- **The grip is the handle**: the chapter is dragged by it, and with the keyboard space picks it up and the arrows move it. The order is applied at once and saved in a transition (`useSortableRows`); the introduction and the closing are neither moved nor sent.
- **«Eliminar»** waits behind the row's three dots, and is offered only where the server would allow it: a draft course and a chapter with no progress, its lessons' included. Where it is refused the menu says why (`deleteHint`), rather than not being there.
- **What fails is said here.** The list's actions — create, reorder, delete — come back to «Temario» with `?error=`, and this component turns it into a `PlatformNotice` above the panel; before the list had a page of its own they landed on «Curso», where the list no longer is.
- **A new chapter** is typed in the last row of the block and added with Enter; the «Agregar» button stays out of sight while the field is empty (`:placeholder-shown`). It is in the soft accent of the page's buttons, at the size the field allows, instead of the dark one it used to be.

## Markup & styling

None of its own: what is drawn is [`SyllabusList`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/syllabus-list) inside a [`StaffPanel`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/staff-panel).

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-editor.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { CourseSyllabus } from "./course-syllabus/course-syllabus.comp";

<CourseSyllabus courseId={course.id} chapters={course.chapters} isDraft={isDraft} />;
```
