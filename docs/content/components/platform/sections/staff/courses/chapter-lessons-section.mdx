## Purpose

The same move «Temario» made, one level down: the lessons left the chapter editor's column for a page of their own, where the list takes the whole width (2026-09-30). What is drawn is [`SyllabusList`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/syllabus-list), the very list the course's chapters are drawn with, because a chapter's lessons are the same thing one level down.

## Props

| Prop        | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                     |
| ----------- | -------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `chapter`   | `ChapterAdminDetail` | yes      | The chapter with its lessons.                               |
| `errorCode` | `string`             | no       | What the last attempt refused; drawn as a `PlatformNotice`. |

## Behaviour

- **The facts of a lesson** are built here (`lessonFacts`): "Con PGN"/"Sin PGN", its exercise count and its estimated minutes when it has them — "Con PGN · 3 ejercicios · 12 min". A chapter says what it HOLDS and no minutes; a lesson is where the figure means something.
- The name of the chapter and the tabs are not here but in the record's [layout](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/chapter-record-head); the panel's meta says how many lessons there are and how many have PGN.
- «Eliminar» is offered only in a draft course and on a lesson without progress, and the server checks it again; where it is refused the row's menu says why. The actions come back to this page with `?error=`, which is drawn as a notice above the panel.
