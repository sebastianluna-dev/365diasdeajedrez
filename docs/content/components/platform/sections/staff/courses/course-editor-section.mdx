## Purpose

Editing a course as **independent cards**, each with its own form and action, instead of one giant form where a failure in one field throws away all the work. Left column, what is written and ordered: metadata and «Temario». Right column, what is glanced at, touched once and looked at many times: cover, authors and «Procedencia del recurso» ([`CourseSources`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-sources), where the material comes from).

The name of the record and its tabs are not here: they are the [layout](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-record-head) the three pages share.

## Behaviour

- **Header:** the course's name and, next to it, its status as a pill that is also the way to publish or archive it ([`CourseStatusMenu`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-status-menu)).
- **«Imágenes»** holds the course's two pictures, each an [`ImageUpload`](/components/platform/shared/image-upload) that saves itself: the **cover** (21:9), which the information page and the catalog show, and the **square** one (1:1), which is what a course's row shows in «Mis cursos». What the formats and the size are is in the card's «i».
- **Metadata** form: name and slug in [`CourseNameFields`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-name-fields), the form's one client piece (the slug is rewritten from the name when the name loses focus having changed; pattern-checked), description, the content types as a [`FormFieldset`](/components/platform/shared/form-field) of [`PlatformCheckCards`](/components/platform/shared/platform-check-cards) (legend «Contenido (selecciona al menos uno)»: the rule is in the label, not in a hint under the cards; with `layout="line"`: two columns, each card the type's name alone on one line, without its description; several at once; saving none answers «Elige al menos un tipo de contenido.»), the **levels** as [`CourseLevelsField`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-levels-field) — the same cards in order of difficulty, also `layout="line"`, each with the level's name and its orientative range as the card's `meta`, beside the name on the same line and in parentheses («Intermedio (Elo 1200–1599)», «Iniciación (Elo menos de 800)», «Maestro (Elo 2200 o más)»), without the level's description — where **what can be chosen is a stretch of the scale**: the cards that would leave a gap are offered switched off. The cover is NOT a field of this form: it lives in the aside and saves itself, through `updateCourseCover`, the moment it is uploaded or taken away ([`ImageUpload`](/components/platform/shared/image-upload)). What it admits — the formats, the 1 MB, the 21:9 crop and that it saves on upload — is the "i" in the header of its card, built from `IMAGE_LIMITS_TEXT`; under the box there is no small print.
- **Chapters:** not here. They are «Temario», [a tab of their own](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-chapters-section), because the list is rows and rows want the whole width (2026-09-30).
- **Authors:** [`CourseAuthors`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-authors), the card where authors are added to the course through a search, given a role and taken out; its «Gestionar» opens the dialog where an author is created or edited.
- **Status:** there is no card for it. The pill next to the title says it and changes it, and what the card used to repeat is already elsewhere: «Contenido» in «Temario»'s meta and the publication date in the course list. A published course gets a "Ver en la plataforma" link in the head, to its information page (`platformRoutes.courseInfo(course.slug)`, `/cursos/<slug>`): the one page of the course that does not depend on having added it. `canPublish` greys out «Publicado» while no lesson of the course has content: a PGN with something besides blanks, the linked game's when there is one. It comes from `courseHasContent`, the question `publishCourse` asks again before writing, and answering it reads one lesson's PGN, not every lesson's of the course.

## Markup & styling

Block `.course-editor` (25 lines): almost everything is the cards'. What is left is the metadata form's column and its submit aligned to the right.

**Elements:** `.course-editor__form`, `.course-editor__submit`

**Modifiers and states:** none

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button` — «Ver en la plataforma» and «Guardar metadatos», like every other action of the page.

**Tokens:** none

No media queries of its own: the cards bring theirs.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/administracion/cursos/[courseId]/page.tsx`:

```tsx
import { CourseEditorSection } from "@/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-editor.section";

<CourseEditorSection
  course={course}
  types={types}
  levels={levels}
  authors={authors}
  authorRoles={authorRoles}
  errorCode={error}
/>;
```
