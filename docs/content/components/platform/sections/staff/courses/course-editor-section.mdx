## Purpose

Editing a course as **independent cards**, each with its own form and action, instead of one giant form where a failure in one field throws away all the work. Left column, what is written and ordered: metadata and «Temario». Right column, what is glanced at, touched once and looked at many times: cover, authors, «Procedencia del recurso» ([`CourseSources`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-sources), where the material comes from) and status.

## Behaviour

- **Header:** the course's name and, next to it, its status as a pill that is also the way to publish or archive it ([`CourseStatusMenu`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-status-menu)).
- **Metadata** form (`id="course-metadata"`): name and slug in [`CourseNameFields`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-name-fields), the form's one client piece (the slug is rewritten from the name when the name loses focus having changed; pattern-checked), description, the content types as a [`FormFieldset`](/components/platform/shared/form-field) of [`PlatformCheckCards`](/components/platform/shared/platform-check-cards) (legend «Contenido (selecciona al menos uno)»: the rule is in the label, not in a hint under the cards; with `layout="line"`: two columns, each card the type's name alone on one line, without its description; several at once; saving none answers «Elige al menos un tipo de contenido.»), the **levels** as another `FormFieldset` of `PlatformCheckCards` in order of difficulty, also `layout="line"`, each card with the level's name and its orientative range as the card's `meta`, beside the name on the same line and in parentheses («Intermedio (Elo 1200–1599)», «Iniciación (Elo menos de 800)», «Maestro (Elo 2200 o más)»), without the level's description (several at once, or none; a retired level is still offered, marked «, retirado», because the set is saved whole and leaving it out would take it from the course). The cover lives in the aside but belongs to this form through [`ImageUpload`](/components/platform/shared/image-upload)'s `form` prop, so it saves with "Guardar metadatos".
- **Chapters:** [`CourseSyllabus`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-syllabus), «Temario»: the chapters as a path along a rail, with the introduction and the closing in their fixed places, drag and keyboard reordering, and the field that adds a chapter.
- **Authors:** [`CourseAuthors`](/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-authors), the card where authors are added to the course through a search, given a role and taken out; its «Gestionar» opens the dialog where an author is created or edited.
- **Estado:** facts, an info notice when `canPublish` is false (needs a chapter with a lesson that has a game), and the publish (disabled until allowed) and archive forms. A published course gets a "Ver en la plataforma" link in the head.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/administracion/cursos/[courseId]/page.tsx`:

```tsx
import { CourseEditorSection } from "@/components/platform/sections/staff/courses/course-editor.section";

<CourseEditorSection
  course={course}
  types={types}
  levels={levels}
  authors={authors}
  authorRoles={authorRoles}
  gameCount={gameCount}
  errorCode={error}
/>;
```
