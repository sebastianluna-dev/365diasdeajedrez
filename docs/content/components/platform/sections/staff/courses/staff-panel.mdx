## Purpose

The card every course-screen section sits in. The header carries its own full-width rule (negative margins cancel the padding) because a line that does not reach the edges reads as an underline of the title instead of the card's division. It is the courses panel's variant of `.platform-card`, with a stricter header.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Required | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | `string` | yes | The `<h2>`. |
| `meta` | `string` | no | A short fact next to the title: "5 capítulos · 162 lecciones". |
| `action` | `ReactNode` | no | Aligned to the right edge of the header. |
| `description` | `string` | no | Context sentence under the header, when the card does not explain itself. |
| `children` | `ReactNode` | yes | The content. |

## Behaviour

None.

## Markup & styling

Block `.staff-panel` (50 lines).

**Elements:** `.staff-panel__head`, `.staff-panel__title`, `.staff-panel__meta`, `.staff-panel__action`, `.staff-panel__description`

**Tokens:** own `--staff-panel-gutter` (the padding the header rule cancels); `--platform-border`, `--platform-font`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`

No media queries.
