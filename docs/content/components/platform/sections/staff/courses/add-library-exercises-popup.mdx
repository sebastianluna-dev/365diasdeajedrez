## Behaviour

`AddLibraryExercisesButton` is the lesson panel's secondary button, beside «Agregar ejercicio», with the popup behind it. The form is submitted by hand (the cards are controlled, AGENTS.md): the chosen ids travel in the order they were ticked and the action copies each — `createExerciseRecord` with the content whitelist, owned by the lesson at its next place, moved DRAFT → PUBLISHED at once (D28: what the library approved the lesson serves) — in one transaction; a refusal keeps the choice. Done, the popup closes and the page reloads with «Ejercicios agregados a la lección desde la biblioteca, publicados.». With nothing to add it says so.
