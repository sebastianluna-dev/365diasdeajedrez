## Purpose

A study as the library shows it, in the new design system (2026-10-23, «M4» of the owner's canvas): Material's outlined card (`mdCard("outlined")`), the study's board on the left at 128px (96px until 2026-10-23, the owner asked for it larger) — the blue board with the «elegida» pieces, where the game the study opens on ends —, and beside it:

- **its kind**, a [`StudyChip`](/components/platform/sections/studies/study-chip) in the kind's colour — «Mis partidas» blue, «Estudio» green, «Torneo» amber, «Colección» the secondary container — or, for a study that is not theirs, where it comes from: the course, the teacher who shared it (grey), «Tus clases»;
- **its name**, in two lines at most;
- **how many games and how long ago** it was touched («9 partidas · hace 2 horas», the exact date in the tooltip), or, when it is not theirs, «sólo lectura».

Until 2026-10-23 it was a «cover» (the board across the card's top, 2026-10-07) on the page's grey with a shadow.

**The whole card is the link.** The name is the only `<a>`; its `::after` is stretched over the card, so a click anywhere goes to the game the study was left on, or its first (`href`; the study's empty page when it has none). The board is `aria-hidden`: the name says what the study is. Keyboard focus on the link draws the primary's ring around the card (`:has(:focus-visible)`).

## Props

| Prop    | Type           | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------- | -------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `study` | `StudySummary` | yes      | `id`, `name`, `href`, `kindCode`, `kindLabel`, `origin?`, `boardFen`, `boardOrientation`, `gameCount`, `citedGameCount`, `updatedAtLabel`, `updatedAgoLabel`, `permissions: { canDelete, canEditGames, … }`. The rest is the list's. |

## Behaviour

- **The board** is `boardFen` seen from `boardOrientation`, both from the mapper: the final position of the last game the viewer opened in the study (`StudyVisit`), or of its first game when they never opened one. A study without games shows the starting position.
- **Under the pointer** the card's fill turns to the canvas's blue-grey; nothing moves.
- **The cross.** `permissions.canDelete` renders the [`DeleteStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/delete-study) cross — Material's standard icon button — at the card's top right, raised above the stretched link. A study that cannot be deleted («Mis partidas», what was received) has none.

## Markup & styling

Block `.study-card` (90 lines). It sets the Material card's own properties — `--container-color`, `--outline-color` (the container-high grey) and `--container-shape` (`--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`) — and lays it out in a row.

**Elements:** `.study-card__board`, `.study-card__body`, `.study-card__name`, `.study-card__meta`, `.study-card__actions`

**Other classes it renders:** `card card-outlined` (`mdCard`).

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-panel`, `--platform-m3-canvas`, `--platform-m3-container-high`, `--platform-m3-on-surface`, `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`, `--platform-m3-primary`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`, the type and space scales.

**Breakpoints:** none of its own; the grid sets its width.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-browser.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudyCard } from "./study-card.comp";

{
  ownShown.map((study) => <StudyCard key={study.id} study={study} />);
}
```
