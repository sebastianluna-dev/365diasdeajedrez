## Purpose

A study as a card, in the manner of a lichess study card: a 2×2 tile (orange squares on the card's surface, inside an orange border), the name, one byline and the first games inside. The byline states the kind, the size, **where it comes from** for what is not the student's (the course that brings it or the teacher who handed it out; without that, two read-only collections would be indistinguishable) and how long ago it was touched ("hace 3 meses", the exact date in the tooltip). Under it, up to four games as the study page names them (a title, or "Blancas – Negras") and "y n más".

**The whole card is the link.** The name is the only `<a>`; its `::after` is stretched over the card (`position: absolute; inset: 0`), so a click anywhere goes to the study while the markup keeps one link with the study's name. The delete cross sits in `.study-card__actions`, raised above it with `z-index`, so it is still what gets pressed. Keyboard focus on the link outlines the card (`:has(:focus-visible)`).

## Behaviour

- `permissions.canDelete` renders the [`DeleteStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/delete-study) cross with the counts it needs for its warning; otherwise, a read-only study shows the "Sólo lectura" mark. An editable study that cannot be deleted ("Mis partidas") shows neither.
- Game count, the two date labels and `previewGames` come from the mapper: the preview is the study's first four games in the study page's order (`studySummaryInclude`, `take: 4`), and the total is counted by the service in one grouped query, because the include's own relation count is taken by the cited games.

## Markup & styling

Block `.study-card`.

**Elements:** `.study-card__tile`, `.study-card__body`, `.study-card__head`, `.study-card__name`, `.study-card__actions`, `.study-card__readonly`, `.study-card__meta`, `.study-card__kind` (no style of its own: the byline reads as one line), `.study-card__origin`, `.study-card__dot`, `.study-card__description`, `.study-card__games`, `.study-card__game`

**Modifiers:** `.study-card__game_more` (the "y n más" row, without bullet).

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-surface-neutral`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-faint`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`

No media queries.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-browser.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudyCard } from "./study-card.comp";

{
  ownShown.map((study) => <StudyCard key={study.id} study={study} />);
}
```
