## Purpose

A study as the library shows it (2026-10-07: the «Biblioteca» direction, card «T2», the «cover», which the owner chose among three directions and then five cards). A study is recognised by its board, as on Lichess: the card opens with its board across its whole width, where the study's first game ends, and under it says, from top to bottom:

- **what it is**, with its icon, in the accent's ink — «Mis partidas» (`UserIcon`), «Estudio» (`BookOpenIcon`), «Torneo» (`TrophyIcon`), «Colección» (`BooksIcon`) — or, for a study that is not theirs, **where it comes from**, in grey: the teacher who shared it (`ChalkboardUserIcon`), the course (`GraduationCapIcon`), «Tus clases» (`UsersIcon`);
- **its name**, in two lines at most: a library is scanned;
- **how many games and how long ago** it was touched («3 partidas · anteayer», the exact date in the tooltip).

It replaced a card with the site's logo mark as its tile, the description and the first four games listed by name: tall cards in two columns, all of them with the same tile. The description and the games are still what the [search](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-browser) finds.

**The whole card is the link.** The name is the only `<a>`; its `::after` is stretched over the card (`position: absolute; inset: 0`), so a click anywhere goes to the study's first game (`href`; the study's empty page when it has none) while the markup keeps one link with the study's name. The board is `aria-hidden`: the name says what the study is. Keyboard focus on the link draws the accent's ring around the card (`:has(:focus-visible)`), the ring of everything outside a form.

## Behaviour

- **The board** is `boardFen` seen from `boardOrientation`, both from the mapper: the final position of the study's first game in the aside's order (`finalFenOf` over its PGN; `studySummaryInclude` takes that one game), from the side that game is set to open from. A study without games shows the starting position. The class-games card shows the latest class's game.
- **Under the pointer** the shadow grows (`--platform-shadow-card` to `--platform-shadow-lift`); nothing moves.
- **The card went through four shapes the same day.** First «T1», the board beside the text, at 92px in four columns and then in three; then 120px in two; the owner then took the cover. Beside the text the board read as an icon.
- **The cross.** `permissions.canDelete` renders the [`DeleteStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/delete-study) cross over the board's top right corner, on a white tile of its own (the quiet button is drawn for a white ground and was lost on the squares), raised above the stretched link with `z-index`; it comes out when the card is pointed at or holds the focus, and is always drawn where nothing hovers (`(hover: none)`). A study that cannot be deleted («Mis partidas», what was received) has none. The «Sólo lectura» pill of the old card went: what is not theirs is in its own block, «Recibido», and says where it comes from.

## Markup & styling

Block `.study-card` (129 lines).

**Elements:** `.study-card__board`, `.study-card__body`, `.study-card__kind`, `.study-card__kind-icon`, `.study-card__kind-label`, `.study-card__name`, `.study-card__meta`, `.study-card__actions`

**Modifiers:** `.study-card__kind_received` (where a study that is not theirs comes from, in grey).

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-shadow-card` (the card and the cross's tile), `--platform-shadow-lift`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`, the type and space scales.

**Breakpoints:** `(hover: none)` always draws the cross; the board takes the card's width, which the grid sets.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-browser.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudyCard } from "./study-card.comp";

{
  ownShown.map((study) => <StudyCard key={study.id} study={study} />);
}
```
