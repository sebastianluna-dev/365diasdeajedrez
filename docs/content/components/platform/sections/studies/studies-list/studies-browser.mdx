## Purpose

The panel of «Mis estudios», rebuilt in the new design system on 2026-10-23 («M4» of the owner's canvas). From top to bottom:

1. **«Sigue donde ibas»** ([`ResumeStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/resume-study)), when the section gives one and nothing is searched.
2. **«Tus estudios»**, a title in the display font over a grid of [`StudyCard`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/study-card)s: three across the panel, two under 1280px, one on a phone.
3. **«Recibido»**, with its count in a pill of the secondary container: what reaches them ready-made, read-only.

Which kinds and in what order arrived already applied by the section, from the address (the side column's links). What is left here is the search, which filters as one types; the rest of the page is on the server.

## Props

| Prop           | Type                                     | Required | Meaning                                                                                     |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `own`          | `StudySummary[]`                         | yes      | Their own studies the view shows, in its order.                                             |
| `received`     | `StudySummary[]`                         | yes      | What reached them that the view shows, in its order.                                        |
| `resume`       | `StudySummary & { resume: StudyResume }` | no       | «Sigue donde ibas»: only on «Todos», and only when a game was opened.                       |
| `initialQuery` | `string`                                 | no       | `?q=`: a search sent from a study's bar opens the library already filtered.                 |
| `viewLabel`    | `string`                                 | yes      | The view's name, for what an empty view says («Nada en «Torneos»»).                         |
| `viewKey`      | `string`                                 | yes      | The view and the order on screen: a new one remounts the groups, which play their entrance. |

## Behaviour

- **The search is in the bar.** [`StudiesSearch`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-search) goes into the shell's bar through `PlatformHeaderContext`, as «Cursos» puts its own, but stays in this component's tree: its state is this one's. It matches each study's `searchText`, which the mapper folds (`foldForSearch`: no case, no accents) from its name, its description, where it comes from and **every game's** title, players and event; what is typed is folded the same way. A live region, out of sight, says how many studies are left; none left, an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state) says what was searched. An empty view without a search says so and points to the column and to «Nuevo estudio».
- **«Sigue donde ibas» steps aside while searching**: what is searched is what is wanted.
- **Entrance.** The groups are keyed by `viewKey`, so a new view or order remounts them and every card plays its entrance again: a 260 ms fade with a short rise, staggered 35 ms per card through `--studies-browser-index`, capped at the twelfth. Typing does not replay it. Off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **What went on 2026-10-23**: the bar of the old library (its search box, the tabs by kind and the order's select) — the tabs and the order are the side column's links now, the search is the bar's — and the dashed «Nuevo estudio» slot that headed the grid, now the side column's FAB.

## Markup & styling

Block `.studies-browser` (104 lines).

**Elements:** `.studies-browser__search` (where there is no bar, a story, the search stays on top of the panel in this box), `.studies-browser__status` (the live region, out of sight), `.studies-browser__group`, `.studies-browser__title`, `.studies-browser__count`, `.studies-browser__grid`, `.studies-browser__item` (the animated wrapper of each card)

**Keyframes:** `studies-browser-item-in`, at the top level because `@keyframes` cannot be nested.

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-on-surface`, `--platform-m3-secondary-container`, `--platform-m3-on-secondary-container`, `--platform-font`, `--platform-font-display`, the type and space scales.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1280px)` takes the grid to two columns, `(width < 640px)` to one; `(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` removes the entrance.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-list.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudiesBrowser } from "./studies-browser.comp";

<StudiesBrowser
  own={shown.filter(isOwnStudy)}
  received={shown.filter((study) => !isOwnStudy(study))}
  resume={resume?.resume ? { ...resume, resume: resume.resume } : undefined}
  initialQuery={query}
  viewLabel={viewLabel}
  viewKey={`${viewLabel}-${sortLabel}`}
/>;
```
