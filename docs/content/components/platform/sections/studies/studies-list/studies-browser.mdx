## Purpose

The body of «Mis estudios», rebuilt on 2026-10-07 in the «Biblioteca» direction (page «P1», search «B1»), which the owner chose among three directions and then a set of variants. Until then it was a row of filter chips over two titled groups of tall cards in two columns, with no search and no order.

From top to bottom:

1. **The bar**: the search at the start, the tabs beside it, the order at the end (on a phone each takes a line of its own).
2. **Their own studies**, in a grid of covers — four across the page's 1240px, three under 1024px, two on a phone —, headed by the dashed slot that creates one (`NewStudy` with `trigger="slot"`, the same popup as the page's button).
3. **«Recibido»**, with its count: what reaches them ready-made and cannot be touched, in a block of its own.

It is a Client Component because it filters; everything it renders arrived from the server in one read, and nothing is fetched or written here.

## Props

| Prop       | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `own`      | `StudySummary[]`    | yes      | Those that are theirs: «Mis partidas», their tournaments and their studies (a teacher's own collections). |
| `received` | `StudySummary[]`    | yes      | What reaches them ready-made: a teacher's collections, the games of their classes.                        |
| `kinds`    | `StudyKindOption[]` | yes      | The kinds they can create, for the slot's popup and to know in which tabs the slot belongs.               |

## Behaviour

- **The search** matches each study's `searchText`, which the mapper folds (`foldForSearch`: no case, no accents) from its name, its description, where it comes from and **every game's** title, players and event; what is typed is folded the same way, so «kasparov» finds «Kaspárov» and «anderssen» finds the study whose games he played. It filters as one types. A live region, out of sight, says how many studies are left («1 estudio»); none left, an [`EmptyState`](/components/platform/shared/empty-state) says what was searched and what one can search by.
- **The tabs**: «Todos», then their own by kind — «Mis partidas», «Estudios», «Torneos», «Colecciones» — and «Recibidos», each with its count. The counts are the library's, whatever is searched, and a kind they have none of has no tab. They are buttons with `aria-pressed`; the chosen one is filled with the ink, which the head's red button does not compete with.
- **The order**: «Recientes» (the default, by `updatedAt`), «Nombre» (Spanish collation, no case) or «Más partidas». It replaced the server's order, which put «Mis partidas» first.
- **The slot** heads their own grid while nothing is searched and the tab is «Todos» or one of the kinds they can create.
- **Entrance.** The groups are keyed by the tab and the order, so changing either remounts them and every card plays its entrance again: a 260 ms fade with a short rise, staggered 35 ms per card through `--studies-browser-index`, capped at the twelfth so a long library does not take a second to come in. Typing does not replay it. Off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Markup & styling

Block `.studies-browser` (215 lines).

**Elements:** `.studies-browser__bar`, `.studies-browser__search` (the box: border, and the focus a form's field takes — the selection's border and its pale ring), `.studies-browser__search-icon`, `.studies-browser__search-input`, `.studies-browser__tabs`, `.studies-browser__tab`, `.studies-browser__tab-count`, `.studies-browser__sort` (the box around the select, which the bar lays out), `.studies-browser__status` (the live region, out of sight), `.studies-browser__group`, `.studies-browser__group-title`, `.studies-browser__group-count`, `.studies-browser__grid`, `.studies-browser__item` (the animated wrapper of each card)

**Keyframes:** `studies-browser-item-in`, at the top level because `@keyframes` cannot be nested.

**Tokens:** `--platform-border`, `--platform-selection`, `--platform-selection-soft`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-text-subtle`, the type and space scales.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` takes the grid to three columns; `(width < 640px)` puts the search and the tabs on lines of their own and the grid in two columns; `(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` removes the entrance.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-list.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudiesBrowser } from "./studies-browser.comp";

<StudiesBrowser
  own={studies.filter((study) => study.permissions.canEditGames)}
  received={studies.filter((study) => !study.permissions.canEditGames)}
  kinds={kinds}
/>;
```
