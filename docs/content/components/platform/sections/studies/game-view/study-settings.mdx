## Purpose

What its owner does to the study itself — change its name, its description or its kind; delete it — from the column beside the board. Three steps got it here, all on 2026-10-05:

1. two words under the study's name, «Editar datos» and «Eliminar»;
2. a menu behind three dots at the end of the head (`StudyMenu`, on Radix's `DropdownMenu`), with those two as its items;
3. this: **three bars that open the form at once**. On "Mis partidas", which is never deleted, the menu had a single item, and a menu of one is a press too many; the owner asked for the button to open the study's form directly. Deleting moved into that form, at its foot.

## Props

| Prop    | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                         |
| ------- | ------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `study` | `StudyDetail`       | yes      | The study: what the two dialogs open on, and its `permissions`. |
| `kinds` | `StudyKindOption[]` | yes      | The kinds the study can change to, for the form.                |

## Behaviour

- **One press, the form.** The button opens [`EditStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/study-detail/edit-study) through its handle with the click (`ref.open(event)`); the popup is mounted here with `trigger="none"`.
- **Deleting is in the form, and the confirmation on top of it.** When `study.permissions.canDelete`, `EditStudy` gets `onDelete` and draws «Eliminar estudio» at its foot, in the danger ink under a rule, as a game's settings draw what destroys. Pressed, [`DeleteStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/delete-study)'s confirmation opens over the form, which stays open beneath (until 2026-10-06 the form closed first and the confirmation took its place). Cancelling leaves the form as it was; deleting closes the confirmation and then, through `DeleteStudy`'s `onDone`, the form (`editRef.current?.close()`), each `close()` returning the focus a step back. The two are mounted as siblings, never one inside the other. "Mis partidas" has no such foot.
- **It receives the study and decides what it offers**; `StudyAside` mounts it only for whoever may edit the study.
- **Focus.** A native `<dialog>` returns the focus, on closing, to whatever had it when it opened, and a click does not focus a button in Safari, nor in Firefox on a Mac: `PlatformPopup.open(event)` focuses the trigger before opening, so the button gets the focus back and it does not land on `<body>`. The bars only pass the click on.
- The button is named «Editar el estudio «study»»; its icon is the bars in Duotone Solid, 20px in a 28px box, in the muted grey and in the accent under the pointer — drawn like the gear of a game's row under it, and in the same column.

## Markup & styling

Block `.study-settings` (34 lines): the button and nothing else.

**Elements:** `.study-settings__open`, `.study-settings__open-icon`

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-text-muted`, `--platform-selection`

No media queries. Where it sits in the head — its margins, which put the bars in the column of the rows' gears — is `study-aside.comp.css`'s.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-aside.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudySettings } from "./study-settings.comp";

<div className="study-aside__head">
  <h2 className="study-aside__name">{study.name}</h2>
  {canWrite && <StudySettings study={study} kinds={kinds} />}
</div>;
```
