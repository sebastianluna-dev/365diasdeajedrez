## Purpose

A study has no page of its own — its URL redirects to a game — so this column is where the study lives. Since 2026-10-23 it is the side column of the new design system's workspace («M4» of the owner's canvas, `MaterialM4Estudio`), on the canvas, with `PlatformSideNav`'s rhythm:

1. **The study**: its name, and under it a [`StudyChip`](/components/platform/sections/studies/study-chip) with its kind (or where it comes from) and its size, «Torneo · 9 partidas»; for its owner, at the right of the row, the sliders that open its form ([`StudySettings`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-settings); «Eliminar estudio» is at the form's foot).
2. **«Nueva partida»**, Material's extended FAB ([`NewGame`](/components/platform/sections/studies/study-detail/new-game)), for whoever may write.
3. **«Partidas»**, the games as the column's rows ([`StudyGames`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-games) over [`GameList`](/components/platform/shared/game-list) with `look="material"`): the name, who played, the result at the right, the one on the board lit in the secondary container, each with its grip and gear for the owner.
4. For a collection's owner, **who it is shared with** (`StudentSharePanel`, compact), on a panel of its own at the foot. The canvas has a share button in the panel's toolbar instead; the sharing card stays here, where it was, so nothing about it changes.

It receives **data**, never components, and decides for itself what to show with the same permission table the server actions apply (`services/studies/study-rules`): the layout only loads and hands over.

**It is the layout's, not the page's** (2026-10-05): Next does not render a layout again when only the segment under it changes, so the study holds still from game to game — its list keeps its scroll and its page —, and only the record and the board change. Nothing here may depend on the game on the board or on the query string; which row is the current one is read from the address by [`StudyGames`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-games).

## Props

| Prop         | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `study`      | `StudyDetail`       | yes      | The study with **every** game (`getStudyWithGames`), `permissions`, `shares`. |
| `results`    | `StudyKindOption[]` | yes      | The results catalog, for "Nueva partida". Empty when nobody here can write.   |
| `classGames` | `ClassGameItem[]`   | yes      | Games seen in class that can be copied; empty hides that tab of `NewGame`.    |
| `kinds`      | `StudyKindOption[]` | yes      | Kinds the study can change to; empty when it is not the viewer's.             |
| `students`   | `StudentOption[]`   | yes      | Students to hand a collection to; empty unless the viewer owns a collection.  |

## Behaviour

- `canWrite = study.permissions.canEditGames` gates the sliders, the FAB and the gear of settings on each game (`settings`, the four actions bound to the study: `updateGameSettings`, `clearGameAnnotations`, `clearGameVariations`, `deleteStudyGame`); whether the form offers «Eliminar estudio» is `StudySettings`' own (`canDelete`). `canShare` is `canShareStudy(study)`: the owner of a collection.
- **The chip's origin**: a course's database says its course, a received collection the teacher who shared it (`originOf`), as the library's cards do.
- **Reordering** is `canReorderStudyGames(study)`: whoever may edit the games, while the study fits on one page of the list (`STUDY_GAMES_PAGE_SIZE`, 100).
- **Every game goes to the list**, each `StudyGameItem` as a `GameListItem` (`listItem`: the title, or the `label` fallback, as the name, and the players as the second line). A study with no games draws no «Partidas».
- **How it stays fresh.** A layout is rendered again after a server action that calls `revalidatePath`, like the page: that is how a renamed, reordered, new or deleted game reaches the list.

## Markup & styling

Block `.study-aside` (60 lines), a column on the canvas.

**Elements:** `.study-aside__head`, `.study-aside__heading`, `.study-aside__name`, `.study-aside__games`, `.study-aside__title` (the group's title, as `PlatformSideNav` writes its own), `.study-aside__share` (the panel's white, with its corners, around the sharing card)

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-on-surface`, `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`, `--platform-m3-panel`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`, the type and space scales.

**The page around it** (`game-page.css`, beside the layout): the page bleeds onto the canvas (`platform-page_width_full` + `platform-page_frame_bleed`) and the layout draws `.platform-workspace`; the board is measured by the **height** of the window (`--game-board-max`: `100vh` less the bar, the panel's padding, the trail, the record, the gaps, the two strips and the tabs of the tools, which are left peeking), handed to the viewer as `--viewer-board-max` with a floor of 320px, and back to the viewer's 600px under 1280px. Under 1024px the workspace puts this column above the panel.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/estudios/[studyId]/partidas/layout.tsx`:

```tsx
<div className="platform-workspace__side">
  <StudyAside study={study} results={results} classGames={classGames} kinds={kinds} students={students} />
</div>
```

The layout loads the study with `getStudyWithGames` and the catalogs only when they can be used (`canEditGames`, `canShareStudy`), passing empty lists otherwise.
