## Purpose

What its owner does to the study itself — change its name, its description or its kind; delete it — from the column beside the board. Until 2026-10-05 those were two words under the study's name, «Editar datos» and «Eliminar»; the owner asked for a menu button at the end of the head instead, level with the name. The head is one line, and there is room for whatever the study does next.

## Props

| Prop    | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                         |
| ------- | ------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `study` | `StudyDetail`       | yes      | The study: what the two dialogs open on, and its `permissions`. |
| `kinds` | `StudyKindOption[]` | yes      | The kinds the study can change to, for «Editar datos».          |

## Behaviour

- **It receives the study and decides what it offers.** `StudyAside` mounts it only for whoever may edit the study; whether «Eliminar estudio» is there is `study.permissions.canDelete` ("Mis partidas" is edited but never deleted), in the danger ink.
- **The dialogs are beside the menu, not inside it.** The primitive unmounts the list when it closes, and a dialog inside it would go with it. `EditStudy` and `DeleteStudy` are mounted with `trigger="none"` and opened through their handles (`ref.open()`).
- **Focus.** Before a dialog opens, the focus goes back to the three dots: a native `<dialog>` returns the focus, on closing, to whatever had it when it opened, and the menu's item no longer exists by then. Checked in the running app: Enter on the dots, Enter on «Editar datos», Escape in the dialog — the focus is on the dots again.
- **On Radix's `DropdownMenu`**, which gives the roles, the arrows, Escape and the click outside. Not portalled, like the rest of the platform's popups, and not modal (`modal={false}`): it is a short list beside a button, and the page behind stays as it is.
- The button is named «Opciones de «study»»; its icon is the ellipsis in Duotone Solid, 20px in a 28px box, in the muted grey and in the accent under the pointer or while the menu is open — drawn like the gear of a game's row under it.
- The list enters in 160ms (`ease-out`) and leaves in 120ms (`ease-in`), on `data-state`; neither under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Markup & styling

Blocks `.study-menu` and `.study-menu__list`, in one sheet (109 lines).

**Elements:** `.study-menu__open`, `.study-menu__open-icon`, `.study-menu__list`, `.study-menu__item`

**Modifiers and states:** `.study-menu__item_variant_danger`; `[data-state]` on the button and the list, `[data-highlighted]` on the item the pointer or the arrows are on.

**Keyframes:** `study-menu-in`, `study-menu-out`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-danger`, `--platform-shadow-card`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`

No width media queries. Where it sits in the head — its margins, which put the dots in the column of the rows' gears — is `study-aside.comp.css`'s.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-aside.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
import { StudyMenu } from "./study-menu.comp";

<div className="study-aside__head">
  <h2 className="study-aside__name">{study.name}</h2>
  {canWrite && <StudyMenu study={study} kinds={kinds} />}
</div>;
```
