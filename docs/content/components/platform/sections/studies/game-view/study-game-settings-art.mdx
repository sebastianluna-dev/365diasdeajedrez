## Purpose

The drawing at the right of each option of a study's «Ajustes del capítulo» (2026-10-23), as [`CourseSettingsArt`](/components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/course-settings-art) draws the course's: a loop of a few seconds that shows what the option does before it is chosen. The owner chose them on the canvas from five styles: the first four from «C · Cálido» (the board in its frame, the sheet of moves), the last three from «B · Con el tablero», all in the board's blues («2 · Azul del tablero»). An **illustration, not an icon**: drawn here by hand in the platform's Material roles, `aria-hidden`, since the option's words say the same.

## Props

| Prop   | Type                       | Required | Meaning                                                                                            |
| ------ | -------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `kind` | `StudyGameSettingsArtKind` | yes      | Which option it shows: `white`, `black`, `normal`, `conceal`, `annotations`, `variations`, `game`. |

## Behaviour

- `white`: the board in its frame, White's pawns below; `black`: the same board turning over and back.
- `normal`: a sheet of moves with the mark walking it; `conceal`: the mark walking with a veil over what follows it.
- `annotations`: the board with an arrow and a circle that fade, its moves staying; `variations`: a branch drawing back into the main line; `game`: the board, in the error's tints, shrinking away under a blinking cross.
