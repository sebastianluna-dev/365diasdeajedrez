## Purpose

«M4» of the owner's canvas (`MaterialM4Estudio`, 2026-10-23) heads a study's panel with a toolbar: the arrow back to «Mis estudios», where one is — «Estudios › Nacional Abierto 2026 › Ronda 3» —, and at its right «Partida 3 de 9» with the arrows to the game before and the one after, so the study can be gone through without the column.

It is the layout's, which is not rendered again from one game to the next, so the game on the board is read here from the address (`useParams`), as [`StudyGames`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-games) reads it; that is why it is a Client Component.

## Props

| Prop        | Type               | Required | Meaning                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `studyName` | `string`           | yes      | The study's name, the trail's middle step.                                                           |
| `games`     | `StudyTrailGame[]` | yes      | `{ id, name, href }` of every game, in the study's order (the list's name: the title, or its label). |

## Behaviour

- The arrows are links while there is a game that way, named «Partida anterior: …» / «Partida siguiente: …», and switched-off buttons at either end. With one game, or none, neither the count nor the arrows are drawn.
- With no game on the board (the empty study's page) the study is the last step, in the weight of where one is.
- The study's step is not a link: the study's own URL leads back to the game on the board.
- The canvas also drew a share button in the toolbar; the study's sharing card stays in the side column (for a collection's owner) and the game's own sharing is the tools' «Compartir» tab, so there is none here.
- On a phone the count is left out and the names give way first; the way back never does.

**Modifiers:** `.study-trail__crumb_state_here` (where one is: the ink, a weight up).

**Other classes it renders:** `icon-btn icon-btn-standard icon-btn-sm` (`mdIconButton`), `.platform-md-icon`.

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-container`, `--platform-m3-on-surface`, `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`, `--platform-m3-outline`, the type and space scales.

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)`, where the panel pads itself less and the count is left out.
