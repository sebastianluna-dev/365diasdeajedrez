## Purpose

A study's chapter is configured at the right of the workspace, as a course is (2026-10-23, the owner's design `MaterialM4EstudioAjustes` on the canvas, its drawings the owner's choice «C + B, Azul del tablero»). It took the place of the popup each row's gear opened ([`GameSettings`](/components/platform/shared/game-settings), which the staff's chapter collections keep). It is opened and closed by the gear in the bar ([`PlatformSettingsToggle`](/components/platform/shared/platform-settings-toggle), which the layout puts there through `PlatformHeaderContext`), as a course's, and by its own ✕. A study's games are its **chapters** (2026-10-23, the owner's nomenclature): the column, its counts and its actions say «capítulo»; «partida» is said only of the game's own data («Datos de la partida»).

Three groups:

1. **«El tablero, desde»**: «Las blancas» or «Las negras».
2. **«Quien lo lee»**: «Análisis normal» or «Ocultar los siguientes movimientos».
3. **«Limpiar el capítulo»**: «Eliminar las anotaciones», «Eliminar las variantes» and «Eliminar el capítulo» (the danger tone, its own confirmation: «¿Eliminar el capítulo?»).

The name is not here: it is the game's record's, «Datos de la partida» ([`EditGame`](/components/platform/shared/edit-game)). Nor is the pinned comment, which the owner does not use: it is no longer edited on a study's game.

## Props

| Prop      | Type                       | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------- | -------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `games`   | `GameListItem[]`           | yes      | Every game of the study (`studyGameListItem`, the list's own rows); the one on the board is read from the address.                                                                              |
| `actions` | `StudyGameSettingsActions` | yes      | `{ setView(gameId, setting), clearAnnotations(gameId), clearVariations(gameId), remove(gameId, formData) }`, server actions bound to the study by the layout (`setGameView` and the clean-ups). |

## Behaviour

- **It is the layout's**, as the study's column is: it holds still from game to game and reads the game on the board from the address (`useParams`). With no game on the board (an empty study) it draws nothing.
- **Each choice saves at once**: a `<form>` whose button is the whole row — the ring, the words and the drawing —, as «Qué aprender» does in the course's settings. `setGameView` writes that one setting and nothing else; the page is rendered again, and the board turns (the viewer derives its side from `orientation` on every render).
- **Each clean-up asks first**, in one `PlatformConfirmPopup` mounted as a sibling and opened over the panel with the words of the row pressed; a chapter some class shows asks for the box that says its block will be left empty (`confirmClassBlocks`). After clearing annotations or variations the page is loaded again, since the server rewrote the PGN the page holds; deleting sends the browser on.
