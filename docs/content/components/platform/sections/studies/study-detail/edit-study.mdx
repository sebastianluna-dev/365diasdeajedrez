## Purpose

Editing a study's own data, in a [`PlatformPopup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-popup) named «Editar el estudio» (size `sm`), like [`NewStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/new-study). The one subtlety is the kind: "Mis partidas" is unique per student and a collection cannot stop being one without leaving the students who received it looking at something else, so for those the kind selector is not rendered at all. A dropdown with a single, unchangeable option would only get in the way, and the action would not accept the field anyway.

## Behaviour

- **On the shell** (2026-10-06): the strip with «Editar el estudio» and the cross is `PlatformPopup`'s; the form and the foot are the content, which pads itself. The trigger (its own boxed button, or the host's bars) calls `open(event)` with the click, so the focus comes back to it on closing, and the popup focuses the first control of the content, the name: the field no longer needs `autoFocus`.
- **On the platform's form** (2026-10-05), like [`NewStudy`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/new-study): `PlatformForm` over `updateStudy.bind(null, id)`, with `onSubmit` closing the popup — it only runs once the browser found the form valid. Until then the fields were hand-made `<label>`s with an `<input>` each, and an empty name got the browser's own bubble; now it gets the field's message, «Ponle un nombre al estudio.», and the popup stays open.
- **Nombre** (required, 120 characters), **Descripción** (optional, 500 characters, a `<textarea>` of three rows, as on creation: it was a one-line input until the owner asked for the textarea) and, when the kind can change, **Tipo** (`PlatformSelect` in a `PlatformFormField`, which gives it its `name` and the `id` its label points at). When it cannot, a line takes the selector's place: «El tipo «Mis partidas» no se cambia.».
- **The row of actions is the two buttons alone**, at its end. It also carried the note about the kind, which beside «Cancelar» and «Guardar» was squeezed into three lines; the note that remains is the line above for a kind that stays, and the select says the rest.
- **The foot only leads to the confirmation.** Until 2026-10-06 «Eliminar estudio» closed the form before calling `onDelete`, and the confirmation took its place; now the form stays open and the confirmation ([`PlatformConfirmPopup`](/components/platform/shared/platform-confirm-popup), mounted by the host as a sibling) stacks over it. Cancelling the confirmation leaves the form as it was; deleting closes both, the host's `onDone` closing this one.

## Markup & styling

Block `.edit-study` (61 lines): the boxed trigger and the content. Until 2026-10-05 it also had an `inline` trigger, a word in the row of actions under the study's name beside the board; that row became [`StudySettings`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/study-settings), three bars that open this popup through its handle and hand it `onDelete`.

**Elements:** `.edit-study__open`, `.edit-study__form` (the content's padding), `.edit-study__kind` (the line for a kind that stays), `.edit-study__actions`, `.edit-study__danger`, `.edit-study__destroy` (the foot with «Eliminar estudio», only with `onDelete`). The fields are `PlatformForm`'s and draw themselves; the strip, the width and the frame are `PlatformPopup`'s.

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-button`, and `PlatformForm`'s `.platform-form` with its fields.

**Tokens:** `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-danger` (the foot's «Eliminar estudio»), `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-muted`

No media queries.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/studies/study-detail/study-detail.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { EditStudy } from "./edit-study.comp";

<EditStudy
  id={study.id}
  name={study.name}
  description={study.description}
  kindCode={study.kindCode}
  kindLabel={study.kindLabel}
  kinds={kinds}
  canChangeKind={study.permissions.canChangeKind}
/>;
```
