## Purpose

Where «Ajustes» leads, from the menu of the bar. Two cards: who they are, and the one thing they decide.

Three cards: who they are, how it looks, and the one thing they decide about studying.

**Their details are shown and not edited.** A name and an email are changed by whoever runs the academy, so the card says so instead of offering a form that would fail. **The daily goal is theirs**, and it is the same setting the dashboard offers beside the streak — the same action, `updateDailyGoal`, with the page it sits on carried in a hidden `returnTo` so a failure comes back here and not there.

**Their profile** (while `PLAY_ENABLED` is on): the user name, which is the profile's address (`setUserSlug`: the CHECK's pattern, not a reserved word, «Ese nombre de usuario ya está en uso» on the unique index; the hint says the old address stops working), and the status line and the flag (`setProfileDetails`, a `PlatformSelect` over `federationOptions()` with «Sin bandera» as its empty option). Two `PlatformForm`s, each with its «Guardar».

**The theme is chosen here in full**: «El del sistema», «Claro» and «Oscuro», one form each with the answer bound into `setTheme` so no field travels that could be forged. What is marked is what was **chosen** — with «El del sistema» the theme on screen is the machine's, which the server cannot know (see [Themes](/architecture/themes)).

## Props

| Prop        | Type                | Required | Meaning                                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `account`   | `AccountSettings`   | yes      | `displayName`, `email`, `memberSinceLabel`, `goalMinutes`.                   |
| `theme`     | `"light" \| "dark"` | no       | What they chose, from the `tema` cookie; absent means «El del sistema».      |
| `errorCode` | `string`            | no       | Code from `?error=`, translated with `STUDENT_ERROR_MESSAGES` into a notice. |

## Markup & styling

Block `.account-settings`: a column of cards capped at 620px — a field of settings is read down, not across. The details are a `<dl>` laid out as a two-column grid (`display: contents` on each pair), with a hairline over every row but the first, since a line under the heading would close it instead of separating two facts. At `(width < 640px)` the grid becomes one column and the term goes over its value.

**Elements:** `.account-settings__card`, `__title`, `__facts`, `__fact`, `__term`, `__value`, `__note`, `__themes`, `__theme`, `__theme-label`, `__theme-note`, `__form` (a `PlatformForm`, its submit at the end of the last row; the goal's form was moved onto it on 2026-10-08, and with it went `__field` and `__field-label`)
