## Purpose

The page of every tool, as the owner drew it on 2026-10-25 («Herramientas A · Lanzador», in the new design system). Until then `/herramientas` only redirected to the analysis board, which keeps its own address (`/herramientas/tablero-de-analisis`). The page bleeds onto the canvas on `.platform-workspace` with **no side column** (`platform-workspace_side_none`), and the bar names it «Herramientas» with the nine squares (`TOOLS_PAGE`, [`PlatformPageName`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-page-name)). It is reached from «Ver todas las herramientas» at the foot of the tools menu ([`PlatformAppsMenu`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-apps-menu)).

**Its tools are the menu's.** The page composes them exactly as the shell does — `buildPlatformApps({ ...roles, hasExerciseSource })`, the widest role's list behind the same switches (`EXERCISES_ENABLED`, `PLAY_ENABLED`, `ADVENTURE_ENABLED`, `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`, `MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`) — so a teacher or the staff see here what their menu lists, and no tile is a tool the menu does not have. Each tool of that list carries its row (`group`) and its one line (`description`), and `groupPlatformApps` sorts them into the rows of `PLATFORM_APP_GROUPS`, leaving out a row with nothing in it:

- **Student:** «Practicar» («Jugar», «Ejercicios», «Puzzle Rush»), «Aprender» («Cursos», «Clases», «Estudiar») and «Analizar» («Tablero de análisis», «Explorador», «Entrenador»).
- **Teacher:** «Enseñar» (the panel, the classes, the calendar, the students, the bank, the academy's classes and «Mi ficha»), «Aprender» and «Analizar».
- **Staff:** «Administrar» (the panel, the classes, the students, the teachers and the courses) and «Banco de ejercicios» («Ejercicios», «Revisión», «Series», «Importar»).

In the panel (`ToolsFinder`):

- **The title**, «Todas tus herramientas», and «Las mismas del botón de cuadritos, con lo que hace cada una.», with **the search** at its right: Material's search bar, as the catalog's (a pill in the container's grey that turns white with a shadow while it has the focus). It is a filter, not a form: it narrows the tiles as one types, by the tool's name or its line, ignoring case and accents («analisis» finds «Tablero de análisis»), never validates nor errs. A row with nothing left goes; with nothing left at all, «Ninguna herramienta coincide con «…».» (in a `role="status"`, always in the page so it is announced).
- **The rows**, in a `<nav aria-label="Herramientas">`: at the left a column with what the row is for («Para resolver y jugar, a tu ritmo.») and how many tools it shows («3 herramientas»), and the tiles four to a row. A tile is a link, the whole of it: the tool's drawing ([`NavArt`](/components/platform/sections/shell/nav-art), the one of its tile in the menu) on a grey well, its name and its line. Pointed at or focused, the well turns white and the drawing tilts and its moving piece bobs, as in the menu; stilled under reduced motion.
- **«Atajo»**, a white pill with a check at the well's corner, on the tools of the menu's «Tus atajos» (`isShortcut`: the role's first `PLATFORM_SHORTCUTS`), and at the panel's foot a strip in the primary's wash with the nine squares: «Las marcadas como **Atajo** son las seis que ves primero al abrir el menú de cuadritos de la barra.» (the count is the shortcuts' own).

**From the design, adapted** (2026-10-25): «Ejercicios» and «Ejercicios por tema» are one tool, «Ejercicios» (the owner's change: the topic is chosen in «Ejercicios»' side column, and `/ejercicios/temas` redirects there), so «Practicar» holds three tools and «Estudiar» is now among «Tus atajos». «Jugar»'s line says «Contra un bot o en el modo aventura.» (the canvas also said «otra persona», which the platform does not have) and drops the adventure with its switch off. The canvas drew only the student's page; the teacher's and the staff's rows are named from what their tools are for.

## Markup & styling

Block `.tools-launcher` (25 lines), on `.platform-workspace__panel`: `__hint`, `__hint-icon`, `__hint-text`.

Block `.tools-finder` (290 lines).

**Elements:** `.tools-finder__top`, `.tools-finder__head`, `.tools-finder__title`, `.tools-finder__subtitle`, `.tools-finder__search`, `.tools-finder__search-icon`, `.tools-finder__input`, `.tools-finder__groups`, `.tools-finder__group`, `.tools-finder__what`, `.tools-finder__group-title`, `.tools-finder__group-line`, `.tools-finder__count`, `.tools-finder__tiles`, `.tools-finder__item`, `.tools-finder__tile`, `.tools-finder__well`, `.tools-finder__art`, `.tools-finder__pin`, `.tools-finder__pin-icon`, `.tools-finder__name`, `.tools-finder__line`, `.tools-finder__empty`

**Other classes it renders:** `.platform-workspace__panel`, `.nav-art__move` (animated on the tile's hover).

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1280px)` — three tiles to a row. `(width < 1024px)` — each row's column above its tiles. `(width < 768px)` — the search under the title and as wide, two tiles to a row. `(width < 640px)` — the title a step down, one tile to a row.

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-*` (panel, containers, divider, row edge, primary and its wash, on-surface and its variant), `--platform-shadow-card`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-md`/`-lg`, `--platform-font-display`.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/herramientas/page.tsx`:

```tsx
await getCurrentUser();
const roles = await getSessionRoles();
const hasExerciseSource = needsStudentExerciseAccess(roles) ? await getStudentExerciseAccess() : false;
const apps = buildPlatformApps({ ...roles, hasExerciseSource });

<div className="platform-page platform-page_width_full platform-page_frame_bleed">
  <div className="platform-workspace platform-workspace_side_none">
    <ToolsLauncherSection apps={apps} />
  </div>
</div>;
```
