## Purpose

K1, chosen on 2026-10-08: two fields with their labels and one «Cargar» that reads whichever has text — the PGN when both do, as the FEN's hint says. A `PlatformForm` that submits nothing: `readPositionInput` checks it in the browser, and a failure is drawn under its own field («No es un FEN válido.», «No es un PGN válido.») through the field's `serverError`, cleared as soon as anything is typed. A good one calls `onLoad` with the PGN to open and empties the fields.

## Props

| Prop     | Type                    | Required | Meaning                               |
| -------- | ----------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `onLoad` | `(pgn: string) => void` | yes      | The board starts again from this PGN. |

## Markup & styling

Since 2026-10-23 it is the white box of the side column's «Cargar» on the analysis board's workspace (`--platform-m3-panel`, `--md-sys-shape-corner-lg`), with Material's tonal «Cargar» across it (`mdButton("tonal", "sm")`). Its 240px ask for compact fields: the block sets `PlatformForm`'s labels and hint a size down in `--platform-m3-on-surface-variant`, the controls in the monospaced face at `--platform-type-2xs` on `--platform-m3-outline` (the placeholders «Pega una posición» and «Pega una partida» in the platform's face), the textarea two rows high, and the focus in the primary's blue — a field with an error keeps the form's red (`:not([data-invalid])`).
