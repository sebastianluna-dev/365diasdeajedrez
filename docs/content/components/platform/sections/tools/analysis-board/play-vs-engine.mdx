## Purpose

Planning decision B, drawn as T3 asks and, since 2026-10-23, as the new design system's side column draws it: the white box under the group «Jugar contra el módulo». Analysing, the colour as two cells of `PlatformChoiceCards` (`layout="line"`), each the disc of its colour before its name — the side to move at the node on the board first — and Material's tonal «Jugar esta posición». Playing, the box turns the pale blue of `--platform-m3-info-container`: «Juegas con blancas» after its disc, «Lo que juegues queda como variante del análisis.» or how it ended («Jaque mate: ganaste.», «El módulo dejó de responder. Lo jugado queda como variante.»), said politely to a screen reader, and «Volver al análisis» on white. Whose turn it is is no longer said here: the panel says it in the engine's place (`GameViewer`'s `play.headline`).

Dropped in the move: the box's own heading (the group's title says it) and its line «A máxima potencia y al instante, sin reloj.», which the design leaves out; the module still plays that way.

## Markup & styling

The discs are the pieces' colours, the same in both themes (`--platform-m3-piece-white`, `--platform-m3-piece-black`). The choice cards keep their radio's mark and the selection's blue; this block only lays each cell out in a row and a size down (rules of four classes, to outweigh the line layout's three). «Volver al análisis» is Material's elevated button made flat and white (`--container-elevation: none`, `--container-color: var(--platform-m3-panel)`).
