## Purpose

The Google-style header (2026-10-19, option A1 of the calendar canvas): every page keeps at the right of the bar only two things, this button and the avatar ([`PlatformUserMenu`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-user-menu)); the rest of the bar is the page's ([`PlatformPageName`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-page-name) and [`PlatformHeaderContext`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-header-context)). The dropdowns of the bar («Enseñar», «Material», «Aprender», «Herramientas»…) and the search field that did not search are gone: this panel is the whole navigation.

| Prop   | Type           | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                      |
| ------ | -------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `apps` | `PlatformApps` | yes      | `{ shortcuts, more }`, composed by role and switches in the shell with `buildPlatformApps(roles)`. Here they are only drawn. |

## Behaviour

- **The button** is a quiet square `.platform-button` with the nine squares (`GridIcon`, Classic Solid), named «Herramientas»; open, it keeps the quiet button's pressed grey (`aria-expanded`).
- **The panel** opens under it, aligned to its right edge, portalled to `<body>`: a grey tray (`--platform-bg`) with «Tus atajos» on a white card — the first `PLATFORM_SHORTCUTS` (six) tools of the role, two rows of three — and the rest under the card, three to a row. Each tool is a tile: its drawing at 48px and its name. «Tus atajos» cannot be edited yet: the six are the role's first six.
- **The page's tile is marked** (`aria-current="page"`, the accent's wash and ink): `activeApp(apps, pathname)` gives the tool whose address is the longest that starts the page's, so `/profesor` and `/profesor/alumnos` never both light up, and the staff's «Revisión» wins over «Ejercicios» on the review queue.
- **A tile closes the panel** as it leads away (the open state is the component's). Escape and a click outside close it too; the focus moves into the panel and back to the button, with the primitive.
- **Pointed at or focused**, a tile's drawing tilts and rises and its moving piece bobs (`.nav-art__move`); all of it is stilled under reduced motion. The panel unfolds in 160 ms and folds in 120.

## The tools of each role

`buildPlatformApps(roles, features)` in `constants/platform/nav-items.const.ts`, the widest role's list:

- **Student:** «Jugar» (with `PLAY_ENABLED`), «Ejercicios», «Ejercicios por tema» and «Puzzle Rush» (with `EXERCISES_ENABLED`: for every student with `"all"`, or only one who has something there with `"withTeacher"`, asked of `getStudentExerciseAccess()` only then), «Cursos», «Clases», «Estudiar», and the tools: «Tablero de análisis» (with `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`), «Explorador» and «Entrenador» (with the board and `MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED`).
- **Teacher:** «Panel del profesor», «Clases que imparto», «Calendario», «Mis alumnos», «Ejercicios» (with its switch), «Tablero de análisis», then «Clases prediseñadas», «Cursos», «Estudiar», «Explorador», «Entrenador» and «Mi ficha». No «Jugar».
- **Staff:** «Administración», «Clases», «Alumnos», «Profesores», «Cursos», and with the exercises' switch «Ejercicios», «Revisión», «Series» and «Importar», each page of the bank reached straight from the menu.

Hiding a tool authorises nothing: every page and action checks the role again.
