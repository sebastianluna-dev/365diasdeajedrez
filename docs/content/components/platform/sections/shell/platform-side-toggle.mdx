## Purpose

The header of the new design system (2026-10-22) opens with Gmail's three bars. Pressed, they write `data-side-nav="hidden"` on the root, and `platform.css` hides `.platform-workspace__side`, so the panel takes the whole width; pressed again, they take it away. The button says what it will do («Ocultar la barra lateral» / «Mostrar la barra lateral») and carries `aria-pressed`.

The shell draws it on every page, always (2026-10-22): every page will have its side column, and when it was shown only where a column existed it came and went on a reload, and the page's name jumped with it. The choice is not kept: a new visit shows the column.

No stylesheet of its own: it is Material's standard icon button (`icon-btn icon-btn-standard icon-btn-sm`, 40px).
