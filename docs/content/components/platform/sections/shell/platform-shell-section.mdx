## Purpose

The internal layout of every authenticated screen: a top bar and the `<main>` the page renders into. It is the counterpart of the public site's header and footer, and the only component the platform layout renders.

It reads the session **only to show** who is logged in and which menu they get. It never protects anything: if there is no user it simply omits the user block. What blocks the way is the DAL inside each page (Next 16 advises against auth checks in a layout, which does not control the render of its segments), and hiding a menu group authorises nothing, because every page and action of `/profesor` and `/administracion` checks the role again.

## Behaviour

- `getSessionUser()` and `getSessionRoles()` run in parallel. `buildPlatformApps(roles)` returns one list of tools, the widest role's — staff, teacher or student —, cut into «Tus atajos» and the rest; the menu is exclusive by role. For a student, while «Ejercicios» is drawn only for one with a teacher or a series (`needsStudentExerciseAccess`), the shell first asks `getStudentExerciseAccess()` (`services/exercises/exercises.service.ts`, one query per request, memoised) and passes it as `hasExerciseSource`.
- The wordmark is the same inline SVG the public site draws ([`LogoWordmarkIcon`](/components/site/shared/logo#logowordmarkicon)), `aria-hidden` inside a link whose accessible name is `LOGO_LABEL`. It links to `homeRouteFor(roles)`, the role's home rather than the student dashboard, because with the exclusive menu that page is no longer in a teacher's or staff's menu.
- **The bar is Google's** (2026-10-19, option A1 of the calendar canvas): at the left the wordmark and the page's name ([`PlatformPageName`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-page-name)); in the middle an empty slot (`#platform-header-context`) where a page puts its own controls through [`PlatformHeaderContext`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-header-context) — the calendar's «Hoy», arrows, title and view —; at the right the only two things every page keeps: [`PlatformAppsMenu`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-apps-menu), the nine squares that open the role's tools and are the whole navigation, and [`PlatformUserMenu`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-user-menu), the avatar that opens everything that is the person's. The bar's dropdowns and the search field that did not search went with it. The avatar shows the initials of the first two words of the name (`initialsOf`), which the shell computes and hands down.
- **The new design system's header** (2026-10-22, the «M4» canvas): the three bars of [`PlatformSideToggle`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-side-toggle) first, on every page (every page will have its side column, and a bar that changed shape from one page to another, or while a page loaded, made the page's name jump); the page's drawing and name in the wordmark's place (the wordmark is hidden while [`PlatformPageName`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-page-name) draws one, and stays on a page no tool names); and before the tools a student's streak, [`PlatformStreakChip`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-streak-chip) — a student's only, so `getStudyStreak()` (cached per request) is asked only for someone who neither teaches nor runs the academy.
- **The bar spans the whole width**, with `--platform-space-4` (16px) of padding at each side and `--platform-space-4` at the right, while `<main>` is capped at **1040px** and centred — 944px of room at that padding —, **1200px** from 1536px, where the type and the spaces take their step up and the side padding grows to 56px (1088px of room: without it the text would have less room on a large screen than on a laptop): the wordmark sits at the left edge of the screen and the tools and the avatar at the right (`margin-left: auto`). The bar is the frame of the window, not one more column of the page.
- **A page of the board is not capped by the frame.** `<main>` steps aside for a page that carries `.platform-page_width_wide` or `.platform-page_width_full` (`:has(> …)`), and the page states its own width: 1240px for a study's game, the teacher's view of a student's game and the staff's game of a chapter, what those screens were drawn at; nothing at all for the lesson, which takes the window whole.
- **A page can BE the window.** For one carrying `.platform-page_height_screen`, `<main>` measures exactly `100vh` minus the 70px bar, trims its padding to `--platform-space-6` (24px) above and below — there is no title to give air to, the board of a lesson starts close under the bar, and every px given here is one the board, measured by the height, loses (48 above until 2026-10-06) — and keeps its scroll to itself, so each of the page's columns moves on its own. Such a page also gives up the entrance animation, since `animation: … both` leaves a transform behind and a transform would make the page, not the window, the anchor of anything `position: fixed` inside it — which is how the lesson pins its column of lessons to the window's edges. Under 1280px wide, or on a screen under 620px tall where none of it would fit, it goes back to scrolling like the rest.
- **The bar does not slide with the page.** It is `position: sticky`, and the platform's root carries `overscroll-behavior: none` (see [Styling](/ui/styling#the-platform-does-not-bounce)) so the elastic overscroll of a trackpad does not drag the document — and the bar with it — past its top.
- `<main id="contenido">` is the target of the layout's skip link.
- After `<main>` comes [`ClearResultParams`](/components/platform/sections/shell/clear-result-params), which draws nothing: once a page has drawn what an action said in the address (`?error=`, `?importadas=`), it takes it out of the address, so the next render of the page does not say it again. Being in the shell, every page of the platform has it.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-shell` (204 lines). On a page of the new design system (`:has(.platform-workspace)`) the shell and its bar take Material 3's canvas (`--platform-m3-canvas`) and the bar loses its hairline, so bar, side column and canvas read as one ground. A page that is the whole window (`platform-page_chrome_none`: the adventure's map since 2026-10-25, the owner's «Modo aventura a pantalla completa») hides the bar altogether and brings its own way out.

**Elements:** `.platform-shell__bar`, `.platform-shell__bar-inner`, `.platform-shell__logo` (the link), `.platform-shell__logo-image` (the `<svg>`, 20 px tall like the site's on desktop), `.platform-shell__logo-accent` (the "365", `--platform-logo-accent`: the red of the blog's header, the wordmark's accent on a light background), `.platform-shell__logo-word` and `.platform-shell__logo-marks` (`fill: currentColor`, the link's `--platform-text`), `.platform-shell__context` (the page's slot, which takes the room between the name and the tools), `.platform-shell__tools` (the tools menu and the avatar, pushed to the right edge by `margin-left: auto`), `.platform-shell__content`

No modifiers.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-font`, `--platform-logo-accent`, `--platform-shadow-bar`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-subtle`, `--platform-m3-canvas`

**Breakpoints:** `(width >= 1536px)` raises the cap of `<main>` to 1200px; `(width < 1280px)` narrows the padding of `<main>`; `(width < 1024px)` gives the page's controls a second row of the bar (a page without any keeps one row: the empty slot is not drawn); `(width < 640px)` tightens the padding.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/layout.tsx`:

```tsx
import { PlatformShell } from "@/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-shell.section";

<body className="platform-theme">
  <a className="skip-link" href="#contenido">
    Saltar al contenido
  </a>
  <PlatformShell>{children}</PlatformShell>
</body>;
```
