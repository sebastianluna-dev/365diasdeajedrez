## Purpose

The internal layout of every authenticated screen: a top bar and the `<main>` the page renders into. It is the counterpart of the public site's header and footer, and the only component the platform layout renders.

It reads the session **only to show** who is logged in and which menu they get. It never protects anything: if there is no user it simply omits the user block. What blocks the way is the DAL inside each page (Next 16 advises against auth checks in a layout, which does not control the render of its segments), and hiding a menu group authorises nothing, because every page and action of `/profesor` and `/administracion` checks the role again.

## Behaviour

- `getSessionUser()` and `getSessionRoles()` run in parallel. `buildPlatformNavGroups(roles)` returns exactly one group: staff, teacher or student; the menu is exclusive by role.
- The wordmark is the same inline SVG the public site draws ([`LogoWordmarkIcon`](/components/site/shared/logo#logowordmarkicon)), `aria-hidden` inside a link whose accessible name is `LOGO_LABEL`. It links to `homeRouteFor(roles)`, the role's home rather than the student dashboard, because with the exclusive menu that page is no longer in a teacher's or staff's menu.
- At the right end of the bar sit the two things that are not the menu: [`PlatformSearch`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-search), the field that does not search yet, and [`PlatformUserMenu`](/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-user-menu), the avatar that opens everything that is the person's — where the name, the link to the public site and «Cerrar sesión» now live. The avatar shows the initials of the first two words of the name (`initialsOf`), which the shell computes and hands down.
- **The bar spans the whole width**, with `--platform-space-12` (48px) of side padding, while `<main>` is capped at **1040px** and centred — 944px of room at that padding —: the wordmark sits at the left edge of the screen, the menu next to it and the user block at the right (`margin-left: auto`). The bar is the frame of the window, not one more column of the page.
- **A page of the board is not capped by the frame.** `<main>` steps aside for a page that carries `.platform-page_width_wide` or `.platform-page_width_full` (`:has(> …)`), and the page states its own width: 1240px for a study's game, the teacher's view of a student's game and the staff's game of a chapter, what those screens were drawn at; nothing at all for the lesson, which takes the window whole.
- **A page can BE the window.** For one carrying `.platform-page_height_screen`, `<main>` measures exactly `100vh` minus the 70px bar, trims its padding to `--platform-space-12` (48px) above and `--platform-space-6` (24px) below — there is no title to give air to, and 48 is the air the board of a lesson is given on every side — and keeps its scroll to itself, so each of the page's columns moves on its own. Such a page also gives up the entrance animation, since `animation: … both` leaves a transform behind and a transform would make the page, not the window, the anchor of anything `position: fixed` inside it — which is how the lesson pins its column of lessons to the window's edges. Under 1280px wide, or on a screen under 620px tall where none of it would fit, it goes back to scrolling like the rest.
- **The bar does not slide with the page.** It is `position: sticky`, and the platform's root carries `overscroll-behavior: none` (see [Styling](/ui/styling#the-platform-does-not-bounce)) so the elastic overscroll of a trackpad does not drag the document — and the bar with it — past its top.
- `<main id="contenido">` is the target of the layout's skip link.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-shell` (137 lines).

**Elements:** `.platform-shell__bar`, `.platform-shell__bar-inner`, `.platform-shell__logo` (the link), `.platform-shell__logo-image` (the `<svg>`, 20 px tall like the site's on desktop), `.platform-shell__logo-accent` (the "365", `--platform-logo-accent`: the red of the blog's header, the wordmark's accent on a light background), `.platform-shell__logo-word` and `.platform-shell__logo-marks` (`fill: currentColor`, the link's `--platform-text`), `.platform-shell__tools` (the search field and the avatar, pushed to the right edge by `margin-left: auto`), `.platform-shell__content`

No modifiers.

**Tokens:** `--platform-accent`, `--platform-accent-ink`, `--platform-bg`, `--platform-border`, `--platform-font`, `--platform-logo-accent`, `--platform-shadow-bar`, `--platform-surface`, `--platform-text`, `--platform-text-subtle`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1280px)` lets the navigation wrap under the wordmark, and there `.platform-shell__tools` takes what is left of the top row so the search field shares it with the avatar; `(width < 640px)` tightens the padding.

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/layout.tsx`:

```tsx
import { PlatformShell } from "@/components/platform/sections/shell/platform-shell.section";

<body className="platform-theme">
  <a className="skip-link" href="#contenido">
    Saltar al contenido
  </a>
  <PlatformShell>{children}</PlatformShell>
</body>;
```
