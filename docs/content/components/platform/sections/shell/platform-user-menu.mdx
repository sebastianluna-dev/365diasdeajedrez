## Purpose

Who is signed in, and everything that is theirs, behind the avatar at the right edge of the bar. It replaced the block that spelled the name out loud with a link to the public site and «Salir» beside it: the bar gets its right edge back, and what is the person's gathers in one place.

## Props

| Prop          | Type     | Required | Meaning                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | -------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `displayName` | `string` | yes      | Whoever is signed in: the name the menu heads with, and the avatar's label.                                                                           |
| `initials`    | `string` | yes      | The first letters of that name, which is what the avatar draws. The shell computes them (`initialsOf`), so this stays a component that is given data. |
| `profileHref` | `string` | no       | Their own page, when their role has one. Today only the teacher's (`/profesor/perfil`); without it the name heads the menu and leads nowhere.         |

## Behaviour

- **The day is asked for when the menu is opened**, not handed down by the shell. `readStudyGoal()` costs three queries, and the bar is drawn on every page of the platform: read in the shell it would be three queries on each one — and, the shell being a layout, they would go stale until a full reload. Asked for on opening they cost nothing until someone looks, and what they say is true at the moment of looking. Read once per visit and kept; if the read fails, the block is left out and the menu works.
- **It is drawn by hand, not on Radix**, unlike the platform's other popups. Radix moved it 145px to the right of the avatar under the zoom the platform had until 2026-10-02 — at the right edge of the bar, out through the side of the window — while the select's list, in the middle of a page, is placed correctly by the same machinery; the difference was not run down (IMPROVEMENTS #40). Hung from the avatar with CSS (`position: absolute; right: 0`) there is nothing to measure.
- It follows the platform's menu pattern all the same: `role="menu"` with `role="menuitem"` rows, `aria-haspopup`/`aria-expanded` on the avatar, closing on Escape (returning the focus to the avatar) and on a press outside, up and down walking the rows and wrapping around, and the focus moving into the menu when it opens.
- It stays mounted while it leaves (`useLingering` + `_state_closing`), so it enters in 160ms and leaves in 120ms, both off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The rows are, in order: the name (a link to `/profesor/perfil` for a teacher, a heading for everyone else), **«Ajustes»** ([`AccountSettingsSection`](/components/platform/sections/account/account-settings-section)), **«Modo oscuro»**, «Sitio público» and «Cerrar sesión».
- **The theme row is rendered twice and the stylesheet shows one.** One form asks for dark, the other for light, each with its switch drawn in the state the current theme is in. Which theme that is cannot be known here when nobody has chosen — it is the machine's preference, and only the browser reads it — so `@media (prefers-color-scheme)` and `:root[data-theme]` pick the row (see [Themes](/architecture/themes)). Both are plain forms over `setTheme`, so the switch works without JavaScript.
- **«Cerrar sesión» is a `<form action={logoutAction}>`**, as it always was: signing out does not depend on JavaScript.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-user-menu`, 300px wide, on `--platform-shadow-card`, hung 10px under the avatar and aligned to its right edge, growing out of `top right`.

At the top, the day: the flame on its own disc of `--platform-warning-soft`, the streak in figures, the goal's bar (`--platform-accent-bright`, like the dashboard's: what it measures is the day and not a course) and, under it, what is left of the goal and the minutes done. A hairline that reaches from edge to edge of the card — pulled out of the menu's padding with a negative margin — separates it from the rows.

**Elements:** `.platform-user-menu__day`, `__figure`, `__figure-mark`, `__flame`, `__flame-core`, `__figure-text`, `__figure-value`, `__figure-label`, `__track`, `__fill`, `__day-note`, `__day-count`, `__rule`, `__item`, `__icon`

**Modifiers:** `.platform-user-menu_state_closing` (leaving), `.platform-user-menu__item_role_name` (the name, when it is a heading and not a link), `.platform-user-menu__icon_turn_out` (the arrow of a link that leaves the platform)

**Outside the block:** `.platform-user-menu-anchor` (what the menu is hung from) and `.platform-user-menu__avatar` (the trigger: the 38px disc of the brand's black, which takes the accent while the menu is open).
