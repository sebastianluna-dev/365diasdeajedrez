## Purpose

The Google-style header (2026-10-19) names the page beside the wordmark — «Calendario», «Ejercicios», «Clases» — after a hairline, in the display font. The name is the label of the tool the page belongs to (`activeApp`, the longest address that starts the page's), so a deep page keeps its tool's name («Mis alumnos» on one student's page), and a page no tool leads to (the home, a lesson) shows none. Client, because it follows the address.

## Markup & styling

Block `.platform-page-name` (15 lines): `--platform-font-display` at `--platform-type-xl`, a left hairline (`--platform-border`). **Breakpoints:** `(width < 640px)` hides it: on a phone the bar keeps the wordmark and its two buttons.
