## Purpose

Owns the active review and whether the reviews take turns. Every review's stat card and chat stay mounted in two stacks; each gets a `position` (`center`, `left`, `right`) relative to the active index so CSS can slide them (under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the two stylesheets drop the transition and the review changes in place), and `hidden` marks the inactive ones `aria-hidden`.

Content that moves on its own for more than five seconds needs a way to stop it (WCAG 2.2.2, Pause, Stop, Hide), so a round button beside the dots pauses and plays the reviews.

## Behaviour

- `activeIndex` state. While the reviews advance, an effect sets a timeout of `AUTO_ADVANCE_MS` (6000) that moves to the next one modulo the length; it depends on `activeIndex`, so clicking a dot, or resuming, gives the review on screen its full six seconds.
- They advance when they are playing and nothing holds them: not while a **mouse** is over the carousel (`pointerenter`/`pointerleave` with `pointerType === "mouse"`; a touch is not a hover), and not while the **keyboard's focus** is inside it (a focus that matches `:focus-visible`; a dot clicked with the mouse keeps its focus after the pointer leaves, and would otherwise stop them for good). Both resume on their own.
- **Playing** is the button's choice once made (`chosen`), and until then the system's preference: under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the reviews start paused, read with `useSyncExternalStore` over `matchMedia` (the server assumes they play). The button reads «Pausar los testimonios» or «Reproducir los testimonios» as its `aria-label`, with the pause or play icon; it is not drawn when there is a single review.
- `getSlidePosition(index, active, total)` returns `center` for the active one, `right` for the next half of the ring and `left` for the rest.
- Dots call `setActiveIndex` with the label "Ver testimonio de …" plus the name.
