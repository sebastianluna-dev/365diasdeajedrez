## Purpose

`#equipo`: «Un equipo de maestros, _no una sola cara_» (the owner's decision: the academy is a team, never one teacher as a brand), the panel «Te asignamos un maestro del equipo» with its three steps and «Agenda tu clase de prueba».

**The teachers.** The canvas draws five portraits with «[Foto]», «[Nombre]» and «[Título FIDE]». No one is invented: `academyHome.team.members` is empty and the list is not drawn. When the owner fills it (name, title, one line), each teacher takes the canvas's frames in turn — an arch, a ringed circle, a cookie, a rounded square, a clover — with an icon in place of the photo until there are photos (a photo would go through `next/image`).
