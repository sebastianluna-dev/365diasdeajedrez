## Purpose

`#plataforma`: «Lo que haces _entre clases_». Six things the account brings (the teacher's exercises, the academy's series, practice by theme and level, Puzzle Rush, studies and explorer, streak and statistics); the one at `platform.shown` is lit as a chosen row and drawn beside the list: Puzzle Rush in its 3-minute mode, with the clock, the count and the run of solved and missed puzzles. The window is one `role="img"` with its name; the card of the streak and the daily goal (a ring) floats over its bottom right corner, where it hides no square.
