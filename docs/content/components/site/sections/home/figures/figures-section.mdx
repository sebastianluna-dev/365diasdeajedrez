## Purpose

Students, classes given, titled teachers and years teaching (`#cifras`), each on a shape of its own — the cookie, the arch, the clover and the rounded square — with «Conoce al equipo» (`#equipo`).

**No figure is invented** (the owner's decision): the canvas has «[N]» in each, and the config keeps `value: null` until the owner gives the real one. The section renders nothing while any value is null, so today it is not on the page; it appears, as drawn, the day the four values are written in `config/academy-home.config.ts`.
