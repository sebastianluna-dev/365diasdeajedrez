## Purpose

`#como-funciona`, the header's «Clases»: the two formats, the classroom, the three steps (book on WhatsApp, join from the platform, review the summary, recording and exercises) and the two promises (paid classes do not expire; a missed class gets another date).

**The facts over the canvas.** The canvas titled it «Tu clase, _dentro de tu plataforma_» and opened with «Sin Zoom ni Meet», which contradicted its own FAQ. Today a class is joined with its link from the platform and the built-in classroom is «Próximamente» (the owner's decision), so the heading says «Tu clase, _desde tu plataforma_», the lead says the classroom is coming, step 2 says «con el enlace de tu clase», and the classroom mock carries its «Próximamente».
