## Purpose

The first screen of `/` (`#inicio`): the eyebrow, the `h1` «Aprende ajedrez con un maestro, _todos los días_», the lead, the three promises, «Agenda tu clase de prueba» (WhatsApp, with the booking message) and «Ve cómo funciona» (`#como-funciona`), and the note that the main button opens WhatsApp. Beside them, the Spanish Opening after 3.Ab5 on the material board, with a seal and a caption that names the move.

## Behaviour

- **The cookie.** The board is clipped to a nine-lobed cookie that turns a lobe in four steps, comes back to its first shape and opens into the whole board, in 4.7 s after a 0.4 s pause; the halo behind it turns with it and fades. It plays **once**, on load, and stays open (`animation-fill-mode: both`). The outlines are CSS `shape()`s in percentages of the board, computed on the server with the same 181 points each so the browser can morph one into the next, and handed over as custom properties on the element; `path()` would be in pixels and would not follow the column as it narrows.
- **Where `shape()` is not drawn** (`@supports`), the board is simply whole and the halo stays still.
- **Reduced motion** shows the board open from the start and no halo.
