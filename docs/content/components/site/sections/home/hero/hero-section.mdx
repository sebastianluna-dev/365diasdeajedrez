## Purpose

The first screen of `/`. Its image is the Largest Contentful Paint of the whole site, so the section is where the LCP work lives: `preload` announces the image in the `<head>`, `fetchPriority="high"` raises its priority in that preload and in the `<img>`, and `sizes` describes what actually renders (the card: 300 px under 1024 px, 420 px above) rather than the viewport width. Without `sizes`, mobile requested twice the pixels.

## Props

None.

## Behaviour

- Awaits `getHeroData()`; the title, description, CTA (`label`, `href`) and image (`src`, `alt`) come from the CMS.
- The six badges ("Cálculo", "Visualización", "Estrategia", "Finales", "Aperturas", "Partidas") are fixed in code, each with a black piece glyph and one of three offset modifiers.
- `id="inicio"` so the header can link back to the top.

## Markup & styling

Block `.hero` (190 lines).

**Elements:** `.hero__inner`, `.hero__glow`, `.hero__grid`, `.hero__copy`, `.hero__title`, `.hero__text`, `.hero__actions`, `.hero__visual`, `.hero__stage`, `.hero__card`, `.hero__card-inner`, `.hero__badges`

**Tokens:** `--font-gramatika`, `--site-header-height`

**Breakpoints:** `(max-width: 1024px)` stacks the grid and shrinks the card; `(max-width: 1023px)` scales the portrait to 1.25 and adds 20 px under it, which `(max-width: 419px)` undoes because the narrowest screens have no room for the bigger circle; `(max-width: 720px)` hides the badges, drops the vertical padding and gives the section `min-height: calc(100svh - var(--site-header-height))` so it covers the whole first screen under the header, with its content centred in that space (`100vh` is declared first as the fallback for browsers without `svh`).

**Other classes:** `.button.button_variant_primary.button_size_hero` from `globals.css`.
