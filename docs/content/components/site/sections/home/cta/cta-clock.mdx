## Purpose

The sense of urgency on the CTA, reusing the analogue dial of `/reloj-de-ajedrez` in its `compact` form. The countdown lasts `STICKY_CTA_DURATION_SECONDS` (900) and restarts at zero.

## Props

None.

## Behaviour

- `useCountdownTimer(900, 1000)`: one re-render per second is enough for a `mm:ss` readout that stays on screen for the whole visit; the remaining time is computed from `Date.now()`, so a slow tick does not drift.
- Passes `hands` to the dial, `active` always on, `flagged` never, and `lowTime` when 60 seconds or fewer remain.

## Markup & styling

Elements of `.sticky-cta` (25 lines): `.sticky-cta__clock`, `.sticky-cta__clock-time`.

**Tokens:** `--color-dark`

**Breakpoints:** `(max-width: 1023px)` hides the clock.

## Usage

From `components/site/sections/home/cta/cta.section.tsx`:

```tsx
import { CtaClock } from "./cta-clock.comp";

<div className="sticky-cta__inner">
  <CtaClock />…
</div>;
```
