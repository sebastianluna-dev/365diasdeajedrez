## Purpose

`#testimonios`: «Lo que cuentan _nuestros alumnos_». The testimonials stay the CMS's, as the old chat-style reviews were, mapped onto the canvas's cards: a review's messages are its quote, its initial is the avatar, its name the signature and its highlighted figure («Torneos: 3») the chip. They are laid round-robin into three columns, and the first card of the middle one is tinted and bigger.

The canvas's «[Cita]», «[Nombre]», «[Nivel]» and «desde [año]» are never drawn, nor its line «Publicados con permiso de cada alumno»: the CMS's reviews have no field for any of them, and the page does not claim what it cannot back. With no review in the CMS the section returns nothing.
