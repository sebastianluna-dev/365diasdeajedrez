## Purpose

A physical clock drawn on the page: a case with two dials, the two press buttons above it, and the controls under it. The hook returns everything the markup needs (`white`, `black`, `controls`, `playLabel`, `statusText`, `toggle`, `reset`); the component only renders it.

## Props

| Prop      | Type      | Required   | Meaning                                                                             |
| --------- | --------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `enabled` | `boolean` | no, `true` | Passed to the hook; when `false` the clock does not tick and ignores the space bar. |

## Behaviour

- Each side exposes `time`, `hands`, `active`, `flagged`, `lowTime`, `knobPressed`, `onPress`; the knob buttons ("Botón blancas", "Botón negras") and the dials both call `onPress`.
- `controls` are the time controls from `CLOCK_TIME_CONTROLS`, each with `label`, `active`, `onSelect`.
- The instructions mention the space bar; the hook listens for it when enabled.
