## Purpose

The closing band of every public page except the chess clock. Its links and the academy's e-mail are fixed in `config/site.config.ts`; the WhatsApp number and the message its link opens with are the `site-settings` Global's. There is no «Aviso de privacidad» link: the page does not exist yet (it is one of the owner's open points).

## Props

| Prop    | Type            | Required                         | Meaning             |
| ------- | --------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| `links` | `SiteNavLink[]` | no, `siteConfig.shell.footerNav` | The footer's links. |

## Behaviour

- Three ways in, as pills: WhatsApp (a new tab, said to a screen reader), e-mail (`mailto:`) and «Iniciar sesión».
- The legal line writes the year it is rendered in, the e-mail, the WhatsApp number and «Clases en línea · Español».

## Markup & styling

Block `.site-footer` (120 lines).

**Elements:** `.site-footer__inner`, `.site-footer__top`, `.site-footer__links`, `.site-footer__link`, `.site-footer__ways`, `.site-footer__way`, `.site-footer__way-icon`, `.site-footer__legal`, `.site-footer__language`

**Tokens:** `--color-m4-panel`, `--color-m4-divider`, `--color-m4-on-surface`, `--color-m4-on-surface-variant`, `--color-m4-container`, `--color-m4-container-high`, `--color-m4-primary`, `--font-site-text`

**Breakpoints:** `(width < 1024px)` narrows the gutter; `(width < 640px)` stacks the wordmark, the links and the pills, and the legal line.
