## Purpose

The lesson's notation in the new design system (2026-10-22), after the owner's design of the lesson: what is read is **one move at a time**, not a page of prose. The line being followed is a row of pills; what the author says is the note on the move on the board, not every comment of the game at once; and the way on is the board's own: its keys and the arrow keys (a «Siguiente» button under the note went on 2026-10-23: it only repeated them).

A variation is not printed after the main line, as [`MoveTree`](/components/chess/game-viewer/move-tree) does: it is **entered** from the move it branches at, offered in the note as «Otras jugadas», and from then on it is the line (`lineThrough` follows the path to the move on the board and then each move's main continuation). Going back to the main line is a pill of the moves played before the branch.

## Behaviour

- **The line** (`aria-label="Jugadas"`): every move of it as a pill, White's with its number before it, and a line opening on Black's move with «N...». The moves played are the ink, the one on the board is the primary with `aria-current="step"`, and the ones to come are dimmed (`--platform-m3-outline`, which still reads at 4.5:1 on the white panel). A move's NAGs are drawn as their symbols and said in words to a screen reader.
- **The note** (`<section aria-label="La jugada">`), on the container: the move on the board, numbered («6. Ae3»), its comment, and «Otras jugadas» — the variations that branch where the next move is played (`alternativesAt`), each numbered since it stands alone. Before the first move it is «Para empezar», with the owner's pinned note and the PGN's opening comment. A move with no comment and no alternatives has no note. The card comes in with a fade and a rise of 4px in 160 ms (`ease-out`, `move-notes-note-in`) when a move with a note follows one without. From one note to the next it **stays** (2026-10-23): only its text is keyed by the move and fades to the new one (`move-notes-body-in`), while its height goes from the old to the new in 300 ms on Material's standard curve (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`). `height: auto` is not animatable everywhere yet, so a layout effect pins the height it had, lets it go to what the new text needs and puts `auto` back at `transitionend`; a move pressed mid-transition starts from where the card is. All of it is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
