## Purpose

The lesson's notation in the new design system (2026-10-22), after the owner's design of the lesson: what is read is **one move at a time**, not a page of prose. The main line is a row of pills that never changes; what the author says is the note on the move on the board, not every comment of the game at once; and the way on is the board's own: its keys and the arrow keys (a «Siguiente» button under the note went on 2026-10-23: it only repeated them).

A variation is not printed after the main line, as [`MoveTree`](/components/chess/game-viewer/move-tree) does: it is shown **whole in the note of the main-line move it branches from** (`buildVariationBlocks`: its moves as pills, its comments in the running text, a variation of it in parentheses). Pressing one of its moves puts it on the board, and the row of the main line does not change (2026-10-23; until then the row became the variation): it stays lit on the move the variation branches from (`mainLineAnchor`, the run of leading zeros of the path), the note stays that move's, and the variation's move is the one lit inside it. «Jugada N de M» under the title counts the same main-line move.

## Behaviour

- **The main line** (`aria-label="Jugadas"`): every move of it as a pill, White's with its number before it, and a line opening on Black's move with «N...». The moves played are the ink, the one on the board — or the one a variation on the board branches from — is the primary with `aria-current="step"`, and the ones to come are dimmed (`--platform-m3-outline`, which still reads at 4.5:1 on the white panel). A move's NAGs are drawn as their symbols and said in words to a screen reader.
- **The note** (`<section aria-label="La jugada">`), on the container: the main line's move, numbered («6. Ae3»), its comment, and under «Otra línea» / «Otras líneas» every variation that branches where the next move is played (`alternativesAt`), each whole; the variation's move on the board is the primary pill there. Before the first move it is «Para empezar», with the owner's pinned note and the PGN's opening comment. A move with no comment and no alternatives has no note. The card comes in with a fade and a rise of 4px in 160 ms (`ease-out`, `move-notes-note-in`) when a move with a note follows one without. From one note to the next it **stays** (2026-10-23): only its text is keyed by the main line's move and fades to the new one (`move-notes-body-in`), while its height goes from the old to the new in 300 ms on Material's standard curve (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`). `height: auto` is not animatable everywhere yet, so a layout effect pins the height it had, lets it go to what the new text needs and puts `auto` back at `transitionend`; a move pressed mid-transition starts from where the card is. On a move with nothing said the card **leaves** (2026-10-23): [`useLingering`](/components/chess/game-viewer/use-lingering-hook) keeps the last note mounted for 120 ms with `.move-notes__note_state_closing`, which plays the way in backwards (`move-notes-note-out`, `ease-in`), and then it goes; the note is memoised by the move, since the hook tells one note from the next by identity. All of it is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, where the leaving card is hidden at once.
