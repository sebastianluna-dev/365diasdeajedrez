## Purpose

A list of lessons shows each lesson's game as a line of moves («1.e4 c5 2.Cf3 …»). Reading eight moves in one's head is what makes a line hard to follow, so pointing at a move shows the position it leads to on a small board — as the engine's lines do on the analysis board ([`EnginePanel`](/components/chess/game-viewer/engine-panel)) (2026-10-23).

## Props

| Prop          | Type                 | Required               | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `line`        | `GameLine`           | yes                    | The line: `initialFen?` (absent for the standard position) and `plies`, each `{ label, san }` — as the list writes it («1.e4», «c5», «12…Ab4») and its English SAN, which replays it. `gameMoves` builds it from a PGN. |
| `suffix`      | `string`             | no                     | Written after the last move, after a «·»: how the game ended.                                                                                                                                                           |
| `orientation` | `"white" \| "black"` | no · default `"white"` | Which side is at the bottom of the preview.                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Behaviour

- The moves are one line, cut with an ellipsis where its place ends; each is a `<span>` that, pointed at, shows the board. Leaving the line hides it.
- **The positions are replayed on the first move pointed at**, not before (`sanLineSteps` from the line's start): a list of lessons sends the moves and nothing else. A move that does not fit ends the replay, and the moves after it show no board.
- **The board hangs at a fixed place**, under the start of the line, not under the pointer: following a line move by move does not move it from side to side. When there is no room below (the line's bottom plus 232px past the window's), it opens above.
- It does not catch the pointer (`pointer-events: none`), so it never covers the next move, nor a click on the row that holds it; and it is `aria-hidden`: the moves are already read as text, the board only pictures them. With no pointer that hovers (a phone), it never shows.
- The move shown is lit in the line (`_state_pointed`, the secondary container), and on the board in the amber of the last move.

## Markup & styling

Block `.move-line-preview` (76 lines), `position: relative` so the board hangs from it.

**Elements:** `.move-line-preview__line` (one line, `overflow: hidden` with an ellipsis), `.move-line-preview__move`, `.move-line-preview__board` (200px, the panel's white on a soft shadow, `--md-sys-shape-corner-md` corners)

**Modifiers and states:** `.move-line-preview__move_state_pointed`, `.move-line-preview__board_side_{below,above}`, `.move-line-preview__board_state_closing`

**Tokens:** `--platform-m3-secondary-container`, `--platform-m3-on-secondary-container`, `--platform-m3-panel`, `--platform-m3-row-shadow`, `--platform-space-1`, `--platform-space-3`

The board enters in 160ms (`ease-out`) and leaves in 120ms (`ease-in`), kept mounted for its exit by `useLingering`; both animations are off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. The container that holds it must not clip it: the chapter's cell sets `overflow: visible` (`chapter-study__moves_kind_line`), since the line cuts itself.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/courses/course-study/chapter-study.section.tsx`:

```tsx
<div className="chapter-study__moves chapter-study__moves_kind_line">
  <MoveLinePreview line={lesson.line} suffix={lesson.result} />
</div>
```
