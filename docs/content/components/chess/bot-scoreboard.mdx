## Purpose

H6, chosen on 2026-10-08: the record against one bot reads as a match's scoreboard — you and the rival with your portraits, «2 – 1» large between you, «1 tabla» small underneath. Only finished games with a result count (`countsForScore` in `services/play/play-rules.ts`); an aborted game is not a game. Before the first game it says «Primera partida» under a 0 – 0.

The figures are `aria-hidden`; the group's `aria-label` says the whole record in words («Marcador contra Don Chuy: 2 ganadas, 1 perdidas, 1 tabla»).
