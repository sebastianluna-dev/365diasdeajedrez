## Purpose

A rival's page of the passport, opened by pressing its stamp on the map. The popup's strip (the platform's) says «Ficha de rival · N.º 05 de 12» and carries the cross; the card is what is under it and pads itself:

- the **stamp large** on four corners of tape, the name and the role (a locked rival's stamp is the greyed proof with a red padlock over it);
- **where the person stands**: beaten — a postmark, the date and «Ganaste en 24 jugadas» (half the plies of the game that first won); available — «Intento 2» and «Ya lo intentaste una vez: la historia te espera en esta página.»; locked — «Supera a Doña Cuca para llegar aquí.»;
- the rival's **phrase** in pencil, and the facts of a passport page: the level («≈ 783»), the difficulty (its word and five pawns), the favourite opening with its moves, the style as two stamps, where it is played, the **stars** («Al primer intento» / «En 2 intentos» for a beaten rival; «si ganas en el intento 2» for the next, from `starsForAttempts`) and the **keepsake** (the drawing when beaten, or «Al ganarle: un recuerdo para tu cuaderno» with the sealed envelope);
- the **form**: the colour on `PlatformChoiceCards` (blancas, negras, al azar) with `adventure` and `encounter` as hidden fields, «Cancelar» and the button — `_variant_ink`, with the arrow on its right and `pendingLabel="Preparando…"`: «Empezar» for the available rival, «Volver a retar» for a beaten one (it touches neither stars nor keepsake). A locked rival has no form: the server would bounce it anyway.

With a game against a bot already open the card warns («Empezar otra cierra la que tienes en curso contra Lupita…») and its button does not submit: it hands the form's data to the map (`onReplace`), which asks in a `PlatformConfirmPopup` opened over the card and then submits with `replace=1`.

## Props

| Prop           | Type                                  | Required | Meaning                                                                                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `adventure`    | `string`                              | yes      | The adventure's slug: sent by the form and used to find the portrait and the keepsake. |
| `encounter`    | `EncounterView`                       | yes      | The rival with its state, attempts, stars and the game that beat it.                   |
| `postmark`     | `string`                              | yes      | The place written round the stamp's postmark.                                          |
| `previousName` | `string \| null`                      | yes      | The rival before this one, for a locked card.                                          |
| `playerName`   | `string`                              | yes      | Written on the membership card keepsake.                                               |
| `openGame`     | `OpenGameSummary \| null`             | yes      | The person's open game against a bot, if any.                                          |
| `action`       | `(FormData) => void \| Promise<void>` | yes      | `startAdventureEncounter`, or a stub.                                                  |
| `onReplace`    | `(FormData) => void`                  | yes      | With an open game: the button was pressed; the host asks for confirmation.             |
| `onCancel`     | `() => void`                          | yes      | «Cancelar»: the host closes the popup.                                                 |
