## Purpose

An `li` for the shelf's list: the cover, the «Próximamente» stamp across its label, the name (an `h3`, under the shelf's heading) and what it will explore, with its first letter capitalised («Son jarocho, jarana y el puerto»). It has no action and no date: it is a promise, not a link.

`NotebookCoverArt` draws each cover in **two inks** — the colour of the cloth and one ink for the motif, some tints being the same ink at less strength: Veracruz a jarana over waves (blue), Chihuahua the sun over the canyon walls (terracotta), Yucatán a cenote over a band of hipil embroidery (cream), Ciudad de México the Angel on her column and a trajinera (magenta), Puebla two volcanoes over talavera tiles (pale) and Tlaxcala a carpet of flowers (green). The cover is decorative (`aria-hidden`, the name is written under it); a slug it has no motif for gets plain brown cloth. The covers are art and keep their colours in both themes.

## Markup & styling

Block `.notebook-spine` (73 lines); the root also carries `.adventure`.

**Elements:** `.notebook-spine__cover`, `__art`, `__soon`, `__name`, `__reason`

**Tokens:** `--adventure-on-wood`, `--adventure-on-wood-soft`, `--adventure-brick`, `--adventure-cream-bright`, `--adventure-font-title`, `--adventure-font-data`

**Media queries:** `(width < 768px)`. No animation. The cover is 150px wide, or the width of its place on the board when that is less.

## Usage

From `components/adventure/shelf/adventure-shelf/adventure-shelf.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
{
  shelf.coming.map((notebook) => <NotebookSpine key={notebook.slug} notebook={notebook} />);
}
```
