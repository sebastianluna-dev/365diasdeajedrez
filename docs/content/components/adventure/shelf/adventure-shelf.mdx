## Purpose

The way to choose an adventure: a block of dark wood with the notebooks that can be opened at the left — open, with the person's progress — and the six that are coming on two boards at the right, each with its cloth cover, «Próximamente» and no action. The shelf is built for several playable adventures: `shelf.notebooks` is a list, and every one of them stands open, the one «Jugar» draws big above included.

**History.** Until 2026-10-25 the shelf was a page of its own, `/jugar/aventura`, a wooden room that filled the window under the bar, with a «Jugar» button back to the cover. That day «Jugar» moved to the new design system and the shelf became Material cards on it, and these components went. On 2026-10-26 the owner asked for the paper shelf back: it is now the lower half of «Jugar» ([`PlayHome`](/components/platform/sections/play/play-home), `#cuadernos`, where `/jugar/aventura` still redirects), **an island of the adventure's paper inside the Material panel** — its wood, its covers, its fonts and its `--adventure-*` tokens, as [Adventure mode](/platform/adventure) allows —, so it lost the room's height and its way back, took the corner of a highlighted block of the panel (`--md-sys-shape-corner-xl`) and its heading became an `h2` («Elige tu cuaderno») under the page's `h1`. Its one button, the open notebook's «Abrir el mapa», is Material's, as on the rest of the page.

## Props

| Prop    | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                       |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `shelf` | `AdventureShelfView` | yes      | The notebooks to open with their counts, and the ones coming. |

## Behaviour

Server only, and it reads nothing itself: `PlayHomeSection` asks `getAdventureShelf()`, one query for all the playable adventures' games, shared (`cache`) with the progress of the notebook drawn above. Nothing is written. It is a block under its `h2`, not a landmark of its own (a second shelf on the same sheet, as on the Storybook overview, would repeat the landmark's name), and the closed notebooks are a list, «Cuadernos que vienen».

## Markup & styling

Block `.adventure-shelf` (116 lines); the root also carries `.adventure`, which declares the paper's tokens.

**Elements:** `.adventure-shelf__head`, `__title`, `__lead`, `__shelves`, `__open`, `__closed`

**Tokens:** `--adventure-wood`, `--adventure-shelf-edge`, `--adventure-leather-deep`, `--adventure-on-wood`, `--adventure-on-wood-soft`, `--adventure-font-title`, `--adventure-font-data`; the panel's `--md-sys-shape-corner-xl` and the platform's space scale

**Media queries:** `(width < 1280px)` (the panel is narrower by the side column: the open notebook stands over the boards), `(width < 640px)` (two notebooks to a board, three boards). No animation.

## Usage

From `components/platform/sections/play/play-home/play-home.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
<div id={SHELF_ID} className="play-home__shelf">
  <AdventureShelf shelf={shelf} />
</div>
```
