## Purpose

The way to choose an adventure: a dark wooden room with the notebooks that can be opened at the left — open, with the person's progress — and the six that are coming on two boards at the right, each with «Próximamente» and no action. A «Jugar» button (the platform's) goes back to the cover. The shelf is built for several playable adventures: `shelf.notebooks` is a list.

Since 2026-10-18 the shelf is the window, like «Jugar»'s desk: the page is `platform-page_height_screen platform-page_frame_bleed`, the wood fills everything under the bar edge to edge, and the room stretches to the page's height, so from 1280px wide and 800px tall nothing scrolls. On a short window (up to 960px, and again up to 860px tall) the room's air tightens and the closed notebooks stand smaller, through `--spine-width` on the boards, which `NotebookSpine` reads.

## Props

| Prop    | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                       |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `shelf` | `AdventureShelfView` | yes      | The notebooks to open with their counts, and the ones coming. |

## Behaviour

`AdventureShelfSection` asks `getAdventureShelf()`, which makes one query for all the playable adventures' games. Nothing is written.

## Markup & styling

Block `.adventure-shelf` (160 lines).

**Elements:** `.adventure-shelf__room`, `__head`, `__title`, `__lead`, `__shelves`, `__open`, `__closed`

**Tokens:** `--adventure-wood`, `--adventure-shelf-edge`, `--adventure-leather-deep`, `--adventure-on-wood`, `--adventure-on-wood-soft`, `--adventure-font-title`, `--adventure-font-data`

**Media queries:** `(width < 1024px)` (one column), `(width < 768px)` (two notebooks to a board, three boards).

## Usage

From `app/(platform)/jugar/aventura/page.tsx`:

