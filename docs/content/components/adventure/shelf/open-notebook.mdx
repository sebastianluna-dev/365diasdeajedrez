## Purpose

An article with the notebook's cover (the wide journey), its place («LA CIUDAD · ABIERTO»), its name, «4 de 12 rivales» with the stars, a bar of twelve segments and «Abrir el mapa», a platform button to the adventure's map. The shelf only knows how many rivals are beaten (`ShelfNotebook`), so the bar and the miniature's states are drawn from that: the first `beaten` segments are filled and the next one is the one to play. The bar is decorative (`aria-hidden`): the count is in the text.
