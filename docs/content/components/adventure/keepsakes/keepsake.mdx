## Purpose

The twelve keepsakes — the bottle cap, the popsicle stick with «¡jaque!», the club's membership card with the person's name written on it, the sticker, the bookmark with a position in pencil, a badge, a shell with a move, a sailor's knot, the 1994 score sheet with no result, the key of room 64, a photograph and the last page signed — each drawn in its own viewBox and generated by `npm run adventure:art`. Stuck on the map (`position`) it is placed in percentages of the world, tilted, and held by a strip of tape; without it, it is only the drawing as wide as what holds it. With `isSealed` it is the envelope with a wax seal, the keepsake still to win. The component is `aria-hidden`: the rival's card says in words what it is.

| `playerName` | `string`                         | yes      | Written on the membership card.                                               |
| `position`   | `{ left, top, width, rotation }` | no       | Stuck on the map; left out, it is only the drawing.                           |
| `isSealed`   | `boolean`                        | no       | The sealed envelope instead of the keepsake.                                  |
| `isSticking` | `boolean`                        | no       | The victory is sticking it down now (it comes in and settles, the tape last). |

## Markup & styling

Block `.keepsake` (86 lines).

**Elements:** `.keepsake__drawing`, `__tape`

**Modifiers and states:** `.keepsake_stuck`, `.keepsake_sealed`, `.keepsake_order_N` (where the tape goes), `.keepsake_phase_stick`

**Tokens:** none: the drawings are art and keep their own colours in both themes. No media queries. **Animation:** `keepsake-stick` and `keepsake-tape` while the victory sticks it down, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
