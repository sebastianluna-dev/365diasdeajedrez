## Purpose

The cover of «Jugar» chosen on 2026-10-09: the notebook open in the middle as the protagonist and two tickets beside it. The left page is the whole journey ([`JourneyMiniature`](/components/adventure/cover/journey-miniature), taped to the page); the right page is [`NotebookCard`](/components/adventure/cover/notebook-card). The tickets are «Partida libre» (the old gallery, now at `/jugar/partida-libre`) and «Contra una persona», which is not valid yet. A pencil and a mug are left on the desk from 1280px. Since 2026-10-18 the desk is the window: the page is `platform-page_height_screen platform-page_frame_bleed`, the wood fills everything under the bar edge to edge, and the notebook takes the height the title leaves (up to 800px), so from 1280px wide and 800px tall nothing scrolls; under 860px tall the lead hides and the notebook's page tightens.

`AdventureCoverSection` reads the person's progress in the one adventure there is and hands it to `AdventureCover`; the split is what lets Storybook draw the cover with a fixture. The block carries the class `.adventure` itself, so the paper's tokens are declared where it is drawn; inside the platform's page it is a rounded wooden panel, not a full-bleed screen.

## Props

| Prop       | Type                    | Required | Meaning                                                                             |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `progress` | `AdventureProgressView` | yes      | From `getAdventureProgress`: every rival with its state, the totals, the open game. |

## Behaviour

Server only. The section makes the one read (the games against the adventure's rivals, plus the open game: two queries on top of the session); nothing is written and nothing is revalidated here. «Elegir un bot» and the notebook's links are plain `Link`s.

**Reflow.** At 1280px and up the tickets stand in a column to the right of the notebook. Below 1280 they go under it in a row; below 768 the book is one column — the wide journey above, the person's page under it — and the tickets stack.

    </div>
