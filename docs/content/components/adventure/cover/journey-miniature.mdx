## Purpose

The picture on the cover's left page and on the shelf's open notebook: the journey from the plaza to the palace, small. Two variants: **tall** for the open notebook and **wide** for a phone and for the shelf's card. The scenery is drawn by hand here, in cut-paper layers with a soft shadow, and keeps its own colours in both themes (it is art); the path, the stamps and everything that says where the person is read the paper's tokens.

The path comes from the adventure's art file (`miniature.tall` and `miniature.wide`): its stops are the stamps' places, and a stop with a third value is the rival whose stamp sits there. It is smoothed with a Catmull-Rom curve, printed as a tape with a dashed red thread over it, and sewn solid as far as the stop of the available rival (a solid copy with `pathLength={100}` and a `stroke-dasharray` of the progress). Beaten stamps are filled and postmarked, the available one is larger and framed in brick with a pin, a locked one shows a padlock, and the last is a wax seal that carries a tick when it is written.

**A placeholder:** when the adventure's art is exported (A3, `public/aventuras/<slug>/miniatura-*.svg`) this scenery is replaced by it; the path and stamps stay.

## Props

| Prop      | Type                 | Required | Meaning                                                 |
| --------- | -------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `art`     | `AdventureMiniature` | yes      | The viewBox and the path of the variant.                |
| `states`  | `EncounterState[]`   | yes      | Each rival's state in the order of the story.           |
| `variant` | `"tall" \| "wide"`   | yes      | Which scenery to draw.                                  |
| `label`   | `string`             | yes      | What a screen reader says: the drawing is `role="img"`. |

## Markup & styling

Block `.journey-miniature` (97 lines).

**Elements:** `.journey-miniature__tape`, `__thread`, `__sewn`, `__stamp`, `__paper`, `__postmark`, `__pin`, `__lock`, `__wax`, `__check`

**Modifiers and states:** `.journey-miniature_variant_tall|wide`, `.journey-miniature__stamp_state_beaten|available|locked`, `.journey-miniature__stamp_final`

**Tokens:** `--adventure-cream-bright`, `--adventure-kraft-pale`, `--adventure-kraft`, `--adventure-empty`, `--adventure-graphite`, `--adventure-thread`, `--adventure-brick-fill`, `--adventure-brick-fill-deep`, `--adventure-on-brick`

No media queries and no animation.

## Usage

From `components/adventure/cover/adventure-cover/adventure-cover.comp.tsx`:

```tsx
<JourneyMiniature art={art.miniature.tall} states={states} variant="tall" label="El viaje entero: …" />
```
