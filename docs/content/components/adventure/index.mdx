# Adventure components

`components/adventure/` is the adventure of «Jugar» («El cuaderno del viajero»): a notebook on a desk, drawn in cut paper. It mirrors no folder of `app/` of its own, because its screens live under `app/(platform)/jugar/`; it has a palette and four fonts of its own (`adventure.css`, described in [Styling](/ui/styling#the-adventures-paper)) and the platform's buttons and popups. What stays of the rest of the platform is the shell: the bar above, the session and the routes.

Every piece sits under an element with the class `.adventure`, which declares the tokens, and every colour is a pair of light and dark, tested by `components/adventure/adventure-tokens.test.ts`.
