# Adventure components

`components/adventure/` is the adventure of «Jugar» («El cuaderno del viajero»): a notebook drawn in cut paper — its map, its rivals and the picture of its journey; «Jugar» itself is a page of the new design system. It mirrors no folder of `app/` of its own, because its screens live under `app/(platform)/jugar/`; it has a palette and four fonts of its own (`adventure.css`, described in [Styling](/ui/styling#the-adventures-paper)) and the platform's buttons and popups. What stays of the rest of the platform is the shell: the bar above, the session and the routes.

Every piece sits under an element with the class `.adventure`, which declares the tokens, and every colour is a pair of light and dark, tested by `components/adventure/adventure-tokens.test.ts`.

## `cover/`

| Page                                                                | Kind   | What it is                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`JourneyMiniature`](/components/adventure/cover/journey-miniature) | server | The whole journey in one drawing, with the stamps in their state: the art of «Jugar»'s block. |

The cover of «Jugar» (`AdventureCover`, `NotebookCard`, `Ticket`) and the shelf (`AdventureShelf`, `OpenNotebook`, `NotebookSpine`) went on 2026-10-25: «Jugar» moved to the new design system ([`PlayHome`](/components/platform/sections/play/play-home)) and holds the notebook the person is in and the shelf on one page, so `/jugar/aventura` redirects to `/jugar#cuadernos`. Only the journey's picture stays, inside that page.

`PaperFrame`, the page of the notebook a game of an adventure was drawn on, went on 2026-10-25: a game's page moved to the new design system and says its page, place and attempt in its own head ([`LiveGameSection`](/components/platform/sections/play/live-game/live-game-section)). The twelve portraits (`portraits/`) and the keepsakes' drawings (`keepsakes/<slug>-keepsakes.const.tsx`) are generated by `npm run adventure:art` ([Scripts](/operations/scripts-and-seed)) and are not edited by hand.
