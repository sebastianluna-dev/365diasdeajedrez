# `MapLayer`

|            |                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| File       | `components/adventure/map/map-layer/map-layer.comp.tsx`                                                                                 |
| Kind       | A leaf of the client map (it has no state of its own; `useMemo` for the seam)                                                           |
| Stylesheet | `map-layer.comp.css` → block `.map-layer`                                                                                               |
| Story      | none of its own: drawn by the stories of [`AdventureMap`](/components/adventure/map/adventure-map) (`adventure-map.stories.tsx`)        |
| Used by    | [`AdventureMap`](/components/adventure/map/adventure-map), three times                                                                  |
| Uses       | `EncounterStamp`, `NotePage`, `Keepsake`, `PlayerToken`, `Needle` (generated), `seamOf` (`lib/adventure/map-geometry.ts`), `next/image` |

## Purpose

Draws one of the three layouts of the world. The scenery is the exported SVG (`/aventuras/<slug>/{escritorio,tableta,movil}.svg`), a `next/image` that is wider than the world: the paper runs 700 units past each side and 40 above, and the layer clips it. Over it, in this order:

1. **A veil** (`--adventure-scenery-wash`): nothing by day and a dark wash at night, since the scenery is drawn once, in daylight.
2. **The seam, an SVG in the art's own units** (`viewBox="0 0 W H"`): the stretch walked, printed over the tape in its cream with the squares and the marks in brick, the red stitches over it and the stretch ahead in pencil; the start's red dot; the needle with its thread; the pencil notes with their arrows; and the «¡me toca!» with its arrow for the rival to play. The geometry is `seamOf(layout, currentOrder)`: the path is a Catmull-Rom spline through the layout's waypoints, split at the waypoint of the available stamp (a complete adventure walks it all).
3. **The live layer**, in percentages of the world's box: per zone, its `NotePage` (open when the zone is reached, closed before), and per rival its `Keepsake` (only when beaten) and its `EncounterStamp`; the «Salida» flag and the person's token beside the stamp to play (offset from `token.byOrder` or the default).

Everything placed goes in `left/top/width` percentages of the world; what is measured (type, labels) is in `--u`, one unit of the art as a length (`100cqw / --art-width`, the world being a size container), with a floor so a phone still reads it.

## Props

| Prop       | Type                                | Required | Meaning                                                   |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `size`     | `"desktop" \| "tablet" \| "mobile"` | yes      | Which layout it is; the stylesheet shows it by the width. |
| `layout`   | `AdventureLayout`                   | yes      | Its size, path and placements.                            |
| `zones`    | `AdventureZoneSeed[]`               | yes      | The pages of the notebook.                                |
| `progress` | `AdventureProgressView`             | yes      | Every rival with its state.                               |
| `player`   | `{ initials, name }`                | yes      | The token's initials; the membership card's name.         |
| `onOpen`   | `(order, trigger) => void`          | yes      | A stamp was pressed.                                      |

## Markup & styling

Block `.map-layer` (202 lines); the root is a `<section aria-label="Mapa de …">` with `data-size`.

**Elements:** `.map-layer__world`, `__scenery`, `__wash`, `__seam`, `__thread`, `__thread-dot`, `__pencil`, `__note`, `__hint`, `__live`, `__zone`, `__encounter`, `__zone-name`, `__flag`, `__token`

**Modifiers and states:** `.map-layer_size_desktop|tablet|mobile` (shown from 1024, from 768 and below), `.map-layer__note_tone_light` and `.map-layer__hint_tone_light` (over the night)

**Tokens:** `--adventure-paper`, `--adventure-ink`, `--adventure-ink-soft`, `--adventure-thread`, `--adventure-graphite`, `--adventure-brick`, `--adventure-on-wood-soft`, `--adventure-cream-bright`, `--adventure-scenery-wash`, `--adventure-font-note`, `--adventure-font-data`

**Media queries:** `(width >= 768px)`, `(width >= 1024px)`. **Animation:** `map-layer-hop` on the token, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
