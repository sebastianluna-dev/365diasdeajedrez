## Purpose

What stays on the window over the map, under the platform's bar: «Jugar» and «Cuadernos» (platform buttons, small, with their icon on the left; on a phone only the icon shows and the text stays for a screen reader), the zone the middle of the window is over (a kraft tag with its kind and name, announced politely as it changes) and «4/12 rivales · ★ 9 estrellas» (read to a screen reader as a sentence). The row is sticky under the bar (`top: var(--map-bar, 70px)`, which `AdventureMap` measures) and takes no height, so the map's first pixel is under it; the row lets clicks through and only its tags catch them.

## Props

| Prop     | Type             | Required | Meaning                      |

| `total`  | `number`         | yes      | Rivals in the adventure.     |
