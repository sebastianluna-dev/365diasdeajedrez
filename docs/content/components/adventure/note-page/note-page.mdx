## Purpose

The story is told by a page of the notebook per zone, read on arriving: a label taped to the sheet (the zone's kind, its name and its number in a circle) and the ruled sheet with a red margin — the chapter, the note and who signed it. Three states:

- **`closed`** — the zone has not been reached: a flap covers the top half and says «Se abre al llegar»; the sheet is `aria-hidden` and a screen reader hears only that it opens on arriving.
- **`folded`** — reached, not read yet: the sheet is clipped to its top half.
- **`open`** — the flap turns up (`rotateX`) and the sheet unrolls (`clip-path`); it passes to it the first time 45 % of the page comes into view. For whoever asks for reduced motion a reached page is open from the start, in the stylesheet, with no transition.

A page that was torn out (`zone.torn`, the club's) has a ragged lower edge — a seeded polygon, the same every time — and no signature. On a phone (`isCompact`) the type, the lines and the padding are smaller, as the mockup's.
