# Analysis board

`/herramientas/tablero-de-analisis`, behind `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED` (off until the owner has checked it). Reached from «Herramientas» in the top bar, from a finished game against a bot («Tablero de análisis» under the board) and by URL. It needs no migration and no calibration: it writes nothing until «Guardar en un estudio».

## What it is

The study's own viewer — the notation with its variations, the engine, the tools under the board, arrows, the move menu, ⌘Z — with nothing beside it but the game (T3, chosen on 2026-10-08: «más parecido a la partida de un estudio»): two fields with their labels, «FEN» and «PGN», and one «Cargar» (K1), «Jugar esta posición contra el módulo» and «Guardar en un estudio».

**In the new design system** (2026-10-23, «M4» of the owner's canvas, [`AnalysisBoardSection`](/components/platform/sections/tools/analysis-board/analysis-board-section)): the page bleeds onto the workspace. The side column holds «Guardar en un estudio» as Material's extended FAB and two groups that fold from their titles, «Cargar» (the FEN and PGN box) and «Jugar contra el módulo» (the colour and «Jugar esta posición»). The panel opens with a toolbar — the source's label at the left, «Girar el tablero» and the share at the right, which copies the link to the position on the board (`?fen=`) — over the viewer in its material look: the evaluation bar at the board's left, the tools under the board (Comentario, Calidad, Evaluación, Compartir), and at the right the engine's card (its switch, three lines with their evaluations as pills, «Ver más jugadas»), the notation's card and Material's keys. The page's name and drawing are the bar's.

What the design drew and the page does not have, and how it was mapped: the source chip reads the source's own label («Partida: Ana Torres – Don Chuy») and not an event and round, which no source carries; the share is the position's link, the one way the board is shared without saving; the keys keep the viewer's settings menu (sound, full screen) after the four arrows, since the flip went up to the toolbar; the box to play the module lost its line «A máxima potencia y al instante, sin reloj.», which the design leaves out.

**It persists nothing** (planning decision A3): the work is kept in `sessionStorage` under `365:herramientas:tablero` — it survives a reload and dies with the tab — and only once something changed, so an untouched board never flips the one it opened with. The kept work is read in the browser only (`useSyncExternalStore`, read once per visit), and the board is keyed by what it opened with.

## Where it starts

`resolveAnalysisSource` (`services/analysis/analysis.service.ts`) reads the URL through each source's own visibility rule; a source the person may not see opens an empty board, as if nothing had been asked.

| URL                                   | Opens                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `?fen=<FEN>`                          | The position, if it can exist (`pgnOfFen`, ≤ 120 characters; no impossible check or material). |
| `?partida=<playedGameId>`             | A played game (`getPlayedGame`, the profile's visibility).                                     |
| `?estudio=<studyId>&partida=<gameId>` | A study's game (`getGameById`, the studies' visibility).                                       |
| nothing                               | What the tab kept, or the starting position.                                                   |

A PGN never travels in the URL. `platformRoutes.analysisBoard(source?)` builds the three.

**Loading a FEN or a PGN** is checked in the browser by `readPositionInput` (`lib/chess/position-input.ts`, pure and tested): a FEN that can exist on a board, kings and all, with no check or material no game could reach (`positionPlausibility`); a PGN with a move or a starting position, whose `[FEN]` is read as strictly as an exercise's (`readSolutionStart`: a broken one, or another `[Variant]`, is refused rather than opened as the standard start); the PGN when both fields have text. A failure says «No es un FEN válido.» or «No es un PGN válido.» under its own field and leaves the board as it was.

## Playing a position against the module

Planning decision B: the module plays **at full strength and at once** — `FULL_STRENGTH_PROFILE` (`lib/chess/bots/full-strength.const.ts`), Skill 20, no strength limit, a search of 300 ms, the project's one search bounded by time —, **never with a clock**, and **nothing is saved or counted**. The colour is offered as the side to move at the node on the board.

`useEngineSparring` (`components/platform/sections/tools/analysis-board/`) adds every move — the person's and the module's — after the line's last move with `addMoveToPgn` (`lib/chess/pgn-edit.ts`): at the end of the line, or as a new variation when the node already went on. So what is played is a variation of the analysis while it is played, and stays one after «Volver al análisis». It ends on the board's own ending (`gameEnd`) or with «Volver al análisis».

The viewer plays it with `interaction="play"` and `play.fromPath`/`play.leafPath`: the board moves only for the person's colour at the line's end; the line's moves are painted in the selection's blue in the notation; the evaluation bar and the engine's lines are off. In the engine's place the panel says whose turn it is in a card of the primary («Te toca mover.» and «El módulo jugó 11… b5.», or «El módulo piensa…»: `play.headline`); the side column's box turns pale blue and says the person's colour, that the line stays as a variation or how it ended, and «Volver al análisis». Starting a line turns the board to the person's colour.

**One engine for both.** The board owns one `EngineClient` (`useEngineClient(true)`, created on arrival) and hands it to the viewer's analysis (`engineClient`) and to the module (`useEnginePlayer(FULL_STRENGTH_PROFILE, …, shared)`). Playing stops the analysis; the player sets its options and `ucinewgame`; back to the analysis, `useEngine` sets `MultiPV 3` again. The WASM is compiled once per visit (verified on 2026-10-09: one Worker on arrival — two under React's strict mode in development —, none more after loading, playing and going back).

## Saving and importing

- **«Guardar en un estudio»** ([`SaveToStudyPopup`](/components/platform/shared/save-to-study-popup), the same popup a played game uses): the person's own studies (`getOwnStudyOptions`) and a name; `saveAnalysisToStudy` is `createStudyGame` with the PGN in a hidden field — moves, variations and comments —, indexed, and ends on the new game.
- **No «Importar partida» of its own.** It was a second tool of «Herramientas» until 2026-10-09, when the owner dropped it: a game pasted in «PGN» is saved with «Guardar en un estudio», and a study imports its own games («Importar»).
