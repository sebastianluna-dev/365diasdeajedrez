# Classes («Clases»)

A live class is given by a teacher to one student or to a group, over a meeting link, at a time the academy's staff set. On 2026-10-14 the owner rewrote who does what — the staff creates every class and registers every payment; the teacher sees only their assigned classes, prepares their content, takes attendance and finalizes them; the student sees their classes and how many paid ones they have left — and the module is being rebuilt around those rules, in phases (the plan lives in `planning/clases/PLAN.md`, local). This page is the part that is built; what each phase adds is noted at its end.

## The rules that do not bend

- **A paid class is spent by being in a class that was not cancelled**, whether or not the student came. A no-show spends it; the staff can give that class another date at any time, also after it happened, and the seat moves with its payment, so the payment never pays twice.
- **Attendance never moves money.** The teacher passes the list; the staff may change a mark after a report, with a reason that is recorded and that the teacher sees. Neither changes what a payment covers.
- **Paid classes never expire.** There is no expiry anywhere, and the student is told «No caducan».
- **«Gratis» is not «Sin pago».** A free seat is a staff decision (a diagnosis, a courtesy): it is charged to nobody, counts in no balance and sits in no queue. «Sin pago» is a payment still pending, which the queues chase.
- **The student never sees an amount**: the day of their payment, how many classes it bought and how many are left.
- **In a group class the students see each other's names**, never each other's payments.
- **A class is never deleted: it is cancelled**, and a cancelled or finished class never comes back. A mistake is fixed with another class.

## The phase of a class

The status (`ClassStatus`: SCHEDULED, COMPLETED, CANCELLED; an old LIVE row reads as SCHEDULED) is what somebody wrote. The **phase** is what the clock makes of it, derived on every read by `classPhase` (`services/shared/class-phase.ts`) and never stored:

| Phase            | When                                                                   | What the screens say                        |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `UPCOMING`       | Scheduled, before the link opens (or without a link, before it starts) | «Programada»                                |
| `OPEN`           | From the instant the link opens (`meetingVisibleFrom`) until it starts | «Enlace abierto»                            |
| `IN_PROGRESS`    | Between `scheduledAt` and `endsAt`                                     | «En vivo»                                   |
| `AWAITING_CLOSE` | Ended and nobody finalized it                                          | «Por finalizar» (the student: «Finalizada») |
| `DONE`           | COMPLETED                                                              | «Finalizada»                                |
| `CANCELLED`      | CANCELLED                                                              | «Cancelada»                                 |

The link is offered from `meetingVisibleFrom` (the chosen instant, or half an hour before) to `meetingVisibleUntil` (half an hour after the end): past it, no mapper sends it, whatever the status. `classPhaseInstants` lists the instants the phase changes at, so a page can refresh at each one, and `studentClassView` decides whether the student's page looks ahead (how to join, what to prepare) or reviews the class, which happens when the link closes.

Every function receives `now` and a zone; none reads the clock or the process's zone. The teacher reads in `Teacher.timezone` when it is valid and in the academy's (`America/Mexico_City`) otherwise, never in UTC (`classTimeZone`, `lib/timezone.ts`); «Hoy» is cut in that zone with `dayRangeIn`.

## What a payment covers

`services/shared/payment-seats.ts`, counted on read:

- `paymentSeats(payment, seats)`: the seats that **count** (class not cancelled, seat not moved) split into `taken` (ended or finished, no-shows included) and `scheduled`; `toSchedule = classCount − counting`, never negative; `returned` (cancelled classes) and `moved`; and `ordinalOf`, «clase n de m» in chronological order. A free seat, an unpaid one, or another payment's never enters.
- `studentSeatBalance(payments)`: what is left across the student's payments, oldest first, with no amount in it.
- `planPaymentSeats`: how a new payment's classes are spread between the seats it covers, the ones dated now and the ones placed in a group, and the error when there are more than it bought.
- `isUnpaidSeat` and `unpaidSeatWhere`: the ONE definition of «Sin pago» (no payment, not free, not moved, class not cancelled), as a function and as the Prisma `where`; a test keeps them the same.

## Who may do what

`services/shared/class-rules.ts`:

- `editableClassFields(phase)`: before the link opens, everything; open, no rescheduling and no change of students; in progress, also finalize; ended, finalize, cancel (with a warning), change the teacher and the content; done, only the attendance, the seat of a no-show, the content and the notes after the class; cancelled, nothing but duplicate.
- `attendanceEditableBy({ role, phase, lastChange, seatMoved })`: the teacher passes the list while finalizing and changes their own marks of a finished class; the staff finalizes on the teacher's behalf only once the class ended, and overrides a mark after a report. A mark the staff overrode is closed to the teacher; one the staff wrote while finalizing on their behalf is not.
- `canTransitionClassStatus(from, to, role)` (`class-status-transitions.ts`): the teacher only to COMPLETED; the staff to COMPLETED or CANCELLED; nothing leaves COMPLETED or CANCELLED; LIVE is never a destination.
- `canAddParticipants`, `classShapeError`, `canFinalizeClass`, `canMoveSeat`, `classDisplayTitle` («Clase con GM Profesor Demo» for the student, «Clase de Valentina Cruz» for the teacher and the staff, when a class has no topic), `isBlockVisibleToStudent` (to prepare: always; after the class: from its end; the teacher's script: never), `isNewClassBlock`, `planCancellation` (the lines the confirmation reads out, one per coverage) and `blockOrderAfterDrop` (the complete order of a class's blocks after a drop between sections).

## Dating a class

`services/shared/class-schedule-rules.ts`: `validateClassSchedule` refuses a link that would open after the class starts and only warns about an overlap of the teacher or of a student and about a date already past (documenting a class after the fact is legitimate); `weeklyDates`, `suggestRhythm` (the weekday, time and duration of the student's last class, from its next occurrence) and `slotInstant` make the dates a payment's classes are prefilled with; `durationOptions` keeps an old duration on the list so rescheduling does not change it in silence. `storedMeetingProvider` (`meeting-provider.ts`) reads the platform from the link: nobody picks «Zoom» in a form any more.

## Saying when and how much

`lib/format-class-when.ts` writes when a class is the one way, in a zone: «mié 14 oct · 12:00–13:00», «Hoy · …», «Mañana · …», «Ayer · …», the year only when it is not the current one, «(hora de Ciudad de México)» when the reader is told the zone; `formatAgendaDay` heads an agenda's day («Hoy · miércoles 14 de octubre»). `lib/format-class-amount.ts` writes a payment («$1,400.00 MXN») and the per-class figure («$350.00»), in whole cents.

## The model

Since 2026-10-14 (`prisma/migrations/20261014120000_class_payments_and_roles`, the field-by-field in [Data model](/platform/data-model#teachers-staff-and-classes)):

- **`ClassPayment`**: what the academy charged a student — an amount, a currency, the day it was paid in the academy's calendar, how many classes it bought, a reference and a staff note. The seats it pays point at it; nothing stores what is left.
- **`Class`** gains its format (individual or group, with a capacity), its end as a column so SQL can filter on it, a nullable title (a class without a topic is named by its teacher or its students), and the audit of who created it, moved it, cancelled it (an internal reason and a note for the students) and closed it. `LIVE` is no longer written.
- **`ClassParticipant`** is the seat: paid (`paymentId`), free (`isFree`) or «Sin pago»; `attended` is null until somebody passes the list; a no-show's seat the staff gives another date keeps its row as history (`seatMoved*`), so it stops counting while the new seat counts.
- **`ClassAttendanceChange`** keeps every mark: who, when, from what to what and why; `isOverride` is a staff change after a report.
- **`ClassBlock`** says when its students see it (`visibility`: to prepare, after the class, or never — the teacher's script) and when it was added.

The migration converts what was there: one payment of one class per seat that carried an amount (a guard refuses to run while a seat has a payment with no usable amount or an unknown currency), `LIVE` to `SCHEDULED`, the staff's «[Soporte dd/mm]» prefix from the summary to `cancelReason`, the meeting platform re-read from the link, an old explicit «Faltó» to null, every block of a finished class to «después de la clase» and one log row per existing mark. `prisma/class-migration.test.ts` reads it.

## The writers

Every derived column has one writer in `services/shared/`, which receives the Prisma client (or a transaction) as its first parameter and runs inside the caller's transaction; the schema comment of each column names it. None imports `server-only`, so the seed and the tests drive them with a client of their own.

| Writer                                                                                       | File                          | Writes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `createClassRecord`, `syncClassSchedule`, `syncClassMeeting`                                 | `class-schedule.service.ts`   | The row of a class, `SCHEDULED`, its shape checked; its date, duration and end (through `classScheduleColumns`, the one computation of `endsAt`); its link, platform and «visible from» (through `classMeetingColumns`); the last reschedule, with who and why.                                                                                                                   |
| `transitionClassStatus`                                                                      | `class-status.service.ts`     | The status after creation, with `closedAt`/`closedById` or the cancellation columns, after `canTransitionClassStatus` says the actor may; an `updateMany` conditioned on the class still being open, so two closes write once.                                                                                                                                                    |
| `syncClassSeat` (modes `add` and `cover`), `removeClassSeat`, `moveClassSeat`, `lockClasses` | `class-seats.service.ts`      | Who is in a class and how the seat is covered: a given payment, the student's oldest with classes left (`auto`), free, or none yet; covering pays a seat that was «Sin pago». The move of a no-show's seat, which marks the old row BEFORE adding the new one so the payment does not look exhausted by the seat that is leaving, and undoes the mark if the destination refuses. |
| `recordAttendance`                                                                           | `class-attendance.service.ts` | The marks, the log, `attendanceTakenAt` the first time, and the `CLASS_ATTENDED` activity once per student and class. The mode (`finalize`, `change`, `override`) has to be what `attendanceEditableBy` gives the actor's role in the class's phase; finalizing needs a mark for every seat not moved; the staff always gives a reason; unmarking keeps the activity.             |

**Locking.** The payment's invariant — a payment of n classes never covers more than n seats that count — holds under concurrency by locking in one order everywhere: first every `Class` row the transaction touches, at once and by id (`lockClasses`: `SELECT … FOR UPDATE … ORDER BY id`), then the student's payments by `(paidOn, id)`. A student already in a class is found by a read after the lock and reported without writing: a `P2002` inside a transaction would abort it. The actions still recognise one, outside the transaction, with `isUniqueConstraintError(error, "ClassParticipant_pkey", "classId")` as a safety net.

The tests run the writers against `lib/testing/fake-class-db.ts`, an in-memory stand-in with READ COMMITTED's visibility and row locks, and replay two registrations racing for the last seat of a payment, two that cover the same classes in a different order, a student already there, and a mark written twice.

**The seed is the one exception**: it writes the facts of a class as data by fixed id, spreads the same pure functions for the derived columns, and seats students through `syncClassSeat` ([Data model › Seed](/platform/data-model#seed)).

## The two guards

- `services/shared/class-writers.test.ts` reads every `.ts`/`.tsx` of `app/`, `components/`, `lib/`, `services/` and `scripts/` (not the tests, not `prisma/`) with the TypeScript compiler and fails on a `data` of a class table that writes a column outside its writer, nested writes included (`participants: { create }` inside a class is a seat being created), on a `data` it cannot read outside the four writers, on an `endsAt` or a meeting column not spread from its pure function, and on raw SQL that changes a class table (a `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` is a lock). Its tables of pairs are the ones above; phases 4–6 extend them. Temporary exception, with its reason: `setRecordingUrl` in `services/staff-classes/staff-classes.actions.ts`, until phase 5.
- `services/shared/class-roles.test.ts` walks the imports of the teacher's side (`services/teacher-classes/`, `services/teacher/`, `components/platform/sections/teacher/`, `app/(platform)/profesor/`) with `lib/testing/module-reach.ts` and fails if any reaches the seat or schedule writers, the staff's class services or the payments, or so much as names a payment. Temporary exception: the old teacher actions, reported but not followed, until phase 6 retires them.

## What the old screens do meanwhile

Until phases 5–7 draw the new screens, the old ones run on the writers: the teacher's `createClass` makes an individual class (a group with a capacity when several students are ticked) and seats each student «Sin pago»; `updateClassMeta` leaves the date to `syncClassSchedule` and the link to `syncClassMeeting`; `setClassStatus` only finalizes; `markAttendance` finalizes while the class runs or after it ended and changes a mark of a finished one; `cancelClassAsStaff` is `transitionClassStatus` with the staff's role. The student's mapper names a class without a topic «Clase con [el profesor]», lists the classmates by name, offers the link only while it is open and filters the blocks with `isBlockVisibleToStudent`; the staff sheet says how each seat is covered and sees every block with its visibility; the teacher reads «Sin pasar lista», «Asistió» or «Faltó» and no longer sees payments.

## Payments

The staff's «Pagos y clases» (ADM-7), «Registrar pago» (ADM-2) and «Programar» (ADM-6), built in phase 4 (2026-10-15):

- **Reads** (`services/staff-payments/`): `getStudentPayments(userId)` gives the balance in three figures (por programar, programadas, tomadas), each payment as a card (`mapPayment`: its figures, its seats in order with «clase n de m», whether it opens folded because every class was given, and whether it can still be deleted) and the classes that wait for a payment (`unpaidSeatWhere`); `getPaymentContext(userId, now)` gives «Registrar pago» what it says of a student before anything is typed — «Profesor: … · Le queda 1 clase (hoy 19:00) · Último pago: …» — plus the rhythm `suggestRhythm` takes from their last class, their last meeting link, and the calendars the overlap warnings read. Every amount leaves the service formatted («$1,400.00 MXN», «$350.00 por clase»).
- **Writes**: `registerPayment` creates the `ClassPayment` row — the one file that may — and does the rest through the writers in one transaction: covers the «Sin pago» classes ticked (`syncClassSeat`, mode `cover`), creates the dated classes (`createClassRecord`) with their seats, and seats the student in the group classes chosen; the `Class` rows it touches are locked first, by id. A writer's refusal undoes everything and comes back as a field error with what was typed. `updatePayment` never lets `classCount` go below the seats that count (`countingSeatsOf`); `deletePayment` works only while no class of the payment started, and its seats stay «Sin pago». `scheduleSeat` dates a paid class without one. The form is read by `readRegisterPaymentForm` (`payment-form.ts`): the payment's fields, the classes it covers, and the rows `slot.<i>.*`, with `planPaymentSeats` refusing more dated classes than it buys.
- **Screens**: the student's record became a layout with tabs («Cuenta», «Pagos y clases»); the head carries «Registrar pago». The pieces: [`RegisterPaymentPopup`](/components/platform/shared/register-payment-popup), [`ScheduleSeatPopup`](/components/platform/shared/schedule-seat-popup), [`ClassSlotFields`](/components/platform/shared/class-slot-fields), [`PaymentCard`](/components/platform/sections/staff/payments/payment-card), [`StudentPaymentsSection`](/components/platform/sections/staff/payments/student-payments-section). The amount is written with its currency beside it and the per-class figure under it (D14); a payment that cannot be deleted keeps its entry on the «…» menu, greyed, with the reason as a second line (D19).
- **Notices**: `paymentRegistered.<n>.<dated>.<later>`, `paymentUpdated`, `paymentDeleted.<unpaid>` and `seatScheduled.<minutes>.<duration>`, read by `readNotice`.

## What is built

- **Phase 1 (2026-10-14)**: the rules, the formats, the codes and the fixtures above, with their tests. Nothing in the app reads them yet.
- **Phase 2a–2b (2026-10-15)**: `recordUserActivity` runs in the caller's transaction; the model, the migration (applied locally; pending in production until the release), the four writers with their fake-database tests, the two guards, and every reader and old screen on top of them.
- **Phase 2c (2026-10-15)**: class material reaches a student only through a block they may see, in the four reads that start from the blocks (`studentVisibleClassBlockWhere`).
- **Phase 3 (2026-10-15)**: the primitives (page actions, tag tones), the extended form pieces, nine shared pieces of a class, the popup-form pattern with `?aviso=`, and the renderer with a head per block.
- **Phase 4 (2026-10-15)**: payments — «Pagos y clases», «Registrar pago» with its classes, «Programar» a paid class.
- **Phase 5 (2026-10-15)**: the staff's agenda with its queues (ADM-1), «Crear clase» (ADM-3) and the class's record (ADM-4, ADM-8) with its popups: reschedule, teacher, link, add and remove, cancel with a reason (ADM-5), change a mark with a reason (ADM-9), give a no-show another date (ADM-10). The old staff screens and `setRecordingUrl` are gone.
- **Phase 2d (2026-10-15)**: the demo — five classmates, five payments, twenty classes with a class in every phase, a group class, a free one, a no-show moved to a new date and a staff change of attendance (`prisma/seed-classes.ts`; [Scripts & seed](/operations/scripts-and-seed)).
