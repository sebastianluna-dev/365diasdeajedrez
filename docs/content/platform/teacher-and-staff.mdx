# Teacher & staff panels

Both panels are ordinary platform pages with a stricter border: `requireTeacher()` / `requireStaff()` as the first `await`, ownership inside every `where`, and guards before writes. Error codes map to `TEACHER_ERROR_MESSAGES` and `STAFF_ERROR_MESSAGES` in `constants/platform/`.

## Teacher panel — `/profesor`

### Dashboard

`services/teacher/teacher.service.ts` → `getTeacherDashboard()`: today's classes, the next 7 days, classes that still need attendance marked, and the number of actively assigned students. `getTeacherProfile()` and `updateTeacherProfile` (`teacher.actions.ts`) manage display name, title, bio (≤ 1000 chars), photo and IANA timezone (`constants/platform/timezones.const.ts`).

### Students — `/profesor/alumnos`

`services/teacher-students/`: only students with an **active** `TeacherStudent` row, and that condition is always inside the `where`. `getAssignedStudents(query?)`, `getAssignedStudentDetail(studentId)` (course progress, recent activity, shared classes), `getStudentStudy` and `getStudentGame` for the **read-only** view of a student's studies. Opening the study of an unassigned student by URL returns nothing, so the page 404s without revealing a name.

While `PLAY_ENABLED` is on, the student's page also carries «Partidas jugadas» — their last eight games against the bots, each opening read-only, and «Ver su perfil» — and the teacher's menu gains «Jugar» before «Explorador»; the teacher's public card, `/profesor/perfil`, is «Mi ficha» in that menu, because «Mi perfil» (the user menu) is now everyone's profile at `/perfil` ([Play](/platform/play)).

### Classes — `/profesor/clases`

The teacher sees the classes the staff assigns them, prepares their content, takes attendance and finalizes (2026-10-15, phase 6 of the [classes rebuild](/platform/classes)); creating a class, dating it, seating a student and everything about money are the staff's, and `services/shared/class-roles.test.ts` keeps the teacher's side from reaching those writers or naming a payment.

`services/teacher/`: `getTeacherDashboard()` — the next class with how to join it (`MeetingJoinData`), the classes that ended and wait, and the week by day, all cut in the teacher's zone (`classTimeZone(teacher.timezone)`, the academy's when they have none; D7) —, `teacherGreeting` and `groupTeacherWeek` in its mapper.

`services/teacher-classes/`:

- `getTeacherClasses({ vista, q, pagina })`: «Próximas», «Por finalizar», «Pasadas», «Canceladas», twenty at a time; each row is `ClassRowData` with the teacher's chips («2 para preparar», «1 para después», «1 en tu guion», «Sin contenido», «Sin tema», «Sin enlace · lo agrega administración») and «Finalizar» or «Preparar» as its action (`teacher-classes.mapper.ts`).
- `getTeacherClassDetail(classId)` (cached per request): the blocks to edit and as the student's renderer draws them (every visibility), the students with their marks and the staff's changes (`AttendanceMarkView`: a change after a report locks the mark for the teacher, D3), whether each is the teacher's student, the join card's data (the link always), what was written after, the staff's facts. Never a payment.
- `listUpcomingClassesWith(studentId)`, `listReferenceableGames`, `getPgnForReference`; `block-rules.ts` (`gameBlockSource`: the transcribed PGN rules over a cited game).
- The actions, each with `requireTeacher()`, `assertTeacherOwnsClass` and a check of what the phase allows: `updateClassTopic`, `finalizeClass` (one transaction: `recordAttendance` in mode `finalize` with every seat marked, the summary and the recording, `transitionClassStatus` to COMPLETED), `changeAttendance` (mode `change`, D3), `updateClassAfterNotes`, `addClassBlock` / `updateClassBlock` (with the visibility; kinds Texto, Partida, Lección, Video), `deleteClassBlock`, `reorderClassBlocks` (the FULL order, `planFullReorder`, and the moved block's visibility when it crossed a section), `fetchReferencePgn`. The columns the teacher writes by hand are theirs alone: title, description, summary, recording and the blocks.
- The staff's `finalizeClassAsStaff` (`services/staff-classes/staff-classes.actions.ts`, D1): only once the class ended and waits, only the attendance, with a reason the teacher reads; the marks stay the teacher's to change.

«Escríbenos» and «Escribe a administración» go to the academy's WhatsApp: `ACADEMY_WHATSAPP_NUMBER` in `constants/platform/academy-contact.const.ts` (D9; a constant and not a read of Payload, so no page of the platform opens the CMS's database — the duplication is noted in IMPROVEMENTS).

## Staff panel — `/administracion`

### Dashboard

`services/staff/staff.service.ts` → counts of students (accounts that are neither teacher nor staff), active/inactive teachers, courses by status, classes in the next 7 days, and the actionable list of **students without a teacher**.

### Students (accounts) — `/administracion/alumnos`

`services/staff-students/`: staff sees every account but never its private content (no studies, no study games). The one exception is what a person plays on the platform: the staff, like the student's teachers, may open any profile and its played games, read-only ([Play](/platform/play#who-may-see-what)); this panel still lists none of them. `createStudentAccount` (email + display name → account and default study in one transaction, with a password typed by staff or a generated temporary one, shown once), `resetStudentPassword` (new temporary password, all sessions destroyed), `updateStudent`. The detail shows the assignment history.

### Payments — `/administracion/alumnos/[userId]/pagos`

`services/staff-payments/` (2026-10-15; every reader opens with `requireStaff()`, the student and the teacher never read it): `getStudentPayments` (the balance, each payment with its seats as `paymentSeats` counts them, the classes «Sin pago»), `getPaymentContext` (what «Registrar pago» needs about a student: their teacher, their last payment, their rhythm from their last class, their last meeting link, their balance in words, their calendar and their teacher's for twelve weeks), `listSeatsToSchedule` (the «Por programar» queue), `listPaymentStudents` (every student grouped by teacher). The actions, each with a one-minute window of attempts: `registerPayment` (the payment and, in the same transaction, the «Sin pago» classes it covers through `syncClassSeat`, the classes it dates through `createClassRecord` and their seats, and the group classes chosen; every `Class` row locked first by id; a writer's refusal undoes everything and comes back as a field error with what was typed), `updatePayment` (its classes never below the seats that count), `deletePayment` (only while nothing of it was given; the seats stay «Sin pago»), and the two read actions the popup calls, `loadPaymentContext` and `loadTeacherBusy`. `scheduleSeat` (`services/staff-classes/staff-classes.actions.ts`) dates a paid class in a new individual class or in a group class with a seat free. The pages: the student's record is a layout with tabs (`StudentRecordHead`, `(ficha)/layout.tsx`), «Cuenta» and «Pagos y clases». The whole of it: [Classes › Payments](/platform/classes#payments).

### Teachers and assignments — `/administracion/profesores`

`services/staff-teachers/`: `createTeacher` links an existing account (`listLinkableUsers`), `updateTeacher`, `setTeacherActive` (never delete), `assignStudent` and `endAssignment`. `assignment-rules.ts` plans a reassignment (close active rows, create a new one, or no-op if already assigned to that teacher); the partial unique index `teacher_student_one_active` is the final arbiter and its P2002 is caught by name.

### Courses — `/administracion/cursos`

`services/staff-courses/` is the content editor:

- Course: `createCourse` (name, slug and at least one content type, from the «Nuevo curso» dialog), `updateCourse` (name, slug — in the editor it is rewritten from the name whenever the name changes, and can be adjusted before saving; it is the address of the course's information page, `/cursos/<slug>`, so one that page would never answer to is refused as `invalid`: `explorar`, or eight digits, which are read as a course id (`isReservedCourseSlug`) —, description, content types — at least one, `contentTypeRequired` otherwise —, levels by slug (several or none)), `updateCourseCover` and `updateCourseThumbnail` (the course's two images — the wide 21:9 and the square 1:1 —, each stored the moment `ImageUpload` finishes uploading it from the browser with a signed Cloudinary request, or emptied when it is taken away; it answers `{ ok }` instead of redirecting, accepts only an image `next/image` can render, and `withCoverCrop` has rewritten the URL to `c_fill,ar_21:9,g_auto`. It is not part of `updateCourse`, so saving the metadata never touches the cover. Both read the previous URL before writing and delete from Cloudinary the file that stops being pointed at, as `updateAuthor` does with a photo), `publishCourse` (requires a lesson with content, below), `archiveCourse`, delete only while draft and without progress.
- **The minimum to publish** is a lesson with content: its ruling PGN — the linked game's when there is one — with something besides blanks (`lessonHasContent`). `courseHasContent` (`services/staff-courses/course-content.service.ts`) answers it for `publishCourse` and for the editor's `canPublish`, which greys out «Publicado», so the two cannot disagree. It reads ONE lesson's PGN: the database narrows to the course's lessons whose ruling PGN is not empty (`lessonPgnNotEmptyWhere`, beside `lessonPgnOf`) and `lessonHasContent` decides on the first; only a blank one makes it read the rest, because the database cannot tell blank from content. Until 2026-10-03 `getCourseAdminDetail` brought over the PGN of every lesson of the course, on each tab of the record, to answer this.
- A course record is three pages, which are its tabs: `/administracion/cursos/[courseId]` (metadata, images, authors, sources), `…/temario` (its chapters) and `…/partidas` (its games). A chapter's is three too: the chapter, `…/lecciones` and `…/partidas`. The syllabus and the lessons have a page of their own because the list is rows, and rows want the whole width. The three pages of each record live in a `(ficha)` route group whose **layout** draws the name, the status and the tabs — so they cannot drift apart and the header does not blink between them —; what hangs from the record (a lesson, a game on the board) is outside the group. The tabs are built in one place (`courseTabs`, `chapterTabs` in `staff-tabs.const.ts`), and which one is current is read from the address, because a layout is not told the path.
- The actions of a list come back to the page that draws it: the chapter ones to `…/temario` and the lesson ones to `…/lecciones`, with `?error=`, and they revalidate the record as a layout so the three pages and the counts of the tabs follow.
- Chapters: `createChapter`, `updateChapter` (name, description and `isPriority`, the mark that makes the whole chapter priority; role intro/closing enforced by the unique index), `reorderChapters`, `deleteChapter`. **A chapter's duration is not typed**: it is the sum of its lessons' minutes, cached on `Chapter.estimatedDuration` by `syncChapterDuration` in the transaction of the lesson write that moved it, so showing it never reads the lessons (2026-09-30). The helper locks the chapter's row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`) before it sums, so two saves of lessons of the same chapter that overlap leave the sum of both instead of the slower one's stale total (2026-10-02). The panel says so under the figure.
- Chapter collection (`…/capitulos/[chapterId]/partidas`): `importChapterGames` pastes PGN into the chapter's `GameDatabase` (created on demand) using the same `pgn-import` and indexing as students, and comes back with the same `?importadas=` result the page turns into a notice, said once: the shell takes it out of the address when the page has drawn it, so removing a game just imported re-renders the list without «Se importaron 12 partidas.» over eleven; `deleteChapterGame` refuses if a lesson uses the game; `reorderChapterGames` writes the collection's order from the board screen's list, taking the whole list at once (`Game.order` is only indexed, so it does not need `services/shared/reorder.ts`, which exists for the orders that are unique per parent).
- A game on the board (`…/capitulos/[chapterId]/partidas/[gameId]`): `getChapterGame` reads it with its lessons and the chapter's other games, and two actions write it. `autosaveChapterGamePgn` saves the PGN as it is annotated and reindexes its positions; when the MOVES changed (`sameMoves`, not a comment nor an arrow) it stamps `pgnUpdatedAt` on every lesson that uses the game and remakes their derived exercise, in the same transaction, and refuses the save (`training`) if a trainable lesson would be left without a line. `updateChapterGameDetails` saves the record and writes it into the PGN's headers. The record's schema and that writing are `services/shared/game-record`, the same module the student's studies use: what a record is does not depend on who saved it.
- Lessons: `createLesson`, `updateLesson` (name, description, order, priority, role, estimated minutes, orientation, topics, `isTrainable`, training colour, own PGN), `setLessonGame` (link/unlink a game of the chapter's collection; the game's PGN becomes the content), `deleteLesson`, `reorderLessons`, `fileLessonPgnAsGame` (files a lesson's own PGN as a game of its chapter's collection and links it — the per-lesson version of `collections:by-chapter`, for content that no board could otherwise reach). Saving the PGN or toggling trainable calls `syncLessonTrainingExercise` so the **derived** exercise follows the mainline (`extractMainline`, `deriveExerciseData`); the action rejects a mainline with an illegal move or a colour with no moves to train. Creating, saving and deleting a lesson also call `syncChapterDuration`, in the same transaction and by the same shared-helper pattern, so the chapter's minutes follow — and a save that is refused takes the figure back with it.
- Exercises: `createExercise`, `updateExercise`, `deleteExercise`, `moveExercise` with the `ExerciseMovePicker` (moves are chosen on a board, never typed).
- Authors: `createAuthor` and `updateAuthor` from the «Autores» dialog (`AuthorsManager`) of the catalog and of the course editor — authors have no page of their own —. They take name, biography and photo and **return** their outcome (`AuthorFormState`, through `useActionState`) instead of redirecting, because the dialog stays open; the slug is never typed: `authorSlugFor` takes it from the name, with a number behind when another author has it, and an author who keeps their name keeps their slug. `manageCourseAuthors` adds an author to a course (`add`, after the last one), changes their role (`role`) or takes them out (`remove`), from the course's card (`CourseAuthors`); there is no reordering.
- Catalog readers for forms: `listContentTypes` (with `shortLabel` and `description`), `listLevels` (slug, name and the Elo range already worded; the levels in use, the easiest first), `listCourseStatuses`, `listTopics`, `listExerciseModes`, `listAuthorRoles`. Checkbox groups over a catalog (content types, levels, a lesson's topics) are written with `syncCatalogJoin` (`services/shared/catalog-join.service.ts`), which replaces the whole join set inside the caller's transaction; for the levels it matches the catalog by `slug` (`catalogKey`), which is what that catalog goes by.

### Sources of a course

`services/staff-sources/`: «Procedencia del recurso», where a course's material comes from. The sources are edited in the card of that name in the course editor's narrow column (`CourseSources`): `saveCourseSource(courseId, formData)` creates one (with `order = nextOrder(count)`) or, with a hidden `sourceId`, rewrites it, scoped to the course (`sourceMissing` otherwise); only `sourceTypeCode` is required — title, authors (free text), URL, year (1400–2100), edition, specific reference and staff-only notes are optional. `deleteCourseSource(courseId, sourceId)` deletes one, both ids bound by the source's popup and nothing read from the form, from the confirmation opened on top of it. `getCourseAdminDetail` returns them in order, and the student's `getCourseById` returns the same rows without the notes. The one catalog reader is `listSourceTypes`.

There are no source entities any more: the page `/administracion/entidades` and the field of the form that cited one (the publisher of a book, the platform of a digital course) were taken out on 2026-09-27.

### Classes (staff) — `/administracion/clases`

`services/staff-classes/` owns the staff's classes (2026-10-15, phase 5 of the [classes rebuild](/platform/classes)). The readers, each opening with `requireStaff()`:

- `getStaffAgenda({ vista, teacherId, q, hasta, pagina })`: «Próximas» by day from today until `hasta` (six days by default), «Pasadas» paged fifty at a time, «Canceladas»; the headline counts today's classes and the week's. The rows are `ClassRowData` for `ClassRow`, mapped by `staff-classes.mapper.ts` (chips «Sin enlace», «1 sin pago», «Gratis», «Profesor desactivado»; a class without a link carries the meeting action).
- `getStaffQueues()`: «Por programar» (`listSeatsToSchedule` of the payments), «Por finalizar» (SCHEDULED and ended; warning from a day after), «Sin enlace» (the next 48 hours), «Sin pago» (`unpaidSeatWhere`), «Profesor desactivado», «Por renovar» (`listStudentBalances`, one class or none left).
- `getClassAdminDetail(classId)` (cached per request): every seat with its coverage («Pago del jue 1 oct · clase 3 de 4» — the ordinal from `paymentOrdinals` of the payments —, «Gratis», «Sin pago»), its mark with who wrote it and who changed it (`AttendanceMarkView`), whether the student is assigned to the teacher, what the staff may do with it (`can`), what was written after the class, the blocks for the student's renderer with their visibility, and the history (`buildClassHistory`, `services/shared/class-history.ts`: read from the columns the writers keep, never a log of its own).
- `listSchedulableStudents()`, `getClassDuplicate(classId)`, `listOpenGroupClasses`, `listActiveTeacherOptions`, `listTeacherFilterOptions`.

The actions (`staff-classes.actions.ts`), each with `requireStaff()`, a one-minute window of attempts and a check of what the phase allows (`editableClassFields`), writing only through the writers of `services/shared/`: `createClass` (ADM-3: `createClassRecord` and a `syncClassSeat` per student with the coverage the staff chose — «Con su pago» is `auto`, the oldest payment with a class left; «Con el pago pendiente» is `unpaid`; «Gratis» is `free` — in one transaction; redirects to the record with `?aviso=classCreated`), `rescheduleClass` (`syncClassSchedule`), `setClassTeacher` (the one column written by hand: the teacher has no derived twin; active teachers only), `setClassMeeting` (`syncClassMeeting`; the «visible from» choice is `meetingVisibleChoice`), `addClassParticipants`, `removeClassParticipant` (`removeClassSeat`: only a seat with no mark and never moved), `cancelClass` (ADM-5: `transitionClassStatus` with the staff's role, the reason and the note; the notice counts the paid seats that went back), `changeAttendanceAsStaff` (ADM-9: `recordAttendance`, role `staff`, mode `override`, reason required), `rescheduleStudentSeat` (ADM-10: `moveClassSeat` to a new class made with `createClassRecord` — the old teacher, topic, description and link —, to a group class, or back to its payment) and `scheduleSeat` (ADM-6). `setRecordingUrl` and `cancelClassAsStaff` are gone, and the writers' guard has no exception left.

The amounts stay in `services/staff-payments`: a seat says which payment it spends, never what it cost.

## Forms

Panels are Server Components with plain `<form action={serverAction}>` elements; the only `useActionState` forms are account creation, password reset, the author form of the «Autores» dialog and the exercise editor (`saveTeacherExercise`, from 2026-10-16), whose board and fields live in the browser: a refused save comes back with its errors, a saved one redirects to the exercise with `?aviso=`. Tables use `PlatformTable` (semantic `<table>`, filters via search params). Uploading a cover uses `components/platform/shared/image-upload.comp.tsx`: it checks type and size in the browser (`constants/platform/upload.const.ts`, 1 MB since 2026-09-28, jpg/png/webp/avif), asks `createUploadSignature()` (staff only, 60 signatures per minute), posts the file straight to Cloudinary with the signed `folder`, `timestamp` and `allowed_formats`, and stores it at once with `updateCourseCover`. An author's photo travels in the form instead, so one uploaded and then replaced, taken away or left in a dialog closed without saving goes to `discardUnsavedImage()`, which deletes it from Cloudinary unless an author, a teacher or a course points at it (`isPlatformImageInUse` in `services/media/media.service.ts`). The size is a browser check alone: Cloudinary's upload API takes no maximum per request, so three covers of about 2 MB uploaded before that day are still stored and still work.
