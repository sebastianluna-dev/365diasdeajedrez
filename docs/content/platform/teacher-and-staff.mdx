# Teacher & staff panels

Both panels are ordinary platform pages with a stricter border: `requireTeacher()` / `requireStaff()` as the first `await`, ownership inside every `where`, and guards before writes. Error codes map to `TEACHER_ERROR_MESSAGES` and `STAFF_ERROR_MESSAGES` in `constants/platform/`.

## Teacher panel — `/profesor`

### Dashboard

`services/teacher/teacher.service.ts` → `getTeacherDashboard()`: today's classes, the next 7 days, classes that still need attendance marked, and the number of actively assigned students. `getTeacherProfile()` and `updateTeacherProfile` (`teacher.actions.ts`) manage display name, title, bio (≤ 1000 chars), photo and IANA timezone (`constants/platform/timezones.const.ts`).

### Students — `/profesor/alumnos`

`services/teacher-students/`: only students with an **active** `TeacherStudent` row, and that condition is always inside the `where`. `getAssignedStudents(query?)`, `getAssignedStudentDetail(studentId)` (course progress, recent activity, shared classes), `getStudentStudy` and `getStudentGame` for the **read-only** view of a student's studies. Opening the study of an unassigned student by URL returns nothing, so the page 404s without revealing a name.

### Classes — `/profesor/clases`

`services/teacher-classes/`:

- `createClass`, `updateClassMeta` (title, description, `scheduledAt` typed in the teacher's zone and stored in UTC through `lib/timezone.ts`, duration 15–480 min, meeting provider and URL, visibility instant, recording URL, summary).
- `setClassStatus` obeys `class-status-transitions.ts`: `SCHEDULED → LIVE | COMPLETED | CANCELLED`, `LIVE → COMPLETED | CANCELLED`; completed and cancelled are final. Cancelling **is** deleting; there is no physical delete.
- Participants: `addParticipant` (must be an assigned student), `removeParticipant` (blocked if the student attended or has a payment), `markAttendance` (records `CLASS_ATTENDED` activity).
- Blocks: `addClassBlock`, `updateClassBlock`, `deleteClassBlock`, `moveClassBlock` (through `services/shared/reorder.ts`). Kinds: text, video (http(s) URL), game reference (a game from the teacher's own studies or an assigned student's, checked by `assertTeacherCanReferenceGame` at insert time; later reassignments do not break past classes), lesson reference, position reference (teacher's saved diagrams), or a game **transcribed in the block** (`ClassBlock.pgn`, edited on the analysis board). `movePath` captures "this position" from the viewer via `onPathChange`; `isMovePathBroken` flags paths that no longer resolve.
- `fetchReferencePgn` lets the block editor preview a referenced game or lesson; `listReferenceableGames` groups the games by study and marks the teacher's own.
- The teacher always sees `meetingUrl`; hiding it before `meetingUrlVisibleFrom` is a student-side rule.

## Staff panel — `/administracion`

### Dashboard

`services/staff/staff.service.ts` → counts of students (accounts that are neither teacher nor staff), active/inactive teachers, courses by status, classes in the next 7 days, and the actionable list of **students without a teacher**.

### Students (accounts) — `/administracion/alumnos`

`services/staff-students/`: staff sees every account but never its private content (no studies, no games). `createStudentAccount` (email + display name → account and default study in one transaction, with a password typed by staff or a generated temporary one, shown once), `resetStudentPassword` (new temporary password, all sessions destroyed), `updateStudent`. The detail shows the assignment history.

### Teachers and assignments — `/administracion/profesores`

`services/staff-teachers/`: `createTeacher` links an existing account (`listLinkableUsers`), `updateTeacher`, `setTeacherActive` (never delete), `assignStudent` and `endAssignment`. `assignment-rules.ts` plans a reassignment (close active rows, create a new one, or no-op if already assigned to that teacher); the partial unique index `teacher_student_one_active` is the final arbiter and its P2002 is caught by name.

### Courses — `/administracion/cursos`

`services/staff-courses/` is the content editor:

- Course: `createCourse` (name, slug and at least one content type, from the «Nuevo curso» dialog), `updateCourse` (name, slug — in the editor it is rewritten from the name whenever the name changes, and can be adjusted before saving —, description, content types — at least one, `contentTypeRequired` otherwise —, levels by slug (several or none), cover uploaded from the browser with `ImageUpload` and a signed Cloudinary request; `withCoverCrop` rewrites the URL to `c_fill,ar_21:9,g_auto`), `publishCourse` (requires at least one chapter with a lesson that has PGN), `archiveCourse`, delete only while draft and without progress.
- Chapters: `createChapter`, `updateChapter` (role intro/closing enforced by the unique index), `reorderChapters`, `deleteChapter`.
- Chapter collection (`…/capitulos/[chapterId]/partidas`): `importChapterGames` pastes PGN into the chapter's `GameDatabase` (created on demand) using the same `pgn-import` and indexing as students; `deleteChapterGame` refuses if a lesson uses the game.
- Lessons: `createLesson`, `updateLesson` (name, description, order, priority, role, estimated minutes, orientation, topics, `isTrainable`, training colour, own PGN), `setLessonGame` (link/unlink a game of the chapter's collection; the game's PGN becomes the content), `deleteLesson`, `reorderLessons`. Saving the PGN or toggling trainable calls `syncLessonTrainingExercise` so the **derived** exercise follows the mainline (`extractMainline`, `deriveExerciseData`); the action rejects a mainline with an illegal move or a colour with no moves to train.
- Exercises: `createExercise`, `updateExercise`, `deleteExercise`, `moveExercise` with the `ExerciseMovePicker` (moves are chosen on a board, never typed).
- Authors: `createAuthor`, `updateAuthor`, `manageCourseAuthors` (role per author, ordered).
- Catalog readers for forms: `listContentTypes` (with `shortLabel` and `description`), `listTopics`, `listExerciseModes`, `listAuthorRoles`. Checkbox groups over a catalog (content types, levels) are written with `syncCatalogJoin` (`services/shared/catalog-join.service.ts`), which replaces the whole join set inside the caller's transaction; for the levels it matches the catalog by `slug` (`catalogKey`), because that catalog is edited by staff and has no codes.

### Levels — `/administracion/niveles`

`services/staff-levels/`: who each course is aimed at, with an orientative Elo range. It is the one catalog edited from the interface, which is why it is identified by `slug`:

- `listLevels()` — every level, retired ones included, with its range worded and how many courses have it. `listLevelOptions()` — the same rows as the course editor offers them (name and range, without the description); the retired ones come too, because the editor saves the whole set.
- `createLevel` — name, slug (unique, `levelSlug` otherwise; `levelSlugShape` when it is not lowercase words joined by hyphens), description, `eloMin` (0–4000), `eloMax` (optional: empty is "no upper bound", stored as `null`) and `order` (empty = after the others).
- `updateLevel(levelId)` — the same fields plus `isActive`. Only the row is written: renaming, moving or retiring a level never touches `CourseLevel`.
- Both refuse a range that is not whole numbers within 0–4000 or whose maximum is below its minimum (`levelElo`; `isValidEloRange`), and the database check `level_elo_range` is behind them. A typo in `eloMax` fails (`formOptionalInt`) instead of being read as "no bound". Ranges are not forced to be contiguous nor disjoint.
- There is no delete. The ranges guide whoever chooses a course; no action reads them to decide what a student may open.

### Source entities — `/administracion/entidades`

`services/staff-sources/`: the platforms, publishers and providers the courses cite as the origin of their material («Procedencia del recurso»). Like authors, editorial content edited from the interface and never deleted: `createSourceEntity` (name, unique ignoring case through the hand-written index `source_entity_name_ci`, answered with `entityTaken`; at least one kind from `SourceEntityKind`, `entityKindRequired` otherwise; website; description) and `updateSourceEntity` (same fields; the kinds are replaced whole with `syncCatalogJoin`). Catalog readers: `listSourceTypes`, `listSourceEntityKinds`, `listSourceEntities` (with kinds and how many sources cite each), `listSourceEntityOptions` (id and name, for the source form's select). `source-rules.ts` decides what the entity field is called for a kind of resource: «Editorial» for a book, «Plataforma o proveedor» for a digital course, «Entidad de origen» otherwise.

The sources themselves are edited in the course editor's «Procedencia del recurso» panel (`CourseSources`): `saveCourseSource(courseId, formData)` creates one (with `order = nextOrder(count)`) or, with a hidden `sourceId`, rewrites it, scoped to the course (`sourceMissing` otherwise; `entityMissing` for an entity that is gone); only `sourceTypeCode` is required — title, authors (free text), entity, URL, year (1400–2100), edition, specific reference and staff-only notes are optional. `deleteCourseSource` removes one. `getCourseAdminDetail` returns them in order, and the student's `getCourseById` returns the same rows without the notes.

### Classes (support) — `/administracion/clases`

`services/staff-classes/`: global read view with filters (teacher, status, date range, text), payment data included, but exactly **two** actions: `setRecordingUrl` and `cancelClassAsStaff`. Blocks, attendance, participants and metadata belong to the teacher so that no two roles edit the same thing.

## Forms

Panels are Server Components with plain `<form action={serverAction}>` elements; the only `useActionState` forms are account creation and password reset. Tables use `PlatformTable` (semantic `<table>`, filters via search params). Uploading a cover uses `components/platform/shared/image-upload.comp.tsx`: it checks type and size in the browser (`constants/platform/upload.const.ts`, 5 MB, jpg/png/webp/avif), asks `createUploadSignature()` (staff only, 60 signatures per minute), posts the file straight to Cloudinary with the signed `folder`, `timestamp` and `allowed_formats`, and leaves the URL in a hidden field of the form.
