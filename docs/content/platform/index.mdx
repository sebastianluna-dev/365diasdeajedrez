# Learning platform

Everything behind the login: accounts and roles, the Prisma data model, and what each role can do.

- [Authentication & roles](/platform/authentication) — sessions, passwords, the identity DAL, roles by row existence, the proxy and the login flow.
- [Data model](/platform/data-model) — the Prisma schema: catalogs, courses and lessons, users and progress, studies and games, classes, activity, hand-written constraints and migrations.
- [Student features](/platform/student) — dashboard, courses and lessons, exercises, Move Trainer, studies, classes, position explorer, daily goal and streak.
- [Classes](/platform/classes) — live classes: their phases, what a payment covers, who may do what, and the single writers of seats, attendance, status and schedule.
- [Exercises](/platform/exercises) — the exercise bank, its editor, the series a teacher gives, and the student's «Ejercicios»: solving rules, recording an attempt, visibility, writers and the flag.
- [Play](/platform/play) — games against the bots and the profile: routes, permissions, services and actions, and why playing never counts toward the streak.
- [Adventure mode](/platform/adventure) — «Modo aventura», the illustrated journey of «Jugar».
- [Teacher & staff panels](/platform/teacher-and-staff) — assigned students, classes and class blocks, the exercise bank and its series, accounts, teachers and assignments, the course editor and class support.
