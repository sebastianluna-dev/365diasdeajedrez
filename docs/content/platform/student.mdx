# Student features

Everything here requires a session; the pages live in `app/(platform)` and the logic in `services/*`. Students see only **published** courses and only the games the visibility rules allow.

## Dashboard — `/inicio`

`services/dashboard/dashboard.service.ts` → `getUserDashboard()`, which four sections read (profile, continue studying, stats, recent activity) and is therefore wrapped in `cache()`: one run of its queries per request, not one per section. Until 2026-10-03 it was not, and each section ran every query again — the sums four times over.

- **Profile**: display name, email, member since.
- **Continue studying**: the first course in progress, by name — the order of «Mis cursos» — with the lesson left off at (`CourseProgress.lastLessonId`) and its percentage (`getContinueStudyingCourse()` in `services/courses`).
- **Next class**: the first class ahead that is not cancelled (`getNextClass()`, `services/classes`), drawn as the same `ClassTicket` «Mis clases» uses, with how many classes are left under it («Te quedan 3 clases»). On the day of a class the section comes first, across the row, and «Continuar estudiando» steps back to a secondary button.
- **Stats** for four calendar ranges (`week` from Monday, `month`, `year`, `all` — `lib/date-ranges.ts`) summed over `UserStatDaily` (metric totals and topic breakdown) in **one query**, labelled from the catalogs. `statRangeSumsQuery` (`dashboard.mapper.ts`) is raw SQL because Prisma's `groupBy` cannot sum conditionally: one `SUM("value") FILTER (WHERE "day" >= <start>)` per range, its start from `rangeStart`, `all` without a filter, grouped by metric and topic. `bucketStatRangeSums` splits the rows per range and leaves a group out of a range where its sum is `NULL` (no row in it), which is what the four `groupBy` it replaced returned; the tests compare both on the edges of each range. The day of a bucket is the study day in `STUDY_DAY_TIMEZONE` (`statsDay`), the same day the streak uses, so an evening lesson in Mexico counts on that day and not on the next one in UTC.
- **Recent activity**: last 8 `UserActivity` rows with their type, subject type and topic in one query (Prisma's join strategy, the default since `relationJoins` is on), and the name of each subject read by type — one query per subject type present among the eight, none for a type that is not (an exercise is named by its `Exercise` title, and an id that table does not hold — the dormant line trainer's old activity — after its `TrainingExercise`'s lesson, joined in the same query; both are read at once).
- **Daily goal and streak** (`services/study-goal`): `getStudyGoal()` brings both for the dashboard, `getStudyStreak()` the streak alone as `{ days, studiedToday }` (`streakOf`), and `getStreakOverview()` the streak column of «Mis cursos» (2026-10-07): the streak, the last sixteen weeks of activity day by day (`weeksUpTo`, `activityLevel`), the days studied in them, the best streak in the window (`longestStreak`), and this week's days and minutes (`minutesSince` its Monday) — without activity today the streak still stands, and the card shows the figure it becomes by studying. The streak is derived from any activity in the last 400 days using day keys in `STUDY_DAY_TIMEZONE` (`America/Mexico_City`) via `lib/study-streak.ts`; minutes today = `estimatedDuration` of lessons completed today. The goal (`User.dailyGoalMinutes`, options 10…120) is changed by `updateDailyGoal`. Both cards are on the dashboard, under its first row (`StudyGoalSection`); they were the right column of «Mis cursos» until that page was redesigned.

With the student's session already read, the dashboard's own reads are at most nine queries in two parallel rounds: the activity, the sums and the two catalogs, then up to five name lookups. `getContinueStudyingCourse()` runs beside the first round with its own reads: the progress row and, when there is a course in progress, the course and the student's state in it.

## Courses and lessons — `/cursos`

A course is **added** before it is studied. There is no table of enrolments: a course is the student's from the moment it has a `CourseProgress` row (`isAdded` in the mapper), which is written by the button of its information page (`addCourse`, as `NOT_STARTED`) or by opening one of its lessons (`touchLesson`, as `IN_PROGRESS`).

| Page                                     | What it is                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/cursos`                                | «Mis cursos»: the courses the student added as covers in two columns, «Explorar cursos» in the head and, beside the courses, the streak's column with the last four months of activity. |
| `/cursos/explorar`                       | «Explorar cursos»: every published course; `?nivel=<slug>` narrows the list to a level.                                                                                                 |
| `/cursos/<slug>`                         | The course's **information** page: what it is, who it is for, its chapters, where its material comes from. Where it is added.                                                           |
| `/cursos/<id>`                           | The course to **study**: its chapters with the student's progress. Redirects to the information page when the course is not added.                                                      |
| `/cursos/<id>/<chapterOrder>`            | A chapter: its lessons, each with the moves of its game. Same redirect.                                                                                                                 |
| `/cursos/<id>/<chapterOrder>/<lessonId>` | A lesson: the column of the chapter's lessons, the board and the commentary. It hangs from the chapter so the column can live in a layout; `/lecciones/<lessonId>` answers 308 to it.   |

`services/courses/courses.service.ts`:

- `getCourseCatalog()` — every published course with what the student has in it: `isAdded`, `thumbnail` (the course's square image, or its cover cropped square), progress (`completedLessons/totalLessons` **and** minutes), content types, authors, levels (`{ slug, name, description, eloRange, eloMin, eloMax }`, the easiest first), a CTA (`Empezar` / `Continuar` / `Repasar`), `href` (the course to study) and `infoHref` (its information page).
- `getUserCourses()` — «Mis cursos»: of the published courses, the ones the student added, as `MyCourseItem` (`mapMyCourse`): the summary of the catalog plus `learnHref`, the next lesson to learn, what its «Repasar» says (`CourseReview`: `reviewCount`, the exercises of the lessons already learned whose review is **due** today, and `isReviewUpToDate`, true when something learned has exercises and none is due — the button then says «Al día») and `reviewHref`. Every course's figures come from one pass (`getCourseReviews`: the student's schedule, then the due exercises and the learned lessons of every course, added up by course), not from a count per row.
- `listCourseLevels()` — the levels in use, in order of difficulty, for the catalog's filters and for the labels and the scale that say who a course is for. The catalog filters in the browser (`CatalogBrowser`, 2026-10-07): several levels, topics and whether a course is the student's at once; `/cursos/explorar?nivel=<slug>` ticks that level on arrival (a slug the catalog does not have is ignored). The filter is a lens: **no course is restricted by Elo** and the student has no rating stored.
- `resolveCourseReference(reference)` — where `/cursos/<reference>` leads: `{ kind: "study" }` for an id (`isCourseId`: eight digits) and `{ kind: "info" }` for a slug. An id nobody has is tried again as a slug, for a course whose slug happened to be eight digits before that was refused (`isReservedCourseSlug`, which also reserves `explorar`).
- `getCourseInfo(slug)` / `getCourseStudy(courseId)` — the same `CourseDetail` either way (with both images: the 21:9 `cover` the information page shows and the 1:1 `thumbnail` the study pages head with): chapters ordered by role (intro, content, closing — `services/shared/content-order.ts`), each with its `name` (whole, as the staff wrote it: «Los años de aprendizaje (1957–1959)» — what a name carries in parentheses is part of it and is never split off), its duration, how many lessons it has, whether the staff marked it as priority (`isPriority`), and the student's progress as a count (`completedLessons`/`totalLessons`, and `isCompleted` once every lesson in view is learned — a chapter has no percentage: the bar is the course's); `lessonCount` and `totalMinutes` of the whole course; `learnHref`, where «Aprender» leads (`learnTarget`: the lesson left off at if unfinished, the first unfinished one otherwise and, in a finished course, its first lesson — the button starts the course over, it is not switched off; absent only without lessons in view); `sources`, the course's «Procedencia» (the staff's notes stay in the panel). It honours `onlyPriorityLessons` — with it on, what passes is a lesson marked as priority and every lesson of a chapter marked as priority, and nothing else (a lesson the student already opened or finished used to pass too, until 2026-10-07; the progress, counted over what passes, then covers the priority lessons only) — and reports how many lessons the filter hides. `isCompleted` says the course is whole, which is what draws its check. The study view adds what its «Repasar» says (`getCourseReview`): `reviewCount`, the exercises of the lessons already learned whose review is due today, and `isReviewUpToDate`.
- `getChapterView(courseId, chapterOrder)` — the lessons of a chapter (by `order` number) with the `CourseDetail` around them, the neighbour chapters (by name), `hiddenLessons` and, for each lesson, `isNext`, `moves` and `result`: every move of its game's main line in Spanish notation and how it ended (`gameMoves` in `lib/chess/game-moves.ts`), read for this chapter alone.
- `getCourseOutline(courseId, chapterOrder)` — what the column beside a lesson shows: the course and **every chapter of it** (`id`, `name`; a chapter's name is not a link) with its lessons (`id`, `name`, `href`, `statusCode`), in reading order and filtered by the student's preferences; a chapter the filter empties is left out, and the whole is null when the course has no chapter of that order (2026-10-06; it was the open chapter alone, `getChapterOutline`). It is read by the chapter's **layout**, so it is not fetched again on a move to another lesson, and it costs no PGN — not the chapter's, which `getChapterView` does read to show each lesson's moves, and not the lesson's, which is the page's.
- `getLessonView(lessonId)` — the lesson: name, description, PGN (`lessonPgnOf`: linked game or own `pgn`), orientation, position inside the chapter, previous/next, trainable flag. It no longer carries the chapter's lessons: that is the column's, and the column is the layout's.

Actions (`courses.actions.ts`): `addCourse` (writes the progress row and redirects to the course to study; idempotent), `touchLesson` (marks course/chapter/lesson in progress and updates `lastLessonId`), `completeLesson` (completes lesson, cascades chapter and course completion, records `LESSON_COMPLETED`/`COURSE_COMPLETED` activity with the lesson's topics), `setOnlyPriorityLessons` (the form carries `returnTo`, the course or chapter page it sits on) `resetCourseProgress` (requires `confirm=on`; deletes the student's lesson and chapter progress of the course and takes the course's back to `NOT_STARTED`, in one transaction; the course stays added, and the activity log, the streak, the trainer's attempts and the course's settings are not touched) and `resetChapterProgress` (the same for ONE chapter, 2026-09-30: its lessons' rows and its own, and then the course's row resealed around what is left — `IN_PROGRESS` while any lesson of the course has been touched, `NOT_STARTED` when none is, `completedAt` cleared either way, and `lastLessonId` only moved when it pointed at a lesson of that chapter). `removeCourse` (takes the course out of «Mis cursos», 2026-09-30: requires `confirm=on` and deletes, in one transaction, the lesson and chapter progress, the trainer's per-lesson state, the chapters added to the trainer, **the student's settings of the course** and the `CourseProgress` row itself — nothing hangs off it, so each table goes by hand —, leaving the activity, the streak and the attempts untouched, and ends on `/cursos`). All of them only act on published courses.

**When an action refuses.** The student's forms follow the panels' pattern: a failure a person can produce (a throttle, an empty PGN, a goal outside the list, a reset without its box ticked) redirects back with `?error=<code>`, the page reads `searchParams.error` and translates it with `STUDENT_ERROR_MESSAGES` (`constants/platform/student-messages.const.ts`) into a `PlatformNotice`. What only a forged request produces (a foreign study, an unpublished lesson) still comes out silently. `touchLesson` never reports: it fires on open and a lost touch costs nothing.

**When an import has gone well.** It is the one result that is told and not only shown: both PGN imports redirect with `?importadas=…` (`withImportReport`, `services/shared/pgn-import-report.ts`), the page reads it back with `readImportReport` — which returns nothing for a value that could not have happened, since anybody can type the parameter — and the section writes the notice with `pgnImportNotice` (`constants/platform/pgn-import-messages.const.ts`): green when everything pasted went in, neutral when something stayed out. The redirect also takes the place of an `?error=` left in the address by an earlier attempt, and neither stays in the address after the page has drawn it: the shell takes both out ([`ClearResultParams`](/components/platform/sections/shell/clear-result-params)), so saving the record of a game just imported re-renders the page without the notice instead of saying it again, and a reload does not repeat it.

The lesson page fills the window and scrolls nowhere — `platform-page_width_full` and `platform-page_height_screen`, with the board measured by the height that is left — in three columns: `LessonOutline` (the course, the chapter and the chapter's lessons, with the one open marked), the board, and the commentary. **The first of the three belongs to the chapter's layout** (`(lesson)/layout.tsx`), so moving to another lesson fetches that lesson's PGN and nothing else: the column stays on screen, keeps its scroll and lights the new row from the browser, while the board and the panel are replaced by `LessonSkeleton` — the same shapes, empty — until the lesson lands. It renders `GameViewer` in `moveList="flow"` mode — a lesson is read, so the moves run on until a comment breaks the line — with `LessonTracker` calling `touchLesson` on open. Under the board there is only the bar of arrows and one button: «Saltar» while there are moves left, «Continuar» at the last one, which calls `completeLesson` — and that action now **redirects** to the next lesson (or back to the chapter when there is none), so finishing a lesson and moving on is one press. The column and the «Siguiente lección» button walk the same visible sequence, so the priority filter hides a lesson from both at once; it costs no extra query, since `mapLessonView` already has the whole course in hand.

## Exercises — `/ejercicios`

Behind `EXERCISES_ENABLED` (off; phase 1 since 2026-10-16). «Ejercicios» is where a student solves the exercises their teacher gave them, as series. The whole of it — the rules, the attempt, the visibility, the writers — is [Exercises](/platform/exercises).

- **The menu** draws «Ejercicios» after «Mis cursos» only for a student with an active teacher or a series they may open (`getStudentExerciseAccess()`, the `"withTeacher"` value of `PlatformNavFeatures.exercises`). From phase 4, with the library, it is drawn for every student.
- **The home** (`/ejercicios`, `getStudentExercisesHome()`) holds one card, «De tu profesor»: each series given to the student by the teacher they are assigned to now, newest first, with «4 de 10» and «Empezar» or «Continuar». The page's accent is «Continuar con «…»», for the newest series with an exercise left that can count today: one tried this study day is skipped, since it would open unscored. Empty, it says «Tu profesor aún no te ha dado ejercicios.», or, for a student with no teacher, «Todavía no tienes ejercicios. Cuando un profesor te dé una serie, aparecerá aquí.»
- **A series** (`/ejercicios/series/[seriesId]`, `getStudentSeries(id)`) lists its exercises in order, each with the student's mark in that series: «Resuelto», «Fallado» or nothing. Only the exercises the student may solve through it are listed, numbered and counted.
- **An exercise** (`/ejercicios/series/[seriesId]/[n]`, `getStudentSeriesExercise(id, n)`) opens on the solve screen, [`ExerciseSolver`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-solver). It opens **unscored** when the exercise was attempted this study day: one scored attempt per exercise per study day.
- **The attempt** is sent by the solver the moment it is decided (`submitExerciseAttempt`). The server replays the moves, stores the attempt with its first mistake and moves the review ladder. On the first pass it records `EXERCISE_PASSED` once, which «Ejercicios superados» and the streak count.

## Move Trainer — `/entrenador`

> **Switched off since 2026-09-28**, and to stay so (Sebastian, 2026-10-03). `MOVE_TRAINER_ENABLED` (`constants/platform/features.const.ts`) is `false`: the Move Trainer and a course's «Repasar» are hidden from the student, not removed. What the switch does, and nothing else:

- `/entrenador` answers 404 (`notFound()` at the top of the page).
- «Repasar» is not drawn: the row of «Mis cursos» leaves it out, and the course's frame only draws it for a count above zero or a course up to date, which `getCourseReview` / `getCourseReviews` no longer give (they answer without querying).
- «Entrenar esta lección» is not drawn: `getLessonView` drops `trainerHref`.
- The confirmation that erases a course's progress does not name the trainer.

Everything below still describes the code, which is whole: the services, the actions, the tables and the students' attempts are untouched, and so is the staff's side — exercises are still written and a lesson is still marked as trainable. Setting the constant to `true` brings all of it back.

- **Home** (`getTrainerData()`): chapters with exercises, split into "my chapters" (`UserTrainerChapter`) and available ones; `toggleTrainerChapter` adds or removes (its form carries `returnTo`; today the trainer's home is the only page with the button).
- **Session** (`getTrainerSession({ lessonId | chapterId | courseId | none })`): exercises of a lesson, a chapter, a course's review (`/entrenador?course=<id>`, what «Repasar» opens: the exercises of the lessons of that course the student has **completed** whose review is **due**, with the very `where` the button counts with — see _What «Repasar» trains_ below), or all chapters added by the student, always filtered by published course. Each `TrainerExercise` carries the frozen `startFen`, `lineSans`, `userColor` (the colour to move in `startFen`; `Lesson.trainingColorId` lets black repertoires start with white's move played), `modeCode` and `isStale`.
- **Play** (`components/platform/sections/trainer/trainer-session.comp.tsx`, client): the student plays their plies on `ChessBoard`; the opponent replies after 450 ms; SAN comparison ignores `+ # ! ?`; mistakes are counted; a summary closes the session.
- **Record** (`recordTrainingAttempt` in `trainer.actions.ts`): validates the exercise against the database, clamps `mistakes` (≤ 999) and `durationMs` (≤ 2 h), stores the `TrainingAttempt` row on `trainingExerciseId` and records `EXERCISE_PASSED` activity when passed. The column was `exerciseId` until 2026-10-16: the exercises' migration renamed it and made it nullable, and `exerciseId` now points at an `Exercise` ([Exercises](/platform/exercises)). A CHECK says each attempt has exactly one of the two. It returns whether the attempt was stored; the session counts the ones that were not and the summary says so. **It writes nothing else**: no `TrainingMistake` rows and no `UserLessonTraining` — `applyReview()` has no caller yet.

Spaced repetition (`lib/training/review-schedule.ts`): intervals `[0, 1, 3, 7, 21, 60]` days; only a **perfect** attempt (no mistakes, no hints) climbs one step; any failure returns to step 0 (due today). `isDueForReview` and `accuracyPercent` are pure and tested.

**What «Repasar» trains** (decided 2026-10-03): only what is **due**. The figure on a course's button and the session it opens are the exercises of the lessons the student completed in that course whose review is due — `UserLessonTraining.nextReviewAt` is now or past, or the lesson was never trained (no row, or a row without a date). One function builds the `where` for both, `dueReviewExerciseWhere` (`services/shared/course-review.service.ts`): it reads the student's schedule, asks `isDueForReview` — the one definition of "due" — which lessons are not due yet, and leaves those lessons out, by id, of what was learned (`reviewExerciseWhere`; with nothing left out it is everything learned, which is how a course tells «Al día» from having nothing learned to review). `getCourseReview`, `getCourseReviews` and `getTrainerSession({ courseId })` all take it, so the count and the session cannot disagree; its test covers a lesson never trained, one due, one due right now and one not due yet. With nothing due but something learned, the button stays, switched off, and says **«Al día»**. **Today the figure still does not go down**: since nothing writes `UserLessonTraining` (see _Record_), every learned lesson is never trained, so every one is due — the count is what it was until the attempts schedule the lessons.

## Studies — `/estudios`

A study is a `GameDatabase`. `getUserStudies()` lists, with `study-rules` permissions already resolved:

| Kind                      | Who creates it                                                        | Notes                                                          |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `MY_GAMES` "Mis partidas" | The system, at account creation (`services/studies/default-study.ts`) | Exactly one per student; cannot be deleted or re-typed.        |
| `STUDY`                   | Student or teacher                                                    | Free material.                                                 |
| `TOURNAMENT`              | Student or teacher                                                    | Games from one competition.                                    |
| `COLLECTION`              | Teacher                                                               | Shared with students through `StudyShare`; read-only for them. |
| Course collections        | Staff (per chapter)                                                   | Visible once the course is started; read-only.                 |

Actions (`studies.actions.ts`): `createStudy`, `updateStudy`, `deleteStudy` (warns about games cited in classes), `rememberOpenedGame` (the game a study was left on, `StudyVisit`), `importPgnGames` (reads up to 500 games / 2 MB with `readImportedGames` of `services/shared/pgn-import.ts`, maps headers to columns, indexes positions, all in one transaction with a 120 s timeout, and redirects to the first game that went in with `?importadas=<stored>.<without moves>.<over the limit>`, which the game page turns into «Se importaron N partidas.» or into how many of how many went in and why the rest stayed out), `createStudyGame` (blank, from a FEN, or from a PGN sent with the record: its first game — loose text such as a title above it is skipped — fills the fields left empty, read by `readPastedGame` of the same `pgn-import.ts`, so a date or an Elo means the same as in an import), `updateGameSettings` (the list's settings of a game: its name, the side its board opens from, its analysis mode and its pinned comment; the PGN is not touched), `clearGameAnnotations` and `clearGameVariations` (the two clean-ups of the list's settings: they rewrite the PGN with `clearAnnotations`/`clearVariations` of `lib/chess/pgn-edit` and index its positions again), `updateGameDetails` (players, Elo, titles, federations, result, date, event, round, ECO), `autosaveGamePgn` (the viewer saves edits in place; returns an `AutosaveResult`), `deleteStudyGame`, `reorderStudyGames` (drag/keyboard; `planFullReorder`), `copyClassGamesToStudy`, `shareStudyWithStudent` / `unshareStudyWithStudent` (teachers).

- **The list is a library** (2026-10-07): each study a cover with its board, a search, tabs by kind and an order, all in the browser ([`StudiesBrowser`](/components/platform/sections/studies/studies-list/studies-browser)). `getUserStudies()` reads, with the studies, every visible game's title, players and event in one query, and folds them with the study's name, description and origin into the `searchText` the search matches (`lib/fold-for-search.ts`: no case, no accents); a game's PGN never travels for it.
- **A study opens where it was left** (2026-10-07). Which game: `StudyVisit` (one row per person and study, its only writer `rememberOpenedGame`, called by the game page from the browser once the game is shown, so a prefetch writes nothing); the list reads it for the card's link, its board — where that game ends — and «Recientes» (by when it was opened; a study never opened, by when it was created), and the study's own URL redirects to it (`getLastOpenedGameId`). Which move inside the game: the browser's `localStorage`, per game, written on every move ([`GameViewSection`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/game-view-section)), so it follows the device and not the account.
- A study has no page of its own: its URL redirects to the first game, and the column beside the board carries the study. That column is mounted by the **layout** of the game pages (`app/(platform)/estudios/[studyId]/partidas/layout.tsx`, 2026-10-05), not by the page: going from one game to another renders the board and the game's record again and leaves the study as it is, so its list does not blink or lose its scroll. The layout loads the study with every game (`getStudyWithGames`; light rows, no PGN) and the list shows them with `title` or a label built from round/position, the players and the result token, a hundred at a time (`STUDY_GAMES_PAGE_SIZE`): it opens on the page the current game falls on and turns its pages in the browser, with no `?pagina=` any more. The current game is read from the address by the list's client leaf (`StudyGames`), since a layout is not told when it changes; after a server action that revalidates, the layout is rendered again like the page. The owner reorders by drag or keyboard, only when the study fits in one page, and edits the study from the three bars at the end of the card's head (`StudySettings`), which open its form, with «Eliminar estudio» at the form's foot; the owner of a collection gets the sharing card. A study without games renders `/estudios/[studyId]` as its empty page, with the same actions.
- Game page (`/estudios/[studyId]/partidas/[gameId]`) renders `GameViewer` with `engine`, editing enabled when `canEdit`, player strips with federation flags (`lib/chess/federations.ts`) and FIDE titles, game tools (comment, NAGs, share/export), and the game review. For a teacher on their own game, with `EXERCISES_ENABLED` on, `getGameById` sets `canCreateExercise` (the mapper adds the ownership to the service's word) and the viewer offers «Crear ejercicio desde aquí» on every move — in the move's menu and in the tools' «Compartir» — a link to `/profesor/ejercicios/nuevo?estudio=&partida=&jugada=` ([Exercises](/platform/exercises)).
- `/estudios/clases` lists the games of the student's classes (derived from `ClassBlock` references), with a copy-to-study action. **A game reaches the student only through a block they may see** (`studentVisibleClassBlockWhere`, `services/shared/class-block-visibility.ts`, the SQL twin of `isBlockVisibleToStudent`): of a class they are in that is not cancelled, «para preparar» always, «después de la clase» once the class ended or was finalized, the teacher's script never. The same `where` feeds the visible games of the explorer (`buildVisibleGamesWhere`, which takes `now` since 2026-10-15), the class a game was seen in, and the copy to a study.

## Classes — `/clases`

`services/classes/classes.service.ts` reads the student's seats and payments ONCE per request (`readStudentSeats`, `cache()`), and every reader derives from it: `getUserClasses(pastPages)` — today, upcoming, how many classes are paid without a date, the past by month ten at a time, the balance and the join data of today's open link —, `getNextClass()`, `getStudentSeatBalance()` and `getClassById(classId)`, which adds the one query for the blocks and the transcript. The student reads in the academy's zone (`STUDY_DAY_TIMEZONE`, D7) and never sees an amount: a payment reaches the mapper as a day and a count of classes (`paymentSeats`, `services/shared/payment-seats.ts`, gives what is left), and a classmate as a name. **Nor which payment a class belongs to** (the owner's rule of 2026-10-15): no «Clase 2 de 4», no «de tu pago del …» on any student screen; the balance is counts, and a month of past classes is a month, never a payment.

`classes.mapper.ts` is pure and tested: the phase (`classPhase`) and which page the student gets (`studentClassView`: ahead until half an hour after the end, review after, whether or not the teacher finalized); the title («Clase con GM Profesor Demo» without a topic); the people line («con … · Grupal, con Mateo Ríos, Diego Salas y Lucía Herrera»); the join data (the link only from the instant it opens — the one the staff chose, or 30 minutes before — to half an hour after the end; before that only the instant, so the countdown asks for the page again; a class without a link gets neither); the coverage («Esta clase es gratis.», or nothing); a moved seat («No asististe · tu clase pasó al vie 16 oct», a link, or «… tu clase queda por programar» — only for a paid seat) and the make-up it became («Es la reposición de tu clase del mié 14 oct.», with the staff's note); a new date and a cancellation with their notes («Tu clase pagada sigue en pie: la programaremos contigo.» only when the seat was paid); the blocks the phase lets the student see (`isBlockVisibleToStudent`), «Nuevo» on one added after the class started (`isNewClassBlock`), and a game with its players, result, orientation, the study's «Ocultar los siguientes movimientos» and the pinned comment, with «Abrir la partida» only when the study is the student's own; the board of a past class (`pastBoardOf`: the first block with chess the student may see — a position's FEN, a game's or a lesson's at the move the block points at or at the end of its main line; none for a class without chess); the balance line («Te quedan 3 clases: 2 programadas y 1 por programar.»); the past by month.

The pages: `/clases` (`ClassesListSection`: the tabs «Próximas» and «Pasadas» (`?vista=pasadas&pagina=<n>`), the classes ahead as `ClassTicket`s by day with the paid ones without a date in one notice, the past ones as `PastClassCard`s on a line of months, and `PaidClassesCard` in counts beside them, sticky from 1024px and under the list below it; with today's link open, `ClassJoinBar` as a sibling of the page, D20; the design is the owner's «8 · Boletos y tarjetas, sin pagos» of 2026-10-15), `/clases/[classId]` (`ClassAheadSection` or `ClassReviewSection` by `studentClassView`, wide; a class the student has no seat in is a 404 with «Ver mis clases»), both with a `loading.tsx`. `saveClassGameToStudy` (`classes.actions.ts`) is the student's one write about a class: a copy of a game block's game into a study of their own, like `copyPlayedGameToStudy`, back with `?aviso=gameSaved.<studyId>.<gameId>`. `components/platform/shared/class-content-renderer/class-content-renderer.comp.tsx` renders blocks: text as paragraphs (lines starting with `## ` become headings), games and lessons in `GameViewer` opened at `initialPath`, positions in `StaticDiagram`.

## Position explorer — `/explorador`

Shared by students and teachers. The client board sends a FEN to `searchPosition(fen, filters)` (`services/game-explorer`), which validates legality, hashes it with `createPositionHash` and reads `GamePosition`:

- `totalGames` (distinct games that reached the position), `nextMoves` (continuations by UCI with counts and percentages), and up to 20 games, newest first, each with its **origin** (`services/shared/game-origin.ts`: student study, course, teacher/editor), whether it was seen in class, and an `href` if the viewer can open it.
- Visibility comes from `getVisibleGamesWhere()`; teachers additionally see games of actively assigned students. Filters by origin exist; the signature leaves room for player, colour, Elo and date.
- A search refused by the rate limit comes back with `throttled: true` and empty figures; the explorer shows a notice instead of "0 partidas" and does not cache that answer.

## Tools — `/herramientas`

Behind `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED` (off). «Herramientas» in the top bar gathers the analysis board (a FEN or PGN on the study's viewer, the module to play the position against, «Guardar en un estudio»; it persists nothing), the explorer and — while it is on — the trainer. See [Analysis board](/chess/analysis-board).

## Play and profile — `/jugar`, `/perfil`

Behind `PLAY_ENABLED` (off). Games against the bots at `/jugar`, one URL per game, saved move by move and never among the studies' games; the profile at `/perfil/<slug>` with the level against bots, the games and the study streak, and every game at `/perfil/<slug>/partidas`. «Ajustes» gains the user name, the status line and the flag. **Playing never writes `UserActivity`**: the streak, the minutes and the days studied do not move. The whole of it is [Play](/platform/play).

## Activity and statistics

`services/shared/user-activity.service.ts` → `recordUserActivity(db, { userId, typeCode, subjectTypeCode, subjectId, topicId?, occurredAt?, meta? })` appends the fact and upserts the daily aggregate for the metric (and per topic). Like every shared writer it receives the Prisma client (or a transaction) as its first parameter and runs inside the caller's transaction, so a write that is refused afterwards leaves no activity behind. Callers: lesson/course completion, class attendance (marked by the teacher, in the same transaction as the marks), exercise passed (`recordExerciseAttempt`, once per student and exercise, on the first pass; and the dormant trainer's `recordTrainingAttempt`), game analysed.
