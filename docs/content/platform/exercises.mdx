# Exercises («Ejercicios»)

A teacher records a chess exercise once: a starting position and a solution **played on the board**, with variants and comments. The student solves it on any screen: they play their moves and the platform answers the opponent's until the line ends. The exercise can go into a series the teacher gives their students, and every attempt is kept with its first wrong move. The owner made 44 decisions on 2026-10-16 (D01–D44, `planning/ejercicios/DECISIONES.md`, local), and the module is being built in phases from them (`planning/ejercicios/PLAN.md`, local). This page describes what is built. Each phase adds itself here when it lands.

Everything sits behind `EXERCISES_ENABLED` (`constants/platform/features.const.ts`), which is **off**. The owner switches it on locally to check a phase ([The flag](#the-flag)).

## The rules that do not bend

- **One table holds every authored exercise.** `Exercise` holds the teacher's exercises, the academy's library and, from phase 2, a lesson's (owned by the lesson). `TrainingExercise` stays only for the dormant line trainer's derived line (D36). In the code, «exercise» always means `Exercise`.
- **The solution is a PGN, and it is the source of truth.** It holds `[SetUp]`/`[FEN]` and the moves, with variants, comments and NAGs. Nobody types moves (D10). The kind, the N of «Mate en N» and the four frozen columns are derived from it and written only together with it.
- **Only the author's lines are accepted** (D02). That includes «Mate en N»: a legal move outside the tree is wrong, even one that gives checkmate. An author who knows a second mate adds it as a variant. The opponent always answers with the main line.
- **The first wrong move decides** (D19). The attempt fails at that moment and is sent at once, so leaving the page cannot hide a failure. The student may keep trying, but nothing after the decision belongs to the attempt. «Ver la solución» is a failure too (D20).
- **One scored attempt per exercise per study day.** For the rest of that day the exercise runs unscored («Ya lo intentaste hoy: este intento no cuenta.») and stores nothing. A failure therefore never becomes a pass seconds later, in «Ejercicios superados», the streak, the review ladder or the teacher's view.
- **The streak and «Ejercicios superados» count each exercise once**, on its first pass. A pass the same day as a failure is unscored, so it never counts.
- **A teacher's series reaches a student only while that teacher is assigned to them** (D07). The share row survives a reassignment but shows nothing; the series comes back if the student is assigned to that teacher again.
- **No engine anywhere in the exercise path** (D03). «Mate en N» is checked by the rules of chess: every line ends in the player's mate within N moves, and for N ≤ 2 a search of every defence proves the key move forces it.
- **A student writes attempts, never exercises.** `services/exercises/exercise-roles.test.ts` keeps the student's side from reaching the exercise writers or the teacher's and staff's services.

## What phase 1 delivers

Phase 1 is D40's first delivery: the teacher creates, the student solves. Commits 1–5, 7, 8 and 10 on `feat/ejercicios` (2026-10-16), not pushed; 6 and 9 are phase 1b (`feat/ejercicios-1b`, 2026-10-16).

- **The teacher's bank** (`/profesor/ejercicios`) has three views: «En uso», «Archivados» and «Series».

  - «Posición»: place the pieces, paste a FEN or PGN, or start from a move of one of the teacher's study games;

## The life of an exercise

Every insert is a `DRAFT` (`createExerciseRecord`). A button that asks for another status calls the status writer in the same transaction. The moves are a pure table, `EXERCISE_TRANSITIONS` (`services/shared/exercise-status-transitions.ts`, tested). The editor and the bank draw their buttons from it, so no button offers what the save would refuse.

| Status (`ExerciseStatus`)      | What it means                                                                                                                                                            | The teacher's moves (phase 1)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DRAFT` «Borrador»             | Saved, offered to nobody. It only needs a readable start.                                                                                                                | «Guardar y marcar como listo» → `READY`; «Archivar» → `ARCHIVED`                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `READY` «Listo»                | Complete. The teacher's students may get it in a series.                                                                                                                 | «Guardar» keeps it `READY` and refuses an incomplete save; «Archivar» → `ARCHIVED`                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `IN_REVIEW` «En revisión»      | Proposed to the library (phase 4). Its content is locked.                                                                                                                | phase 4                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `PUBLISHED` «En la biblioteca» | In the library (phase 4). Its content is the staff's. For a lesson's exercise (phase 2) it reads «Publicado»: the lesson's students see it once the course is published. | phase 4; the staff's «Guardar y publicar» on a lesson's                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `ARCHIVED` «Archivado»         | Kept for its history, offered nowhere. It leaves its series' students; a lesson's loses its `lessonOrder` and the ones left close the gap.                               | «Restaurar» → `READY` for the teacher's (so it must be complete); the staff's «Restaurar» → `DRAFT` for the academy's and, since 2026-10-16, for a lesson's, which comes back at the end of the lesson (`lessonOrder` given back) and is published again from there |

- **Readiness.** `exerciseReadiness` needs a solution that passes `analyzeSolution`, a title and at least one topic. The difficulty is always present: it is required, from 400 to 3000 in steps of 50. Proposing to the library also needs an explanation.
- **What a draft must pass.** `exerciseSolutionProblem` blocks even a draft: a PGN that does not read (`unreadable`), a start chess cannot have (`badPosition`), another variant (`unsupportedVariant`), or a kind and N that do not go together (`mateInMissing`, `mateInOnSequence`).
- **Locked content.** An exercise in review or in the library is locked (`isExerciseContentLocked`). The editor shows it read-only with its reason and offers «Duplicar».
- **«Duplicar».** It copies the content whitelist (`exerciseCopyInput`) with `copiedFromId`, and the copy starts as a `DRAFT`. It never copies the review's stamps or `sourceGameId`, and the frozen columns are derived again from the PGN.
- **«Eliminar».** It sits behind a `PlatformConfirmPopup` and only deletes an exercise nobody has tried, in `DRAFT`, `READY` or `ARCHIVED`. The condition travels in the `deleteMany`'s `where`, so a refusal is `deleteBlocked`, never a lost history. Deleting also removes the exercise's place in a series, which leaves a gap; the next addition goes after the highest position.
- **The level** is never stored. «Nivel: Intermedio (1400–1799)» comes from `levelOfDifficulty`: the easiest active `Level` whose Elo range holds the difficulty.

**The editor's save** (`saveTeacherExercise`) is one of the documented `useActionState` exceptions ([Teacher & staff panels → Forms](/platform/teacher-and-staff#forms)):

- The board and the fields live in the browser, so a refused save comes back with its values and each error's tab, and a successful one redirects to the exercise with `?aviso=`.
- The save is one transaction: `createExerciseRecord` or `syncExerciseSolution`, the other columns, the topics through `syncCatalogJoin`, and the status move when the button asked for one.
- The status check is part of the update itself, so a change made in between is refused.
- The component is [`ExerciseAuthoring`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-authoring).

## Solving

The rules are those of `lib/chess/exercise-solver.ts`, written out in [Chess → Library → Exercises: the format and the solver](/chess/library#exercises-the-format-and-the-solver):

- **Matching:** a move matches any child of its node, the main line or a variant, compared as moves, not as text.
- **The reply** is always the main child.
- **Completion:** the exercise ends when a matched move, or its reply, has no children.
- **The first unmatched move** fails the attempt and is recorded as its mistake.
- **«Pista»** shows the author's texts in order, then circles the piece to move.
- **«Ver la solución»** plays the main line from where the board stands, and counts as a failure.
- **The outcome:**
  - PASSED: completed and never failed;
  - perfect: passed with no hint;
  - for the review: clean, hinted or failed.

**The solve screen** is one client component, [`ExerciseSolver`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-solver), over the pure reducer `exercise-solver-session.ts`. It runs in three modes:

- `solve`: submits the attempt;
- `unscored`: a second try the same study day, «Reintentar», or a reload. It submits nothing.
- `preview`: the editor's «Probar». It submits nothing.

It behaves like this:

- a correct move gets «¡Bien!» and, 450 ms later, the reply with its sound;
- a wrong move shows in the danger colour for 600 ms and is taken back;
- the result panel says «¡Resuelto!», «Resuelto con pista», «Fallado» («Jugaste Cf7+; la jugada era Dg8+.») or «Viste la solución», with the explanation and «Ver la solución completa»;
- after a retry, or when the server answers that the attempt did not count, it says only how it went this time, never «¡Resuelto!»;
- the layout is designed for the phone first (D39), including a phone on its side.

**The solution reaches the browser.** The solver runs on the client, so a curious student can read the answer in the page's data, as on Lichess. The server replays every attempt, so forging a result takes the right moves anyway, and there is no ranking at stake. The risk is accepted in `WARNINGS.md` (local), which also says how to undo it: a check per move on the server.

## Recording an attempt

`submitExerciseAttempt` (`services/exercises/exercises.actions.ts`) is a plain server action, called by the solver the moment the attempt is decided. It returns `{ ok: true, scored, firstSolve }`, or `{ ok: false, reason }`, which the solver shows as «No se guardó el intento.». It runs these steps:

1. Takes the user from the DAL, checks `EXERCISES_ENABLED`, and limits submits to 120 a minute (`${user.id}:exercise-attempt`).
2. Validates the input with zod (`parseExerciseSubmission`, `services/exercises/exercise-submission.ts`). It caps the moves at 200 and clamps the duration to 0–2 h and the hints to 0–20 (`EXERCISE_ATTEMPT_LIMITS`), the total and each move's `hintsBefore` alike: «Pista» has no cap, so a count above the limit lowers nothing further and never refuses the attempt. The context is `SERIES` with its `seriesId`, or `LESSON` (phase 2, with no id: the lesson is the exercise's own); `PRACTICE` and `REVIEW` are accepted by the schema for phases 4 and 6, `CLASS` waits for phase 3.
3. Reads the exercise through `solvableInContextWhere(context, viewer)`, the same `where` the page read it with. An exercise outside that path is `notFound`.
4. Replays the moves on the server (`replayAttempt`). The result, the mistake and its SANs are the server's. A sequence that decides nothing, or goes on after the decision, is refused and stores nothing.
5. Calls `recordExerciseAttempt` in one transaction, then revalidates `/inicio`, `/ejercicios` and the series — or, for a lesson's attempt, every lesson page and practice page (`lessonPattern`, `lessonPracticePattern`, spelled with their route groups as `revalidatePath(…, "page")` wants — `/(platform)/cursos/…/(lesson)/[lessonId]` —; without them the call matched nothing until 2026-10-16, and `lib/platform-routes.test.ts` now holds each pattern to its page file).

`recordExerciseAttempt` (`services/shared/exercise-attempt.service.ts`) does the rest:

- **The lock comes first.** `lockExerciseFamily` runs `INSERT INTO "UserExerciseState" … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` for the solve key, then `SELECT … FROM "UserExerciseState" WHERE "userId" = $1 AND "exerciseId" = $solveKey FOR UPDATE`. That row lock serialises every submit of the family:
  - a wrong-move submit and a «Ver la solución» before its answer;
  - two tabs;
  - a retry.

  A row created only to hold the lock has `attemptCount` 0 and no dates, which no reader takes for an attempt.

- **The family.** The solve key (`exerciseSolveKey`) is the exercise's own id, or the original's id for a lesson copy (`lessonId` and `copiedFromId` set), so an original and its lesson copy count as one exercise (D22, D28); a teacher's «Duplicar» makes a new exercise, with its own key. The family's ids (`exerciseFamilyIds`, read under the lock) are the key, the exercise and every lesson copy of the key, so solving the original and then the copy — or the copy and then the original — writes one activity, and a fail at one gates the other for the day.
- **The day gate.** It reads the family's `lastAttemptAt` under the lock. If any row of the family was attempted in the current study day (`isSameStudyDay`, `America/Mexico_City`, the streak's day), it writes nothing and answers `scored: false`. The exception is a «Contrarreloj» attempt (phase 7), which still stores its row for the run's score and nothing else.
- **What it writes otherwise:**
  - the `TrainingAttempt` row (`exerciseId`, `seriesId`, the result and the context, the counters frozen at the decision, `hintsUsed`, `solutionShown`, `isPerfect`);
  - at most one `TrainingMistake`: the position's hash and FEN, the expected and the played move in canonical SAN, its ply within the solution, and whether a hint came before;
  - the student's `UserExerciseState`, through `foldExerciseAttempt`, the rule the rebuild shares;
  - on a pass, the first-solve `updateMany` (`firstSolvedAt: null` → now). When it wins and no other row of the family is solved, it writes the `EXERCISE_PASSED` activity. The subject is the solve key, and the topic is `exerciseActivityTopic`: the lowest-order root among the exercise's topics, ties broken by id, so the dashboard keeps its top-level breakdown.

**The review ladder** (`applyExerciseReview`, `lib/training/review-schedule.ts`) has intervals of 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days, each dated at the start of a study day. «Contrarreloj» never moves it.

| Before           | clean           | hinted          | failed         |
| ---------------- | --------------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| not in review    | stays out       | step 1 (3 days) | step 0 (1 day) |
| step k < 4       | step k + 1      | stays k         | step 0         |
| step 4 (60 days) | graduates (out) | stays 4         | step 0         |

`rebuildUserExerciseState(db, userId)` folds a student's attempts again with exactly the writer's rules: the day gate, the ladder and the activity's once. The seed calls it, and it is the drift fixer. An attempt marked `isLegacy` — the phase 2 migration's, moved from the hand-made `TrainingExercise` rows — is folded as the migration folded it (`foldLegacyAttempt`): its facts, never the ladder, never refused by the gate, so the rebuild on a migrated database never rewrites what the migration chose.

**Concurrency is tested twice.** The unit tests run on a fake client with row locks and an ordered statement log (`lib/testing/fake-exercise-db.ts`): two submits of the same exercise racing on the same day store one attempt, and the lock is taken before any read of the state. A fake lock is not PostgreSQL's, so the real one is checked in the app: «Ver la solución» pressed in two tabs of the same session at the same moment stores one attempt ([Verification in the app](#verification-in-the-app)).

## «Crear ejercicio desde aquí»

D11, phase 1b. On any move of a game in the teacher's **own** study, the move's right-click menu ([`MoveContextMenu`](/components/chess/game-viewer/move-context-menu), `createExerciseHref`) and the «Compartir» tab of the tools under the board ([`GameTools`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-tools), `exerciseFromHereHref`; the tab exists because a long press on iOS Safari fires no right click) carry «Crear ejercicio desde aquí»: a **link** to `/profesor/ejercicios/nuevo?estudio=&partida=&jugada=<path>`, so nothing is saved until the author saves.

- **Who gets it.** `getGameById` (`services/studies/studies.service.ts`) resolves `EXERCISES_ENABLED && getTeacherContext() !== null` and hands the mapper that one boolean; `mapGameView` (pure, tested) sets `GameView.canCreateExercise` only when the viewer may also edit the game, i.e. it is their own. A student's game seen by their teacher, a shared collection and a course collection never offer it. [`GameViewSection`](/components/platform/sections/studies/game-view/game-view-section) passes the viewer `teacherRoutes.newExerciseFromGame(studyId, gameId)` — also in the «Ver como lector» view, since the link changes nothing — and the viewer appends `&jugada=` for the move the menu opened on, or the current one in «Compartir».
- **The prefill.** The `nuevo` page reads the three parameters and calls `getNewTeacherExerciseEditor(from)`, which asks `getExercisePrefillFromGame(teacher, from)`: the game is read with `database.userId` **in the `where`** (another teacher's game is not a row to discard, it is not found), `exerciseSeedFromGame` gives the position after the move as the start and everything that follows — variants, comments, NAGs — as the solution, and `exercisePrefillFromGame` (pure, tested) adds the title (the game's own, or «Blancas – Negras, 1999» from its players and date), the source fields and `sourceGameId`. The editor opens on «Solución» (`initialTab`), where its usual warnings list the refuted tries a study often holds («14. Cxe5? Dxe5»: `mistakeVariant` blocks readiness until «Eliminar las variantes marcadas como error»). A game that is not theirs, a broken path or a game that does not read seed nothing: the editor opens as a plain new exercise, with no error, because nothing was asked to be saved. `teacher-exercises.service.test.ts` covers the refusal over a fake database.

## The teacher's view of attempts

D24 and D45, phase 1b. Two screens, both read-only, both from `services/teacher-exercises/teacher-exercises.service.ts`, over the attempts phase 1 records with their first mistake.

- **The student's «Ejercicios» tab** (`/profesor/alumnos/[studentId]/ejercicios`, `getStudentExerciseAttempts(studentId, page)`): the service opens with `requireTeacher()` and answers only for a student with an **active** `TeacherStudent` to this teacher, the same criterion as the student's studies; another teacher's student, or a former one, is a 404 (`teacher-exercises.service.test.ts`). The attempts are `teacherVisibleAttemptsWhere` ([Who sees what](#who-sees-what)), the latest first, the first `page` pages of 25 («Ver más» adds one, with «25 de 41»); above them «Ejercicios superados» (distinct exercises with a pass among the visible attempts), «Intentos» and «Últimos 7 días». Each row is an [`ExerciseAttemptRow`](/components/platform/shared/exercise-attempt-row): the exercise (a link to the editor when it is the teacher's own), where («Serie: Táctica 1», «Práctica libre», or «De una serie eliminada» when the series is gone), when, the time, the outcome as a tag with the four words the student saw — «Resuelto», «Resuelto con pista», «Fallado», «Vio la solución» (`attemptOutcomeOf`: «Ver la solución» is failed whatever the moves were) — and, for a failure at a wrong move, «Ver el error», a disclosure with the position at the mistake and the move played (danger) and the one expected (success) drawn over it ([`StaticDiagram`](/components/chess/static-diagram) with `arrows`, the squares from `moveSquaresOfSan`) and said: «Jugó Td7 en vez de Td8#». «Archivar» changes a status, and the rule never reads one, so an archived exercise keeps every attempt in the tab.
- **The series' «Progreso»** (`/profesor/ejercicios/series/[seriesId]?vista=progreso`, `getSeriesProgress(seriesId)`): a [`SeriesProgressTable`](/components/platform/shared/series-progress-table) over the students the series reaches now (`activeSeriesSharesWhere`) by its exercises in order, from the attempts made **in** it — a cell is solved with a pass there, failed with attempts and no pass (the cross with the count: «Fallado, 2 intentos»), untried with none, the student's own marks (`seriesMarks`) so the two screens agree; «3 de 5» per student and «Resolvieron N de M», the students who solved every exercise. Counted on reading; nothing is stored. The pure mappers (`mapExerciseAttemptRow`, `mapSeriesProgress`) are tested in `teacher-exercises.mapper.test.ts`.

## Who sees what

One where-builder, `services/shared/exercise-visibility-rules.ts` (pure, tested), answers every question of visibility. `exercise-visibility.ts` binds it to the current user and their active teacher, once per request.

- **`buildSolvableExercisesWhere`: what a student may solve.** It is the `OR` of two branches:
  - **the library:** `PUBLISHED`, from phase 4;
  - **a series given to them:** a series reaches them while its teacher is assigned to them (`studentVisibleSeriesWhere`). It holds either the series owner's **usable** exercise (`READY`, `IN_REVIEW` or `PUBLISHED`) or one in the library now. Prisma cannot compare two columns, so the owner is a value: the viewer's active teacher, of whom there is at most one (`teacher_student_one_active`).

  - **a published lesson** (phase 2, D04, D05): a `PUBLISHED` exercise owned by a lesson (`lessonId`) of a published course (`publishedLessonWhere`). The courses are everyone's, so there is no viewer to compare with.

  Phase 3 adds a class block. `DRAFT` and `ARCHIVED` are never solvable. The library is `PUBLISHED` **and `lessonId` null**: a lesson's exercise is never the library's, nor anyone's to pick for a series.

- **`solvableInContextWhere`** narrows the rule to the path an attempt claims:
  - one series;
  - practice: the library;
  - a lesson: the lesson branch;
  - review: any path.
- **`buildPickableExercisesWhere`: what an author may put in a series** (D08). A teacher may pick their own usable exercises and the library's, never another teacher's. The staff may pick only the library's.
- **`buildOwnExercisesWhere`:** what the teacher lists in their bank: their own, in every status.
- **`teacherVisibleAttemptsWhere`** (the teacher's view, phase 1b): the attempts made in the teacher's own series; those left with no series by «Eliminar la serie» (`SetNull`, the `SERIES` context kept) at the teacher's own exercise (D45: shown «De una serie eliminada»; at anyone else's exercise, whose series it was can no longer be told, so they stay out); and those in a neutral context at their own exercise, at one that was ever in the library, or at a lesson's. Never by current status, so archiving hides no history.
- **`seriesItemAvailability`** is the series branch's own condition on one item. The series editor uses it to warn «Archivado: tus alumnos ya no lo ven.» or «Ya no está disponible: …», and the student's pages use it to count and number only what the student may solve.

## Series and sharing

A series (D18) is an ordered list of exercises (`ExerciseSeries`, `ExerciseSeriesItem`).

- **Creating and editing.** «Nueva serie» and «Editar la serie» are popups with `useActionState`. «Nueva serie» goes to the new series.
- **Adding exercises.** «Agregar ejercicios» offers `buildPickableExercisesWhere` minus what the series holds, and the server checks it again. The new items go after the highest position. A collision on `ExerciseSeriesItem_pkey` or `…_seriesId_order_key`, recognised with `isUniqueConstraintError`, answers «La serie cambió mientras tanto: vuelve a intentarlo.».
- **Order.** Reordering sends the full list through `planFullReorder`, and «Quitar» closes the gap (`planDenseRenumber`). A series holds at most 100 exercises (`SERIES_LIMITS`).
- **Deleting.** «Eliminar la serie» keeps the exercises and the students' attempts; their `seriesId` becomes null.
- **Giving it to students.** «Dar a mis alumnos» is [`StudentSharePanel`](/components/platform/shared/student-share-panel), the same panel a collection uses. `shareSeriesWithStudent` and `unshareSeriesWithStudent` require three things, the same three a collection's share checks:
  - an active teacher;
  - a series of theirs;
  - a student currently assigned to them.

  The list shows only the shares to students still assigned, because a share reaches no one else.

- **Rate limits,** per teacher: series edits 120 a minute (`series-edit`), shares 60 (`share-series`).

## The flag

- the class block «Ejercicio» and its routes.

It does **not** hide the staff lesson panel «Ejercicios» nor its editor routes (phase 2): they replace a panel that was live and unflagged over data the phase 2 migration moved, so they render whatever the flag says.
