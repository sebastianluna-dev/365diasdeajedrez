# Play («Jugar»)

Games against Stockfish bots of 400–2 800, saved and listed on the person's profile (`/perfil/<slug>`), and the «Tablero de análisis» under «Herramientas». Being built in phases since 2026-10-08. Both switches in `constants/platform/features.const.ts` stay off (`PLAY_ENABLED`, `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`) until the bots are calibrated and the owner has checked the screens: off, every page answers 404 — also a game opened by its id —, every action `notFound()` or `disabled`, and «Jugar» is not in the menu.

What is in place: the engine client, the bots and their strength ([Bots](/chess/bots)), the rules of a live game (`lib/chess/game-end.ts`, `lib/chess/live-game.ts`, `lib/play/`), the tables ([Data model → Played games](/platform/data-model#played-games-jugar)), the services and actions below, the lobby ([`PlayLobbySection`](/components/platform/sections/play/lobby/play-lobby-section)) and the game itself ([`LiveGameSection`](/components/platform/sections/play/live-game/live-game-section)), played on the one [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer), the profile ([`ProfileHeaderSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-header-section), [`ProfileOverviewSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-overview-section), [`ProfileGamesSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-games-section)), the profile's fields in «Ajustes» and the teacher's «Partidas jugadas». The analysis board lives under «Herramientas», behind `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`: [Analysis board](/chess/analysis-board).

## Rules that do not bend

- **Playing never counts toward the streak.** No game, against a bot or a person, writes the activity log or its daily aggregate: the streak, the minutes and the days studied come from studying content, exercises, lessons and classes only. `services/play/no-activity.test.ts` walks the imports of `services/play`, `services/bots`, `services/profile` and `services/adventure` (`lib/testing/module-reach.ts`, the walker `components/loading-boundaries.test.ts` also uses) and fails if any reaches `services/shared/user-activity.service.ts`, or if a file there names the activity tables. Only `play.actions.ts` may reach the position index, to copy a game into a study.
- **A played game is never a study game.** It is a `PlayedGame`, not a `Game`: «Mis estudios», the explorer and the classes never see it. «Guardar en un estudio» makes a copy.
- **No clock, no undo, no hints against a bot.** The bot answers after a human-like delay (`thinkDelayMs`); the engine on the analysis board plays at full strength, instantly, never with a clock, and is never saved.

## Routes

| Route                               | What it is                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/jugar`                            | The way in: the lobby while `ADVENTURE_ENABLED` is off.                                      |
| `/jugar/partida-libre`              | «Partida libre», the lobby: the bots by group, «Continuar» for the open game.                |
| `/jugar/partidas/<id>`              | A game: played while it is open and the viewer holds a seat; read-only for everyone else.    |
| `/perfil/<slug>`                    | The profile: header, streak, level against bots, scoreboard by bot and the last eight games. |
| `/perfil/<slug>/partidas`           | Every game, twenty a page, filtered by rival and result.                                     |
| `/herramientas/tablero-de-analisis` | The analysis board (phase 4).                                                                |

The three prefixes are in `PROTECTED_PATH_PREFIXES` and in the proxy's matcher; `lib/platform-routes.ts` builds every address (`play`, `freePlay`, `playAgainst`, `playedGame`, `profile`, `userProfile`, `userProfileGames`, `analysisBoard`).

## Who may see what

`Viewer` (`services/play/play-rules.ts`) is the person, the teacher row they hold and whether they are staff, resolved by `currentViewer()` from the DAL. Every read puts the rule **inside its `where`**, so a game or a profile the viewer may not see is never read:

| Viewer  | Played games (`playedGameVisibleWhere`)                  | Profiles (`profileVisibleWhere`)                 |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Anyone  | The games where they hold a seat.                        | Their own.                                       |
| Teacher | Also those of their students under an active assignment. | Also their students' under an active assignment. |
| Staff   | Every game.                                              | Every profile.                                   |

Reading is not writing: every action demands a seat (`seatOf`), and a teacher or the staff see a student's game read-only.

## Services

- `services/bots/` — `listBotGroups()` (the lobby, by group and order) and `getBotBySlug(slug)`; only active bots of a listed group (`isListed`).
- `services/play/play.service.ts` — `getOpenPlayedGame()` (the viewer's open game against a bot), `getPlayedGame(id)`, `listPlayedGamesOf(userId, { page, rival?, result? })` (open first, then the last move, then the start; `PLAYED_GAMES_PAGE_SIZE` 20), `recentPlayedGamesOf(userId)` (`PROFILE_RECENT_GAMES` 8), `botScoreboardOf(userId)`, `botScoreAgainst(userId, botId)` and `botLevelSummaryOf(userId)`.
- `services/profile/` — `getProfileOf(slug)` (the whole profile, for «Visión general»), `getProfileHeadOf(slug)` (only its head — the person, the figures and the level —, for «Partidas»), `getOwnSlug()` and `getOwnPlayerCard()` (the person's own name and portrait, from the session).
- `services/study-goal` — `streakSummaryOf(userId)`, the streak shown on a profile (read, never written by playing).

**«Nivel contra bots»** (`botLevelOf`, `levelSeries`): over the last ten finished games against bots, the average of the bots' Elo plus 400 per net win spread over the games, rounded to 25 and kept within 400–2 800; nothing under five games. An aborted game counts for nothing (`countsForScore`); a resignation or an abandonment is a loss. Computed when read, never stored, and never a rating: `isRated` is false against a bot, by a CHECK.

## Actions

`services/play/play.actions.ts`. Each one checks the switch, resolves the user in the DAL, goes through `allowAction`, validates with zod, keeps the seat inside its `where` and writes only through `writePlayedGameRecord`, in a transaction.

| Action                                           | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `startPlayedGame(formData)`                      | Opens a game against a bot of the gallery (`bot`, `color` white/black/random; a bot of a hidden group is `botNotFound`). With `replace`, the open game against a bot is closed first — abandoned after two moves of one's own, aborted before — else the open game is returned. A person keeps one open game against a bot, which the database backs with two partial unique indexes (one per colour); two tabs pressing at once land on the same game through their `P2002`. |
| `rematchPlayedGame(gameId)`                      | The same bot, colours swapped. Refused (`botNotFound`) for a bot of a hidden group.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `appendPlayedMove(gameId, { expectedPly, san })` | One move, versioned by the ply the browser saw: `ok` with the server's reading of the board (`gameEnd`), `retry` answered as written, `stale` (another tab moved first), `invalid` (illegal) or `finished`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `abandonPlayedGame(gameId, { expectedPly })`     | «Abandonar»: lost by whoever presses it, versioned like a move.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `storePlayedGameReview(gameId, pgn)`             | The review's evaluations into a finished game; refused if one move differs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `copyPlayedGameToStudy(gameId, formData)`        | «Guardar en un estudio»: a `Game` with source `PLATFORM_GAME` in one of one's studies, indexed in the same transaction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

`services/account/account.actions.ts` adds `setUserSlug` (the user name: the CHECK's pattern, not reserved, `slugTaken` on the unique index) and `setProfileDetails` (`statusLine`, `country`). A slug is proposed from the name before every account is created (`proposeUserSlug`); changing it leaves the old address answering 404.

The rules the actions apply are pure and tested without a database: `checkAppend` (`append-rules.ts`), `play-rules.ts`, `profile-rules.ts` and the single writer (`services/shared/played-game-record.ts`, tested with an in-memory writer that applies the same guards). What the actions do against the database is checked in the app (phase 3).

## Modo aventura

Built in phases since 2026-10-09; this is the part that exists without a screen, behind `ADVENTURE_ENABLED` (off, `as boolean`, and it needs `PLAY_ENABLED` too). The story, the rivals and the placements of the art are code, in `lib/adventure/` ([Bots → Borrowed strengths](/chess/bots#borrowed-strengths)); the full page, with the map and the paper frame, comes with the screens.

- **Progress is derived, never stored.** Each rival is a `Bot` in a hidden `BotGroup`; stars, attempts and which page is open are computed from the person's `PlayedGame`s against those bots (`services/adventure/adventure-rules.ts`, pure). Only closed games with a result count — an aborted one does not, a resignation or an abandonment is a loss, a draw is an attempt —; attempts run up to the first win inclusive, and stars follow them (first try ★★★, second or third ★★, later ★); a second win changes nothing. The story waits: only a win opens the next page, the first is open from the start and the last only after the other eleven.
- **The server enforces the story.** `startAdventureEncounter(formData)` (`services/adventure/adventure.actions.ts`) takes the usual order — both switches, the user from the DAL, `allowAction`, zod — and then refuses an encounter still locked with `?error=encounterLocked`. The available rival is «Empezar»; a beaten one «Volver a retar», which touches neither stars nor keepsake. It opens the game with `openGame` (`services/play/open-game.ts`), the same helper `startPlayedGame` and `rematchPlayedGame` use; with `replace` another open game against a bot is closed first, as there. `startPlayedGame` and `rematchPlayedGame` refuse the rivals' bots, so `?rival=` cannot skip the story.
- **Reads.** `getAdventureShelf()` and `getAdventureProgress(slug)` (`adventure.service.ts`) ask for the games against the adventure's rivals in one query — the bot's slug comes with each row, so no query of the bots is needed — and the map adds `getOpenPlayedGame()`: **one query for the shelf, two for the map**, on top of the session. `PlayedGameView.adventure` (slug, title and the encounter: order, name, role, place, zone, final) is derived from the bot's group in the mapper, with no query of its own.
- **Playing still never counts toward the streak:** `services/adventure` is one of the folders `no-activity.test.ts` walks.

## What the server cannot check

Both moves of a game against a bot leave the same browser: the server demands legality, turn order and a seat, not that the bot's move came from Stockfish at the bot's strength. So the scoreboard and the level are a record of what was played, never a rating, and nothing compares people by them.

## Seed and query budget

`prisma/seed-play.ts`, called by the seed: the three catalogs and the 57 bots (45 of the gallery and the 12 rivals of the adventure) upserted from the same `allBotSeeds()` the migrations' SQL is generated from, and the demo student's games, written through the single writer: three in the gallery — won against Tere, lost by resignation against Don Chuy, and one open against Lupita on the student's turn — only when they have played none, and nine in the adventure (`DEMO_ADVENTURE_GAMES`) only when they have played none against its rivals, which leave the map at 4 of 12 (★★★, ★★ after a loss, ★ after three, ★★★, and one lost game against the fifth, «Intento 2»). `prisma/seed-play.test.ts` replays them, the adventure's with `encounterStatesOf`.

The pages have a query budget (`/jugar` ≤ 3, `/perfil/<slug>` ≤ 7, without the session). `PLATFORM_DB_LOG=query` makes `getPlatformDb()` print every query, and the figure that counts is the one of `npm run build && npm run start`, one load at a time: `/ajustes`, whose page reads one row, gives two, that row and the session. Measured that way on 2026-10-09, with the demo seed, the same on every load: `/jugar` 3 (the open game, the groups with their bots, and the record against each; the person's own card comes with the session), `/perfil/<slug>` 6 (the profile with its counts, the last games, the level, the scoreboard, the activity by day and this week's minutes) and `/perfil/<slug>/partidas` 5 (the profile and the level for its head — `getProfileHeadOf`, not the whole profile —, the page's rows and count, and the rivals).
