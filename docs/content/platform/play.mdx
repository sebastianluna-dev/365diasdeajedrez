# Play («Jugar»)

Games against Stockfish bots of 400–2 800, saved and listed on the person's profile (`/perfil/<slug>`), and the «Tablero de análisis» in the tools menu. Being built in phases since 2026-10-08. Both switches in `constants/platform/features.const.ts` stay off (`PLAY_ENABLED`, `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`) until the bots are calibrated and the owner has checked the screens: off, every page answers 404 — also a game opened by its id —, every action `notFound()` or `disabled`, and «Jugar» is not in the menu.

What is in place: the engine client, the bots and their strength ([Bots](/chess/bots)), the rules of a live game (`lib/chess/game-end.ts`, `lib/chess/live-game.ts`, `lib/play/`), the tables ([Data model → Played games](/platform/data-model#played-games-jugar)), the services and actions below and the game itself ([`LiveGameSection`](/components/platform/sections/play/live-game/live-game-section)), played on the one [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer), the profile ([`ProfileHeaderSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-header-section), [`ProfileOverviewSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-overview-section), [`ProfileGamesSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-games-section)), the profile's fields in «Ajustes» and the teacher's «Partidas jugadas». The analysis board lives under «Herramientas», behind `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`: [Analysis board](/chess/analysis-board).

**Every game against a bot is an encounter of the adventure** (2026-10-25, the owner: «todas las partidas contra bots serán en modo aventura»). «Modo aventura» has [its own page](/platform/adventure); «Partida libre», the gallery of 45 bots it used to sit beside, and «Revancha» went away with free play — see [Free play, removed](#free-play-removed). Playing against a person does not exist in the platform; the owner's canvas draws a «Contra una persona» card on `/jugar`, and it is left out on purpose until there is something behind it.

## Rules that do not bend

- **Playing never counts toward the streak.** No game, against a bot or a person, writes the activity log or its daily aggregate: the streak, the minutes and the days studied come from studying content, exercises, lessons and classes only. `services/play/no-activity.test.ts` walks the imports of `services/play`, `services/bots`, `services/profile` and `services/adventure` (`lib/testing/module-reach.ts`, the walker `components/loading-boundaries.test.ts` also uses) and fails if any reaches `services/shared/user-activity.service.ts`, or if a file there names the activity tables. Only `play.actions.ts` may reach the position index, to copy a game into a study.
- **A played game is never a study game.** It is a `PlayedGame`, not a `Game`: «Mis estudios», the explorer and the classes never see it. «Guardar en un estudio» makes a copy.
- **No clock, no undo, no hints against a bot.** The bot answers after a human-like delay (`thinkDelayMs`); the engine on the analysis board plays at full strength, instantly, never with a clock, and is never saved.

## Who may see what

`Viewer` (`services/play/play-rules.ts`) is the person, the teacher row they hold and whether they are staff, resolved by `currentViewer()` from the DAL. Every read puts the rule **inside its `where`**, so a game or a profile the viewer may not see is never read:

| Viewer  | Played games (`playedGameVisibleWhere`)                  | Profiles (`profileVisibleWhere`)                 |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Anyone  | The games where they hold a seat.                        | Their own.                                       |
| Teacher | Also those of their students under an active assignment. | Also their students' under an active assignment. |
| Staff   | Every game.                                              | Every profile.                                   |

Reading is not writing: every action demands a seat (`seatOf`), and a teacher or the staff see a student's game read-only.

## Services

- `services/bots/` — `listBotsById(ids)`: the rivals of games already played, whatever their group, for the profile. (`listBotGroups` and `getBotBySlug` fed the gallery and went with it.)
- `services/play/play.service.ts` — `getOpenPlayedGame()` (the viewer's open game against a bot), `getPlayedGame(id)`, `listPlayedGamesOf(userId, { page, rival?, result? })` (open first, then the last move, then the start; `PLAYED_GAMES_PAGE_SIZE` 20), `recentPlayedGamesOf(userId, take?)` (`PROFILE_RECENT_GAMES` 8; «Jugar»'s side column asks for 4), `botScoreboardOf(userId)` (a game's page finds its rival's record in it, and sums every record for the side column) and `botLevelSummaryOf(userId)`.
- `services/profile/` — `getProfileOf(slug)` (the whole profile, for «Visión general»: the head, the record and the record against each bot named by `listBotsById` of `services/bots`, the streak's sixteen weeks and this month's minutes against the last, `studyOverviewOf`, and the last games), `getProfileHeadOf(slug)` (only its head — the person, the figures and the level —, for «Partidas»), `getOwnSlug()` and `getOwnPlayerCard()` (the person's own name and portrait, from the session).
- `services/study-goal` — `streakSummaryOf(userId)`, `streakOverviewOf(userId)` and `studyMonthComparisonOf(userId)`: the streak, its sixteen weeks and the month's study minutes shown on a profile (read, never written by playing; they live outside `services/profile` so `no-activity.test.ts` keeps watching that folder whole).

**«Nivel contra bots»** (`botLevelOf`, `levelSeries`): over the last ten finished games against bots, the average of the bots' Elo plus 400 per net win spread over the games, rounded to 25 and kept within 400–2 800; nothing under five games. An aborted game counts for nothing (`countsForScore`); a resignation or an abandonment is a loss. Computed when read, never stored, and never a rating: `isRated` is false against a bot, by a CHECK.

## Actions

`services/play/play.actions.ts`. None of them opens a game: the one action that does is the adventure's `startAdventureEncounter` ([Adventure mode](/platform/adventure)), through `openGame` (`services/play/open-game.ts`), which keeps one open game against a bot per person — two partial unique indexes, one per colour, back it, and two tabs pressing at once land on the same game through their `P2002` — and, with `replace`, closes the open one first (abandoned after two moves of one's own, aborted before). `services/play/open-game-callers.test.ts` keeps `openGame` to that one caller. Each action here checks the switch, resolves the user in the DAL, goes through `allowAction`, validates with zod, keeps the seat inside its `where` and writes only through `writePlayedGameRecord`, in a transaction.

| Action                                           | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `appendPlayedMove(gameId, { expectedPly, san })` | One move, versioned by the ply the browser saw: `ok` with the server's reading of the board (`gameEnd`), `retry` answered as written, `stale` (another tab moved first), `invalid` (illegal) or `finished`. |
| `abandonPlayedGame(gameId, { expectedPly })`     | «Abandonar»: lost by whoever presses it, versioned like a move.                                                                                                                                             |
| `storePlayedGameReview(gameId, pgn)`             | The review's evaluations into a finished game; refused if one move differs.                                                                                                                                 |
| `copyPlayedGameToStudy(gameId, formData)`        | «Guardar en un estudio»: a `Game` with source `PLATFORM_GAME` in one of one's studies, indexed in the same transaction.                                                                                     |

`services/account/account.actions.ts` adds `setUserSlug` (the user name: the CHECK's pattern, not reserved, `slugTaken` on the unique index) and `setProfileDetails` (`statusLine`, `country`). A slug is proposed from the name before every account is created (`proposeUserSlug`); changing it leaves the old address answering 404.

The rules the actions apply are pure and tested without a database: `checkAppend` (`append-rules.ts`), `play-rules.ts`, `profile-rules.ts` and the single writer (`services/shared/played-game-record.ts`, tested with an in-memory writer that applies the same guards). What the actions do against the database is checked in the app (phase 3).

## Modo aventura

An adventure is a journey of twelve rivals, each a bot of a hidden group with the strength of a calibrated bot of the roster, with its progress **derived** from the person's `PlayedGame`s: the story, the rules, `startAdventureEncounter`, `getAdventureProgress`, the map, the art and the switch `ADVENTURE_ENABLED` are in [Adventure mode](/platform/adventure). What «Jugar» keeps of it:

- `/jugar` is the adventure's, and needs `ADVENTURE_ENABLED`: without it «Jugar» is off (`PLAY_PAGE_ENABLED`: no page, no tile in the tools menu, no «Jugar» on the profile), while the games already played still open from the profile.
- `startAdventureEncounter` is the only way to open a game against a bot, and it checks the story on the server first.
- A game against a rival is an ordinary `PlayedGame`; its page says the page of the notebook, the place and the attempt in its head, and it ends with «Volver al mapa».
- **Playing still never counts toward the streak:** `services/adventure` is one of the folders `no-activity.test.ts` walks.

## Free play, removed

Until 2026-10-25 «Partida libre» (`/jugar/partida-libre`) was a gallery of the 45 bots by group, with a suggested rival and a popup per bot (`?rival=<slug>`), and a game over offered «Revancha» (`rematchPlayedGame`) and «Otro rival». The owner chose to make every game against a bot an encounter of the adventure, so all of that went: the gallery's components (`PlayLobbySection`, `BotGallery`, `BotCard`, `BotSheet`, `OpenGameNotice`, `StartGameForm`), `startPlayedGame`, `rematchPlayedGame`, `listBotGroups`, `getBotBySlug`, `suggestedBot`, the route builders `freePlay` and `playAgainst`, and the cover's two tickets. **No data went**: the bots, their groups (`isListed` included) and the migrations stay, and the 45 listed bots are simply no longer offered.

- **Old free games** stay in the history and the profile: `/jugar/partidas/<id>` shows any `PlayedGame`, and the level against bots and the record against each bot count them as before.
- **An open free game**, if one was left, is not lost and does not block the adventure: its page still plays it — moves and «Abandonar» are the same actions —, and once it is over it goes on to «Ir a Jugar». Starting an encounter while it is open asks to replace it, as before, and `openGame` closes it (abandoned or aborted) in the same transaction. This was chosen as the least surprising path: nothing the person started disappears, and nothing new can be started outside the story.
- `/jugar/partida-libre` (with or without `?rival=`) redirects to `/jugar`, which answers 404 while «Jugar» is off.

## What the server cannot check

Both moves of a game against a bot leave the same browser: the server demands legality, turn order and a seat, not that the bot's move came from Stockfish at the bot's strength. So the scoreboard and the level are a record of what was played, never a rating, and nothing compares people by them.

## Seed and query budget

`prisma/seed-play.ts`, called by the seed: the three catalogs and the 57 bots (45 of the gallery and the 12 rivals of the adventure) upserted from the same `allBotSeeds()` the migrations' SQL is generated from, and the demo student's games, written through the single writer: three in the gallery — won against Tere, lost by resignation against Don Chuy, and one open against Lupita on the student's turn — only when they have played none, and nine in the adventure (`DEMO_ADVENTURE_GAMES`) only when they have played none against its rivals, which leave the map at 4 of 12 (★★★, ★★ after a loss, ★ after three, ★★★, and one lost game against the fifth, «Intento 2»). `prisma/seed-play.test.ts` replays them, the adventure's with `encounterStatesOf`.

The pages have a query budget (`/jugar` ≤ 5, `/perfil/<slug>` ≤ 7, without the session). `PLATFORM_DB_LOG=query` makes `getPlatformDb()` print every query, and the figure that counts is the one of `npm run build && npm run start`, one load at a time: `/ajustes`, whose page read one row, gave two, that row and the session (since 2026-10-25 it also reads today's minutes against the goal, `getStudyGoal`, as the home page does). Measured that way on 2026-10-09, with the demo seed, the same on every load: `/jugar` 3 (then the gallery: the open game, the groups with their bots, and the record against each; since 2026-10-25, by reading the code, 4 — the games against the adventure's rivals, the open game, the last games and the scoreboard), `/perfil/<slug>` 6 (the profile with its counts, the last games, the level, the scoreboard, the activity by day and this week's minutes; since 2026-10-25, by reading the code and not yet measured, 7: the week's minutes became the minutes of each day of this month and the last, `studyOverviewOf`, and the bots the scoreboard names, `listBotsById`, were added) and `/perfil/<slug>/partidas` 5 (the profile and the level for its head — `getProfileHeadOf`, not the whole profile —, the page's rows and count, and the rivals).
