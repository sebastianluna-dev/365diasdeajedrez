# Play («Jugar»)

Games against Stockfish bots of 400–2 800, saved and listed on the person's profile (`/perfil/<slug>`), and the «Tablero de análisis» under «Herramientas». Being built in phases since 2026-10-08. Both switches in `constants/platform/features.const.ts` stay off (`PLAY_ENABLED`, `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`) until the bots are calibrated and the owner has checked the screens: off, every page answers 404 — also a game opened by its id —, every action `notFound()` or `disabled`, and «Jugar» is not in the menu.

What is in place: the engine client, the bots and their strength ([Bots](/chess/bots)), the rules of a live game (`lib/chess/game-end.ts`, `lib/chess/live-game.ts`, `lib/play/`), the tables ([Data model → Played games](/platform/data-model#played-games-jugar)), the services and actions below, the lobby ([`PlayLobbySection`](/components/platform/sections/play/lobby/play-lobby-section)) and the game itself ([`LiveGameSection`](/components/platform/sections/play/live-game/live-game-section)), played on the one [`GameViewer`](/components/chess/game-viewer/game-viewer), the profile ([`ProfileHeaderSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-header-section), [`ProfileOverviewSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-overview-section), [`ProfileGamesSection`](/components/platform/sections/profile/profile-games-section)), the profile's fields in «Ajustes» and the teacher's «Partidas jugadas». The analysis board and «Importar partida» live under «Herramientas», behind `ANALYSIS_BOARD_ENABLED`: [Analysis board](/chess/analysis-board).

## Rules that do not bend

- **Playing never counts toward the streak.** No game, against a bot or a person, writes the activity log or its daily aggregate: the streak, the minutes and the days studied come from studying content, exercises, lessons and classes only. `services/play/no-activity.test.ts` walks the imports of `services/play`, `services/bots` and `services/profile` (`lib/testing/module-reach.ts`, the walker `components/loading-boundaries.test.ts` also uses) and fails if any reaches `services/shared/user-activity.service.ts`, or if a file there names the activity tables. Only `play.actions.ts` may reach the position index, to copy a game into a study.
- **A played game is never a study game.** It is a `PlayedGame`, not a `Game`: «Mis estudios», the explorer and the classes never see it. «Guardar en un estudio» makes a copy.
- **No clock, no undo, no hints against a bot.** The bot answers after a human-like delay (`thinkDelayMs`); the engine on the analysis board plays at full strength, instantly, never with a clock, and is never saved.

## Routes

| Route                               | What it is                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/jugar`                            | The lobby: the bots by group, «Continuar» for the open game.                                 |
| `/jugar/partidas/<id>`              | A game: played while it is open and the viewer holds a seat; read-only for everyone else.    |
| `/perfil/<slug>`                    | The profile: header, streak, level against bots, scoreboard by bot and the last eight games. |
| `/perfil/<slug>/partidas`           | Every game, twenty a page, filtered by rival and result.                                     |
| `/herramientas/tablero-de-analisis` | The analysis board (phase 4).                                                                |

The three prefixes are in `PROTECTED_PATH_PREFIXES` and in the proxy's matcher; `lib/platform-routes.ts` builds every address (`play`, `playAgainst`, `playedGame`, `profile`, `userProfile`, `userProfileGames`, `analysisBoard`, `importGame`).

## Who may see what

`Viewer` (`services/play/play-rules.ts`) is the person, the teacher row they hold and whether they are staff, resolved by `currentViewer()` from the DAL. Every read puts the rule **inside its `where`**, so a game or a profile the viewer may not see is never read:

| Viewer  | Played games (`playedGameVisibleWhere`)                  | Profiles (`profileVisibleWhere`)                 |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Anyone  | The games where they hold a seat.                        | Their own.                                       |
| Teacher | Also those of their students under an active assignment. | Also their students' under an active assignment. |
| Staff   | Every game.                                              | Every profile.                                   |

Reading is not writing: every action demands a seat (`seatOf`), and a teacher or the staff see a student's game read-only.

## Services

- `services/bots/` — `listBotGroups()` (the lobby, by group and order) and `getBotBySlug(slug)`; only active bots.
- `services/play/play.service.ts` — `getOpenPlayedGame()` (the viewer's open game against a bot), `getPlayedGame(id)`, `listPlayedGamesOf(userId, { page, rival?, result? })` (open first, then the last move, then the start; `PLAYED_GAMES_PAGE_SIZE` 20), `recentPlayedGamesOf(userId)` (`PROFILE_RECENT_GAMES` 8), `botScoreboardOf(userId)`, `botScoreAgainst(userId, botId)` and `botLevelSummaryOf(userId)`.
- `services/profile/` — `getProfileOf(slug)` and `getOwnSlug()`.
- `services/study-goal` — `streakSummaryOf(userId)`, the streak shown on a profile (read, never written by playing).

**«Nivel contra bots»** (`botLevelOf`, `levelSeries`): over the last ten finished games against bots, the average of the bots' Elo plus 400 per net win spread over the games, rounded to 25 and kept within 400–2 800; nothing under five games. An aborted game counts for nothing (`countsForScore`); a resignation or an abandonment is a loss. Computed when read, never stored, and never a rating: `isRated` is false against a bot, by a CHECK.

## Actions

`services/play/play.actions.ts`. Each one checks the switch, resolves the user in the DAL, goes through `allowAction`, validates with zod, keeps the seat inside its `where` and writes only through `writePlayedGameRecord`, in a transaction.

| Action                                           | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `startPlayedGame(formData)`                      | Opens a game against a bot (`bot`, `color` white/black/random). With `replace`, the open game against a bot is closed first — abandoned after two moves of one's own, aborted before — else the open game is returned. A person keeps one open game against a bot, which the database backs with two partial unique indexes (one per colour); two tabs pressing at once land on the same game through their `P2002`. |
| `rematchPlayedGame(gameId)`                      | The same bot, colours swapped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `appendPlayedMove(gameId, { expectedPly, san })` | One move, versioned by the ply the browser saw: `ok` with the server's reading of the board (`gameEnd`), `retry` answered as written, `stale` (another tab moved first), `invalid` (illegal) or `finished`.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `abandonPlayedGame(gameId, { expectedPly })`     | «Abandonar»: lost by whoever presses it, versioned like a move.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `storePlayedGameReview(gameId, pgn)`             | The review's evaluations into a finished game; refused if one move differs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `copyPlayedGameToStudy(gameId, formData)`        | «Guardar en un estudio»: a `Game` with source `PLATFORM_GAME` in one of one's studies, indexed in the same transaction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

`services/account/account.actions.ts` adds `setUserSlug` (the user name: the CHECK's pattern, not reserved, `slugTaken` on the unique index) and `setProfileDetails` (`statusLine`, `country`). A slug is proposed from the name before every account is created (`proposeUserSlug`); changing it leaves the old address answering 404.

The rules the actions apply are pure and tested without a database: `checkAppend` (`append-rules.ts`), `play-rules.ts`, `profile-rules.ts` and the single writer (`services/shared/played-game-record.ts`, tested with an in-memory writer that applies the same guards). What the actions do against the database is checked in the app (phase 3).

## What the server cannot check

Both moves of a game against a bot leave the same browser: the server demands legality, turn order and a seat, not that the bot's move came from Stockfish at the bot's strength. So the scoreboard and the level are a record of what was played, never a rating, and nothing compares people by them.

## Seed and query budget

`prisma/seed-play.ts`, called by the seed: the three catalogs and the 45 bots upserted from the same roster the migrations' SQL is generated from, and three games for the demo student — won against Tere, lost by resignation against Don Chuy, and one open against Lupita on the student's turn — only when they have played none, written through the single writer. `prisma/seed-play.test.ts` replays them.

The pages have a query budget (`/jugar` ≤ 3, `/perfil/<slug>` ≤ 7, without the session). `PLATFORM_DB_LOG=query` makes `getPlatformDb()` print every query, and the figure that counts is the one of `npm run build && npm run start` on a cold load. A first reading on 2026-10-08, on `next dev` with the local database, counted Postgres's committed transactions (`pg_stat_database`) around three warm loads of each page and took off the 2–3 that the session and the shell cost on `/ajustes`, whose page reads one row: `/jugar` ≈ 3–4 (the groups, the open game, the record, and the person's own card — the session carries no user name or flag —: one over the budget), `/perfil/<slug>` ≈ 5–6, `/perfil/<slug>/partidas` ≈ 8 (the profile, the page's count and rows, and the rivals). To be confirmed on a production build before the switch goes on.

## Verification in the app

What was checked on 2026-10-08 with the switch on in a local copy, against `next dev` and the seeded local database, driving system Chrome with Playwright and the demo accounts (`constants/platform/demo-user.const.ts`). The seed leaves `alumno.demo` a game won against Tere, one lost against Don Chuy and one open against Lupita on their turn; repeat the steps after `npm run db:seed` on a database where the student has played nothing.

1. `alumno.demo` opens `/jugar`: «Tienes una partida en curso contra Lupita» with «Continuar», the suggested rival «después de Tere» (Beto), the record on Tere's and Don Chuy's cards, «Partida en curso» on Lupita's.
2. «Continuar» opens the game with «Te toca mover»; a move on the board shows «Pensando…» and the turning arc on Lupita's strip, and her move arrives after its delay.
3. Reloading the page keeps every move: they were saved one by one.
4. «Abandonar» opens «¿Abandonas la partida?»; confirming tints Lupita's strip «Ganó» and the student's «Perdió», both «por abandono», with «Revancha», «Otro rival», «Analizar», «Guardar en un estudio» and the scoreboard (0 – 1) under the board.
5. `/jugar?rival=beto` opens Beto's popup; «Negras» and «Jugar» open a game in which Beto moves first.
6. The same game in a second tab: after a move in the first, a move in the second brings «Esta partida cambió en otra pestaña.» with «Recargar».
7. `profesor.demo`, the student's teacher, opens the finished game: the board, the moves and the strips, with no «Abandonar» and no «Revancha».

8. `/perfil` takes `alumno.demo` to `/perfil/alumno-demo`: the head with «Editar perfil» and «Jugar», the five tabs, the three cards and the games as cards; «Partidas» lists them as a timeline grouped by day, with both filters.
9. In «Ajustes», a new user name opens the profile at the new address and leaves the old one on the not-found page; a name already taken comes back with «Ese nombre de usuario ya está en uso»; a status line and a country appear under the name and beside it. (Put back afterwards.)
10. `profesor.demo` opens the student's profile without «Editar perfil» and with only «Visión general» and «Partidas»; their student's page shows «Partidas jugadas» with «Ver su perfil». `staff.demo` opens the same profile, read-only.
11. The student's activity on `/inicio` does not change after playing: no game writes it.
