# Pages & sections

All public pages live in `app/(frontend)` and share the root layout there: `metadataBase` from `lib/site-url.ts`, default Open Graph and Twitter card metadata, a skip link to `#contenido`, Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel (only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_ENV=production` and the ids are set) and Vercel Analytics (only when `VERCEL=1`, otherwise the script 404s locally).

## The site's new look («M4»)

On 2026-10-26 the owner moved the public site to the blue of the platform's new design: Material 3 Expressive, no orange and no dark theme. The home page is built in it, the teachers' landing reuses it, and every public page wears its header and footer; the blog, `/nosotros`, `/mentor/[slug]` and the clock keep their own dark or light body until each is redone.

- **Tokens.** `app/(frontend)/site-m4.css`, imported only by the site's root layout, declares the `--color-m4-*` roles (canvas, panel, the two containers, the inks, the blue and its containers, success, error, the streak, the board, three shadows) and the two families (`--font-site-display`, Poppins; `--font-site-text`, the system's sans-serif) on `html.site-m4`, with `color-scheme: light` on the same element. It points Material's `--md-sys-color-*` and typefaces at them, as `platform.css` does on the platform. Because the rules hang from the class the site's layout writes, `/admin` (Payload's own root layout) and the platform (whose layout imports `globals.css` but not this sheet) never see them, and the old pages' `--color-*` keep their values.
- **Material.** `app/(frontend)/material-styles.ts` imports `@material/web`'s `labs/gb` system sheet and the button, chip, icon button, ripple and focus ring sheets: only CSS, in cascade layers that sit above `globals.css`'s `@layer reset`, so the page stays static. Buttons are written with `mdButton(color, size)` from `lib/material/material-classes.ts`; an SVG icon inside one is `.site-md-icon`.
- **Type.** Poppins (500, 600, 700, 800; `sitePoppins` in `app/(frontend)/fonts.ts`, not preloaded) for titles and figures; text in the system's sans-serif, as the canvas draws it.
- **A page** of the new look wraps its content in `.site-page` (the canvas, the text font, and the body's background through `:has()`), so the focus ring turns blue and the skip link too.
- **Shared pieces.** [`SiteHeader`](/components/site/sections/shell/site-header/site-header-section), [`SiteFooter`](/components/site/sections/shell/site-footer/site-footer-section), [`SiteSection`](/components/site/shared/site-section), [`SiteEyebrow`](/components/site/shared/site-eyebrow), [`SiteHeading`](/components/site/shared/site-heading), [`SiteLead`](/components/site/shared/site-lead), [`SiteWhatsappButton`](/components/site/shared/site-whatsapp-button), [`SiteLogo`](/components/site/shared/site-logo) and [`SiteBoard`](/components/site/shared/site-board).
- **Leads.** There is no form backend: every «Agenda…», «Pide…» or «Pregunta…» opens WhatsApp with a written message, to the number of the `site-settings` Global (`getWhatsappHref(message)` in `services/site-settings`).

Since 2026-10-26 the home page is the academy's «365 días de ajedrez» in the site's new look, section by section as the owner assembled it on the canvas (`LandingAcademiaParte1` and `2`). Its copy is `academyHome` (`config/academy-home.config.ts`, typed in `types/academy-home.types.ts`): new copy goes there and not into Payload, which would need a migration. The CMS keeps feeding what it fed — the FAQ's questions (`getFaqData()`), the testimonials (`getReviewsData()`) and the WhatsApp number — through `services/home` and `services/site-settings`, deduplicated per request and cached between requests (see [Caching](/architecture/caching-errors-logging)). The old sections (hero with its photo, programme, teacher, packages with prices, resources, the chat-style reviews, the sticky bar with the 15-minute countdown) are gone with their components; their Globals (`home-hero`, `home-program`, `home-teacher`, `home-packages`, `home-cta`, `home-mentors`) stay in Payload, unread by the home page.

Every board on the page is a still position on the material board ([`SiteBoard`](/components/site/shared/site-board), a Server Component), so the page loads no chessground. Its only client pieces are the header's menu, the course cards' toggles and the FAQ's accordion.

**What the owner still has to give.** The page draws nothing it cannot back: the four figures (`academyHome.figures`, hidden until all four are real), the teachers of the team (`academyHome.team.members`, empty: the section shows how a teacher is assigned and no faces), and the testimonials come only from the CMS.

## Teachers' landing — `/para-profesores`

The page that sells the platform to teachers, clubs and schools under the name **«Plataforma Ajedrez 365»** (the owner's decisions of 2026-10-26, [Pending decisions](/platform/pending-decisions#the-public-site-and-the-sales-landing-2026-10-26)). `/para-maestros` answers a **308** to it (`redirects()` in `next.config.ts`). `app/(frontend)/para-profesores/page.tsx` is static like the home page (`revalidate = 3600`); the only CMS value it reads is the WhatsApp number of `site-settings`, whose hook already revalidates the whole site. It is in the sitemap and carries its own title, description, canonical and Open Graph site name. It wears the site's shared `SiteHeader` and `SiteFooter` with the teachers' own links and main button (`siteConfig.teachersShell`: «Cómo funciona», «Qué incluye», «Preguntas», «Agenda una demo» on WhatsApp; the footer links to the academy's home, its band asks «¿Buscas clases de ajedrez?» and leads back to the academy, the reverse of the academy's «¿Eres maestro?», and «Iniciar sesión» is the footer's own). The header's wordmark is the academy's: `SiteLogo` has no «Plataforma Ajedrez 365» variant yet.

**There is no lead backend and no database behind the forms.** The demo form and the seats calculator write their fields into a message (`teachers-messages.ts`, tested) and open WhatsApp with it (`buildWhatsappUrl`, the number from `site-settings`), and they say so before and after. Every «Hoy» / «Próximamente» follows [the platform's pages](/platform): today the academy schedules the classes, they are joined by link, and a teacher keeping track of payments, creating classes and the seats are still to come.

## Blog — `/blog` and `/blog/[slug]`

- `/blog` is a **Server Component** driven by search params. `services/articles/article-listing.ts` (pure, tested) parses `?categoria=` and `?pagina=`, filters and paginates (`ARTICLES_PER_PAGE = 9`, `ALL_CATEGORY = "Todos"`) and builds canonical hrefs with `listingHref(query)`. Category filter and pagination are plain links with `aria-current`, so they work without JavaScript and every state has a URL.
- The listing uses `getArticleSummaries()` — every published article **without** its Lexical `content` — so the page payload no longer carries the body of every post.
- `/blog/[slug]` uses `getArticleBySlug()` (drafts visible only to logged-in Payload users, through the collection's `read` access) and renders the Lexical tree with `components/site/sections/blog/article-content`. Custom blocks:
  - `imageBlock` → `next/image` with Cloudinary URL;
  - `calloutBlock` → info / tip / warning box;
  - `chessDiagramBlock` → `StaticDiagram` (Server Component, no chessground) with `context="article"`;
  - `chessGameBlock` → `ChessBoardLazy` with the PGN, title, players, event and date.
- Related articles come from the same summaries. Metadata: canonical URL, `openGraph.type = "article"`, SEO tab overrides (`metaTitle`, `metaDescription`, `ogImage`).

## Other pages

- `/nosotros` — static about page (`components/site/sections/nosotros/*`).
- `/mentor/[slug]` — profile built from the `home-mentors` Global (`services/mentors`), with FIDE title options and a lazy board for the featured game.
- `/reloj-de-ajedrez` — the chess clock. `components/site/sections/chess-clock/chess-clock.comp.tsx` picks the desktop or mobile variant with `useSyncExternalStore` on `matchMedia("(max-width: 766px)")` so only **one** clock instance runs; `hooks/use-chess-clock.hook.ts` owns the state (time controls 3+2, 5+0, 10+0, 15+10, 30+0), ticks every 100 ms from `Date.now()`, and handles the space bar only when the event does not come from a focused control and is not a key repeat.

## Accessibility and performance notes

- Global `:focus-visible` outline (recoloured on the light platform theme), skip link in every layout, `id="contenido"` on each `<main>`, `visibility: hidden` for the closed mobile menu, ARIA on the header dropdown, `aria-modal` + Escape on the promotion dialog.
- Contrast: `--platform-text-subtle` is `#737373`, text for the white surfaces only (4.7:1 on `surface`); on a grey or tinted fill the text is `--platform-text-muted` (see [Styling](/ui/styling)); muted greys on the public site are tokens (`--color-muted-on-dark`, `--color-muted-on-light`).
- Fonts are woff2 subsets (Latin + punctuation + arrows + check mark); `chessGlyph` and SF Pro are not preloaded.
- Lighthouse is measured only against `npm run build && npm run start`. On 2026-09-09 the mobile performance score of `/` went from 80 to 92; what remains (ten blocking stylesheets, which merging the sections' files would not reduce) is described in [Deployment › Performance](/operations/deployment#performance) and in `IMPROVEMENTS.md` #70.
